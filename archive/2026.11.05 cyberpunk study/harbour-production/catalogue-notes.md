It follows the [active brief](../design-brief.md), [production direction](README.md) and [icon workflow](../../../docs/icon-guide.md). Each entry names the exact item, its production family, visible subject, retained ancestry and nearest misleading interpretation. `size` records the final square source tier: **320** for simple parts, **448** for ordinary objects and route diagrams, **640** for nested or composite creations, and **fixed576/fixed768** for registered scenery. Fixed artwork uses scene scale, not portable-item sizing. These tiers were checked at playing size; large reading panels carry comparisons that need more room.

## Continuity decisions

- Reuse the actual ship, bottle, tree and room components. Inversion changes the enclosure order: ship fittings stay with the ship; window, blue corners and furniture stay with the room; round blue-tree cradle or square gold-tree cradle stays with its tree. Glass is always the middle layer.
- A copy initially looks identical to its source. Show one copied object per portable item, without invented colours, badges or duplicate trunks to signal independence. Plural parts supplies, by contrast, visibly contain multiple parts.
- Every silver-tree descendant retains the original translucent leaf fan as well as its notched fork. Blue bells and added gold fans do not replace that starter foliage. The merging frame deduplicates one shared tree; ordinary garden assembly does not. The complete two-tide garden contains three tree structures: tall merged tree, low blue tree and gold island tree.
- Garden sphere and Regatta garden sphere preserve the earlier two-tide garden, including its orbiting droplet and visiting bench, before the ray and firefly. Theatre globe and Blue-hour theatre globe preserve the earlier two-puppet play before the garden backdrop. The live window shows the current garden play.
- The coloured performer has yellow wings and no long red tail. The opaque black performer has the tailed glass parent's outline and initially no movement. The luminous regatta descendant comes from that still-existing tailed glass parent, never from the black outline.
- Use shared route geometry: Up, Across, Around, Up–Across, Up–Around, and Around–Up. Mark stops and the complete retraced return. Up–Around belongs to the black puppet/firefly; Around–Up belongs to the ray. No path gains a third outward segment. No path carries the source's body, light or place names into its receiver.
- Keep the five fixed stage appearances on one registered canvas, and all eight pool appearances on another. Every pool state retains exactly its installed subset of raft, creature and forest. The raft crosses at water level; the creature starts above the near bank. Reflections are appearances, not additional installed objects.
- The final ship retains its mandatory established garden and play as the touring regatta enters the side bay. Optional creature, seat, room and harbour-light choices must not be silently selected by the final illustration. The gallery keeps the source regatta.
