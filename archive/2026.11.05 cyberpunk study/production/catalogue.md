## Adopted production decisions

- Circular repair-sticker tokens: strong navy/cream silhouettes, cyan, warm amber, restrained vermilion and sparse screenprint grain. These are representative objects, supplies, records and system states; backgrounds supply atmosphere.
- A shared `drawing_id` means one exact runtime asset URL, while story identities and names stay distinct. Software configuration, authorization, test results, provenance and evidence conclusions usually change no visible hardware. The same neutral record picture can carry several named findings without a parade of subtly different page icons.
- Give physical additions distinct drawings: cooler filter/fan alternatives, independent logger and backup power; tablet microphone/button/hood combinations; paired radio reserves; pump sensor/direct stop; rack leads/selector; wicket/sign/opening; all eight workshop shutter/paint/display combinations.
- Moth remains the same chipped orange terminal, stubby antenna, ASK ME sticker and monochrome display. Retain the new fan and reachable off switch into dawn. No brain, humanoid or duplicate Moth. The other controller is low, wide, orange-grey and has two antennas; general service handsets are tall dark teal-grey with neutral screens.
- `clinic-delivery` shares the sealed clinic crate image. June’s trolley remains separately available, so a loaded-trolley picture would duplicate it. Only the dawn cart states depict the actual trolley with its fitted insulated carrier.
- Two radios require two protected reserves. Solar states show two matched charging sets with dark panels at night. The tested-link token shows the invariant radio-and-reserve core, omitting the optional charging perimeter without asserting it absent.
- `migration-monitor` is the sole fixed hotspot: a stable circular iconic read-only service display with battery clock. No literal room registration, transparent scenery cutout, changing installed assembly or overlays.

The JSON records further family continuity requirements and every exact-sharing rationale. Record drawings are notation, not invented printing actions; neutral strokes must not encode unsupported pass/fail, trust or safety claims.
