## Visual research and selected language

The [Met's screenprinting demonstration](https://www.metmuseum.org/perspectives/materials-and-techniques-printmaking-screenprint) shows separate ink layers and registration, including Liz Zanis's *Memory Sticks*: everyday technical objects can become a coherent printed vocabulary. Its fine linework would be too delicate for these small pieces, so this story uses larger solid silhouettes. The [V&A's printmaking feature](https://www.vam.ac.uk/articles/creating-screenprint-with-adam-bridgland) supplies a concrete example of bright overlapping color and economical poster communication. These are technique references, not shipped source images or instructions to imitate an artist.

The selected original direction is a neighborhood repair printshop: opaque navy circles, warm cream rims, amber hardware and cyan screens, sparse vermilion faults, worn ink edges and restrained grain. Shapes carry object identity at 64px; names and readings carry invisible configuration, provenance and findings. Records vary by physical form and useful diagrams. An evidence icon does not claim to communicate its entire conclusion unaided.

All movable artwork is symbolic. Large infrastructure uses the same token scale as small tools; physical scale remains in the story. Moth retains one orange wedge case, chipped lower-right corner, short upper-left antenna and ASK ME sticker. Courier equipment must be a different tall gray/teal family. Backgrounds depict architecture and atmosphere, with quiet working space and no changing inventoried devices painted into the scene. The dawn monitor is a stable circular hotspot.

## Production and export

Use the built-in image-generation tool. Selected source atlases, exact requests, input references and extraction geometry belong here; game-ready exports live in [the story assets](../assets). Portable illustrations are exported at 256×256 RGBA for nominal 64px circles at the 390×844 reference viewport; the fixed migration monitor is 384×384 for 96px. Mechanical crop, padding, resizing and alpha cleanup preserve the generated drawing; creative repairs use the image tool. Backgrounds are uniformly cropped/resized to 780×1688, with original masters retained. No runtime tint is added.

The pilot atlas is a style/identity anchor, not a guarantee of all depicted meanings. Recognition and informed art-direction reviews are separate fresh assignments. Their concise outcomes are in [art-review](../art-review/); raw guesses, browser captures and review packets are disposable scratch. Selected contact sheets are retained deliverables.

## Retained provenance

The catalogue freezes the accepted pre-art manuscript and its original source lines. Exact requests, input-path JSON, extraction records and author manifests preserve the study layout and production-time status; they are historical provenance, not current paths or final review verdicts. The final [development outcomes](../working-notes.md#production-family-reviews-and-browser-integration) and dated `art-review/` summaries own acceptance. Scripts likewise retain their original study-layout defaults; do not run them unchanged against the archived directory.

`icons/root-production.json`, `icons/b02-b06.json`, `icons/source-sign-repair.json`, `icons/neighbor-bypass-repair.json` and `backgrounds/requests.json` identify selected sources and repairs. Final contact sheets use the selected runtime exports. Creative work used the built-in image tool; export scripts only register, crop, matte and resize those images.
