# Cyberpunk study — abandoned

The user abandoned the entire Cyberpunk project on **6 November 2026**, after playing **The Travelling Harbour** for roughly ten chapters. Both this rewrite and **No Fixed Address** are retired. This is a failure archive for future authors, with no pending redevelopment or release. Historical statements that a draft is current, accepted or ready for review describe their original development phase.

The user's decisive finding was that the many arbitrary game objects were not worthwhile. Mars's real, relatable objects gave construction meaning; the invented fiction here was not a sufficiently good story to earn the required investment. Valid moves were easy to find, and the user's enjoyment of reading Theology and Ideas ruled out treating reading volume itself as the central failure. The [dated decision record](../../notes.md#6-november-2026--worthwhile-hook-and-retirement-of-cosmic-and-cyberpunk) preserves the diagnosis. The standing [worthwhile-hook requirement and cautionary tale](../../docs/story-authoring.md#require-a-worthwhile-hook) govern future authoring.

## Preservation and use

The original supporting archive and all material moved from `projects/cyberpunk-study/` share this first-date archive home. The two earlier overview documents are retained as dated records. The rewrite's production directory is now `harbour-production/`, keeping both distinct production families intact. The manuscripts, artwork, production inputs and structured playtest deliverables are preserved. Source paths, hashes, prompts and commands inside historical records identify their original versions; old preview URLs and tool commands are not current instructions. There is no live Cyberpunk/Harbour manuscript or active brief.

Both versions remain excluded from the published catalogue and hosting through the existing archive exclusion. Archival does not migrate or erase browser saves at their former URLs.
