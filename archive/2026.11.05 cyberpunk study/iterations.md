# Reconsideration outcomes

## 5 November 2026 — Reopen the premise

Human feedback: the shipped story was hard to play because it asked small round illustrations to carry many subtle things. The user requested divergent brainstorming at every stage, independent reviewers and several iterations toward a story carried by the objects or ideas it creates. Adopted: set the old treatment aside; explore two or three different combining grammars per round; judge causal continuity and forward understanding before length or art.

The earlier six AI campaigns, component-continuity reviews and browser passes remain evidence for their recorded scopes. They did not predict this human experience. No aggregate old score is used to reject the new feedback.

C's branches are mainly two supplied upgrade kits, and local deployment geometry does not automatically fit a new place after merge. Adopted response: make the descendants carry patterns actually composed in two places, with artistic rather than unproved navigation/shade claims. The final result preserves authored contributions and common ancestry, while both original works remain. Do not ban direct assembly just to make merging indispensable.
