# Gate checkpoint: test protocol

## What this tests

One short problem using a different useful property of earlier creations. The direct route adds no new assembly; that may be satisfying rather than insufficient. The alternate route uses an earlier material in a newly weighted carrier. Do not demand strategy or difficulty at every step.

Use `FRESH-PLAYER-ASSIGNMENT.txt` unchanged. Default `new` replays the frozen earned prefix in `third-job-checkpoint.json`. Earlier creation history must be identified as authored setup, not attributed to the fresh checkpoint player. `new-full` exists only for separate whole-candidate tests and author smoke checks.

## Observe without coaching

1. Which available object does the player consider first, and why?
2. Does the scene's human purpose and need for a clear opening guide their judgment?
3. Does the outcome read as people being able to carry parcels, or only as a badge turning gold?
4. Is direct reuse satisfying or disappointingly automatic?
5. If they make a weighted basket, do they recognize the earlier basket and material rather than seeing an unrelated new token?
6. Do rejected crate/loose-gravel placements seem physically understandable or arbitrary? Preserve objections rather than defending the model.
7. Does any unimplemented action again become the main curiosity?

No pre-play mention of tension, counterweights, Aristotle, gravity, the two intended routes, or which property is supposed to become useful. The ordinary scene and item descriptions remain available. Logical descriptions may be richer than browser access; this is not visual UI evidence.

## After the first report only

Ask a few open diagnostic questions:

- “Could another available object have done the job differently?”
- “What mattered about the thing you used here, compared with what the description says it was used for earlier?”
- “Would a crate holding the gate open be enough if it blocked the parcels?”
- “Did the gravel need to have been sorted in order to have weight?”

The last question guards against a false causal lesson: sorting did not create mass. Responses are prompted diagnostic evidence, not actions actually played.

## Evidence boundaries

- Taking the direct rope route does not fresh-test the weighted basket route.
- A checkpoint player did not personally create the earlier objects in this session.
- Narrated successful deployment is not simulated force, load, or gate motion.
- This does not establish full Aristotelian teleology or a long campaign's appeal.
- The v2 release and its positive/negative evidence remain independent.

Stop at completion or the player's chosen stopping condition. Do not force a second solution to improve the result. A later separate assignment may test another route, but must be labelled accordingly.
