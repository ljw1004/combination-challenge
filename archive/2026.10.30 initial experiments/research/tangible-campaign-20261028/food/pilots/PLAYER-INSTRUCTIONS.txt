Play the kitchen scene from the view the program gives you. Make the food requested; you may try other plausible combinations. There is no score or speed target. You do not need to inspect the underlying code or files.

Use a new session name. Choose one segment supplied by the facilitator:

node pilot-player.ts init YOUR_SESSION SEGMENT
node pilot-player.ts view YOUR_SESSION
node pilot-player.ts inspect YOUR_SESSION THING_ID
node pilot-player.ts hint YOUR_SESSION THING_ID
node pilot-player.ts drop YOUR_SESSION SOURCE_ID TARGET_ID
node pilot-player.ts undo YOUR_SESSION

Places have @ before their target ID. A drop may place an ingredient at a station or combine it. Completing the requested dishes opens the next scene automatically. Stop when the program says the pilot ended.

Please think aloud: what are you trying, what did you expect, and did the result make sense? If you get stuck, describe why before asking for a hint. After the segment, say what felt like real making, what felt routine, and whether something you made earlier was genuinely useful.

Do not read scenario.json, author.py, routes/, docs/, dependency-plan.json or evidence/. They contain the author's solution and would spoil an independent attempt.
