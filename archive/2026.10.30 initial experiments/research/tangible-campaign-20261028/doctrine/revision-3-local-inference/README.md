# Revision 3 — local inference before a wider doctrine

This is a separate candidate, not an overwrite of frozen v2. It responds to the preserved **stopped simulated session** `fresh_doctrine_a`: five attempts, two accepted, three rejected. The player understood the apparent thesis but stopped because semantically reasonable applications/comparisons were rejected. It is negative evidence about the design, not an unsuccessful player to be rescued.

## Why earlier tests missed it

The 40 route-order permutations and seven alternative-route combinations in v2 selected only authored legal edges. They established execution and lifecycle safety. They did not sample a fresh player’s proposed meanings, check whether labels matched the logical scope assigned to them, or notice that a newly created local observation had no currently available partner. The label “Loss, not another thing” sounded like the very claim needed against the objection; its graph role silently required a missing theological intermediate. The invitation also promised body/will comparison while the graph rejected it.

## Opening redesign

There are three distinct inferential levels:

1. **Local case:** a wound heals without an evil substance departing. The empirical case or Augustine’s text applied to the case can support this observation. It can immediately answer the local substance objection.
2. **Moral comparison:** a deliberate wrong can be compared with bodily damage, or interpreted through the text. The product distinguishes faulty choosing from injured flesh rather than treating sickness as guilt.
3. **Wider interpretation:** the visible Genesis premise and the local case support reconstructing Augustine’s more general account. Its reading explicitly says an example illustrates rather than proves a universal claim.

The first two goals are visible in parallel. Genesis itself now occupies `created-goodness`; there is no redundant combine-two-sources placeholder to manufacture before the observation can be useful. A local result has an immediate partner. The core is **six earned combinations**, not seven forced steps padded to hit a quota.

The candidate has 69 recipes, 62 authored combinations, 116 items and eight chapters. Five independent output-choice groups yield 72 tested whole-route variants (3 local-case routes × 3 moral routes × 2 wider-interpretation routes × 2 philosophical routes × 2 mercy routes). Some choices require different prerequisite ordering; tests now schedule available prerequisites rather than blindly replacing an action at a fixed index.
