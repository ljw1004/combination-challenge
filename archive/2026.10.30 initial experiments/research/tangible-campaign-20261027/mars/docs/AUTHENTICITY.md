# Grounding and deliberate abstractions

The fiction is an already-equipped expedition, not an astronaut making industrial apparatus from raw Mars minerals. Primitives appear in broad pre-positioned field caches or recovered mission vehicles, not as gifts when a task changes. Components are matched manufactured modules; no assembly is a real procedure.

- NASA's **The Fact and Fiction of Martian Dust Storms** describes sunlight loss and pervasive dust, not Earth-strength wind forces at the same speed. Weather motivation uses obscured route and reduced power; it should not claim a hurricane flings vehicles. https://www.nasa.gov/solar-system/the-fact-and-fiction-of-martian-dust-storms/
- NASA/JPL's water-ice mapping motivates an accessible resource and a sampling story; it does not establish that a particular fictional outcrop is readily mined, potable, or uncontaminated. Water treatment uses recovered certified equipment and test records, with no promise that filtration alone makes unknown water safe. https://www.nasa.gov/centers-and-facilities/jpl/where-should-future-astronauts-land-on-mars-follow-the-water/
- MOXIE demonstrated engineered oxygen generation from the Martian atmosphere. The scenario restores a fictional scaled mission plant using manufactured modules; it does not infer that this demonstration supplies an entire crew/ascent vehicle or offer chemical process settings. https://www.nasa.gov/space-technology-mission-directorate/tdm/mars-oxygen-in-situ-resource-utilization-experiment-moxie/
- Ingenuity supports a purpose-built rotorcraft reconnaissance premise in the very thin atmosphere, not a generic drone built from arbitrary scrap. The scout is recovered designed hardware with matched parts. https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/missions/ingenuity/

Growth, observations, signal acquisition and flight time are compressed by narrative transitions, not literally instantaneous. Crops use brought substrate and an enclosed qualified growing system; no implication that untreated Martian soil is safe for food. Geological records remain observations and interpretations, not automatic proof of ancient life. Antenna alignment, seals, pressure vessels and ascent are major abstractions requiring specialist review before any claim of technical realism.

Sources were read during authoring. These notes establish useful bounds, not certification of each recipe. Specific concerns remain: naming an enclosed pressure-sensor unit a barometer must retain a pressure port, the vehicle-restoration arc is overcompressed, and too many spare parts conveniently fit. An independent authenticity pass should reject weak recipes rather than merely decorate them with sources.
