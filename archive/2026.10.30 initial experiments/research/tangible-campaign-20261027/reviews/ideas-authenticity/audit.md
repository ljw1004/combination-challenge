## What the verified sources actually support

1. **Hutton:** IUGS, *Siccar Point*, description and scientific-tradition sections (web lines 58–69), identifies the unconformity as evidence for cycles and long time. This supports the README's rejection of an instant deep-time eureka. It does not support all of Hutton's system, numerical Earth ages, or fossil correlation. Discovery should say an existing account is being tested. Edinburgh's intellectual setting is not present in the playable chapter; an Edinburgh discussion plus a concrete Siccar field observation would meet that interest without conflating locations. [IUGS](https://iugs-geoheritage.org/geoheritage_sites/siccar-point/)

2. **Vesalius:** NLM, paragraphs on the *Fabrica* (lines 9–12), explicitly describes both dependence on Galen and confirmation/refutation, strong contemporary reactions, and multiple artists. Keep that complexity. Replace abstract `inherited-diagram` with a specifically sourced plate/claim and a corresponding observation; select and verify that example before authoring. Do not claim a particular jaw/bone correction on this source alone. Illustration is knowledge work, not decoration; a plate comparison can reuse perspective/drawing meaningfully. [NLM](https://www.nlm.nih.gov/exhibition/historicalanatomies/vesalius_bio.html)

3. **Harvey:** OU's book exhibit summary (line 49) specifically lists quantitative, experimental, teleological and analogical arguments. A quantity contradiction is a legitimate slice, not the whole method. Give a transparently illustrative numerical estimate, or a verified historical calculation with units; `heartbeat-count` × volume is a volume, whereas a heart **rate** × volume is flow per time. Presently `circulation-01/02` risks applying time twice unless the input is clarified. [OU Libraries](https://galileo.ou.edu/exhibits/anatomical-exercises-dr-william-harvey.html)

4. **Capillaries:** APS's historical research states that Malpighi's microscopic work followed Harvey's death and discusses his 1661 letter to Borelli. Existing recipe caveats are correct. The item attribution and generic discovery should be repaired, not the distinction removed. Full-page fetch was 403, but the search index returned the relevant section; no claim of reading the full article. [APS research](https://journals.physiology.org/doi/10.1152/ajpheart.00704.2007)

5. **Longitude:** RMG's sections on H1 trials, the rival schemes, and awards (lines 180–224) support repeated trial, lunar comparison, and replication as an award condition. This is strong controversy material: different parties can disagree over what counts as a successful demonstration without making Maskelyne a cartoon villain. Smithsonian's indexed explanation explicitly compares reference time and local ship time. Direct Smithsonian open was rejected; this latter verification is indexed text only. [RMG](https://www.rmg.co.uk/stories/time/harrisons-clocks-longitude-problem), [Smithsonian](https://timeandnavigation.si.edu/multimedia-asset/using-lunar-distances-to-find-longitude)

6. **Alexandria:** Schironi, printed pp.55–56, says the ancient format differs from modern critical editions and explains lemmata, critical signs and commentary. Particularly decisive is p.55: “in the absence of line or paragraph numeration”. This is a direct mismatch with `texts-02`, not merely missing evidence. Adopt the source's concrete variants/signs rather than a generic modern stemmatic workflow. [Schironi research PDF](https://sites.lsa.umich.edu/schironi/wp-content/uploads/sites/259/2024/10/43_Textual-Scholarship-in-Alexandria-.pdf)

7. **Calculus:** OpenStax §5.4, net-change discussion and equations, distinguishes the integral of velocity from the integral of its absolute value. This directly supports the displacement repair; it does not establish historical priority or seventeenth-century notation. Keep modern teaching labels explicitly modern. [OpenStax](https://openstax.org/books/calculus-volume-1/pages/5-4-integration-formulas-and-the-net-change-theorem)

8. **Chance:** York's translated 1654 correspondence supports a disputed interrupted-stake problem and different methods, not a unique invention of all probability. Direct open returned 403 but indexed text included the dated letters. ## Three strong / three weak: next iteration

**Strong — chance:** preserve the concrete interrupted game, show disagreement between score ratio and future chances, and let alternative methods agree. Current numerical payoff is already written. Keep modern inference as a clearly separate extension.

**Strong — longitude:** retain complementary methods and real sea context. A concrete discrepant fix with consequences for approaching a coast would make this more than two badges. Distinguish instrument reliability from repeatable manufacture. Repair synchronized time inputs first.

**Strong — rocks:** the ordering of deposition, tilting, erosion and renewed deposition can make missing material into positive evidence. Use a recognizable labeled section, test competing event orders, and distinguish Hutton from later fossil mapping. Current chain still needs discovery prose.

**Weak — causes:** no specified historical object of explanation. A placeholder “rival explanation” cannot test a sweeping account. Select a bounded exchange/printing/adoption case with evidence that discriminates between material access and institutional incentives. Publication gate: name two hypotheses, one observation that would favor each, and one unresolved limitation. Do not retrofit *Guns, Germs, and Steel* as a settled answer.

**Weak — future-memory:** privacy controls are a real design issue but not the science-fiction premise. Proposed fictional brief: a city reconstructs a demolished neighborhood from incompatible personal memories; a withdrawn contributor was the only witness to a disputed eviction. Meaningful pairs could be **conflicting recollections + surviving street photograph → competing reconstructions**, **withdrawn testimony + surviving public evidence → partial reconstruction**, **reconstruction + ownership claim → disputed restitution**. These are design suggestions, not historical facts or a promise of automatic just resolution. Make the archive unable to recover an unknowable truth by adding “consent”.

**Weak — future-world:** no specified intervention or affected population, so reversibility is asserted by adding a plan. Proposed fictional brief: orbital mirrors lengthen a settlement's growing season but remove another community's dark sky. Pairs: **mirror orbit + seasonal crop model → extended growing season**, **night-sky survey + mirror schedule → lost observing window**, **alternative orbit + crop shortfall → reduced-harvest compromise**. A reversible actuator does not reverse all ecological/social effects. Keep dissent and stop conditions tied to those concrete outcomes rather than as the ultimate inventions.

These briefs require author selection, scientific checking and playtesting. They demonstrate a grammar, not approved content or added route counts.

## Acceptable abstraction versus misleading compression

Acceptable: pigment + binder → paint; an explicitly idealized lens experiment; an archive juxtaposing different generations with visible dates; modern diagrams explaining an old question; a conditional result with stated assumptions. Two-input recipes need not reproduce every material or historical cause.

Misleading: adding inference-rule automatically makes any premises valid; adding independent-account automatically corroborates; adding an inverse-operation solves every equation; adding a revocation mechanism guarantees forgetting; adding a reversibility plan guarantees reversibility. These outputs should describe a **candidate**, a scoped demonstration, or a genuinely checked result. A generic historical-causation disclaimer does not repair a false conceptual entailment.

Perspective's current “one chosen viewpoint, not the only art” wording is good. But mandatory camera-obscura use in a Renaissance reconstruction must remain this workshop's modern teaching experiment, not a claim that perspective historically depended on that device. Signals likewise needs a specified code/error budget: a correction rule added to an arbitrary error-detecting code is not sufficient to correct arbitrary corruption.

## Plurality without token chapters

- A **Mohist standards dispute** could contrast inherited exemplars, testimony/observation and social consequences in an argument about fatalism. Stanford's specialist account identifies these three criteria in *Mozi* books 35–37, and also warns that some reconstruction is conjectural. Do not relabel this simply “Chinese scientific method” or erase its political/ethical aims. Before implementing, read a primary-text translation of the selected passage. [SEP, sections 3 and 6](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/mohism/)
- A **Kerala approximation problem** could ask how a finite computation approximates a circle, with a stated error comparison, rather than another generic “calculus invention”. Ranjan Roy's source-oriented Cambridge chapter discusses surviving Kerala works and attribution of series to Madhava. This is a research lead; inspect translated worked passages before recipes. No unsupported transmission-to-Newton story, and no claim that every component of modern calculus is thereby present. [Cambridge, Power Series in Fifteenth-Century Kerala](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/sources-in-the-development-of-mathematics/power-series-in-fifteenthcentury-kerala/4B3570D4505BBADE0567CB23264C8C0A)

Prefer replacing generic steps with these different intellectual problems over adding eight steps solely to reach 200. The two current Islamic-world settings should also gain named manuscripts/problems and reasoning, not just remain bridges to Europe.
