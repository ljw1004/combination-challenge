It is fine to retain them as background to the parallel picture-making strand, if labeled as such. The USGS source directly supports bearing/direction utility with increasing high-latitude area distortion.

## Historical/scientific checks and caveats

- **Greek political arguments:** [Politics III](https://classics.mit.edu/Aristotle/politics.3.three.html) explicitly distinguishes residence from political citizenship, discusses participation in judgment/office, and distinguishes common from sectional advantage. The game limits the excluded voting example to Athens. It need not assert that women had no civic/familial status in any sense. [Republic VI](https://classics.mit.edu/Plato/republic.7.vi.html) connects stable objects of knowledge, guardianship and the ship comparison. This supports reconstructing Plato's argument, not proving expert rule works.
- **Roman statuses:** [Institutes I.2–5](https://droitromain.univ-grenoble-alpes.fr/Anglica/just1_Moyle.htm) places slavery contrary to nature alongside its legal regulation and manumission. It distinguishes freeborn, enslaved and freed people. Justinian also says freed persons gain Roman citizenship; the game does not claim freed persons remain uniformly noncitizens. The legal division is not the same axis as Athenian voting. [Berkeley's exhibited glossed manuscripts](https://www.law.berkeley.edu/research/the-robbins-collection/exhibitions/medieval-law-school/) support the specific medieval reception, unlike a generic claim that all learning passed through a single chain.
- **Hobbes/Locke:** [Leviathan XVII–XVIII, XXI](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/3207/3207-h/3207-h.htm) supports mutual authorization among subjects and obligation bounded by capacity to protect. [Second Treatise §§95, 221–222](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/7370/7370-h/7370-h.htm) supports consent, majority action and breach-of-trust resistance. Labels keep these distinct. Teaching comparisons with earlier ideas do not require proof of direct influence, because the claims do not say such influence occurred.
- **Haiti:** [1801 constitution, arts.1, 3, 14–16](https://www.marxists.org/history/haiti/1801/constitution.htm) explicitly retains the colony within the French Empire, abolishes slavery, and regulates uninterrupted plantation cultivation and workers' movement. The coercion interpretation does not need to be invented from the single short cultivation label. [BnF's archival account](https://heritage.bnf.fr/france-ameriques/en/haitis-independence-debt-slave-unit-account-1794-1922) supports the 1791 uprising, 1793 local emancipation, 1794 extension, defeated expedition and distinction between 1804 independence and later recognition. [The 1804 declaration](https://declarationproject.org/?p=210) explicitly opposes re-enslavement and demands separation from France; it does not promise democratic equality or an end to every coercive relation. This bounded sequence need not cover every faction, violence or subsequent labor regime to be valid.
- **Relativity:** [Einstein §§8–9](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/5001/5001-h/5001-h.htm) supplies the midpoint convention, moving midpoint reception and frame-dependent simultaneity. The game's separate equal-train-distance/invariant-speed premise is essential and remains mechanically required. The inherited later light-deflection card is framed as a confrontation of theory with evidence, not unique proof from mere deflection or a claim that the 1919 observations were beyond dispute.
