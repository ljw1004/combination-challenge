# Earned endings considered

## 1. Supper and provisions aboard the overnight train — selected

The caravan reaches a railway kitchen whose catering cart is delayed. Its earlier bean stew and soda loaf become tonight’s meal; a separate professionally processed tin remains unopened for later in the train journey. The player finishes an earlier sponge as either a vanilla sandwich cake or iced chocolate cake. The apple juice reserved from the roadside breakfast supplies the drink.

The final action is concrete: fit these actual foods into a supper trolley and place it at the dining-car hatch. Each fitted food remains visible. The train departs supplied, and the cooks can finally sit down. This pays off the existing station/late-catering story rather than inventing a prize or abstract completion record.

Independent reading caught and corrected an earlier draft’s can-then-immediately-open detour. The final version serves a fresh portion now and sends the sealed tin for later. It also establishes the cooks’ pantry cart, managed cold storage and reserved juice, rather than treating old food as an unexplained stock.

## 2. Open the market’s shared celebration table

A public feast could combine bread, savoury dishes, pastries and the birthday cake, ending when the cooks and guests sit together. It is an emotionally clear culmination of hospitality. But making it the endpoint here would either discard the later tea-room, ice and preserving chapters the player enjoyed, or awkwardly send the journey back to an earlier market. Better for a separately shortened campaign than this revision.

## 3. Send the caravan home with provisions

Pack the preserved stew, bread, fruit and cakes for the caravan’s return. This gives preservation a natural purpose and echoes the roadside opening. It risks making the last achievement another inventory bundle, with fewer specific people and a weaker visible consequence than feeding the departing train.

## What the selected finale is not

- No branded snack, printing machine, injector, special mould or arbitrary industrial destination.
- No “feast record,” completion certificate, skill token or unnamed reward object.
- No destruction of a previously loaded dish when another cake is fitted. The two optional cake places are distinct; either suffices and both remain visible if used.
- No canned food opened merely to tick the next objective. The processed stew tin remains sealed through completion.
- No claim that source verification proves emotional satisfaction: this still needs a later player review, not a browser or human playtest performed here.
