## Limits

No blocking harness defect found in this independent pass. The previously identified loose-movement omission was already repaired before the continuous adaptation.

The JSON host cannot establish scrolling cost, pointer travel, spatial layout, offscreen retrieval difficulty, human enjoyment, or a 200-decision experience. In particular, 102 retained things is **not by itself a blocker** under the latest human preference for a growing world and scrollable catalog. Conversely, successful identifier commands do not prove those things will be comfortable to find. Future evaluation must separate logical construction/progress from physical/catalog interaction costs instead of treating this harness as evidence for either.

No deployed UI/Site files were changed, no player was recruited, and no immutable publication was made by this reviewer. Root owns the freeze/publication and any subsequent evaluation authorization.

Future spatial implementation must also preserve existing object positions as new areas extend the world, rather than globally rescaling or reflowing old work. This coordinate-free host cannot verify that requirement; it remains a separate implementation/review gate.
