## Primary texts checked

- [Marburg Articles, 3 October 1529, GHI](https://germanhistorydocs.org/en/from-the-reformations-to-the-thirty-years-war-1500-1648/ghdi:document-4311). Modern translation Ellen Yutzy Glebe: paraphrase, do not copy substantial translated text. Articles6–10 connect gifted faith, preaching, baptism and works. Article15 records both agreement about spiritual reception and unresolved corporal presence. A shared statement is not evidence that the dispute vanished. This creates a possible desk in which the player discovers precisely what two parties can sign without merging their positions. Earlier Luther faith/service and Aquinas substance/appearance creations should be genuine comparison operands, not generic keys. Need separate primary accounts for each disputant's positive reasoning before authoring those claims.
- [Trent Session VI, 13 January 1547, Hanover](https://history.hanover.edu/texts/trent/ct06.html). J. Waterworth1848 translation, pp30–53; public-domain source translation. Chapters5/7/8 distinguish grace preceding unearned adult preparation, cooperative response, renewal and instrumental baptism. Chapter8 explicitly denies that preceding faith or works merit initial justification; do not caricature the Catholic position as buying salvation. Chapter7 enumerates final, efficient, meritorious, instrumental and formal causes. This could make earlier Aquinas instrumental agency and Augustine assisted will matter again. Chapter10's increase in received justice is distinct from earning the initial gift. Further source verification needed before item wording and comparisons.
