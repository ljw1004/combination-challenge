## Minimal changes and hypothesis

1. **Expose the work in inherited methods.** `merchant-manual` now shows the actual 18 ÷ 3 × 5 merchant example and labels the unit-rate operation. `predictive-sky-record` now explains dated positions, predicted changes, discrepancies and a stated interpolation assumption. `revisable-agreement` now preserves the particular downstream shortfall and affected users’ ability to challenge the allocation. Readings are attributed authored reconstructions, not invented quotations. The hypothesis is that these creations can be recognized as useful methods rather than unlocked badges. No direct aliases were added for the two rejected calculation guesses; this does not establish that those joins now feel natural.
2. **Allow an optional initial comparison.** Raw clock + lunar longitude produces a worksheet showing their 30′ centre difference. Crucially, 31° W lies **inside** the lunar 29°55′–31°05′ band. The comparison does not prove error, supply a clock rate or calibrate the clock. It can use the **independent voyage-rate record** to make the same corrected clock worksheet. The ordinary correction route remains available; the comparison is never a completion prerequisite. It is one new observation with a meaningful next relation, not an extra mandatory qualification ritual.
3. **Use the adopted rule and reveal its cost.** The winter agreement and operating city state that 5 at night is 1 short of the stated need of 6. After the observed biological change, the final join is changed city + **current seasonal agreement**, not the obsolete 6/6 test. Its existing fallback gives A 3 by day and 4 at night, visibly 2 short of its night need. Production, distributions, repair diversion and all longitude numbers remain unchanged. The allocation is an acknowledged negotiated ration, not a claim that higher regional output satisfies everyone.

The exact content delta is three inherited items and their producer descriptions, four city result readings/labels and producer descriptions (including one changed operand), plus one optional navigation item and two recipes. Longitude carry/metadata include that new item. Other content is unchanged. `build_revision.py` reconstructs the overlay from local v3 snapshots; `base-v3-*.json` are not modified source files.
