# Elements: tangible areas (controlling 15:00 policy)

This supersedes the earlier automatic-retirement experiment. No finished discovery disappears. Every surviving canonical token is rendered physically; Elements does not invent consumption or upgrades.

## Physical journey
- The existing all-undiscovered-recipes/remaining-goals predicate selects what to **carry**, never what to show. Optional recipes and absent partners still count.
- Chapter transitions translate that group together, preserving pairwise arrangement; a tall group receives one mild uniform 0.92 scale, never a sorting/grid reflow. Unneeded things remain at their exact physical coordinates and can be scrolled back to.
- If every piece is still needed, the now-empty old area is not retained as blank canvas. Otherwise the new area begins near the last object left behind.
- The one Warm cellar remains at its original bakery site. “Back to warm cellar” and “Current work” navigation preserve selected ingredients; later fermentation remains legal in the original context.
- Legacy automatic-hide saves migrate once to tangible areas while keeping finished positions and discovery identity. Undo snapshots migrate too.

- Invitations name concrete desired outputs: bowl/brick; flatbread or bread plus juice or cider; two of light/cloth/basket; two of home/boat/shared meal.
- First travel is woven into the baker’s invitation: “We’re taking what we need with us…”
- Each satisfied capability records a physical witness immediately, including a partial multi-part goal. Witness objects retain a golden glow after later journeys.
- Text tiles are 36px high. Nearby free gaps across the board width are preferred to covering labels; legal discovery never fails for lack of geometry. Ordinary actions preserve sources, with visible birth and destination-based vertical scrolling.

There is no further empty page: maximum scroll is current-area top for short work, otherwise occupied bottom minus viewport height.
- Vertical-only scrolling and drag-edge pan remain. Undo, Reset, cancellation, lost capture and opening a modal stop the drag loop.
