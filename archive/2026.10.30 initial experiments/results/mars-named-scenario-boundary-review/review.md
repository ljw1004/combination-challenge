## Is the boundary honest?

**Yes as a specified logical contract; not yet as verified interaction.** Active home remains explicitly represented and separately accessible. The loadout is an individual transfer manifest, not a generic kit or undisclosed discard. Earlier tokens have not been destroyed or reset merely to clear the screen. Global novelty prevents manufacturing another copy remotely while one is stored at home.

However, the authored walker only executes the departure and forward field route. It does not implement or exercise returning home, retrieving an omitted tool, moving a field invention home, or explaining a blocked duplicate whose unique token is in the other scene. Those access/return operations must be explicit and navigable before claiming an implemented solution to clutter. A visible per-scene inventory/location indicator and individual transfer contract are conceptual requirements, not permission to resume UI work.

The active field is smaller, but the world is not: retained home has 52–68 objects. Scene separation reduces simultaneous visible working set only if navigation honestly exposes that retained state; it does not justify deleting it or pretending obsolete components were consumed. The original completed snapshot must be labeled historical and never offered as a second live copy to retrieve from.

The field also refers to a glacier while a home glacier remains recorded. A later implementation should make scene-scoped place identities explicit (or explain that this is a different view of the same glacier), rather than relying on ambiguous bare place IDs. This does not cause a token/current-goal conflict in the reviewed authored routes.

## Obligations and scope

The candidate preserves ten home current endpoints after departure as a conservative invariant. That is not proof all ten must constrain later field play forever. In particular a warm meal remains a completed episode more naturally than an indefinite obligation. A declared historical home completion can coexist with truthful mutable active-home placements; no need to freeze the meal or claim consumption. Current witnesses preserve it, so no mechanical failure is present.

This scene boundary improves the proposed access architecture and demonstrates genuine retained inventions without repetition. It does not establish a 200-decision experience, strategic choice depth, native browser behavior, or human enjoyment. Keep the completed-home snapshot, current scene candidate, simple/intact alternatives and excluded navigation counts distinct in publication.
