## Goals describe functions

A relevant enhancement can meet the same function: an annotated survey map is still a route map; a relay station still provides powered contact and long-range contact; heated growing shelters remain growing shelters. Capability metadata is authored explicitly, not inferred from every recipe input or from similar names. Cooking potatoes is not universal enhancement: a meal made directly from supplied potatoes does not magically prove a harvested crop. A ventilator does not filter dust, and a clamping bench assembled from an unlit workbench is not automatically lit.

The protected-growth capability belongs to the explicitly planted/enclosed growing forms, including enhanced greenhouse forms. The warm habitat is treated as an authored warm work area, not a size-limited container; physical size/layout compatibility is not modeled by this logical candidate. Actual required target locations still matter. A capability is not permission to satisfy a goal from anywhere.

## A rover, not a ladder of renamed rovers

The shared checkpoint contract is one stable Rover token. Frame plus Wheels creates a chassis with installed wheels. The engine (Motor) and solar-charger slots remain blank until filled; optional Radio is shown only after installation. Drive requires wheels, Motor and charger. Cart is legacy-only compatibility: no fresh Cart recipe, Frame+Motor shortcut or hand-cart transport shortcut is offered. Cart+Motor can handle an explicitly injected legacy Cart, never a new trial supply.

A stored component is a typed contained instance, not another independently movable same-kind token. After a direct installation fills its slot, keep the outside representative only if it still has an authored use: a recipe output that is neither physically present nor retired; an exact placement goal (even future or already achieved), an exact discovery goal not yet known; or another compatible empty slot on a present Rover. Capability similarity does not itself justify retention. The newly filled slot cannot justify retaining its own part. Otherwise absorb that actual outside component and record the structural change. A recipe whose output already exists is not a remaining use.

This is a narrow incorporation/installation rule, not general cleanup. Chassis construction evaluates the actual Wheels operand against remaining uses excluding the completed Rover result. Cart plus motor inherits wheels into the Rover but does not silently remove an unrelated outside wheels token. Ordinary ingredients persist, even if they have become uninteresting. Absorbed/retired kinds remain unavailable for duplicate reconstruction; an inside component cannot be regenerated outside merely because its outside token disappeared. Reusable outside copies and internal records follow the explicit installation exception, not arbitrary new token creation.

The Rover's authored capabilities include working radio when radio is installed, and powered radio/orbiter contact when radio plus charger are installed. These communication capabilities do not depend on wheels. Wheels, engine and charger together govern powered travel. No dish or long-range capability appears merely because a radio is contained. This is explicit assembly functionality, not automatic inheritance of every ingredient property. Synthetic and real-route tests must distinguish an outside part retained for a genuinely novel remaining use from a part absorbed after its uses are satisfied.

## Release returns the source to its origin

After a successful combination, surviving A returns to where it came from. B remains where it was. A placed canister, map or growing shelter does not silently move loose because it was used as a source. New outputs remain portable and loose. The explicit obsolete-radio and no-further-use installed-part exceptions remove only their specifically retired/absorbed outside token.

Plain movement is still free: place a thing at another revealed location or return it to loose workspace. Occupied placement displaces the old occupant; current goal truth regresses honestly. Context still comes from actual target B placement, before novelty checking. No command-supplied location override or ordinary-recipe fallback can circumvent an existing contextual result.
