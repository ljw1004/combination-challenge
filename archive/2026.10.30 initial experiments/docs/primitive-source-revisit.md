# Primary-source revisit after the three primitive plays

Read against revision7 and the actual reports, not a prediction of human enjoyment. All sources below were accessed as interview/chapter text; no video-viewing claim. The publisher PDF of Adams’s chapter failed to open; its full text was readable on ResearchGate.

**Observed starting point.** [Field](../results/logical-players/fun-field-1.json) made a grove, then one elective connection: a local solution became a source. [Imprints](../results/logical-players/fun-imprints-1.json) made five combines, two elective: one crossing enabled learning, return and settlement, and the player chose a shared home. [Resonance](../results/logical-players/fun-resonance-1.json) made seven combines plus waiting, with no elective construction: choosing the ring settled most of the work; unused capabilities did not supply another intention. All three players chose closure. None establishes sustained play.

## Three implications

1. **Advance something already known through a new role.** **Designer evidence:** Tarn Adams reports that mechanically consequential scars and preferences appeared in player stories, whereas unattached mannerism descriptions failed. Replacing an arriving noble with the promotion of an existing dwarf let a familiar character’s history advance. **Inference here:** A2’s successive uses and the grove’s parenthood are better expansion anchors than unrelated unlocks. Keep identity and provenance when a route becomes a home, a home becomes a source, or an earlier creation becomes another actor’s instrument. Merely adding a history label will not do this. [Adams, *Characterization and Emergent Narrative in Dwarf Fortress*, pp.152–154,159](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/353207413_Characterization_and_Emergent_Narrative_in_Dwarf_Fortress).

2. **Give constructions a counterpart with its own continuing activity.** **Designer evidence:** Adams describes dwarf autonomy as making them actors in their own stories, balanced against the player’s ability to shape events; he also rejects bureaucracy that contributed little to stories. Will Wright describes players choosing what kind of city counts as success within interacting systems. **Inference here:** The settled walkers and independent rings exhausted their relationships. Try an actor whose ordinary activity changes the player’s construction, giving the player something specific to welcome, redirect or oppose. Let the desired relationship be chosen in play, rather than supplying successive errands. [Adams interview](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/q-a-dissecting-the-development-of-i-dwarf-fortress-i-with-creator-tarn-adams), [Wright interview](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/the-replay-interviews-will-wright).

3. **Expand what existing laws apply to before expanding the vocabulary.** **Designer evidence:** Arvi Teikari describes developing puzzles from surprising interactions, cutting setups that needed too many supporting rules, and eventually making Baba’s map a level governed by the game’s own logic. **Inference here:** Resonance’s whole-form operand is promising only when applying it changes a valued situation. More diodes, forms or circuit features would currently elaborate a completed toy. Test one earlier creation acquiring a consequential new use. [Teikari interview](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/designing-i-baba-is-you-i-s-delightfully-innovative-rule-writing-system).

## Next alternative — a remembering crossing (unimplemented)

**Rejected proposal:** a sower rooted eligible wet terrain; an archivist fired eligible marked clay. Those monotone activities exhaust a finite map. Different preferences alone do not supply renewal. This is design analysis, not a played rejection; adding destinations would merely postpone saturation.

**Invention, not a source claim:** let using a familiar crossing rewrite another counterpart’s future route. Replace terminal settlement with continuing travel under four public laws:

1. Walkers travel dry paths, continuing at two-way places and turning back at leaves. A combination advances one beat; waiting is free.
2. Physically combining **Memory + Earth at a previously traversed crossing** creates a relay initialized from its actual last incoming path.
3. An arriving walker leaves along the remembered path; the relay then remembers this walker’s incoming path. Simultaneous arrivals read the same previous memory; conflicting incoming paths leave that memory unchanged.
4. Exact-record learning and home deposits remain. Walkers continue travelling after learning or visiting a shared record.

Routes, ingress history, eligibility and forecasts stay inspectable. These are reusable movement laws, not new desire flags unlocked after settlement.

**Hand-run episode 1 — crossing becomes invitation.** X connects familiar homes A and B by one-beat arms, and shared grove G by a two-beat arm. X’s observed last arrival came from G. The player makes X a relay. Blue arrives from A at beat0: it leaves toward G and X remembers A. Amber arrives from B at beat1: it follows Blue’s incoming path to A; X remembers B. The player made the relay, but Blue’s actual use determined Amber’s destination. The old crossing now passes one counterpart’s route to another.

**Episode 2 — shared ending becomes departure point.** Amber returns from A at beat3, follows B and leaves X remembering A. Blue returns from G at beat4, follows A and leaves X remembering G. Amber returns from B at beat5 and therefore travels to G. The former terminal meeting place now participates in circulation between distinct homes. A player could adopt “retain their own homes while making G a recurrent meeting place,” then shape paths in response. That adoption is a hypothesis, not observed renewal.

**Attack:** isolation removes mutual influence and defeats that adopted relationship without needing punishment. Traversable terrain can saturate while arrivals keep rewriting routes. However, if “connect everything” universally supplies the wanted circulation, or a short stable circuit exhausts interest, reject the sustained-game claim. Continuing motion alone is insufficient.

**Status:** hold this as a contrasting co-maker alternative if Frontier’s imminent long/elective tests reduce to combat counters. No implementation yet. Avoid optional-recipe padding, wear/upkeep resets, supplies, quotas or authored stage queues. Next testing should ask whether actual counterpart use prompts a wanted construction involving the old crossing, with closure valid.
