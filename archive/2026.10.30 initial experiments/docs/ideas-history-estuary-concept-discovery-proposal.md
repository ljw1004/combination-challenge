# A future made from ideas: a 24-node Estuary discovery layer

27 October 2026. **Design-only proposal. No engine, browser UI, published checkpoint or player record changes. No new playtest or duration promise.** This is a concept-selection artifact, **not an implementation-ready specification**: it is a candidate intellectual vocabulary to evaluate on paper, not a recipe census for the player to complete.

## The missing pleasure

The current Estuary lets a person live a Tide, make another body express it, branch into relatives and hand a school to a still-living successor. Its latest player valued connecting family and school into one history, then found further activity likely to repeat. Powerful translations and social operations were already supplied. That trial tested their application, not the pleasure of inventing the ideas that make those futures thinkable.

The new hypothesis is **invention followed by consequential reuse**: join understandable ideas, acquire a surprising concrete instrument or social practice, and then decide what to do with it. Making a lens and using it are not inherently redundant. The test is whether the lens has an executable operation, works on different material and leaves something the player can reuse—not whether invention and application happen in one gesture.

The future should feel unfamiliar because ordinary relationships have changed: an experience becomes a medium; a relative can share your past without inheriting your office; an institution can outlive its founder without requiring the founder to die. These are not a ladder from primitive to morally superior civilization. Discovering every institution must not impose it on everyone.

## Research inheritance, not new historical claims

This proposal borrows three structural ideas from the repository's already source-checked [SMAC research](smac-tech-tree-research.md): technology can meet social thought; successful inventions can make their own critiques necessary; knowing an idea differs from adopting a regime. It does **not** copy SMAC's tree, quotes or doctrines, or assert its fictional forecasts are true. No new claim about SMAC's prerequisites or historical thinkers is introduced here.

All names, parent pairs and future practices below are **our speculative design propositions**, not predictions or documented intellectual genealogies. A parent arrow means “this combination makes sense in this fiction,” never “these two ideas historically caused this institution.” The [historical source guardrails](ideas-history-sources.md) remain in force; medieval schools, Nobel affiliations and contemporary political institutions are not assigned correctness scores. No new factual attribution requires verification for this original design pass.

## Reading the catalogue honestly

There are **24 nodes: nine foundations, nine supported-effect inventions, six new-law hypotheses**. This is not 24 discoveries or 24 interesting decisions. Foundations are initially available understandable ideas; particular lived episodes, people and schools are world objects, not extra concept nodes counted to inflate the graph.

- **F — foundation:** explained at the start, not a research chore.
- **E — existing effect, new discovery binding:** the frozen engine can already execute the listed operation. Combining abstract parents into its reusable instrument is **not implemented**. Names and ownership in this proposal do not add laws to the current engine.
- **H — hypothesis:** requires additional state/transition laws, not merely new names or recipes. None is implemented or claimed to have been played.

The companion [machine-readable graph](../results/ideas-history-estuary-concept-discovery/graph.json) records node statuses and dependencies; its validation checks bookkeeping only. Every parent refers to an earlier numbered node. A disclosed alternative recipe can later reach the same operation where it has an equally intelligible rationale. The table is an authored candidate, not a secret ontology to guess.

### Foundations: recognizable questions, not nine setup tasks

| ID | Idea | Plain meaning and public example |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Situated memory | An account comes from someone's actual occurrence. Knowing their account does not make you its original witness. Existing episode ancestry supplies the toy representation. |
| 02 | Bodily languages | One pattern can be expressed through different channels: movement or pressure. These are declared fictional actor ports, not claims about real disabilities or subjective equivalence. |
| 03 | Relative time | A sequence has order and proportions, distinct from how long a rendition takes. |
| 04 | Common pattern | A relation can survive a change of representation. The current finite example is a normalized piecewise-linear trajectory—not a theory of semantic equivalence. |
| 05 | Concurrent action | Several bodies can enact complementary parts together. A person with many memories is not automatically a simultaneous ensemble. |
| 06 | Copyable minds | Speculative premise: a continuing actor can branch with a shared actual past. Not a scientific claim or a supply of disposable workers. |
| 07 | Continuing lives | A person can keep living, remembering and contributing after a relationship or office changes. Not proof of immortality or a particular legal doctrine. |
| 08 | Public office | An institution's address is not the same object as its present holder. Who receives a request can change. |
| 09 | Addressed correspondence | A score or invitation travels to a destination and arrives during later activity; sending is not instantaneous remote knowledge. |

| 17 E | Public office + Addressed correspondence → **Institutional Correspondence** | A role-addressed sending practice places an actual score invitation in transit; delivery resolves the then-current holder. The social surprise is not “a higher office tier.” Fork Kinship creates someone who shares a founder's memories but not their office. Succession Without Death then makes a different relationship possible. Combining the implications is part of playing; we need not mint another collectible badge called “Kinship Succession” for that history to exist.

### Future civic branches: consequences to specify, not powers already present

| ID | Combination → invention | New observable law required; two uses | Failure case and claim boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| 19 H | Reunion Without Erasure + Institutional Correspondence → **Civic Apprenticeship** | A school exchanges demonstrations and counterexamples with a learner; correction changes a specific executable method, not a competence label. Use it for a household controller and for a successor interpreting differently embodied demonstrations. Requires a finite correction/transfer law and remote method payloads. | Current local teaching can retain a method, but no curriculum, correction or remote method transport exists. Repeating a known score must not count as learning an adaptive skill. This node is cut unless two materially different tasks reveal actual learned behavior. |
| 20 H | Fork Kinship + Succession Without Death → **Distributed Stewardship** | A role can divide into explicitly scoped services with different holders while retaining shared institutional history. One relative hosts performances, another receives teaching requests; a later community uses the same practice to separate observation from representation. Requires multiple scopes and deterministic request routing. | Copying a person must not duplicate every mandate or vote. Current engine has one holder per school and no service scopes. This is not a bonus for having more copies. |
| 21 H | Continuing lives + Institutional Correspondence → **Correspondence by Consent** | A standing recipient policy filters an otherwise operative invitation/participation process and returns an observable response to its sender without losing unrelated services. Use it for a score-accepting school that automatically declines hosting requests, or a teaching participant who accepts lessons but refuses appointment requests. Requires an actual request/response process and public policies with effects—not hidden assent or a manual non-action. | Current actors are an authored willing backdrop; receipt is not consent, and no acceptance policy exists. Merely receiving a score and never performing it is already possible: that is **not** a new capability. Without an operative process for the policy to change, cut this node rather than count a consent label. Do not invent forced participation just to make refusal necessary, or insert an obligatory approval click in every invitation. A retained policy applies automatically; an explicit response matters only where it changes a real outcome. |
| 22 H | Succession Without Death + Correspondence by Consent → **Revisable Mandates** | A handover can preserve an ongoing service while successor and affected parties revise its scope. Use it to keep a public performance running while changing its host's teaching remit; use the same revision practice to end a service without deleting the former holder's life. Requires explicit service obligations, responses and transition outcomes. | Current routing alone does not represent a constitution or legitimate governance. “All scopes optional” must not silently satisfy a goal requiring an actual service. Do not manufacture conflict merely to prevent a peaceful solution. |
| 23 H | Situated memory + Institutional Correspondence → **Ancestral Commons** | A school retains a shared, provenance-bearing collection independent of any one holder, and can supply selected scores to later members. Use it for a teaching archive and a changing performance repertoire. Requires an institutional store, explicit access/retention behavior and real delivery to users. | The current global catalogue is a player view, not a public library every actor can access. A second archive is not a second witness. If the existing personal repertoire already solves the stated purpose, do not require this invention. |
| 24 H | Ancestral Commons + Reunion Without Erasure → **Federated Memory** | Separate communities exchange selected experience threads without copying entire archives or merging local histories. Use it for a jointly curated work and for mutual teaching while different local collections persist. Requires actual scoped exchange between distinct institutional stores. | “Federated” must not be a decorative word for ordinary score mail. A one-score exchange may already satisfy a simple joint goal; federation earns a node only when its continuing selective behavior changes later uses. No claim of collective consciousness. |

The six H nodes are an **option pool for later selection**, not six implementation commitments or mandatory late-game stages. The most promising governance question is whether a copied family can share institutions without duplicating authority. The most promising learning question is whether another person's practice can be changed by a concrete counterexample. They need different laws; do not smuggle both into this discovery layer.

## What would actually change in a separate normal-discovery version

Keep the human-feedback browser checkpoint unchanged. In a future branch, foundations and ordinary people/places are available; the advanced render instruments, branching practice, handover practice and role-mail practices are **not all supplied as already-known tools**. Publicly combining their conceptual parents creates reusable operation-bearing pieces. No research currency, finite mind stock, consuming ideas, mandatory chapter unlock or total-recipe completion goal.

A newly invented instrument must have a public signature and examples: input kinds, actual transform/transition, legal targets and a clear non-example. The player physically combines it with material or constructs the particular practice to apply. There is no separate global “unlock,” “install concept” or “certify success” click. No operation checks that a badge was previously earned instead of checking the actual instrument/structure.

This needs an honest new binary binding: several existing actions currently use fixed site/tool/mailbox pieces. A conceptual combination cannot merely set `hasConcept` and leave those supplied super-tools unchanged. For example, 10's result must really carry its temporal transform; 17's result must carry a role destination rule used by an actual score message. How a reusable social practice takes a role/person as its second operand must be settled on paper before implementation. Do not hide a three-field form behind a two-piece animation or proliferate empty half-assembled authorization objects. A useful particularization, such as a reusable Local-school address that routes every later score, is an object with a continuing function—not a one-use approval stub.

### One complete proposed binary signature: an institutional address

`Institutional Correspondence + Local School → Local Role Address`

The result is an inexhaustible addressed sending instrument bound to the **same existing Local school identity**, not a duplicate school, transfer of authority or certificate. Its inspectable data is the destination rule “resolve Local’s current holder at delivery” and the existing delivery law. It has a persistent operation:

`Local Role Address + an actual score → invitation carrying that score to Local`

That second combination emits the real existing `send` transition. Later actual activity delivers to the then-holder; another score can use the same address without reconstructing it. Changing holder does not invalidate or recreate the address. A personal address is a different destination function, not a higher/lower grade of correspondence. The old v2 mailbox already provides this concrete use; the **new discovery binding would construct that useful object rather than provide every mailbox at start**.

Constructing the address particularizes a reusable operation, like choosing the destination of a transmitter. If it serves only one rote message beside its invention, its experiential value remains unproven. There is no extra institution-accepts-address click. Neither raw engine `send` nor inspecting a concept tree is a player bypass: the future player surface would expose actual constructed instruments. Independent equivalent addressed constructions must remain lawful.

Other social bindings—especially a handover practice with both a role and a successor—still need equally honest signatures. The unresolved choices are a reason **not** to implement this whole catalogue yet, not permission to hide multi-argument setup in a nominal pair gesture.

Ordinary firsthand occurrence, actual episode ancestry, automatic score retention and continuing personhood remain base laws. **No discovery stamp is required to make a recorded occurrence real or to preserve a retained capability.** Existing lawful shortcuts remain available once their material operation is constructed; whole methods can replace serial assembly. A direct score replay need not first enact Reunion Without Erasure. A matching two-school festival is not required merely to satisfy a solo invitation.

| **One past, different futures**: a person wants two continuing projects and a later encounter. | Invent Fork Kinship; let both relatives live differently; exchange actual occurrences without merging. | Existing branching/replay. A solo remote enactment or an optional correspondence festival serve different disclosed intentions; neither is a universal superior ending. Retained local repertoire needs no proof replay. |
| **A family shares responsibility without multiplying authority**: two relatives can help different services. | Construct service-scoped stewardship; one responsible receiver per request, rather than two full holders because there are two copies. | H20. | **A public inheritance without a single owner**: successors can use a school collection while another community shares only selected threads. | Construct an institutional commons, then choose whether continued selective exchange has a real purpose beyond sending one score. | H23/24. New persistent institutional memory/access laws. If one willing librarian and ordinary mail already suffice, preserve that shortcut and reduce or drop the grander invention. |

These purposes overlap deliberately: the same invented render operation can serve intimate translation, public art and remote teaching; the same real experience can become family exchange, a shared performance and a school's inheritance. They are not isolated levels resetting materials. No goal may require discovering every node or using a named invention when another lawful construction produces its promised consequence.

## A concrete paper braid, not a prescribed route

A player combines Relative Time with Common Pattern and obtains a temporal instrument, then combines Bodily Languages with Common Pattern for a sensory instrument. The two instruments can compose into Borrowed Seasons—or remain separate. Applying either lawful construction to an actual Tide makes Ilen's work. The invention is retained; another source needs no reconstruction of its conceptual ancestry.

Elsewhere Copyable Minds meets Continuing Lives to make Fork Kinship. One continuation stays; another encounters the Marsh. Their actual histories become material for exchange and for Polyphonic Scores. That same score, not a new catalogue token, is performed locally and sent to a school. Continuing Lives meets Public Office for a real handover; office plus correspondence provides a reusable role address. A founder's memories persist while the recipient of the next invitation changes.

The new future is a **relationship among the inventions**: the person with the founder's past need not hold their office; an invitation can belong to an institution without erasing its holder; a shared work can travel without replacing local repertoire. None of those implications requires an additional seal labelled “advanced society.” If the player ends there, that is a complete coherent construction. The H branches pose different questions only if they actually interest the player.

## Challenge the discovery layer before code

1. **All-concepts-known control:** same people, laws and purposes, all reusable instruments available. It should remove conceptual construction/discovery pleasure, not fabricate lived episodes or automatically solve performances, handovers and delivery. A short capable application is legitimate; do not punish it. This control cannot by itself prove that invention was empty busywork.
2. **Bare-flag challenge:** replace an invented piece with a Boolean and inspect its uses. If nothing but eligibility changes, redesign it as an actual callable operator/continuing arrangement or cut it. A function's discovery can still change access; the distinction is the resulting object's behavior, not a ban on learning capabilities.
3. **Whole-method challenge:** Borrowed Seasons must equal its disclosed composition. The world must accept serial, fused and independently equivalent lawful outputs, not a preferred recipe ID. Retain direct family replay and plain role continuity.
4. **Reuse challenge:** give each E method a second concrete source or social setting. If its only use is the goal immediately beside its discovery, label that limitation rather than claim a systemic vocabulary. Reuse need not always be strategic; it can be expressive and pleasurable, but that must be observed later.
5. **Society-word challenge:** for “consent,” “commons,” “stewardship” and “mandate,” show the state difference when two actors disagree or a holder changes. If the answer is only different prose, it is worldbuilding, not implemented governance.
6. **Saturation/peace challenge:** unlimited copies, complete knowledge, direct replay, whole tools and cooperative actors are legal. If a simple shared arrangement fulfills a purpose, let it. New situations need genuinely different desired consequences, not artificial scarcity or forced conflict.
7. **Discovery readability:** show existing compatible conceptual pairs and intelligible parent meanings under the chosen discovery-exposure policy. Do not make “guess which philosophy noun the author intended” the challenge. A surprising result can be intelligible after discovery without being a concealed passphrase.

## Proposed disposition

Keep this as a **private-publication design artifact** and await human feedback on the unchanged v2 browser. Independently challenge the nine E recipe bindings and reuse claims on paper first; no tiny fresh trial is requested. Select, at most, a coherent future branch only after its actual laws and distinct purposes are specified. The civic hypotheses should not all be implemented just to make the tree look large.

Evidence needed later is whole-world play: what people choose to invent, whether those inventions invite materially different uses, what feels surprising and intelligible, what they voluntarily revisit, and where they stop. One catalogue size, one completion percentage or a longer authored route cannot establish that. The present proposal makes no promise about duration or number of worthwhile decisions.
