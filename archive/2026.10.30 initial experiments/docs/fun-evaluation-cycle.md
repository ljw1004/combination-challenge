## How the evaluator changed

The first implementation's experience summary missed actual narrative/closure fields even though the raw reports survived. Its unchanged next-test sentence also failed to close the loop. The corrected evaluator normalizes reports with source paths, retains unknown fields, locates player versus designer questions, distinguishes availability from understanding and exact prose exposure from appreciation, and separates elective construction from inspection, closure and required actions. Reviewed ledgers retain local value while changing failed renewal hypotheses. This supports a bold next prototype rather than just saying unknown.
