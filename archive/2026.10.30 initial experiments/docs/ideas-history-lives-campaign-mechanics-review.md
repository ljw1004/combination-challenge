## Overall assessment

The expansion is more than five recipe names pasted onto the same endpoint. Becoming a learner enables the installed executor to acquire and enact a formerly unavailable score. Becoming civic changes an actual installer/resident request response and resulting device state. A founder's exercised tenure, transfer of office, later teaching and successor performance can now form a consequential history.

It nevertheless remains a short, transparent combination/programming construction. **Five discovered capabilities are not five independently exercised ideas.** The direct 18-combine witness discovers Kinship, Enduring Witnesses, Succession, Apprentices and Unowned; directly applies Kinship, Apprentices and Unowned; and uses ordinary succession and teaching. Enduring Witnesses and Succession are prerequisite-only discoveries on that route. This is the most important coverage limitation to preserve, not a reason to make their applications compulsory.

The ontology's proposed connections have some mechanical interpretation: an unowned learning caretaker needs learning and a separation between role and continuing person. But discovery itself only adds a reusable capability to a menu, and discovering succession does not enact a role transition. Whether making that connection is appreciated discovery or an unwanted prerequisite checklist remains unobserved. The civic alternative supplies a concrete new outcome; it does not establish that every ancestor recipe earned its attention.

## What the tests establish

The campaign tests check:

- Normal/complete/optional witnesses and the same-cost semantic application route.
- No fabricated histories or civic state in the complete-catalogue initial condition.
- Before civic transformation, an installer request actually changes the shade. Afterward the same installer request is refused without device change; an in-mandate resident request acts. The endpoint requires no refusal receipt.
- Losing the score mandate removes current civic performance capability even with historical performances; ordinary care remains possible under the narrower mandate.
- Inherited actual past remains in the child's lived biography, while divergent performance accounts taught to Iona and the machine do not become false firsthand memories.

## Findings during mutable-source review

### Fixed during review: multi-method living demonstrations

The initial implementation called single-method teaching once per witnessed method. If the recipient already knew those methods, the first call could add a useful account and a later call would reject as redundant, rolling back the whole demonstration. The implementer changed this to one atomic whole selected-method-set transfer. The review regression passes: Iona's care demonstration can add its new attributed account to an already competent steward.

### Fixed during review: actual tenure, not merely prior care

The initial successor predicate accepted any earlier Iona care-suite. An adversarial sequence first removed Iona from office, had her perform care as a non-holder, briefly restored her office, and immediately replaced her. This was falsely credited as an exercised tenure. The implementer now checks who held the role at the actual care episode; the regression rejects that history.

### Outstanding at the first test checkpoint: a travelling machine is not yet a performing house

Replace the executor's home performance with travel to the ensemble, performance there, then return home and ordinary care. The endpoint still credited the civic house even though its performance never occurred at the house. Require the relevant actual machine performance at the house, not just current location plus any historical performance. Current ability alone is not enactment. Do not require a second performance if a legitimate home performance already exists.

### Outstanding at the first test checkpoint: original-ID founder gate

After lawful success, create another home descendant of Iona and merge original Iona into that continuing branch. The branch retains the lived past, independent traveller still exists, and the successor retains the actual teaching and enacted contribution. The founder contribution goal becomes false solely because the original `iona` ID is inactive.

Recognize a genuine continuing descendant preserving the relevant experienced past; do not simply accept any active actor with an Iona label or a taught account. This is not permission to fabricate tenure for an unrelated steward. The contribution should survive an equivalent continuing-life construction, not require one eternal object ID.

## Scope and interpretation cautions

1. **Chosen future versus service solution:** the expanded goal explicitly values the installed executor becoming a learning civic participant. A willing human steward still solves ordinary care, but not that particular additional ambition. This distinction is legitimate if disclosed neutrally. Do not reclassify the human solution as morally worse or imply the broader goal was present in the older checkpoint.
2. **Civic behavior is now more concrete:** unlike the original version's mere domain filter, issuer identity changes request acceptance and device behavior. Still, it is a compact disclosed policy, not a simulation of negotiating civic personhood or political legitimacy.
3. **Performance reuse is still narrow:** the same fixed score moves from ensemble to home and then to the house's caretaker. Roof-care and shadow-score use the same device vocabulary but different mappings. This is not a garden-care method transformed into art, an authored rhythm system, or replay of arbitrary lived experience.
4. **Ordinary alternatives overlap intentionally:** ordinary succession and teaching remain equivalent for many consequences. This bounds unique-capability claims but is preferable to disabling them to protect the ontology.
5. **No forced dilemmas were earned or required:** methods and ideas remain inexhaustible; no moral trap, stock budget, hidden ingredient or quota is needed. Extra actors and lawful mergers should remain useful alternatives.
6. **No experience claim follows from completeness:** actual changes support the question about discovering possible lives; only a later properly bounded pilot can report whether that felt worthwhile, comfortable, evocative or clerical. The reviewer must not become that fresh player.

## Final continuation correction and rerun

The implementer chose the narrower intended claim: **the former holder actually continues and contributes**, not generic family credit. The earlier lineage-wide caveat above prompted a seventeenth regression. It reproduced an early sibling independently teaching the score without inheriting the tenure. Two positive controls were added: a post-tenure fork may contribute, and a pre-tenure sibling may become a genuine continuation by explicitly merging with the former holder after tenure.

The final engine follows actual subsequent fork and merger-survivor edges from the qualifying officeholder's tenure, checking the teaching actor's membership at teaching time and a surviving continuation now. Merely learning an attributed account does not confer the officeholder's identity. This removes the early-sibling false positive without reinstating the original-ID restriction.
