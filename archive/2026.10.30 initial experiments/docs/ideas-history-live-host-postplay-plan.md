## Independent reviewer assignment after raw preservation

An independent source-exposed reviewer may inspect frozen sources, protocol, exact raw capture and player reports; it must not implement fixes or rewrite the player's account. Verify:

1. All final gates and publication identity precede initialization, and exact public assignment/exposure metadata are retained.
2. The complete trace replays. Every selected construction, discovery, teaching, invocation and legitimate declined/blocked request counts once. Automatic controls, receipts, queries and reports do not inflate selection count.
3. Initial station reuse is identified from actual materials/events, not inferred from endpoint labels. Separate required reuse from freely chosen sharing.
4. The taught method contains real demonstrated permitted controls; actual IDs, role routing, standby guard, failure prefix and current optics support each claimed live service. Old physical images alone are insufficient.
5. No manual intervention is silently credited as autonomous operation, and an irrelevant later repair is not artificially treated as lost historical accomplishment.
6. Reports distinguish physical construction, demonstrative teaching, live role change, future-personhood ideas, governance and interface burden. A noncivic host also serves residents: governance appreciation needs the player's actual account, not our endpoint.
7. Identify evidence that the later purpose changes the value/use of an earlier creation, versus merely adding a target-to-pose lookup and authorization chores. Easy progress can be worthwhile; no need to call every action a dilemma.
8. State what remains untested, including human enjoyment, broader policy generalization, later appetite and any longer coherent campaign.

Return retain/revise/reject judgments with exact supporting events/report passages and limitations. Do not turn the source-exposed shortest route into a normative expectation for the player. Do not suggest extra prompting solely to obtain praise. The protocol permits only one neutral thematic clarification if the central distinction remains genuinely unresolved.

## Longer-game synthesis after actual evidence

Do not add earlier lab counts, multiply short families, or equate 200 accepted operations with 200 worthwhile decisions. The next synthesis should describe one continuing world and a reusable ontology, then identify where real new purposes change what prior creations and ideas can do.

A supported bridge would be player construction → meaningful demonstration → useful later operation on the same things. Before proposing expansion, name the existing concepts that transfer, the genuinely new behavioral law required (if any), a concrete later purpose not already solved by static saturation, and a legal static/manual/whole-method control. Investigate whether another purpose changes the method itself rather than merely adding another request name. Keep construction player-performed, recipes inspectable and concepts inexhaustible.

If the pilot values only physical arrangement, retain that finding and do not force a civic wrapper to claim intellectual-history success. If it values future agency but not the current macros, distinguish thematic possibility from implemented depth. If both combine well, a coherent multi-purpose setting can be scoped, with a finite full endpoint and normal/complete controls before play. Any prospective approximately-200-decision campaign must be judged by an actual full run with sparse reports and independent replay; no projected count qualifies it in advance.
