# Observatory / conservatory: prospective geometry review

26 October 2026. Design critique only; no source change, implemented witness or play claim. Companion to the frozen inheritance design notes. Candidate proposed during coordination with the material-campaign lead and parent.

## Difference from earlier joinery

[The actual logical shape report](../results/logical-players/logic-shape-report.md) records three combines for the key and three for the crossing, using the same 5→7→10 construction template. The player appreciated an initial decomposition, then found the similar continuation less worthwhile. It also encountered a no-self-combination rule that caused a representational detour. That rule must not be imported to manufacture depth.

A reconfigurable observatory/conservatory should not ask for two target silhouettes. Its shape should determine whether a camera receives a faithful view, whether growing surfaces receive suitable light, whether a supported boundary shelters a work area, and whether assemblies can coexist. Identical geometry serving a genuinely different purpose can be worthwhile; no inference novelty quota applies.

## Earned minimum law delta

Current retained-project folding makes any hinged root 1×1; a separate prospective model could instead retain each actual panel's dimensions and rotate it around a declared joint. Occupancy and contact are consistent world geometry, not a consumable stock. They must apply to fresh copies, old originals and assemblies alike. Permitted outside construction, replacement and limitless concepts remain.

Do not simulate a detailed hinge sweep or require several angle clicks unless that motion actually matters to the chosen service. A whole lawful reconfiguration may be one selected action. If only endpoint geometry matters, disclose that abstraction rather than implying full mechanics.

## First falsifier: clear enclosure serves everything

A clear sheltered court may already admit faithful camera light and enough light for plants simultaneously. If so, accept it. Do not stipulate that conservatories require a diffuser simply because a camera requires clarity.

To justify another optical law, name its actual consequence. For example, a narrow incoming beam may illuminate one surface while a diffuser distributes light to several spatially separated growing surfaces but destroys image direction. That would be a new disclosed qualitative transport law, not something already implemented by the existing static material model. It needs precise source/extent/visibility rules and a general application beyond the desired fixture. A direct beam plus copied lamps, a reflector, several clear windows, or moving the tray may still be valid alternatives.

## Other mandatory controls

- Put the camera outside the growing boundary if the target remains visible there.
- Keep both service assemblies in different lawful positions.
- Copy a rich supported frame with all useful panels; re-aim or fold only what is needed.
- Move the whole present installation aside and construct another; retain current service elsewhere if the purpose permits it.
- Use several small independent panels instead of a hinged whole.
- Remove and replace the boundary rather than preserving a named original.
- Meet both future purposes simultaneously. A joint solution is success, not cheating.

A fixed location needs a functional reason, such as its water contact, actual subject visibility or public accessible entrance. 'The goal says this square' alone can define a spatial puzzle but does not establish meaningful inheritance. An original component's retention is valuable evidence only when its function/history matters or the player chooses to retain it; identity predicates alone are not that evidence.

## Next decision

The material lead should first provide exact present and successor service predicates plus two concrete arrangements under the proposed laws. Challenge them with these controls before adding source. If a clear enclosure or universal rich scaffold solves the whole purpose immediately, preserve it and decide whether the comfortable reconfiguration is worth a short diagnostic; do not add arbitrary constraints to reach a target action count.

The future-idea contribution remains making something useful now whose existing structure supports later uses. Permanent option destruction is not required. Actual transformation with appreciated continuity would support that narrower claim; administrative future certificates would not.
