# Proposal after Bell2: a world with consequences that travel

This is a designer proposal for the owner's requested180–240 decision opportunities, not an implemented or validated long game. Bell2 is a separate frozen short experience. Its central question may improve a hook, but adding another hundred paired-name revelations would be padding.

## The continuing causal engine

Build one valley or archipelago whose rivers, settlements, living forms and institutions persist. A created relationship **carries something**: water, seeds, voices, authority or promises. Its recipients can transform what arrives and carry consequences onward. The same connection that makes coordination possible can spread a disturbance or erase an independent voice. The world never resets to another independent target vector.

The strongest unit is a **consequence chain with several possible stopping points**, not a prescribed quest chain. For example:

1. A water course saves an orchard and exposes the bell.
2. Public memory changes the village's understanding and draws the river.
3. A bank protects listeners but cuts river contact.
4. A living boundary reconnects water and transforms exact voices.
5. Descendant gardens carry that transformed memory into another community, which interprets it differently.
6. A shared institution can recognize those relations, but binding it may preserve an inherited interpretation after the people or landscape changes.

The content at each step is tied to a world process, and a player can pursue another project or decide not to spread something. A character's request is an offered intention, not a compulsory objective queue. Root's other designer is developing the actor side; this proposal concerns what can propagate and transform.

## Philosophically different combination within the same world

The local combination grammar can change while its earlier creations remain relevant:

| Meaning of combination | What it changes | Skill and consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Material/construction | Paths, barriers, vessels and habitats | Spatial relations determine what can reach what. |
| Efficient cause/process | How an arriving thing is transformed | Order and feedback preserve, amplify or erase properties. |
| Formal/institutional | Which relation has continuing authority | A rule coordinates people but can suppress exceptions or independence. |
| Final cause/promise | What a persistent entity is trying to maintain | Lasting intent may protect a purpose or outlive the circumstances that made it good. |

These need distinct explicit semantics, not the same set operation with new nouns. A river course created during material play could later carry a process, define an institutional boundary, or become the thing a promise protects. That gives established combinations changing roles across time.

## A plausible scale without200 authored chores

Use a compact typed grammar over perhaps6–8 connected places, several persistent characters or communities, and a few interacting carriers. Each creation changes the topology or transformation of a carrier. Characters adopt accessible capabilities or respond to changed conditions under inspectable policies. A new connection can expose a genuinely different interaction elsewhere; an optional project can be revised when another community interprets the result.

Breadth alone does not make180 decisions. The model must support cycles of intention, commitment, world response and renewed intention. A long playtest should record those episodes and the player's reasons for continuing, including moments when a solved construction is worth enacting and moments when it becomes overhead. Do not count every combine or every confirmation as a meaningful decision.

Avoid a global “do everything” optimum by deriving opposed effects from the same process: connection also transmits disturbances; permanence also limits revision; roots that preserve living relations change exact records; public authority also changes independence. Known concepts remain inexhaustible. No ingredient stocks, action credits, automatic damage clock or obligatory maintenance should supply the main tension.

## First implementation boundary

The next model should implement **one reusable propagation grammar and one durable consequence loop**, then extend the same world rather than serialize separate mini-puzzles. A material relation, a living process and one institutional rule are enough to test whether different meanings interact. Use explicit public policies and deterministic world responses before adding many types.

For the real long test, record progress at approximately30,60,120 and180 commitments, while preserving that commitments are not automatically decision episodes. Ask what unresolved intention the player is pursuing, which prior creation now matters differently, and whether they would choose to continue. A player choosing to stop is evidence about absorption, not a failed participant. An evaluator must not transform a forced180-command completion into proof of a30-minute game.

The request for180–240 opportunities is a design target. Only actual sustained play can establish whether this grammar supplies that scale; neither a large action menu nor an authored200-scene plot establishes it.
