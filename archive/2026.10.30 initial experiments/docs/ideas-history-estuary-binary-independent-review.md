# Estuary binary adapter — independent mechanics and grammar review

27 October 2026. Source-exposed adversarial review, not fresh play, human-fun evidence, a minimum route, physical UI verification or a 200-decision result. Reviewed `ideas-history-estuary-binary.ts` and its design against the existing interaction audit; did not modify adapter or frozen substrate.

## Verdict within scope

The adapter does replace the player's multi-field configurations with two existing operands. Persons, actual lived episodes, explicit named places, transforming tools, named two-person assignments and role mailboxes have defined behavior. No player supplies a process/method/channel/actor/destination tuple. Immediate lesson-and-replay and immediate reciprocal exchange remove installation ceremony rather than rename it. Every listed action has a plausible physical two-piece combine/placement analogue, but that physical interface has not been implemented or tested.

Starting tools and sites are deliberately authored capabilities. They are not discovered primitive ontology and should not be sold as such. Watchhouse, Mirror Quay and other sites supply a fixed callable local practice; these are inspectable places with distinct trajectories/interfaces, not user-configured bags. Geography, cultural reasoning and autonomous institutional life are not simulated. Names can evoke more than the actual finite mathematics; keep both visible.

## Findings repaired during review

Initially developer previews returned future IDs without the resulting named pieces or actual output trajectories, and site inspection omitted the process trajectory. This was a real discovery-interface gap: users could not inspect a future ID in the current world. The implementer added `resultPieces`, `newEpisodes`, hypothetical actors/schools/messages and exact site process trajectories. Independent regression checks pass. The earlier inadequacy is recorded here rather than represented as an already-correct initial interface.

## Important limitation: repertoire is not required at sites

Every site invokes `run` with its ready local practice. Returning there with a person who never learned an exchanged controller is legal and produces the same controller behavior. Thus later site use does **not** independently demonstrate that transferred controller knowledge enabled new action. The preserved substrate has `follow` checks, but the adapter does not expose that distinction. The exchange still causes real cross-source memories and teaches stored practice; successor capability can depend on actual teaching; the six endpoint predicates remain reached. Do not elevate those facts into evidence of causally required controller reuse. This is a disclosed shortcut and design boundary, not a reason to add installation chores.

## Remaining empirical question

The adapter solves a bounded interaction-grammar problem. It does not establish an enduring decision structure: all six endpoints have a very short fully informed witness, starting capabilities are broad, and no fresh player has yet evaluated discovery or consequences. Further work should explore genuinely different situations and changed purposes rather than stretching this witness toward 200 through redundant combines. No publication, external communication, UI deployment or fresh-player recruitment was performed in this review.
