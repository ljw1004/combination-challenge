# Observatory/conservatory: geometry proposal and bounded screen

26 October 2026. **Prospective new-version mechanics, not a played game or a length result.** Existing Working Materials, Retained Project V1 and the delivered browser checkpoint remain unchanged. This proposal follows the complete76-action retained play and its independent review; it does not reclassify those actions under new rules.

## Purpose before rules

Turn a functioning conservatory site into a place that still supports growing work **and** a useful common-center survey of two reference faces. Reusing the garden is allowed and may be satisfying; replacement is equally legal. The new question is coexistence and actual arrangement, not another list of records to copy or originals to carry home. No scarce inventory, required object identity, forced dismantling, diffuse-only plant or particular assembly silhouette is introduced.

The exact minimal introduction is the coordinated [arena specification](ideas-history-inheritance-arena-sketch.md). Its initially working garden occupies a later camera recording-plane position. Targets A=(5,1), front west, and B=(2,-2), front south determine a common front-on cardinal optical center (2,1). The camera's disclosed one-cell lens/recording-plane separation puts the recording plane at(1,1) when looking east and(2,2) when looking north. The first overlaps the initial root/tray; a tempting relocation to(2,2) clears one pose but blocks the other. A sidebay at(1,3) clears both. Location follows from an actual common-station comparison of visible front faces, not a hidden required-square flag. Records must expose center/heading; multiple cameras sequentially using that same center remain valid.

Supported staging is open. Whole relocation, demolition/replacement, unlimited copied lamps, clear enclosures, several smaller panels and alternative camera structures remain legal when functional. A garden may be temporarily disconnected: no every-action continuity trap is added. A shorter camera is not forbidden by fiat, but any such construction needs an explicitly implemented optical law; the screen evaluates the stated standard lens and a valid two-plate alternative, not every possible real-world instrument.

## Smallest coherent shared-core change

A separately versioned core may add the following **consistent geometric representation**, from the start of any later integrated campaign:

- Actual solid bodies have explicit occupied cells; supported frame/boundary parts have explicit segments or surfaces and lawful contact/containment relations. An enclosure's interior is not itself a solid body. Copies preserve those shapes and chosen relationships. Self-combination remains legal when the resulting contact is legal.
- Attachments carry explicit relative position/orientation and, where relevant, a hinge axis and connected subtree. One selected placement/join can specify its complete pose; do not add mandatory micro-positioning gestures or one action per cell.
- A pose change rotates that actual subtree and preserves its dimensions. It does not shrink every part of the host merely because some hinge is present. Initially check endpoint geometry only and state that abstraction; do not claim a simulated continuous sweep.
- Existing clear/opaque/reflective material distinctions apply at actual rays. The minimal screen needs no new scattering, heat diffusion, pressure, stocks or time physics. Camera front direction, actual focal separation and target front faces are public properties. Faithful observation retains actual optical center and heading.
- Whole transport remains one selected journey, tested against the actual geometry. Outside construction and actual disassembly/replacement remain lawful. Geometry is physical space, not an inventory quota; do not add a board object-count cap.

This is a substantive law change, not a bug fix to the old coarse model. A new full campaign must revalidate its station purposes under these same rules and actually play its full sequence. Do not silently assign geometric histories to old saved artifacts, forbid their conceptual reuse, or add the old76 to a separately played new level.

### Concrete old-station impact

The independently authored cart/hinge/camera utility took three combinations. Its canonical rigid lens/plate body occupies two cells under the proposed geometry. Attaching an external hinge remains a legal construction with the same three selected combinations, but rotating that connected rigid body leaves two occupied cells, not the legacy folded1×1 envelope. It may still travel through a straight appropriately oriented route; a narrow corner may differ. A player may construct a truly jointed lens/plate arrangement and fold it for transport, but it must restore actual optical alignment to function. No additional click is charged merely for recording geometry.

This is a precise behavioral consequence, **not** a computed new station solution cost. Full station placement/support/transport controls must be re-authored and checked before any campaign claim. Where a geometry law changes an old route, record the actual new action trace rather than assuming all added work is valuable.

## Executed design-level screen

`experiments/observatory-arrangement-screen.ts` evaluates the declared finite coordinate/pose constraints; [`screen.json`](../results/observatory-arrangement-screen/screen.json) retains all inputs/results and its source hash. It is not an import or replay of either old game, not a complete new construction engine, and not a player run. It intentionally does not invent ordinary-catalogue action costs for an unimplemented core.

Results:

- Among72 examined optical centers, exactly(2,1) satisfies both front-on cardinal relations.
- Initial garden blocks the east camera body; north works. Moving it to(2,2) reverses which pose works. This is a genuine changed arrangement consequence rather than a target-name difference.
- Among16 finite sample garden positions,11 support both camera poses and access. There are many valid answers, plus unbounded staging; do not treat the other positions as consumed resources.
- Rigid side relocation, whole replacement and a static future-ready garden+camera installation all pass.
- A richer alternative with one rotating lens and two fixed recording planes also passes at the sidebay. It need not rotate the whole camera body.
- A dimension-preserving roof hinge is geometrically coherent, but rotating only the roof does not remove the root/plate conflict. Hinge use is **not necessary** for this introduction.
- A properly arranged rich clear scaffold succeeds. Adding clear material or lamps to the conflicting initial body positions alone does not remove collision. This does not prove absence of other cheap policies.

The core has not yet built those assemblies, replayed their construction or checked all support/sweep/alternative-optics cases. Those are explicit next mechanical obligations, not facts inferred from the coordinate screen.

## Selection and falsifier

**Retain the narrow arrangement hypothesis:** the same known bodies change which service poses are possible, and a safe sidebay differs from a tempting near relocation for a disclosed reason. A cheap legal move-and-reconnect solution is appropriate as an introduction and must remain available. Earlier joinery's3+3 same-template solutions warn against treating every new silhouette as a new experienced purpose; they do not make all geometry invalid.

**Do not claim the stronger mechanism:** this introduction does not establish valuable hinge planning, irreversible inheritance, sustained play or the about-200 target. There is no need to defeat the successful relocation/static-scaffold controls.

The next implementation, if pursued, should first make these real source-exposed construction/replay witnesses work, then specify a **complete purposeful observatory/conservatory scenario with interacting services under exactly the same laws**. Select actual growing/work/observation/access purposes and consistent site geometry before any desired count. Avoid multiplying this one displacement at renamed anchors. Revalidate the retained station in the shared new-version core; then run complete ordinary/complete/saved-assembly and prefab controls before a prospective fresh combined campaign.

Falsifiers: geometry only adds bookkeeping to unchanged plans; alternative assembly placement eliminates every proposed relation without any context-sensitive choice; later tasks merely repeat cart/pose transport; or the complete scenario's reported value ends after the introduction. Preserve short successes and neutral familiar enactment. Do not demand a dilemma at each step, or repair a short route by blocking copies, adding narrow passages or enforcing demonstrations.
