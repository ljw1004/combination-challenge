# Mars artwork study

Completed 1 November 2026. The live [Mars story](../../stories/mars/mars.md) now has a complete physical equipment collection and integrated landscape targets. Its [design brief](../../stories/mars/design-brief.md) owns the lasting visual direction.

The user selected the restrained retro industrial direction developed in [inspiration/](inspiration/README.txt), then commissioned the complete collection. Small objects have a readable lower size bound; vehicles feel larger; buildings and spacecraft use a compressed upper bound. Natural aspect ratios remain visible. Fixed working places belong to the painted landscapes and rooms.

- [Production sources and tooling](production/README.md): 317 item identities, 35 fitted appearances, 27 chapter backgrounds and 24 landscape targets.
- [Outcomes](outcomes.md): adopted repairs, verification and evidence limits.
- [Review instructions](review-instructions.md): how the recognition and browser checks were conducted.

The original inspiration studies and verdicts are preserved. New source paintings were generated with the built-in image-generation tool; exact requests, selected references, extraction bounds and fitting/scene registration are retained with production. Detailed reviewer answers, browser captures, logs and checkpoints were temporary verification material and are not part of this collection.
