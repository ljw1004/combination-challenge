# Report

Story: projects/creation-study/creation.md

Play style: --fast

**Definitions**
- Worthwhile: counts actions supported by meaningful decision-making or a valued story payoff or reason to keep playing, counting each action once. Show the average credited actions / average total gameplay actions per player, not an average percentage.
- Fun: a narrower count of meaningful decisions: worthwhile deliberation or intentional experimentation. Measured by --detailed, including inspections and unsuccessful well-formed attempts; --fast reports it as not measured. Fast menu-assisted diagnostic ratios are not comparable.
- Hooks: the players' reported desire to continue: "high" means specific eagerness, "medium" means mild or conditional interest, and "low" means indifference or reluctance

## Chapter 1: Create the heavens and the earth

Worthwhile: 6/9 worthwhile decisions (average across 3 players)

Fun: not measured (--fast)

Hook: medium

Strongest moments: Sky stones striking the Rocky world to form the Moon was the shared strongest payoff: all three anticipated an impact, then explicitly valued debris gathering into a companion that reflects sunlight. Each named it most worthwhile and linked it to willingness to continue. Axial tilt + Sun producing Seasons supplied useful understanding explicitly valued by all three and supported continuation interest. Its story value survives the preserved disagreement about whether choosing the strongly hinted pairing was meaningful.

Weakest moments: Air + Rocky world producing Axial tilt left every player questioning the causal connection after expecting cooling or an atmosphere. The motivated experiment retained decision value, but the unexplained transformation weakened the educational chain. Sun + Rocky world producing Light was every player's least-worthwhile passage: it revealed the surface rather than advancing the anticipated Moon route. This was goal-expectation disappointment, not a reported protocol failure.

## Chapter 2: A place for the rain

Worthwhile: 5.7/7 worthwhile decisions (average across 3 players)

Fun: not measured (--fast)

Hook: medium

Strongest moments: Water + Stone crust → Steam cloud, followed by Steam cloud + Air → First rain, delivered the clearest shared educational payoff. ## Chapter 3: Water learns the way home

Worthwhile: 6/7 worthwhile decisions (average across 3 players)

Fun: not measured (--fast)

Hook: medium

Strongest moments: Sediment + Ocean → Delta turns transported fragments into new land through an understood settling process. ## Chapter 4: The stone is not finished

Worthwhile: 6.7/7 worthwhile decisions (average across 3 players)

Fun: not measured (--fast)

Hook: medium/high (2 medium, 1 high)

Strongest moments: Volcano + Water → Hydrothermal vent: all three players name this their most worthwhile moment. The circulation, heat and dissolved-chemical explanation turns a predictable construction into an explicitly valued payoff, regardless of the graders' disagreement about decision meaningfulness. Bringing rock minerals into the sea through rainfall weathering and river transport: all three distinguish release from transport when choosing actions and find the final connection satisfying. Players 2 and 3 also explicitly value the weathering explanation itself.

Weakest moments: Moving plates + Fire → Volcano: all three players identify the unexplained creation label as the thinnest result. This is an explanatory weakness, not interface friction; all three graders nevertheless preserve the meaningful heat-seeking choice. Moving plates + Earth → Continent: the forced action's immediate relevance to mineral delivery is unclear for Players 1 and 2. The criticism is not unanimous: Players 2 and 3 separately value the crust explanation, so unclear purpose should not erase its educational payoff.
