# Visual research

The selected direction is a painted celestial atlas: luminous, plainly drawn forms in circular windows, with the same visual care given to a star, a decomposer and a remembered song. The circles represent phenomena and capabilities; their movement is symbolic. Backgrounds supply scale and continuity rather than literal benches on which galaxies sit.

Two concrete works were inspected on 5 November 2026:

- [Giovanni di Paolo, *The Creation of the World and the Expulsion from Paradise* (1445), Metropolitan Museum of Art](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/458971). Its lapis, ochre, coral and pale rings make enormous scale feel intimate and precious. Borrow the concentrated colour, layered landscape and circular framing. Do not borrow its religious figures, geocentric model, punishment narrative, cracked surface or composition. The museum identifies the image as public domain. It was inspected as research, not supplied as an image-generation input or shipped in the game.
- [Ernst Haeckel, radiolarian plate from *Kunstformen der Natur*, Smithsonian Ocean / Biodiversity Heritage Library](https://ocean.si.edu/holding-tank/images-hide/radiolarians). Light contours against dark grounds separate related organic forms, while silhouettes and internal structures carry distinctions. Borrow that observational attention and family consistency, simplifying strongly for 64-pixel play. Do not copy the specimens, labels, densely packed layout or treat these later eukaryotes as the first cells. The illustration was inspected as technique research, not supplied as a generation input or shipped.

The initial proposal combines opaque gouache masses with selective fine ink contours and sparse ivory-gold highlights. Small-scale recognition, related-state differences and play over an actual scene must approve the treatment before full production. Exact abstract meanings can depend on the game's ordinary names and readings; an untimed model recognition review cannot establish human recognition speed or preference.

The track repair used anatomical reference rather than generic three-toed dinosaur signs. [Texas Parks and Wildlife's Dinosaur Valley description](https://tpwd.texas.gov/state-parks/dinosaur-valley/nature) distinguishes large rounded hind prints from smaller horseshoe-shaped front prints. The [University of Birmingham's Spyway account](https://www.birmingham.ac.uk/news/2024/walking-with-dinosaurs-in-dorset-the-spyway-sauropod-footprints) supplies actual preserved track-site context. The selected fictional trackway simplifies those structural differences into two oval/D-shaped step pairs and preserves their arrangement from mud to stone. It does not reproduce a particular fossil site or establish its maker from the icon alone. No research photograph is shipped or used as a generation input.
