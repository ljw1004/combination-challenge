# Creation working notes

## Initial hypothesis — 5 November 2026

An elemental creator can make systems whose consequences become the next invitation to play. Discovery should grow from cosmic spectacle toward responsibility and independent creativity. A predictable combination can still earn its place when its result changes the world or makes an earlier creation newly meaningful.

Reject the draft if creation collapses into renamed assembly chores, explanations conceal arbitrary ingredient pairs, later chapters merely escalate praise, or the four starting powers are quietly supplemented with ready-made supplies. Test chapter purposes and combinations before commissioning artwork.

Fresh manuscript reviewers receive the current story and applicable brief without earlier verdicts. Logical chapter players receive only the canonical frozen player packets and ordinary checkpoint history. AI assessments and legal completion do not establish human enjoyment or learning.
