## Remaining ambiguity

The recurring medium-confidence readings are contents or materials rather than failures to recognize a broad object. For 01, a bowl and separate crystals can suggest salt or salt production as well as saltwater. For 09, the vessel and fruit do not visually establish fermentation. The free-floating shapes in 10 can be smoke, steam, or heat; A and B show a source that favors smoke. Most versions of 18 could be soil, compost, or manure. Sheet A's 14 reads as a generic masonry or stone block without color, while B's 08 reads more specifically as a patterned rug than a plain woven mat.
