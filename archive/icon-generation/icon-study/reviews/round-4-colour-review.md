## Comparison with the woodcut reference

After recording the guesses, I inspected `references/icon-sets/art-styles/woodcut.png`.

**Colour:** The variant shares the reference's warm paper, dark ink, ochre, rusty red, olive, and muted blue palette. The wind and window are particularly close in colour family. The variant's oranges and fruit colours feel somewhat richer, and its shading gives the individual objects more volume.

**Cut marks:** There is a recognizable print-like texture, especially in the thatch, mat, brick, bread, and wooden frames. Much of it reads as fine hatching and detailed line illustration. The reference uses larger black masses, broader pale cuts, rougher edges, and more forceful simplification. The variant therefore feels more delicately drawn than the reference's bold block printing. The reference was displayed larger, so fine-texture comparisons have a scale limitation; the difference in broad ink masses remains apparent.

**Cute / game-inventory feel:** It still reads strongly as a game inventory: isolated objects, consistent centered presentation, small grounding shadows, and carefully detailed miniature props. The cottage, jug with fruit, and food table feel cozy and slightly quaint. The cut marks and heavier outlines give it more roughness, but the overall mood remains friendly. I would call it a warm illustrated inventory with woodcut texture; overt cuteness is mild, while the inventory impression is strong.

This remains one previously exposed reviewer's judgement, not independent first-use evidence or a player study.
