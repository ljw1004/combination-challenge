# Page ideas and their verdicts

The user’s feedback changed the role of decoration: a cookbook page should have atmosphere and appetite, while making movable food and fixed apparatus distinct.

| Idea | Retained example | Verdict and recreation cues |
|---|---|---|
| Repeated botanical notebook grounds | [Grain ground](../cookbook/production/artwork/background-grain.png) | Pleasant but repetitive, with too many carrots. Begin each new painting from the chapter’s goals and culinary theme. |
| Clear ingredients below, a small object above | [Isolated pastry arrangement](sources/superseded/butter-between-the-layers-isolated.png) | Too much like draggable ingredients. Decorative food needs a setting and relationships. The clear upper object had the same problem. |
| Contextual culinary spread | [Ravioli](sources/ribbons-and-ravioli.png), [salad](sources/leaves-mustard-and-crunch.png) | Successful: bowl, fork, herbs, crumbs, cloth or table suggest a composed spread. These finished paintings also document the earlier, taller lower compositions. |
| Shallow spread bleeding into the bottom | [Approved paintings](../page-composition/README.md) | Selected. Roughly half the earlier height, with natural variation. No blank strip below. A faint decorative top border replaces the isolated upper object. About five of the 35 pages use tablecloths where they help connect the setting. |

Different surfaces follow the cooking: cool pale marble for pastry, warm oat paper for breakfast, quiet mineral or wooden suggestions elsewhere. Keep the central field subtle. Avoid rigid side/bottom borders that look broken when cropped. Generate enough page height for the artist to compose naturally, then use the retained native framing to move the lower spread into a shallow bottom bleed.

[Browse these examples](index.html). All 35 current source paintings and finished station-bearing pages remain intact. The rest of the earlier full chapter sets, duplicate exports and correction attempts have been discarded; these examples and the final chapter briefs carry their useful artistic lessons.
