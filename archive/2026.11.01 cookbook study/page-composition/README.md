# Approved page paintings

All **35 final source paintings** remain unchanged in [images](images/), named for their culinary themes. [plan.json](plan.json) records each chapter’s goals, upper motif, lower spread, working surface, tablecloth choice and crop offset. Its prompt paths point to the retained final art requests. References to discarded intermediate edit inputs have been removed: use the finished painting and its brief when revisiting that direction.

The user approved the pastry, salad and tea previews, then the complete set. Lower spreads are shallow, faint and bleed into the bottom; top decoration has similar faintness. Five pages use cloth. The paintings are complete artwork and remain as PNGs, including the three approved previews.

[Finished pages with pencil stations](../pencil-stations/production/backgrounds/) are the current presentation. [Page development](../backgrounds/README.md) explains the earlier alternatives. Native [page-frame.mjs](tools/page-frame.mjs) preserves the approved cropping method. The [page builder](../pencil-stations/production/tools/build.mjs) recreates grounds temporarily from these paintings before adding registered apparatus; intermediate cropped grounds are not stored permanently.
