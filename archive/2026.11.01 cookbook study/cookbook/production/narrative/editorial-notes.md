# Practical cookbook rework

The current manuscript is [Cookbook](../../../../../stories/cookbook/cookbook.md). [UX](../../../../../docs/ux.md#item-artwork) owns its adopted voice, teaching placement, equipment presentation and serving structure. [Study outcomes](../../../outcomes.md) record verification and remaining limits; [notes](../../../../../notes.md#1-november-2026--practical-cookbook-and-three-serving-beats) hold dated authority and source provenance.

The user rejected the earlier adaptation’s elliptical notebook voice and supplied direct rewrites of the first three chapters. The current prose uses brief captions and occasional principles of culinary judgment. The user’s opening examples set its register; the earlier explanatory paragraphs were rejected as wordy and mechanical. The local *Le Cordon Bleu: Kitchen Essentials* remains a reference for culinary accuracy, while the supplied *Flavor Bible* material informs the few thoughts about flavour, texture and balance. These are reference approaches, not prose or recipes to imitate verbatim.

Breakfast introduces making from basic ingredients. Afternoon tea is chapter 13 of 38. It gathers bread and butter, plain scones, brown-butter biscuits, sponge cake and jam on a tiered stand; a pot of tea is brewed separately on the same page. Further cooking leads to a dinner of Wellington, bean pie, salad, juice and several desserts. The same cooked mushroom filling (duxelles) connects ravioli to Wellington; brown butter first made for biscuits later dresses the ravioli. Dinner is assembled directly on a compound feast table. Preserving remains an earlier lesson, with sealed beans kept out of the final meal.

The original Cook’s Pen source paintings remain intact. The [rework artwork](../../../rework-art/README.md) supplies finished food, sauce vessels, changed apparatus and serving furniture. Afternoon tea and dinner take place in furnished home interiors rather than cookbook pages.

The [original travelling manuscript](../baseline/kitchen-caravan.md) remains a historical source. The earlier 36-chapter adaptation preserved its mechanical sequence and ended with a tin on a supper trolley; that structure has been superseded. The Cookbook project remains open for further refinement and human trials.
