## 6 November 2026 — playable development

The user authorized developing the selected galaxy encounter into a complete combination game, aiming for about 200 combinations, with repeated fresh reviews and `--fast` playtests, followed by artwork. The earlier request also calls for `--detailed` evaluation. The manuscript remains in this project, outside the released catalogue. The existing `cosmic.md` is the unreleased baseline until a replacement is ready.

The starting question is **What can a collision destroy when almost every star survives?** The player follows actual matter through different responses to the same encounter. A star stream can pass through; gas can collide, shed heat and make stars; those newly made stars then behave differently on the return encounter. No human protagonist, domestic framing, portable Gravity or Time, or inventory of subatomic prerequisites is required.

### Experiment sequence

1. Compare three substantially different game architectures: continuous ensemble, reconstruction mystery, and an exploratory atlas with a shorter required spine.
2. Prototype difficult passages using text items. Independently review narrative necessity, forward understanding, scientific plausibility and mechanical continuity.
3. Expand the viable architecture toward approximately 200 worthwhile combinations. Retain concrete alternatives at substantive revisions; cut operations whose only contribution is a renamed state or increased count.
4. Run frozen chapter playtests through the canonical harness, with fresh players, independent graders and chapter synthesis. Use fast mode for hook/pacing development and detailed mode for information-seeking and meaningful decisions. Record mode, exact version, scope, model choices and limits. Revise weak chapters through multiple hypotheses and retest.
5. Only after the textual game is strong, compare art directions using the same representative objects and recurring scenes. Use circular icons for material things and distinct fixed-region artwork. Review recognition and continuity separately, then integrate and inspect the browser at phone and desktop sizes.

The [standing evaluation guide](../../docs/fun-and-evaluation.md) owns all player and grader rubrics. Individual editorial reports and frozen runtime packets are disposable under `runs/cosmic-game/`; selected story/design/art deliverables remain in this project. Requested playtest response arrays and reports remain in timestamped project `playtests/` directories. AI completion and attributed enthusiasm do not establish human enjoyment or duration.
