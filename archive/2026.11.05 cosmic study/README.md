# Cosmic: A Sea Beneath the Moon — completed study

This archive records the first completed development pass. The [unreleased story](../../projects/cosmic-study/cosmic.md), [single active design brief](../../projects/cosmic-study/design-brief.md) and runtime artwork have since returned to the [active study](../../projects/cosmic-study/README.md) because the story is not ready for release. The study provides its local preview route. Development began in `projects/cosmic-study`; the completed supporting material moved here on 5 November 2026.

The story follows matter through early atoms, stellar generations, a solar disk, rocky and icy worlds, the Earth–Moon encounter, then rain, rock and an uninhabited sea. It contains 198 authored combinations, 245 distinct items, 24 playable chapters and a text-only ending. Its 245 circular illustrations and 16 backgrounds use an astronomical chromolithograph/chalk treatment, retaining the same Earth, early Moon and basalt shore.

- [Original brainstorming](brainstorming.txt), preserved unchanged as the supplied starting material.
- [Scientific premises and sources](research.md), including selected models and their limits.
- [Iteration outcomes](iterations.md) and [editorial review outcomes](editorial-review.md), including cuts, repaired affordances, factual corrections and remaining local friction.
- [Fast and detailed playtests](playtests/): requested structured player responses, independent chapter reports and mechanically assembled campaign reports. Historical Story paths and hashes identify the tested drafts and remain unchanged.
- [Artwork production](production/README.md): visual references, meaning catalogue, selected masters, exact generation requests, necessary inputs, export geometry and selected-asset hashes. All generation used the built-in image tool.
- [Artwork reviews](art-review/README.md): concise instructions and outcomes for the pilot, complete families, repaired diagrams and background continuity.

AI chapter trials, informed reviews and Chromium checks provide bounded evidence. They do not establish human enjoyment, retained learning, an uninterrupted human playthrough or physical-device behavior. Retained reports are not replayable sessions. Detailed verification logs, frozen runtime copies, trial panels and screenshots were disposable scratch, following the [retention policy](../../docs/engineering.md#verification-records).

The user authorized autonomous invention, repeated fast/detailed playtesting, artwork after text acceptance, promotion, commit and push. Existing hosting and private-access settings remain unchanged. The supplied brainstorming’s old counts and reported critiques are historical ideas, not measurements of the completed game.
