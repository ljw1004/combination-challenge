# Cosmic study — abandoned

The user abandoned the entire Cosmic project on **6 November 2026**, after playing **Cosmic: The Lights That Remain** for roughly ten chapters. Both this rewrite and **A Sea Beneath the Moon** are retired. This is a failure archive for future authors, with no pending redevelopment or release. Historical statements that a draft is current, accepted or ready for review describe their original development phase.

The user's decisive finding was that the constructions did not feel worthwhile. Creation offered more recognisable real-world things at a useful level of abstraction; Cosmic's many finely distinguished objects did not earn the same attention. Valid moves were easy to find with ordinary compatibility hints. The [dated decision record](../../notes.md#6-november-2026--worthwhile-hook-and-retirement-of-cosmic-and-cyberpunk) preserves that diagnosis, including the correction to the earlier interface/reading hypothesis. The standing [worthwhile-hook requirement and cautionary tale](../../docs/story-authoring.md#require-a-worthwhile-hook) govern future authoring.

## Preservation and use

The original supporting archive and all material moved from `projects/cosmic-study/` share this first-date archive home. The two earlier overview documents are retained as dated records; the first pass's research and iterations have dated filenames to distinguish them from the rewrite. The manuscripts, artwork, production inputs and structured playtest deliverables are preserved. Source paths, hashes, prompts and commands inside historical records identify their original versions; old preview URLs and tool commands are not current instructions. There is no live Cosmic manuscript or active brief.

Both versions remain excluded from the published catalogue and hosting through the existing archive exclusion. Archival does not migrate or erase browser saves at their former URLs. Follow the [archive policy](../README.md#project-evidence) and [verification retention policy](../../docs/engineering.md#verification-records) when consulting this material.
