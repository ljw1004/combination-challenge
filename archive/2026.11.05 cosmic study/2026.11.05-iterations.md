# Cosmic development outcomes

The user authorized autonomous invention, roughly 200 combinations, numerous fast iterations, detailed playtesting, artwork with circular icons and continuity reviews, promotion to `stories/cosmic`, commit and GitHub push on 5 November 2026. The active [design brief](design-brief.md) owns intent; this file records outcomes, not additional rules.

## Opening prototype — first fast cycle

The first playable prototype had eight chapters and 66 recipes. Three fresh Terra Medium players tested each of chapters 1, 3, 6 and 8 through `--fast`; each record received a fresh Astra High grade and each chapter a separate fresh synthesis. The tool-free CLI transport supplied only the canonical player packet and ordinary public checkpoint history. All twelve records replay-verified. The [assembled report](playtests/2026.11.05_00.19.03/report.md) preserves attributed findings; individual chapter reports retain hashes, model/effort, exact guidance and evidence limits.

Transparency, stellar carbon/oxygen, water before the Sun and the cold/warm disk distinction supplied concrete reported hooks. Reviewers also found three specific weaknesses: adding hydrogen to an already hydrogen-rich gathering cloud, adding dust to water already on grains, and an abstract “Open space” cooling operand. The first two recipes were removed and their useful explanatory content retained. Repeated core compression and the thin agency of sole-option stretches remain questions for the full manuscript and detailed tests. Fast play does not measure the headline Fun ratio or establish human enjoyment.

## Full manuscript under review

The expanded draft has 24 playable chapters and 189 authored recipes; the legal reference route uses 177. Its mean arrival inventory is 6.2 objects and its maximum is nine. It adds slower stellar enrichment, a comparison of gas-rich and gas-poor giant formation, differentiated building materials, the surviving Earth after the impact, and a coast whose sea exists before weathering and tides act on it. These are structural measurements, not duration or quality claims.

Three independent full-manuscript reviews found a connected arc but repeated stages in star ignition, icy-body growth, impact preparation, tides and ice-giant naming. Those stages were merged; the useful water-surface intermediate, differentiation, failed rain and dissolved-versus-suspended rock distinctions remain. Carbonaceous wind dust now contributes to the later mixture. The revised manuscript has 181 combinations. Its scale follows the useful material journey rather than an exact recipe quota.

Scientific corrections distinguish helium’s earlier recombination from hydrogen’s last-scattering era, keep the disk-era Sun powered by contraction/accretion, make deep mixing explicit in evolved stars, and use local subsurface rock in hydrothermal chemistry. Pre-impact crystals no longer supply post-impact crust. An interior view is named Earth’s magma ocean so it cannot imply a second Earth alongside the differentiated planet.

The [second fast cycle](playtests/2026.11.05_00.35.32/report.md) replay-verified all 21 players. It independently supported the impact-preparation and ice-giant cuts, identified simultaneous hot/cooled surface confusion, and found the finale’s late sunlight-creation step weak. Sunlight is now required in its early solar chapter. The final water goal offers open-sea moonlight or a still-pool reflection, carrying the pool forward for a real ending choice. Optional scattering and escaping-debris routes received explicit available inputs.

A new independent informed acceptance review inspected all 181 recipes and accepted the overall structure, continuity and ending. Its one required correction was to make the still pool’s condition calm weather rather than cooling alone; that is adopted. Two further stage-compression suggestions were editorial options, not factual blockers.

The coast now explicitly cools unevenly: still-hot lava and cool neighbouring rock coexist, with the lava input preserved as remaining patches. This addresses the three players’ state confusion without implying that the whole planet cools uniformly before any liquid water remains. Fresh fast and detailed trials test this hypothesis and the calm-pool ending. The whole revised manuscript is also in a fresh fast cohort, with eight chapters tested in detailed mode. Artwork has not begun.

## Full coverage and successive detailed revisions

The [full fast campaign](playtests/2026.11.05_00.47.00/report.md) completed all 25 chapter assignments: 75 fresh players, 522 actions, 75 independent grades and 25 chapter syntheses, with replay verification and no transport failures. Fresh detailed cohorts covered the hardest stellar, disk, impact, cooling, tide and ending passages. Subsequent paired fast/detailed cycles retested revised passages rather than reusing participants. Reported concrete hooks included hydrogen traveling inside hydrated rock, the Moon’s pale crust, rain finally remaining on cooler ground and water returning to the sea. The cycles also exposed physically reasonable attempts that failed: illuminating hydrogen outside the star, rain reaching already cool rock, continuing stellar contraction, and the Moon reflecting in an ordinary pool. Those routes now work. Exact star-core fuel is distinct from surrounding hydrogen so illumination cannot create an internal stellar core.

The remaining repeated explosions, disk preparation, asteroid alteration and lava-skin steps were compressed. The same sea remains available during evaporation, and sunlight remains a continuing source. The ending accepts moonlight on open water or in a rock pool. No life is introduced as a reward or implied destination.

The [fast](playtests/2026.11.05_01.24.18/report.md) and [detailed](playtests/2026.11.05_01.24.15/report.md) follow-ups covered chapters 4, 8, 11 and 17 after those cuts. The reports retain negative findings as well as positive ones: repeated compression and inspection can feel routine, and causal inference can be frustrating even when graders credit it as meaningful. We did not optimize the ratios or treat AI enthusiasm as proof of human enjoyment.

Fresh full-source and focused causal reviewers found no remaining scientific blocker in their recorded scopes. Their adopted refinements clarify hydrogen molecules leaving grain surfaces, overlapping solid-concentration mechanisms, the contraction-powered young Sun, and the difference between the surviving Earth and a view of its magma ocean. The final narrow remnant correction uses dense-matter pressure to resist collapse.
