# Report

Story: projects/cosmic-study/cosmic.md

Play style: --detailed

**Definitions**
- Worthwhile: counts actions supported by meaningful decision-making or a valued story payoff or reason to keep playing, counting each action once. Show the average credited actions / average total gameplay actions per player, not an average percentage.
- Fun: a narrower count of meaningful decisions: worthwhile deliberation or intentional experimentation. Measured by --detailed, including inspections and unsuccessful well-formed attempts; --fast reports it as not measured. Fast menu-assisted diagnostic ratios are not comparable.
- Hooks: the players' reported desire to continue: "high" means specific eagerness, "medium" means mild or conditional interest, and "low" means indifference or reluctance

## Chapter 3: Inside the furnace

Worthwhile: 10/10 worthwhile decisions (average across 3 players)

Fun: 6/10 meaningful decisions (average across 3 players)

Hook: medium/high (1 medium, 2 high)

Strongest moments: Combining carbon- and oxygen-burning layers into an onion of burning shells is the shared strongest moment: every player names it most worthwhile, and every grader credits the recovery or assembly reasoning. Its payoff is understanding that different burning regions coexist within one star, not merely completing a construction. Combining the onion of burning shells with silicon-burning material to make an iron-group core gives all three a satisfying explanation of why fusion support ends and a concrete question about collapse. For Player 3, making oxygen-rich material from carbon and helium additionally connects this stellar sequence to later rock, water and worlds.

Weakest moments: Trying to advance silicon-burning material before assembling the other layers exposes an unclear prerequisite for every player. Player 1's subsequent inspection of the persistent star adds a second empty list and explicit mild frustration. These are interface/dependency weaknesses, even though the independent graders credit the diagnostic reasoning. Repeated compression of successive burning regions offers limited fresh deliberation. All graders classify the later compression advances as routine, while disagreeing about the first transfer to carbon. The players still value specific explanations along this sequence, so mechanical thinness should not be mistaken for an absence of educational payoff.

Hook: medium/high (1 medium, 2 high)

## Chapter 24: A sea beneath the Moon

Worthwhile: 7/7 worthwhile decisions (average across 3 players)

Fun: 2.7/7 meaningful decisions (average across 3 players)

Hook: medium

Strongest moments: Returning freshwater through rain on the rocky rise and a stream to the sea combined the shared strongest inference with a valued construction payoff. All three connected terrain-driven uplift to rainfall; the returning stream closed the cycle, with Players 2 and 3 explicitly reporting satisfaction. Making reflected sunlight and placing it across the sea supplied a shared explanatory and scenic payoff. Weakest moments: Cloud made from seawater + wind was the clearest routine connector: all graders classified it nonmeaningful, and Players 1 and 3 named it least worthwhile. Its acknowledged explanatory connection supports modest value, but not substantial deliberation; no interface friction was reported. Evaporating water + cooler air repeated the mineral-separation explanation. Player 2 explicitly found this less interesting and requested greater concision, whereas Players 1 and 3 reported clarity or usefulness without that complaint. This is explanatory repetition, not an arbitrary recipe or interface failure.
