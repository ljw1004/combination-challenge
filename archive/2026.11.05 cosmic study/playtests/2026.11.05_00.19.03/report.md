**Definitions**
- Worthwhile: counts actions supported by meaningful decision-making or a valued story payoff or reason to keep playing, counting each action once. Show the average credited actions / average total gameplay actions per player, not an average percentage.
- Fun: a narrower count of meaningful decisions: worthwhile deliberation or intentional experimentation. Measured by --detailed, including inspections and unsuccessful well-formed attempts; --fast reports it as not measured. Fast menu-assisted diagnostic ratios are not comparable.
- Hooks: the players' reported desire to continue: "high" means specific eagerness, "medium" means mild or conditional interest, and "low" means indifference or reluctance

## Chapter 1: The light gets out

Worthwhile: 6/6 worthwhile decisions (average across 3 players)

Fun: not measured (--fast)

Hook: high

Strongest moments: Primordial gas + Trapped radiation → Freely travelling light is the shared strongest payoff: all three players name it most worthwhile for connecting neutral atoms, reduced electron scattering and transparency. All independent graders also credit its modest causal decision value. Gas gathering in a gravity well + Neutral hydrogen → A dense knot of primordial gas supplies the shared continuation hook: needing to lose heat turns apparent completion into a concrete question about how stars form, despite reservations about the combination itself.

Weakest moments: Adding Neutral hydrogen to gas already described as primordial creates a shared causal-clarity gap. All three question why the extra combination is needed; the inflow explanation partly repairs it, while the cooling hook remains valuable. This is a recipe-framing weakness, not recorded interface malfunction. Early single-option assembly offers little deliberation. Players 1 and 2 identify combining Neutral hydrogen with Neutral helium as least worthwhile; Player 3 instead identifies Helium nuclei + Electrons. Their specific explanations remain useful, so these are relative engagement weaknesses rather than valueless actions.

## Chapter 6: Ice before the Sun

Worthwhile: 7/8 worthwhile decisions (average across 3 players)

Fun: not measured (--fast)

Hook: medium/high (2 medium, 1 high)

Strongest moments: Building water through oxygen adsorption, hydroxyl and a second hydrogen addition was the shared strongest sequence. All three valued the visible intermediate and grain mechanism; producing water before planets supplied a striking or satisfying payoff even where the next action was explicitly prescribed. Creating ice-mantled dust revealed that some interstellar water could survive solar-system formation. All three expressed interest in that survival and wanted to follow ancient ice onward, giving this result story value despite their shared criticism of the combination itself.

Weakest moments: Water ice on grains + Mixed interstellar dust → Ice-mantled dust was the unanimous weak recipe: ice was already on grains, so another dust addition felt repetitive and insufficiently explained. This is a construction-level causal weakness, not interface friction; its valued ice-survival revelation remains a separate strength.

The results clarified cooling, so this is primarily interface shorthand rather than evidence that the material story lacked coherence.
