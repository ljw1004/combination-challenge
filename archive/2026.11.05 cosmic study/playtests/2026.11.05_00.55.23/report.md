# Report

Play style: --fast

**Definitions**
- Worthwhile: counts actions supported by meaningful decision-making or a valued story payoff or reason to keep playing, counting each action once. Show the average credited actions / average total gameplay actions per player, not an average percentage.
- Fun: a narrower count of meaningful decisions: worthwhile deliberation or intentional experimentation. Measured by --detailed, including inspections and unsuccessful well-formed attempts; --fast reports it as not measured. Fast menu-assisted diagnostic ratios are not comparable.
- Hooks: the players' reported desire to continue: "high" means specific eagerness, "medium" means mild or conditional interest, and "low" means indifference or reluctance

## Chapter 14: The iron goes down

Worthwhile: 5.7/7 worthwhile decisions (average across 3 players)

Fun: not measured (--fast)

Hook: medium

Strongest moments: Making metal droplets and drawing them downward with gravity gave all three players their clearest valued picture of differentiation. They specifically appreciated the intermediate droplets and multiple sinking pathways, independently of the limited choices. Creating fragile crustal rafts supplied a shared revelation: Earth's first solid surface was temporary and uneven, not a permanent shell. All three connected this changing surface to curiosity about what follows, despite criticizing the route.

Weakest moments: The convection-to-crust chain became single-option execution: Players 1 and 3 called it repetitive or predictable. Players 2 and 3 also found crystals plus hot melt causally weak; Player 2 remained unclear about gathering, whereas Player 3 found some explanation in the result. This combines menu-led repetition with a recipe-explanation weakness, not protocol friction. Combining sinking metal with its magma-ocean setting felt mechanical to Player 1. Players 2 and 3 instead included the core-forming passage among their strongest experiences; all three valued the explanation that differentiation continues.

## Chapter 17: Rock lets go of its water

Worthwhile: 5.3/8 worthwhile decisions (average across 3 players)

Fun: not measured (--fast)

Hook: medium/high (2 medium, 1 high)

Player 3's repetition complaint extended through cracking and renewed lava, whereas Player 1 reported no purposeless action. The weakness concerns abstraction and limited agency, not a demonstrated interface malfunction.

## Chapter 24: A sea beneath the Moon

Worthwhile: 6/8 worthwhile decisions (average across 3 players)

Fun: not measured (--fast)

Hook: medium

Strongest moments: Making rain over the rocky rise and returning it as a stream combined the chapter's strongest reasoning with a satisfying water-cycle payoff. All three inferred terrain-driven rainfall; Player 1 especially valued that mechanism, Player 2 emphasized closing the loop, and Player 3 valued both. Combining reflected sunlight with the still pool produced an appreciated quiet ending for all three players. Players 2 and 3 linked the image to continuation interest; Player 1 also found it a satisfying stopping point. Its value was aesthetic closure, not an additional meaningful decision.

Weakest moments: Carrying the cloud over land was explicitly predictable and least worthwhile for Players 1 and 3. Player 2 recognized its causal role but reported no distinct payoff. This was thin transitional execution rather than arbitrary combination logic. Preparing the still pool exposed the menu's limited choice: Player 2 named it the weakest prerequisite, and all graders classified it as routine. Nevertheless, all three players appreciated its quiet contrast with the sea, so decision weakness did not eliminate atmospheric value.
