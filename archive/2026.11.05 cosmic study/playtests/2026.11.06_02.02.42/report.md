# Report

Story: projects/cosmic-study/galaxies.md

Play style: --fast

**Definitions**
- Worthwhile: counts actions supported by meaningful decision-making or a valued story payoff or reason to keep playing, counting each action once. Show the average credited actions / average total gameplay actions per player, not an average percentage.
- Fun: a narrower count of meaningful decisions: worthwhile deliberation or intentional experimentation. Measured by --detailed, including inspections and unsuccessful well-formed attempts; --fast reports it as not measured. Fast menu-assisted diagnostic ratios are not comparable.
- Hooks: the players' reported desire to continue: "high" means specific eagerness, "medium" means mild or conditional interest, and "low" means indifference or reluctance

## Chapter 1: A collision without flame

Worthwhile: 4/4 worthwhile decisions (average across 3 players)

Fun: not measured (--fast)

Hook: medium/high (1 medium, 2 high)

Strongest moments: Sending the orange star through the other galaxy and colliding dense gas created the strongest shared contrast: surviving stellar lights versus gas compression, heating and cooling. All three explicitly valued both contributions and named the dense-gas explanation most worthwhile. Combining thin gas with crossing gas to create a hot bridge extended the comparison into the chapter's only independently credited meaningful decision for each player. Players 1 and 3 emphasized surprise; Player 2 valued the clarification while also finding the passage somewhat repetitive.

Weakest moments: Moving the outer cloud to the other galaxy's outskirts left only one gameplay action. Players 1 and 3 called it least engaging, and all graders found no meaningful decision there. The weakness was constrained agency, not arbitrary storytelling: all three still valued the tidal result. The thin-gas crossing repeated the dense-gas interaction enough for Player 2 to name it least worthwhile. This was not shared dissatisfaction: Players 1 and 3 emphasized its surprising outcome, and Player 2 also acknowledged its clarifying value.
