# H — Boundaries that redirect light

Revised concept for [Before the Cloud Grew Thin](../../narrative/round-3/h-dust.md): a very short passage with **two complete operations**, making mineral seeds and growing them through encounters with an existing local population. Change the material to magnesium silicate, specifically forsterite. Change the optical consequence to scattering. The question remains: **what can stay together inside a cloud that is coming apart?**

**Three physical cases.** Before combining, follow three portions of the same escaping band. In a hot, crowded patch, frequent encounters cannot preserve the proposed mineral structures. A cooler, stretched filament has widely separated particles: conditions permit survival, but too few encounters build substantial grains during its expansion. A cooler, crowded knot retains frequent encounters. Plain descriptions expose these differences before the player acts; temperature and density are neither hidden requirements nor draggable ingredients. Each portion keeps its own material. The player never transfers atoms from an unsuccessful case into the successful one.

The playable knot also has the specified magnesium, silicon and oxygen chemistry, at an epoch when destructive ionization has weakened. “Cool and crowded” alone does not guarantee dust. The three cases illustrate contrasting histories, rather than claiming a universal two-condition recipe.

**Supplies and actions.** Three physical supplies occupy a close view inside that knot: silicon-and-oxygen gas, magnesium atoms, and an older population of small mineral seeds. The gas includes silicon monoxide and available oxygen; magnesium supplies the third constituent. The older seeds formed earlier from a separate portion of the same local gas, with that provenance stated at arrival. Nucleation is already occurring unevenly through the knot. Nothing arrives from another stellar layer during play.

1. **Silicon-and-oxygen gas + Magnesium atoms → New mineral seeds.** This complete chemical operation includes molecule formation, small-cluster reactions and competing destruction. It produces a population of magnesium-silicate clusters, rather than promising a macroscopic grain from one encounter. Both gas supplies contribute actual atoms. The resulting seeds replace their local gas representation at a fixed position within the knot; they do not enter a portable inventory. The reading distinguishes assembling existing atoms from the earlier creation of elements through nuclear reactions.
2. **Older mineral seeds + New mineral seeds → Grown grains.** The older population contributes additional solid matter and collision partners. The gesture enacts local random encounters and sticking, including repeated collisions over the growth interval. Its source and receiver are neighboring populations in the same close view, already intermingling. It cannot relocate a dust screen across the remnant. The created seeds enter the larger solids, and both populations are incorporated into the resulting grain population at that location. No gas-accretion step is assumed.

This separates chemical assembly from subsequent growth while reusing the player's material. It has no collectible cooling, deadline, stoichiometric inventory or repetitions to meet a recipe quota.

**Consequence and its limits.** Growing grains automatically changes the light passing through their existing location. There is no additional “apply light” gesture. [Sarangi and Cherchneff's clumpy-ejecta model](https://arxiv.org/html/1412.5522v1) produces large forsterite grains around 0.3–0.4 micrometres in radius. The earlier silica choice is withdrawn: that study explicitly leaves its nucleation and condensation unmodeled. [Bladh and colleagues](https://arxiv.org/abs/1302.6572) model efficient scattering by forsterite grains in the relevant size range. Their stellar-wind setting supports the optical mechanism; it does not establish this remnant's appearance.

The proposed scene assumes a bright far-side patch, weaker surrounding illumination, and enough dust along the intervening path for a scattering optical depth around one. This column and geometry remain unverified model premises requiring a radiative-transfer check. The intended effect is partial dimming with redirected light, without a guaranteed black seam or absorption-powered infrared finale.

**Required prose changes.** In the narrative, revise the paragraph beginning “Above it, a ragged band” to establish magnesium alongside silicon and oxygen. Replace “Within a denser knot” through “A mineral grain moved inside the knot” with overlapping seed formation and coagulation, explicitly acknowledging earlier local seeds. Revise “Other grains formed nearby” to preserve that continuous history. Replace all three paragraphs beginning “Light from the glowing gas,” “A luminous patch,” and “The dust spread” with scattering and partial dimming. Replace the final dark-seam sentence with: *The light had not gone out. The new solids had changed its direction.*
