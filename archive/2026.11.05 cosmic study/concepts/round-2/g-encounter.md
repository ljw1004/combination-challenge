# G — What passes through the second time

Revised concept for [The Lights That Remain](../../narrative/round-3/g-galaxies.md): three encounters across three scenes, followed by a narrated ending. This is an untested short episode, with no duration claim. **What can a collision destroy when nearly every star survives?** The reversal follows some of the dark lane's gas into stars, then asks the player to bring those stars through another encounter.

**Exact action sequence.**

1. **Old stellar stream + Other galaxy's stellar outskirts → Deflected stellar stream.** The opening has already established two bound, gas-rich spirals approaching their first passage. The movable stream contains the orange star and its neighbours; the fixed receiving scene contains the other galaxy's widely separated stars and gravitational field. The stream contributes moving stars; the receiving galaxy bends their paths. The result is the same stars beyond the crossing, still shining, on altered orbits. The reading distinguishes changing a star's journey from breaking the star itself.
2. **Dark-lane cloud + Crossing gas cloud → Young stars.** The view moves inward to the gas overlap during that same passage. The movable cloud contributes the followed material; the fixed crossing cloud contributes opposing gas and relative motion. This operation spans shock heating and compression, radiation carrying heat away, contraction, and eventual sustained fusion over millions of years. The result reading must show that causal sequence: adding gas does not instantly ignite stars, and contraction already produces light before fusion. Only some material becomes stars; radiation and outflows disperse much of the remainder into the surroundings. There is one derived result, the young stellar population, carried forward from this action. No chapter supplies it anew.
3. **Young stars + Returning stellar outskirts → Young stars on changed paths.** After the galaxies separate and return, the player brings the result of action 2 into the later encounter. The fixed scene is the returning configuration of the galaxy already encountered in action 1. Its changing gravitational field redirects the young stars; their compact, widely separated bodies pass among its stars. The result preserves their material identity while changing their trajectories. The interaction neither fuses stars together nor constructs a galaxy by proximity. A playable draft must represent this later receiving context separately from the first-passage context: globally available recipes cannot rely on a later chapter heading to prevent premature execution.

**Vocabulary and continuity.** Movable material is limited to the old stream, the dark cloud and their derived states. Fixed targets are physical regions, visibly distinct from circular material icons. “Stream” means many stars following related paths; “cloud” means extended gas. Shock, contraction and fusion belong to readings. Cooling, gravity and elapsed time are never portable ingredients. The galactic opening supplies orientation, not whole-galaxy inventory tokens. Each closer view identifies its region in that established system; widening again identifies the young lights as descendants of the cloud just handled. There is no extraction gesture or simultaneous stock at two scales.

**Prediction without disclosure.** Before action 3, the novice has seen widely spaced stars survive a crossing and has made compact stars from extended gas. The new scene establishes that those same lights are approaching the returning galaxy. Its goal says “Follow the young stars into the returning galaxy,” without naming their fate. A fresh probe records the expected action, then the predicted consequence and reason before showing the result. Compatibility highlighting can identify a target; it cannot demonstrate this understanding.

**Necessary prose repair.** Replace the diffuse later-passage summary with a definite return crossing for the followed young population. Establish its approach before the action and reveal its changed path afterward. The old orange star's turning path supplies the large-scale orientation. Automatic narration may then carry both populations into this selected rounded remnant; it must not imply every merger ends this way.

**Strongest falsifier.** A novice completes the three gestures but expects the young stars to compress into another cloud or fuse because their material was previously gas. That would undermine the proposed transfer of understanding. Correct prediction alone would still leave enjoyment and the sufficiency of three actions unestablished.
