# Narrative and conceptual review assignments

Follow [reviewer independence](../../docs/fun-and-evaluation.md#reviewer-independence) and [verification retention](../../docs/engineering.md#verification-records). Every revised artifact goes to new participants with no inherited history. They receive the current candidates and the applicable assignment below, without earlier drafts, findings, rankings or an author's preferred choice. Record exact input hashes with concise outcomes.

## Prose, before recipes

Every reviewer is told: **the intended literary approach is a sustained implication of things combining; meetings and transformations should cause consequential changes, rather than merely illustrate scientific facts.** The audience is a curious general reader without astrophysics training. The cosmos itself must sustain interest: accessible scientific wonder, scale and philosophical resonance in the spirit of Carl Sagan, without requiring human protagonists, domestic frames or hand-scale keepsakes. Approximately 1,000 words is a development constraint, not a reading-time or game-length prediction.

**Narrative reader.** Read all current candidates in the supplied order. For each: retell the arc in ordinary language; name the question that pulls the reader forward, what can be lost or gained, the turning point and the ending's payoff. Name the first point where attention weakens or outside knowledge seems necessary. Identify a passage doing real narrative work and a passage that could be cut without loss. Distinguish reactions to the prose from predictions about a game. Compare the candidates, explaining different strengths; rejecting all is permitted.

**Literary and combination editor.** Evaluate drive, coherence, concrete continuity and the intended literary approach. Trace the few consequential encounters and what each participant contributes. Does a transformation change the story, or just rename a technical state? Could a nonexpert anticipate the general kind of change? Identify where the narrator creates interest that the eventual player could not participate in. Suggest different solutions to each central weakness rather than harmonizing all candidates into one formula. Do not derive a long recipe list at this stage.

**Scientific reader.** Check claims necessary to each arc using primary research or official scientific sources. Separate observed facts, established models, disputed reconstructions and speculative futures. Find misleading identity changes, unexplained matter/energy, false inevitability and metaphors likely to teach the wrong thing. Prefer a smaller honest narrative to adding technical steps. Supply exact corrections and source links; factual plausibility does not establish narrative quality.

The organizer integrates these readings, preserving disagreements. Each next round develops two or three different responses to the shared findings. A technically clean draft does not win by default, and a popular metaphor does not excuse a false mechanism.

## Conceptual combinations, after prose comparison

Develop two or three materially different ways the selected narrative could become play. Compare what the player is doing, what persists, what counts as an input or receiving context, the pacing and the final payoff. Keep these conceptual maps distinct from engine-tested recipes.

**Necessity and causal review.** For each proposed operation: why now, what does each input add, what actually changes, what would disappear if the operation were bundled or removed? Classify physical transformations, interactions and interpretive discoveries honestly. Flag process-as-object confusion, arbitrary stations, omitted essential conditions, implausible agency, repeated renamed states and late supplies that do the player's work. Find the smallest convincing version rather than maximizing the count.

**Continuity and scope review.** Follow the same material or body across the whole proposed experience. Check where the point of view, scale or elapsed time changes; what can be revisited; whether separate outputs duplicate a unique body; whether an endpoint follows from prior work. Identify hypotheses needing a playable prototype. This review cannot establish engine reachability or scientific truth without the corresponding checks.

**Forward-understanding probes.** Use the existing two-stage blind method, with one fresh reader per selected scene: ask what they would do next using only the visible context; record that answer before asking what a specified valid combination would do. Do not expose the intended result before either answer. A plausible alternative is evidence about the design. Do not count this as an actual `--fast` or `--detailed` campaign.

Next conceptual candidates must offer different solutions to the findings. Only a concrete playable manuscript can justify runtime checks and playtesting; only an understood interaction model can justify an artwork pilot. Full-story attachment and human understanding remain unmeasured until the corresponding human experience is actually observed.
