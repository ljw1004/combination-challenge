# B — Keep the worlds, shorten the detour

Authoring revision, 6 November 2026. The [active brief](../../../design-brief.md) remains the authority. This comparison responds to the whole-manuscript narrative review and completed local `--fast` chapter reports; it does not claim detailed Fun or human enjoyment.

| Candidate | What the player would do | Provisional size | Main gain and cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. | B. | C. A braid of concurrent physical histories | After grain formation, switch to the actual tail cloud; after the rocky moon, return to actual stellar clearing/outflows; after the giant's encounter, return to the galactic outward stream. Carry the same derived matter between its relevant scenes. | Seven to nine interleaved episodes; roughly 40–50 system recipes plus retained galactic operations. | Keeps both scales active without pretending every disk operation affects the merger. Requires cross-act reordering, new checkpoints and repeated orientation at the switch. |

For C, disk growth belongs to the first millions of years; later rocky impacts overlap the longer outward galactic passage. Each switch would say “during those same early millions of years” or “later in the outward passage,” identify the same star, and name the actual material now followed. A nearby massive star's outflow must occur before that star is gone; the galaxy's later return cannot be used as a convenient clock for early gas-giant growth. At least three additional changes of scale create a real reading and recognition cost. Repeating a sentence about the old orange star without changing the galactic scene would not constitute this candidate.

**Choose A.** The direct evidence supports preserving distinctive finished objects and reducing repeated work. It does not establish that more route options alone would repair the ten-chapter absence of the galactic question. A compact excursion can retain a few useful alternatives from B without keeping B's larger structure. The root author can integrate the shortened block into the galaxy spine without pretending that this local file has reordered other acts.

## Scope of the chosen rewrite

Preserve the actual incoming `young-system`. Preserve the actual `b-rubble-pile` as a required small body for the encounters author; it must not be consumed or turned into a generic reservoir. Preserve required movable exports `rocky-system`, `ringed-giant`, `icy-survivors` and `collision-debris`. The rocky export remains the host-plus-inner-worlds view; the other exports are distinct material selections within that same system.

Keep D's useful shortcuts and physically coherent shared supplies when their scenes survive. A consolidated population can contribute distinct portions; a unique moon or world cannot simultaneously survive and become its own fragment source. No count is protected by assigning a new adjective or by demanding every optional discovery. The final count and required route will be measured after authorship.

## Authored result and checks

The first broken grains can proceed directly into the later body-forming episode or be rebuilt through either sensible grain supply.
