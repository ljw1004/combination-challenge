# Selected artwork references

These three contact sheets are deliberately retained authoring deliverables: a compact visual index for extending the story while preserving material identity, receiver grammar and scene palette. They are assembled from the selected runtime files, not source-atlas crops or earlier candidates.

| Sheet | Coverage and literal image size |
|---|---|
| [Circular appearances](circles-80px.png) | All 145 at 80 × 80 px, including four later appearances. |
| [Fixed receivers](receivers-160px.png) | All 21 at 160 × 168 px, with the accepted transparent label/drop padding. |
| [Backgrounds](backgrounds-195px.png) | All 13 at 195 × 422 px; these are composition thumbnails, half the phone review dimensions. |

View at 100% zoom for the stated sizes. Identity labels sit outside each image. [Sheet metadata](sheets.json) binds each sheet and every displayed file to its hash and image rectangle. The [production manifest](../production/asset-manifest.json) provides full source/crop provenance; the [artwork overview](../README.md) links review outcomes and the separate historical style reference.

Regenerate with `node projects/cosmic-study/artwork/tools/build-review-sheets.mjs --compact` from the repository root. The 25 per-source verification sheets stay under ignored `runs/` and are not retained here. These reference sheets support authorship; they are not browser screenshots or evidence of human play.
