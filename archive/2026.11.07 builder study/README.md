# Builder study — A Fair in the Air

Completed 7 November 2026. The live [story](../../stories/builder/builder.md), runtime artwork and [single active design brief](../../stories/builder/design-brief.md) now live in `stories/builder`. Future story edits belong there.

The original making story follows a child and Aunt Jo from useful garden toys to a tethered flying fair, with one explicitly fictional lift technology. It contains 181 authored combinations, 29 playable chapters and an ending. One legal completion uses 170 combinations; the other eleven support alternate experimental orders. The approximate 200-combination request did not justify keeping repetitive fittings that playtests found unrewarding.

## Retained work

- [Research](research.md): general mechanisms and primary educational sources, with proposed designs distinguished from physical validation.
- Numbered manuscript iterations, from [v1](builder-v1.md) through [v20](builder-v20.md). These are historical inputs, not additional active stories or briefs. Their prototype artwork remains with them; the final illustrated edition is the live manuscript.
- [Evaluation](evaluation.md): ten fast/detailed playtest batches, significant contrary findings, adopted revisions and technical completion checks. The [timestamped playtests](playtests/) retain the requested response arrays and attributed chapter/run reports unchanged. Incomplete assignments remain identifiable and separate from fresh replacements.
- [Artwork sources](art/README.md): original dimensioned Blender assemblies, pose controls, procedural backgrounds, production/export tools and review instructions. Canonical geometry supplies loose parts, construction states and garden rewards, preserving original-size toys when reused.
- [Mechanical outcomes](art/geometry-evaluation.md) and [visual outcomes](art-review/2026.11.07_03.54.44/report.md): concise independent review scopes and limits, including actual receiving and service paths, playing-size continuity and phone arrivals.
- [Playtest and browser tools](tools/README.md): source-isolated CLI orchestration and a technical real-pointer completion witness.

The delivered art comprises 340 item icons, 148 fixed views including a garden-framing variant, five fitted overlays, six page backgrounds, fourteen garden rewards, cover and story icon. There are no copied kit images, layouts, instructions, characters or brand marks. Selected runtime artwork lives only beside the story; editable geometry and render/composition sources are retained here without redundant raster masters.

Animation was explicitly deferred. The source models retain simple geometry and meaningful pose controls for that later phase. Numerical meshes and finite motion samples do not establish material strength, manufacture or safety; AI chapter trials and informed visual reviews do not establish enjoyment or usability for real children. The authored story remains playable despite the linter's existing exhaustive fixed-state analysis limit.
