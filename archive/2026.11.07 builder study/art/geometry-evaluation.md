# Mechanical geometry outcomes

These are concise outcomes of independent exact-source reviews during 6–7 November 2026. The [review instructions](review-instructions.md) record the assignment conditions; [evaluation](../evaluation.md) separates these findings from playtest evidence. Canonical Python geometry and executable checks are retained. Raw proof files and detailed reports are temporary. Acceptance concerns the specified geometry, not manufacture, materials, electrical performance, child safety or enjoyment.

The first dragon review rejected a model whose analytic linkage endpoints were correct: actual pivot bores faced the wrong way, the spring crossed its bracket and retention/service hardware was incomplete. This established the production rule to check actual receiving meshes and stopped assembly paths. Fresh operating/service review subsequently accepted both cam variants at 360 sampled phases each and twelve service states. A later fresh review accepted the removable front guide cover and its clear follower insertion. Subsequent spring-colour and selected-pose changes preserve those dimensions and pose equations.
