# Artwork and geometry review instructions

The active design brief governs the intent. All assignments use the independence rules in [fun and evaluation](../../../docs/fun-and-evaluation.md#reviewer-independence): a new reviewer for each revised candidate, no author role, no earlier verdicts or author proof outputs as evidence. Initial interpretation is separate from informed inspection. The conclusions belong in the evaluation records; raw packets, renders, geometry dumps and answers remain disposable under `runs/`.

For the mechanical assignments, reviewers receive the ordinary chapter text, canonical Python sources and their frozen SHA-256 identities. They must build the actual Blender meshes with factory startup, inspect receiving geometry and personally view several useful detail renders. Analytical endpoints and the model's own check functions cannot be the sole evidence. Check real connections, insertion/service paths and finite operating poses, preserving original scale when a completed toy is reused. Distinguish true solid intersections from intended contact or Boolean/tessellation uncertainty. Report concrete required repairs or scoped acceptance, methods, finite coverage, source stability and untested limits. No animation, material/force certification, production edits or contact with the author is requested. Use two render threads on the shared machine.

The independent assignment scopes were:

- Dragon: cam contact and follower travel, rigid articulated wing links, bearings/pins, spring routing, removable crank/cam retention and stopped service; a later assignment separately checks the removable guide cover and frontal insertion path.
- Grabber: syringe and slider travel, rigid jaw/link geometry, tube routing, actual sphere contact, then a separate fixed-attachment/source-custody pass.
- Wind/optics/drum/tools: developable pinwheel paper, racer axle/saddle and sail retention, two-mirror periscope path/tabs, triangular mirror/cell/diffuser interfaces, rotating drum/slits/seam, and the real cut fish stencil. Revisions receive new focused interface reviews.
- Crane/wave: winch/pawl/spring/support and cord-anchor interfaces; motor, pulley, belt and guard mounting. A final crane review separately checks connected feet, real bores and seated hardware, crank/grip capture and the rope's stud clearance.
- Pinball/rover: finite-radius marble access and slope correction, flipper/plunger motion, retained support/stop hardware, rover wheel/caster/pen mounts and traces, independently fitted overlays. A final repair review focuses on the marble pocket before/after the shim, support capture, stop clamp, connected-wire guard installation and complete trim-tool access.
- Late inventions: compact lift transport, original supports plus supplied extensions, whale skin/rib/fin joints, projector mount/optical reservation, focus and drive service, batteries and lead routes, accessible trim, hatch/stop travel, garden props/tethers and declared mass/force assumptions. Subsequent repair review includes constant-length stored ground-line slack and the physical screen support.

Visual review uses the delivered lossless WebPs at quarter dimensions on their intended background, with an input-hash manifest. Inspect every assigned image, compare loose parts to the next assemblies and later reused toys, and distinguish internal occlusion from a missing component. Check pose changes that carry the learning, colour/edge contrast, actual relative dimensions and complete silhouettes. Garden rewards must preserve the same toy and show credible support and use. Inspect representative browser frames, stating their viewport and whether they show fresh arrival, resizing, an actual drag or merely a screenshot. Do not call informed inspection a blind recognition test or claim human enjoyment.
