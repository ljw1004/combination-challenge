# Builder evaluation outcomes

Development began on 6 November 2026. The [design brief](../../stories/builder/design-brief.md) owns the current intent; the [standing evaluation guide](../../docs/fun-and-evaluation.md) owns the methods and rubrics.

## Experiment design

The initial full-story screens use one fresh player per playable chapter, followed by an independent grader and fresh chapter synthesis. This is an explicitly reduced screening design for identifying revisions, not the normal three-player acceptance sample. Selected later passages use three players. Every revised assignment uses new participants.

Exact player guidance: “This is an original construction story designed for a curious player of about ten. Report your own experience candidly; do not impersonate a child. Consider whether the things you make and the mechanisms you learn are worth the player's time.”

Players: Terra Medium. Independent graders and chapter synthesis: Astra High. The runner freezes the manuscript, shared runtime, actual checkpoint history, rubrics and guidance before dispatch. Reports distinguish fast-mode hook evidence from detailed-mode meaningful-decision counts. Source-unexposed chapter tests do not establish enjoyment by real children or cumulative attachment in one continuous playthrough.

## Development approach

- Research identifies primary sources for the real mechanisms and explicitly separates educational precedent from physical validation of these original designs.
- The isolated CLI transport passed a disposable technical trial through detailed play, closing report, frozen replay, independent grading and synthesis assembly. Those trials supply no creative evidence for this story.
- Broad third-draft detailed and fifth-draft fast screens were followed by focused tests using fresh frozen assignments.
- Following the user's 3D recommendation, dimensioned mechanical prototypes preceded production illustration. The first was a cam dragon with analytic linkage poses and sampled rendered-mesh checks. Production still artwork uses the reviewed geometry and fixed-camera construction states.
- The user deferred animation to a separate phase. This release uses still renders while retaining the models and motion definitions.
