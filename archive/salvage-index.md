## Corpus coverage

The [document inventory](document-inventory.json) accounts for all 1,082 original Markdown files, grouped into 747 byte-distinct contents. Every distinct text was scanned for topics and candidate guidance; 2,223 source-linked passages were extracted. Exact copies share one content record but retain every original path. This is an exhaustive inventory and extraction, not a claim that every line of a million-word research/evidence archive received a new manual factual review.

The current synthesis was reconciled against the specification, accumulated human decisions, latest manuscript notes, research/evaluation ledgers, UI implementation records, story-specific reviews and future-concept proposals. Supporting release snapshots and raw trial accounts remain version-bound evidence. The inventory's dispositions identify reconciled guidance, optional proposals and reference-only evidence; they do not certify old claims as current truths.
