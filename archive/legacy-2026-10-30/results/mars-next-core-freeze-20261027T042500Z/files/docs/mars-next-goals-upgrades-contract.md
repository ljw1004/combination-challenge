# Next Mars contract: useful creations, explicit upgrades, sparse places

27 October 2026. **Isolated implementation and logical audit, not a new player trial or deployment.** Source: `experiments/mars-next/`. The frozen continuous-v3 player host, assignment, source and raw play remain the pre-feedback baseline. No migration work: this checkpoint resets.

## Contract in one page

- Three location roles remain, but no obligation to use all three in every chapter. The compact implementation has three places, not ten: ridge (clear the lander's signal shadow), glacier (actual water source), warm habitat (germination). Water point, canyon parking, relay parking, airlock delivery, garden parking, workshop delivery and repair bench are removed. A pressure patch is made anywhere; a clamping station was not earning its space.
- Opening teaches repair and meaningful contact. Later instructions name broader purposes once: a living foothold, then a base capable of exploration. Their checkable functions are visible together; there is no new instruction modal per watering, germinating, lighting or installing step. This changes progression, not the finger/drag/scroll grammar. Continuous scrolling catalogue and all initial supplies upfront remain integration requirements; this logical module does not pretend to test scrolling.
- Creation/function goals check present functional equipment; achieved history is recorded separately. Enhanced growing shelters and radio-equipped Rover satisfy their actual functions. Prior contact is a historical event; no perpetual obligation to park the same radio at home while wanting it in the expedition.
- Radio → Solar radio → Long-range radio consumes each explicit predecessor. Propagator → Greenhouse and heated equivalents do likewise. Watered/sprouted tray stages and Workbench → Lit workbench are clear evolutions too. No blanket deletion of uninteresting things: cable, tools and ordinary ingredients remain unless an authored physical incorporation/evolution applies.
- A physical module incorporated into an output moves inside when it has no further authored use. Charger, lamp, solar panel/battery, canister, clear cover, wheels and dish have specific assembly reasons. A charger stays outside while an unmade power device **or even a not-yet-created Rover's charger slot** still needs it. Future slot checks prevent order-dependent softlocks. Filtering does not consume the filter. Heating glacier ice does not consume the heater. A heater attached to a heated propagator really is a physical module, but stays reusable while another use remains.
- Rover's radio interface accepts Radio, Solar radio and Long-range radio. Enhanced modules preserve working/powered/long-range communication. A solar-radio's small internal supply does **not** fill the traction charger slot: it is wired to the radio, not the drive bus. This physical distinction is stated, not enforced by an unexplained old-kind name barrier. A better compatible radio can replace an installed weaker module. A Rover never inherits unrelated capabilities just from containing parts.
- No duplicate reconstruction of an incorporated/evolved kind; retained outside representatives follow the explicit reuse exception. Chassis construction includes its wheels and cannot count its own new wheel slot as a reason to leave Wheels behind.

## A catalyst worth its place

At the glacier, Heater + Empty canister produces a **Meltwater canister**, catching water directly where the ice is melted. Filter that canister to obtain a Water canister. Without the glacier, heater plus canister has no water source. The ordinary Heater + Glacier → Meltwater → Filtered water → Water canister route stays legal. The catalyst is a real two-step alternative to a three-step chain, not a compulsory detour or an exact-name lock.

Closed water storage protects usable water from dust and evaporation; it is not required merely because the game calls other water portable. The filter remains outside after either route. Actual target placement establishes context; an `at` assertion cannot invent it. No hidden location accumulation.

Discovery UI integration must consider **both directional roles** when explaining compatibility: a placed canister can be the target for heater even when dragging canister onto heater would lose the glacier setting. Do not highlight an illegal reversed drop as legal, show result previews, or change the target-context rule to hide this distinction. The isolated `partners` API currently reports legal source gestures only; bidirectional contextual guidance remains an explicit UI integration task.

## This is a focused implementation step, not the next tiny isolated trial

The compact module proves upgrade, capability, goal and location semantics against 18 initial supplies and 26 recipes. It intentionally does not port the whole 126-kind, 26-objective catalogue or launch another trial. The **next full experience remains working home → remote observation on the same retained catalogue**. Its scope is not 200 actions; neither every recipe nor every old endpoint is compulsory.

Suggested full sequence: **Be found → Establish a living base → Prepare an expedition with a usable route → Make and return useful remote observations.** This is not four labels pasted over all 26 old tasks: equipment-delivery obligations above are actually removed; optional inventions are not compulsory for chapter completion. The exact chosen observation requirements and lawful simple routes must be authored and executed end-to-end next. Full-world supplies are declared upfront; no automatic remote-mission item grants. Only justified places unfold. No new scenario reset, hidden shelf or deletion sweep.
