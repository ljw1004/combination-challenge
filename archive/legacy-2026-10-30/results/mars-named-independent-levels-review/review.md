## Semantic and experiential judgment

The split removes the previously quantified 70/102-thing persistent-board problem without inventing conservation chores. A shared ontology can legitimately give the same named invention different purposes on independent boards. Declared intact equipment is more honest than pretending every scenario has inherited whichever earlier branch the player chose.

The garden has a real contextual dependency (warm watering, harvest at the actual planted shelter, then cooking); ice uses a camera-equipped contained setup and telescopic camera for distinct photographic scales. Neither claims chemistry, route safety, or biological identification from a photograph. Growth/harvest/cooking remain conventional episode abstractions rather than timers or real survival models.

However, most routes remain short, linear preparation/delivery sequences. Hand cart versus rover and ordinary versus elaborate relay are currently largely optional construction preferences, not proved strategic tradeoffs. Repeated solar radio is plausible practice with a new relay purpose but might still feel repetitive; the changed objective name alone does not establish mastery. A more complex path does not automatically improve fun. The simple route must stay legitimate. Comfortable clear progress is a reasonable baseline to test, not something this review rejects.

The 87-recipe shared sheet exceeds the actually traversed subgraph. Recipe count, saved knowledge, alternate paths and scenario titles supply no extra played duration. Accepted but unattainable optional objects (for example heated propagator without a heater supplied in shelter) should not be advertised as available level solutions merely because they occur in the broad acceptance list; this does not invalidate the witnessed complete routes. There is no evidence here for 200 worthwhile decisions.

No blocking reconstruction defect found. Remaining gates are root-owned immutable publication, then explicitly authorized whole-collection evaluation; no UI or Site changes were made by this review.
