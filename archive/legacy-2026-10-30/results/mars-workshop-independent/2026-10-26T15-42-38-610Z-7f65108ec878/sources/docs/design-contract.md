# Combination is the player's action

This contract records the user's corrections after the first flood prototype. It is a current design constraint, rather than a conclusion inferred from automated scores.

## Current direction: shared ontology, different level goals

The owner's shared-ontology clarification accepts ontology-heavy levels: a large tree of combinations can be reused across levels, each with a different explicit goal. A finite goal-directed level can end successfully without generating another purpose of its own. The broader experience may span multiple levels; neither self-generated continuation nor 180–240 opportunities is a universal per-level requirement. Keep the shared combinations inspectable, established concepts inexhaustible, and the player performing the combines. A large ontology is neither automatically fun nor invalid merely because exhaustive construction can eventually solve it; test the relevant construction work and its value.

## Clear progress is allowed — 26 October 2026, 05:40 UTC correction

The owner explicitly allows comfort in making progress by doing clear things. Not every decision should be meaningful; in the opening, part of the meaning can come from learning the mechanics. Straightforward actions, truthful previews and obvious next steps are not automatically weaknesses. Familiar enactment can also remain welcome without measurable new learning, a changed role or a later payoff on every combine.

Judge the proposed stage and experience, not strategic dilemma density alone. Keep clear progress, mechanics learning, reported comfort, pacing and later purpose distinct. A short opening can orient; a later goal may give familiar actions another use, but neither that transition nor constant novelty is an obligation on every action. Use a few relevant observations and a short retrospective, not a grading burden during play.

This is a correction to current interpretation, not a positive finding about the completed Mars plays. Their four-versus-two combines and agent reports of routine execution remain intact. The [dated postplay addendum](mars-objects-opening-interpretation-2026-10-26.md) preserves that distinction; [doctrine 9](what-is-fun.md) applies prospectively. No frozen protocol, capture, model or evaluator is changed.

## Any narrative belongs in the primitives

The owner clarified after trying the written Bell Orchard and Mneme prototypes: “I don't really want much text narrative at all” and “Any narrative we find must be emergent in the primitives of the game.” Narrative sweep, beauty, philosophical concepts and curiosity remain motivations. The desired vehicle is the game's building blocks: terrain, an avatar, a foe indicator, a changing block or another visible process. Authored dialogue and successive prose scenes are not the current direction. Preserve those experiments as history rather than treating their agent appreciation as evidence of owner fit.

The owner specifically finds combining onto terrain promising. A place can participate in the operation, including as a catalyst in **A + B at P**. Its properties may enable a combination or change its effect; the place persists. This is a hypothesis to test, not a mandatory ternary grammar for every level. Where place or time is used to produce history, it should contribute actual causal rules, with earlier constructions changing later possibilities. Time may be a process or context; it need not become an action allowance. Shared-ontology levels do not require a persistent simulation or a narrative arc.

Extend the praised discoverability to places. Holding or hovering an item can expose compatible places and results; inspecting a place can expose what its current conditions enable. All eligibility rules remain public. The intended challenge is constructing and acting within those conditions, rather than guessing arbitrary location recipes.

The next logical tests should expose the concepts, relations and functional transitions that make a level's goal worth working toward. Where history is claimed, the same primitives must enact it. Compact goals, labels and explanations of actual state are useful. A dramatic paragraph cannot substitute for a world event or a persistent actor's changed behavior. UI polish remains deferred while the game laws are investigated.

## Three givens; disposable implementations

The project keeps three givens from AGENTS.md13–17: combination, fun through interactive play, and an empirical loop testing that fun. A flood, a factory, turn pacing, an ontology, historical setting or research metric earns its place by helping those givens. The original prototype's implementation effort does not justify retaining it. The user's subsequent explicit constraints below further narrow acceptable implementations.

## Gods, not grocers

The user categorically rejects finite physical material supplies, stockpiling and inventory management. Discovered things are inexhaustible capabilities: once established, they can be reused directly. There are no material quotas or depletion costs to reintroduce under another name. World things can be transformed by actual combinations, while access to the concepts remains.

The desired experience is creating a universe from its elements. Creating a capability should expand the ways the player can shape what happens. Challenge may arise from consequences, relationships, placement, sequence or execution. Managing supplies must not become a surrogate for that challenge.

The first finite-supply joining experiment violated this preference. Its resource-dependent planning results are rejected evidence for the requested game, and are archived separately. Unlimited joining can still be inspected as a spatial hypothesis, but an abstract shape puzzle has not established the desired creation experience. It remains a staged experiment.

## The player performs the combination

Ingredients are the primary visible objects and the primary input. Pick them up, bring them together, inspect a reaction and commit the join. The result appears where the join happens, remains manipulable and can enter the next combination. A finished-output card followed by an instruction to manufacture it does not satisfy this requirement, even when it executes the correct recipe internally.

The first handling experiment used a free tabletop and a shelf. The latest candidate unifies them: one movable node for each concept, with both parents remaining and a child appearing beside the contact. No material is consumed or counted. Selecting, dragging or touching actual things replaces the recipe-selection/deployment dashboard. Undo restores arrangement without deleting discoveries.

The shared canvas follows a user proposal that remains open to experiment. It is not a reason to reject every future level with a different physical representation. Pan, zoom and offscreen rendering support a growing board. A large reusable ontology is welcome to test; the number of nouns alone does not establish worthwhile play.

## Legality is information, not the source of challenge

Hovering or holding a thing reveals compatible partners and the resulting possibilities. At contact the specific combination is explicit before release. The user reports this removes the frustrating search for an author's intended ontology. Preserve that discoverability.

**Rejection test: a challenge must remain worthwhile when every legal combination and its result can be inspected.** If explaining the recipes dissolves the challenge, the design is relying on the low-level search this project intends to remove. The worthwhile work can concern choosing which concepts and multi-step construction realize a goal; choosing when, where, in which order or under which conditions to combine; predicting and adapting to the consequences; or executing a manipulation skill.

This does not imply showing a giant recipe catalogue or putting output instructions ahead of ingredients. The current hover behavior exposes possibilities through the object already under the player's attention. Its presentation can be clarified without suppressing the information.

## A human correction to the grader

The first fresh browser agent found pervasive result previews too directive. The user independently praised that same cross-icon hover revelation as a major advance in discoverability, while finding the cross-icon placement slightly confusing. These are different reports, not a consensus to average.

A removal of per-partner result names was queued from the agent report, then cancelled before implementation in response to the user. The original affordance is retained. This is a concrete error signal for the evaluator: agency/curiosity proxies must not automatically reject information that a player finds enabling. Keep the authored-ontology problem distinct from tactical uncertainty.

The user feedback is real human design-review evidence about this feature. It is not a recruited multi-person playtest, proof that the whole game is fun, or evidence that any particular challenge works. Earlier reports stating human participants0 describe the earlier experiment period; they must not erase this subsequent feedback.

## What we test next

The immediate proposed evaluation question is whether clear early combines provide a first-time player with welcome progress or orientation, or feel like unwanted instructions to copy. No new play or implementation is started by this correction. The broader logical direction remains a representative ontology slice with different explicit goals; finite endings, learning, comfortable enactment and optional appetite should be assessed on their own terms. Existing play protocols retain their original conditions; the new doctrine does not relabel their outcomes. On-screen discoverability, truthful highlights, handling and immediate reuse remain later interface obligations. Browser-only agents can reveal affordance errors; mechanical API success cannot validate those obligations. When interface testing resumes, record picked ingredients, contact preview, committed pair, result location and the next manipulation.

Then compare distinct challenges around that same interaction. Candidate families include arrangement/joining, process/order/conditions, and rule/idea composition. Explain each local relation and expose legal moves. Several plans may be possible, but a worthwhile unique-solution insight is also legitimate. A claimed paradigm change should supply a new inference, rather than hide a new vocabulary's recipe passwords. [Research across nine games](combination-systems.md) and [transformations/ideas](transformations-and-ideas.md) supply experiments, not mandatory mechanics.

Reject a claimed challenge if it vanishes with full recipe information; that is not by itself a rejection of an opening's orientation or comfortable progress. Reject output delegation in place of the player's combining, and investigate added handling experienced as unwanted work. Every individual combine need not be a strategic or separately meaningful decision. Human pleasure in the combining gesture, comfort and enjoyment of the surrounding challenge remain empirical questions.
