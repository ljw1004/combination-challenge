# First-pass narrative review

Reviewed early drafts at approximately 19:11–19:13 UTC, 2026-10-27. These are provisional findings, sent to authors for revision. Counts are not release or enjoyment claims.

## Mars

The opening arc has a persuasive causal spine: contact, a refuge, the overdue crawler, route observations, worsening visibility, restored relay, rescue. Bringing early sample records home later is a real payoff rather than a disposable tutorial.

The 223-recipe draft repeatedly names every attachment as a new object: a weather mast becomes several named stations; a relay becomes wired, directional, powered, tuned and scheduled; a flight cabin becomes many adjective variants. These are prime uses of the existing component-with-slots mechanic, not evidence of distinct discoveries. Installations may still count as successful combinations, but the player should see one persisting machine.

The final acceptance/clearance chain is administrative padding. Readiness should follow tangible working systems, not a ladder of documents whose sole purpose is unlocking the next document. Crew should not become a succession of interchangeable status objects (rested, accounted, ready). A rescue outcome can be represented narratively while a real manifest remains a document if needed.

Several invitations contain defensive corrections to movie misconceptions. Move caveats into research notes; characters should describe the observed problem and desired result. The optical alternative must satisfy the actual contact objective, not merely produce an optional unused beacon.

## Elements

A shared river gathering gives the 25-stage draft a sustained social purpose. Preservation, food for crossing and winter stores revisit needs with different consequences. The draft dependency closure contains 169 of 172 unique outputs, so nearly all content is required, not simply optional catalogue expansion. This number says nothing about fun and is not a shortest-route proof.

Repeated generic onward travel conflicts with cellar/headland returns. Every transition should name its destination and purpose. A River atlas made only from a star chart and weather book lacks geographic route evidence. Earlier landmarks or route mapping should feed it.

Several first-draft recipes are unrecognizable or ambiguous under the two-item contract: a stone and wooden gear becoming a hammer; fibre and a chest becoming a brush; repeated cloth+rope for both sail and curtain; wood+iron for several unrelated tools. Merge tool aliases or find genuinely different material operations instead of adding arbitrary inputs.

## Food

Recognizable transformations from bread and cultures through pastry and sponge are promising. The industrial endpoint is clearly framed as an approximation, not an inevitable improvement. The current draft requires returning to an old hearth/oven for later kitchen stages. Either make a coherent visit with transport or introduce distinct local ovens; do not silently relocate the old equipment.

## Theology

The named Latin-Christian teaching-fiction frame is responsible, and the guest's welcome creates a concrete reason for a subsequent question. The final arc nonetheless risks a dozen document-adjective upgrades. An interpretive task should have a recognizable consequence rather than simply produce another codex label. Authenticity requires separate source/expert review; combining an objection and reply must not mechanically establish the reply's truth.

Both meal and washstand are required at the same Guesthouse. Verify that the common engine permits these two independently situated objects simultaneously, without cumulative combination or exclusive-occupant deadlock.

## Ideas

Outline received, full draft pending: a traveling archive of case studies is better than a universal civilizational tech ladder. The principal narrative risk is 24 disconnected textbook worksheets. Earlier methods should return to solve later questions; archive packets should contain evidence and tools, not ready-made conclusions. Later computation/AI/institutions must not imply historical inevitability.

## Additional mini-scenarios

- Restoration has the clearest distinct promise: preserve one flood-damaged map's identity and provenance rather than escalating its power. It will fail this promise if every measured/documented/supported form is a second map on the board. Prefer genuine upgrades and a meaningful revealed story.
- Shoreline has a useful problem–intervention–observation arc. A supplied seasonal photograph followed by a report is not evidence that the game models uncertain intervention outcomes. Test the short narrative honestly, without claiming adaptive ecological play.
- Literature has evocative setting but currently models applying named techniques as document upgrades. The created poem needs an actual readable creative payoff; the output name alone does not convey literary discovery.
- Chinese Thought responsibly preserves disagreement between named perspectives. Brush plus notes should not itself create an interpretation. Substantively different evidence or ideas should be doing the conceptual work; bindings can then be tangible packaging.
- Causation turns four distinct questions into four labeled exhibits. This is an explicit homework risk. A concrete misunderstanding resolved by choosing the relevant explanation could be more interesting than completing labels, but would require a carefully scoped future mechanic experiment rather than silently expanding the current schema.

Provisional comparison priority: Food and Restoration, with Shoreline as a contrast. The other minis are hypotheses, not seven validated campaigns deserving expansion.
