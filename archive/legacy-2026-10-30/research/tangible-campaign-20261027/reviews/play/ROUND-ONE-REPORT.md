That stronger direct human feedback is not overturned by a text-only player's preference about routine steps. The full campaigns and new minis below are different content, not the exact human-played versions.

## Abstraction corrections

The logical player initially had separate raw combine/place commands where the human browser interprets one drop as either a location recipe or placement. Enginev4 adds shared dropAction/CLI drop, preserving actual resolved actions and captures. Empty slot IDs no longer leak hidden component names. Enginev5 adds the browser-equivalent final/next action label, current-only completion narrative and locally scoped empty-hint language. None of these adds new game mechanics or alters old trials.

## Preserve comfort and empirical limits

An understandable familiar chain may be pleasurable, especially with the physical browser interface. Do not rewrite every simple action into a dilemma. Prioritize concrete mismatches, arbitrary rejected expectations, missing examples and repetitive scene shape. Narrated outcomes are not simulated physics. Full 200–300 combination target remains a design goal, not a metric satisfied by a large catalogue or by summing short assigned pilots.
