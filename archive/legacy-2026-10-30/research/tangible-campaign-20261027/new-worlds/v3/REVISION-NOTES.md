Food adds fruit + yogurt = fruit yogurt and bread + butter = bread and butter. They are familiar optional discoveries, not new mandatory objectives. Both operand directions pass. A route making both still completes the whole game in 23 creations versus the unchanged required route of 21. Food now has 25 authored recipes.

Other minis use the engine’s current-scene dependency packing and can recover already-existing needed items from earlier areas through narrated packing.
