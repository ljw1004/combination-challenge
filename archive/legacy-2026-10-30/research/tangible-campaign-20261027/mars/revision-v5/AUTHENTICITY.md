# v5 source-aware bounds

These are primary-source grounds for broad ideas, not certification of a fictional crewed expedition or each two-item recipe.

- **Controlled thermal comparison:** [NASA's thermal-testing knowledge base](https://s3vi.ndc.nasa.gov/ssri-kb/topics/59/) describes thermal testing of spacecraft/subsystems. A chamber makes comparing bare and insulated samples more controlled than a changing outdoor environment. The fictional trace comparison and screen design compress measurements, modelling and engineering judgment. They do not prove the full cabin meets every thermal requirement.
- **The image is not the route:** [JPL's rover-navigation account](https://robotics.jpl.nasa.gov/what-we-do/flight-projects/mars-science-laboratory/surface-system-software-and-rover-navigation/) describes terrain assessment, route selection and wheel-related hazards. The fictional hand-held field probe and its inference about a shallow channel are simplified crewed-mission equipment, not a sourced current rover procedure or a guarantee of safe bearing capacity.
- **Ready weather suite:** [NASA MEDA overview](https://science.nasa.gov/resource/meda-is-ready-weather-or-not/) grounds integrated engineered sensors. The game does not claim MEDA is assembled by fitting a vane and logger. Its exact station is fictional.
- **Oxygen:** the existing [NASA MOXIE source](https://www.nasa.gov/space-technology-mission-directorate/tdm/mars-oxygen-in-situ-resource-utilization-experiment-moxie/) grounds engineered atmospheric oxygen production. Scaling to a depot, storage and flight servicing is fictional future infrastructure. The player reconnects qualified modules; there are no reaction settings, pressure instructions or DIY handling procedures.
- **Crop/water:** the new process-water wording deliberately removes the old implication that a conductivity reading established drinking-water safety. This is a recovered enclosed growing system with brought substrate and compressed elapsed growth. It is not a guide to growing food in untreated regolith or deciding whether unknown water is potable.
- **Scout:** the existing [JPL Ingenuity source](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/missions/ingenuity/) supports purpose-built Mars rotorcraft, not generic household drone assembly. The scout's controller, camera and legs are already engineered and fitted.

Remaining technical abstractions needing review: mission stores are exceptionally convenient; fitting a thermal screen requires more engineering than the simplified graph; the shallow-channel bearing inference is understated but still compressed; whole water-system collection for adjacent camp work is a spatial abstraction; qualified life-support/flight checks cannot realistically be reduced to one record output. These are reasons to constrain claims and improve the story, not fill the graph with fake certification objects.
