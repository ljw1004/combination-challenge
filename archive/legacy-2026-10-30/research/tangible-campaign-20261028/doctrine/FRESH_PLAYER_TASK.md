# Fresh-player task

Play the prototype using only its player interface. Do not open scenario JSON, implementation, authored routes, research notes or tests.

Work in `/workspace/shared/combination-campaign-research-20261028/doctrine`. Choose a new session name and begin with:

```
node play.mjs YOUR_SESSION look
```

Available commands:
- `read ITEM_ID` — read a manuscript on the current desk.
- `partners ITEM_ID` — show the objects that highlight as potential partners; no result preview.
- `combine FIRST_ID SECOND_ID` — make an attempt.
- `undo` — undo an action.
- `look` — see the desk and current task.
- `stop YOUR_REASON` — preserve why you stopped.

Before an attempted combination, briefly state what you expect it to mean. You may read or use partner highlighting whenever you wish. Play for as long as you want, up to the end of the third desk; stopping earlier is entirely valid. There is no required number of combinations, successes or completed desks. Do not request recipe solutions to turn a stalled session into a successful one.

Report what felt understandable, surprising, compelling, arbitrary or tiring, what you thought the story was saying, and why you continued or stopped. Preserve all unsuccessful attempts. Identify the session as simulated play, not human evidence.
