28 October 2026. Reviewed the complete 20-scene candidate, generator, dependency plan, canonical/exhaustive route evidence, critical review and pastry continuation evidence. This is **not a fresh-player trial**. Candidate SHA-256: `6ab0bd320f9d286c3ce44fe07706a5d740cf15abb036f6408032253c98db1ba4`.

**Judgment:** genuine connected cooking, not a bag-of-ingredients or recipe-count trick. No major culinary impossibility or historical falsehood was established in the transformations checked. The long journey nevertheless has not earned promotion from its positive two-scene pastry trial. The strongest concrete criticism is an unfulfilled preservation payoff; the biggest unresolved experience risk is repeated mandatory finishing work.

## Ranked concerns and bounded responses

### 1. The preserving works makes provisions, then removes every preserved provision

**Confirmed authored behavior; its narrative significance is a review judgment, not a runtime defect.** Scene 18, `cannery` (`author.py:218–227`), promises food for travel beyond the harvest. Both required branches immediately undo the useful sealed state:

- `syrup-apricots → filled-fruit-jar → processed-fruit-jar → bottled-fruit → fruit-custard`;
- `filled-stew-tin → processed-stew-tin → opened-stew → canned-lunch`.

Independent replay confirms both processed containers exist briefly and neither remains at chapter completion. The two resulting meals are for immediate serving. The voice explicitly calls these demonstration tins, so this is **not a factual contradiction or food-safety error**. It is a weak payoff: the only new purpose of this stop—keeping provisions for the journey—is accomplished offstage while the player twice packs, processes and reopens food.

**Bounded fix:** let one branch remain the tasting demonstration and make the other finish as genuinely still-sealed travelling provisions. A packed lunch could visibly contain its tin and bread if authored as an assembly. Do not merely retain a ghost tin, add a preservation checklist, or introduce household canning instructions. This should replace, not lengthen, the redundant opening chain.

### 2. The mandatory dessert work risks becoming completion labor rather than discovery

**Hypothesis supported by the graph; not measured whole-journey fatigue.** Chapters 11–14 require 42 combinations in succession. Their distinct techniques are real, but many final requests converge on filling/topping already familiar forms: `cream-tart → fruit-cream-tart`, `filled-choux → caramel-choux`, `choux-buns → cream-puffs`, `jam-roll → cream-jam-roll`, `trifle-base → fruit-trifle → finished-trifle`, then `ganache-cake` and `profiteroles` (`author.py:136–181`). Later, `precision` requires five more branch-specific combinations ending at `vanilla-sandwich` and `iced-chocolate-cake` before the seven-step final cake line (`author.py:228–245`). Those display cakes do not contribute to the Twinkie.

The precision scene's scaling motivation is especially vulnerable: sifting and fine sugar are meaningful, but most of the scene still asks for another sandwich cake and another coating. Calling it “precision” does not itself give that repeated work a new consequence. This is not a demand for factory simulation or exact recipes.

**Bounded next step:** a fresh `custard → chocolate` trial, plus the already prepared final-bakery trial, should ask which discoveries changed the player's expectations and whether they want to continue. If a finishing branch is dead weight, make that branch optional or replace it with a genuinely different food problem; do not compensate by adding steps to restore 200. Keep the structurally different custard/choux/foam mechanisms unless actual evidence argues otherwise.

### 3. The optional historical side branch costs three objects but says very little

**Confirmed content shape; distraction is a hypothesis.** Scene 5 supplies `menu-card`, `humoral-book`, and `modern-note`, then offers `humoral-menu` and `annotated-menu`. Neither resulting text names a food, a particular historical judgment, or an attributed passage: one says the cook uses hot/cold and wet/dry categories, the next says the explanation is discarded. These are two optional catalogue entries, **not padding in the 185-step goal route**. Still, three of the scene's eleven arrival items support a generic paperwork chain rather than the salad work.

**Bounded fix:** one intentionally readable, attributed historical menu/example would carry more meaning with less clutter than two combinations that mostly rename paper. Keep it optional and do not make history a required ingredient or assert medical efficacy. The existing sugar-trade context likewise belongs in a concise cook's line plus optional attributed reading, not additional crafting chores.

### 4. Keep contextual citations distinct from transformation evidence

**Confirmed metadata limitation, not demonstrated recipe misinformation.** `author.py` initially assigns a chapter source to every recipe. Thus `herb-butter`, `herb-toast`, and `compote` all cite only the Aspall vinegar-production page; `flour` and `butter` cite the fermentation overview; `line-batter` cites Hostess's product-identity page. The source notes and reconstruction caveats already limit these claims, but these links do not substantiate those particular methods.

**Bounded fix:** in source metadata/documentation, label these as chapter context rather than recipe-method evidence, or omit an irrelevant per-recipe citation. Keep technical evidence attached to the transformations it actually supports. No need to research every ordinary combination or reproduce complete cookbook formulas. Do not summarize this audit as “all 197 recipes independently verified.”

## Preserve

- **Actual continuity:** all 19 later chapters use earlier creations, often several. Named stocks such as cooked beans, cacao nibs, starch and shortening are concrete ingredients, not generic “recipe bags.” Independent inspection found no authored output supplied by a cache. Requiring the player to manufacture every prepared ingredient would make the abstraction worse.
- **Meaningful contrasts:** cold crumbs versus hot roux; pouring custard versus starch-thickened cream; folded dough versus cooked choux paste; whole-egg sponge versus egg-white meringue; the final injector's real fitted contents. Don't turn every compressed heating step into another oven placement.
- **Older practices stay useful:** bread becomes soup, toast, crumbs and pudding; roots and dairy become pasta filling; previously made foam and creme actually make the final cake. The endpoint does not erase their value.
- **Historical restraint:** the premise is an explicitly fictional collection, not a universal invention timeline. The Twinkie is an inspired endpoint, not a recovered proprietary formula. The preservation descriptions avoid giving unsafe procedures.
- **Automatic handoff:** the pastry trial really missed optional cheese straws during ordinary forward play, but it did not establish permanent loss. Independent source-aware replay returned from supper carrying the existing cheese and made both optional recipes successfully. This is a discoverability/backtracking tradeoff, not a reason for a Continue button or compulsory optional goals. Browser discoverability remains untested here.
