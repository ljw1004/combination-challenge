# Sources, reconstruction and historical limits

Read 28 October 2026. Sources support named processes or particular historical claims. **The sequence of these twenty kitchens is an authored caravan story, not a documented chain of invention.** A practice appearing later in the game is not necessarily historically later. Similarity and material reuse are not evidence of transmission. All recipe definitions include a reconstruction caveat.

## Historical anchors

- [OpenStax, *What Our Ancestors Knew*](https://openstax.org/books/microbiology/pages/1-1-what-our-ancestors-knew): longstanding use of fermentation before microorganisms were understood. The game does not assign a single origin to bread or cultured dairy. Grain, flour, hearth cooking and cultured milk are practical opening techniques, not a dated reconstruction.
- [John Evelyn, *Acetaria* (1699)](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/15517/15517-h/15517-h.htm): an original salad text that joins culinary practice and period bodily categories. Its oil/vinegar dressings support the comparison; the apple-vinegar and roast-root recipes are not claimed to be Evelyn's recipes.
- [National Library of Medicine, *Shakespeare and the Four Humors*](https://www.nlm.nih.gov/exhibition/shakespeare-and-the-four-humors/index.html): historical context and reproduced primary images. Humoral qualities are explicitly discarded beliefs. The two optional paper objects are a historical menu and its annotation, never ingredients, cures, diet advice, or required progress.
- [UK Parliament, *Trade Routes and the Slave Trade*](https://www.parliament.uk/about/living-heritage/transformingsociety/tradeindustry/slavetrade/overview/trade-routes-and-the-slave-trade/): sugar plantation economies and enslaved African labour. The caravan historian retains this context beside the sweets, without turning exploitation into a successful crafting reward. This source supports the social context, not any preserve recipe.
- [USDA National Agricultural Library, *How Did We Can?*](https://www.nal.usda.gov/exhibits/ipd/canning/): the connection between heat processing and sealed containers and canning's relatively recent history. No disputed award date is necessary to the game. The commercial specialists and their separate fruit/stew lines are authored abstractions, not Appert's apparatus.
- [Hostess, *Twinkies*](https://www.hostesscakes.com/everyday-snacks/twinkies): first-party present-day identity—golden cake, sweet filling, individual packaging. The current product has a much more complex ingredient system than the game. No label formulation, shelf life, nutritive evaluation, endorsement, logo or trade dress is reproduced. “Twinkie” names the endpoint; its inspection text says that it is an inspired model.
- [Animas Museum, *1930s Foods*](https://www.animasmuseum.org/online_exhibits/PetersonHouse/foods.html): museum account of a banana-filled 1930 origin and later vanilla. This supports the optional banana comparison only. No proprietary original recipe or exact wartime causal reconstruction is claimed.

## Safety and representational boundaries

[NCHFP, *Ensuring Safe Canned Foods*](https://nchfp.uga.edu/how/can/general-information/ensuring-safe-canned-foods/) explains why food type and a tested process matter. A container or apparent seal alone is not sufficient. The game's fruit and bean-stew processing occurs at distinct professionally controlled commercial lines. There are no recipes, times, temperatures, acidity numbers, pressure schedules, shelf-life rewards or instructions for adapting this to home canning. Its successful combination is not evidence that real food is safe.

The game also omits raw-bean preparation, raw dairy handling, cream storage, hot-sugar temperatures, allergy guidance and exact egg handling. “Cooked beans” and processed ingredients are justified local supplies. Cooking, ingredient proportions, sanitation and elapsed time are compressed, not solved by a two-token action. No health benefit is inferred from fermentation, yogurt or the older methods.

The kitchen caravan is explicitly fictional. It connects recognisable work through material reuse, changing customers and travel needs. It does not claim the same real food could safely sit through this entire journey. Retained stocks are the existing discovery-game abstraction of portions, not a food-storage simulation.
