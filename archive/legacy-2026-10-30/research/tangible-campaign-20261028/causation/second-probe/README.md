## Claim boundary

Basket-first is shorter; these are not perfectly balanced strategic choices. The proposed advance is **more than one fitting means and sometimes no need to upgrade**, not difficulty for its own sake. Normal thoughtful crafting also exhibits end-directed reasoning. A pleasant route is not proof that this has captured a distinctively Aristotelian game.

No fresh-player, human, physical-browser, or enjoyment evidence has yet been collected for this second probe. Author witnesses and runner smoke tests must remain labelled as such.
