# Atlas v3: observations with consequences

**The later chapters now supply the observations and results they previously only named.** The lunar route no longer produces longitude without local time. The future observatory chooses an identifiable experiment, the memory garden creates a new shared episode, and the living city changes its actual resource allocation when its biology changes.

This is a bounded six-chapter research revision, not a claim that the whole campaign is now compelling. It changes `anatomy`, `circulation`, `longitude`, `future-observatory`, `future-memory`, and `future-city`. All **23 other chapters—including the first three—retain their authored items, recipes, narratives and objectives unchanged**. The chronology remains intact; the three futures remain explicitly undated fiction.

The 29-chapter candidate has **262 recipe definitions / 258–260 combinations per full route**. The extra navigation operations do actual time reduction, interpolation and conversion; they are not new qualification certificates. Navigation’s two evolving worksheets keep its logical local-token peak at **17**, rather than retaining every intermediate sheet as a loose item. Peak across the full route remains 21. These counts do not establish native browser layout quality.

## Why this revision exists

The independent author-aware audit found that all 53 results in its six-chapter sample lacked readings and most result labels omitted the actual claim or calculation. The browser and fresh-play runner do not expose descriptions. The author therefore “knew” a discrepancy or range that the player could not inspect.

More seriously, two accepted recipes produced `lunar-longitude` without using the ship’s current local-time observation. A later join with the clock branch did not repair that false intermediate result. The audit and its reproduced route remain at `../../atlas-independent-review/`.

The earlier negative opening trial and its qualified positive retest are also preserved. The retest finished the assigned three chapters, understood their central ideas, and was mildly curious onward, but described some statement-materialization as routine and some transferred literal labels as awkward. **Both trials are simulated logical-play evidence, not human or native-UI evidence.** This revision does not rewrite their conclusions or treat fewer rejected drops as proof of enjoyment.

## The six changed cases

### Anatomy — the correction can be named

The supplied fictional specimen is an **adult human mandible / lower jaw**, with continuous bone across its united midline. The older two-separate-bones claim is visible alongside it. The plate labels the continuous adult midline, and the final edition keeps both old wording and observed correction in separate entries.

The earlier C/D manuscript repair contributes a particular editorial practice: preserve the displaced reading while making the supported correction. It does not certify the new book by prestige. The specimen is not the upper jaw, a child’s developmental anatomy, or evidence that every inherited anatomical statement is false. The historical comparison is supported by Gysel’s research on the earlier *Tabulae* and the *Fabrica*. [Gysel abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8209579/)

### Circulation — the rival account now has a testable supply limit

The invented dossier gives **5 mL × 60 beats/minute × 30 minutes = 9 L passed**. The competing one-way account allows **5 L initially + at most 1 L replenishment = 6 L available** over that same interval. Thus the contradiction is not the invalid comparison of cumulative flow with instantaneous stock alone.

The vessel sheet supplies the vascular evidence: outward arterial delivery and observed venous valves favoring return toward the heart. The inherited jaw edition contributes the observation-versus-claim format, **not a vessel map**. The resulting loop names heart → arteries → an unseen tissue connection → veins → heart. A separate generic “recirculation account” step was removed; the resulting diagram carries the repeated-passage argument and the still-unseen link together. All quantities are fictional teaching premises, not clinical data or Harvey’s exact estimates. Harvey’s own argument joined quantity, inadequate replacement and venous return. [Harvey IX and XIV](https://bertie.ccsu.edu/naturesci/evolution/unit10background/HarveyNine.html)

### Longitude — actual times, sign, drift and two ranges

**Convention:** fictional voyage day 20, in an imagined 1768 voyage. All observations refer to the same instant and same day. The interface uses modern midnight-based 00–24-hour notation, not the original almanac’s astronomical-day notation. These numbers are not an ephemeris for a genuine calendar date and are not usable navigation data.

The supplied solar correction is **apparent = mean − 2 minutes**. The clock is also estimated to have **gained 6 seconds/day for 20 days**, so its **120-second gain is subtracted**, separately from the solar correction.

| Step | Clock method | Lunar method |
|---|---|---|
| Supplied observation | Clock reads 14:06 mean time | Moon–Sun distance 45°20′ before clearing |
| Reduction | 14:06 − 2 min = 14:04 apparent, before rate correction | Supplied net clearing +10′ gives 45°30′ ±1′ |
| Reference scale | Voyage gain 120 s; corrected apparent time 14:02 | Table: 12:00 → 44°29′; 15:00 → 45°59′; slope 0.5′ per minute |
| Independent local time | Observed local apparent noon 12:00 ±20 s | The **same independent observation**, not supplied by the lunar table |
| Comparison | Corrected reference time is 122 min later | 61′ above the 12:00 row gives 122 min: reference 14:02, then comparison with local 12:00 |
| Longitude | 122 min × 15′/min = **30°30′ W** | **30°30′ W** |
| Stipulated worst-case bound | Clock residual 20 s + local 20 s = 40 s → **±10′** | Angle 1′ → reference 2 min; plus local 20 s → **±35′** |
| Resulting band | **30°20′–30°40′ W** | **29°55′–31°05′ W** |

The raw clock answer was 31° W. Correcting it puts the entire narrower band east of the fictional chart’s 30°45′ W hazard meridian. That is a visible consequence, not a promise of an unspecified “checked range.” The bands are **not averaged or falsely tightened**: the two methods share the local-noon uncertainty. The chart records the clock-supported band and the wider lunar check separately.

Raw angle + supplied clearing works directly. The inherited predictive record is used with the nautical table to form the actual numerical reference-time scale, not as a compulsory “checked observation” badge. The full altitude-dependent spherical clearing calculation is deliberately outside this bounded exercise; its net result and bound are supplied explicitly.

The historical method requires both reference time and local time. Early almanacs used apparent time; the mean-time change belongs to 1834, not this scene. The three-hour rows and linear interpolation here are invented teaching data. [Smithsonian lunar method](https://timeandnavigation.si.edu/navigating-at-sea/longitude-problem/observing-the-skies), [USNO almanac chronology](https://aa.usno.navy.mil/publications/na_history), [Greenwich history of the almanac](https://www.royalobservatorygreenwich.org/articles.php?article=935), [ESO time/longitude conversion](https://www.eso.org/public/outreach/eduoff/seaspace/navigation/navastro/navastro-5.html)

### Future observatory — an apparatus changes its question

Two explicit models predict glow from light alone or from current fresh food alone. The four-case table makes their predictions visible. The lab chooses the **dark-but-fed** row; glow rejects the light-only model. A later **dark, unfed glow** contradicts both original models rather than merely confirming the food model.

The next candidates are stored colony energy and a sensor echo. The inherited weighted-outcome method ranks an empty-bay test: under the explicitly invented equal weights, either outcome leaves one candidate, whereas repeating an ordinary illuminated colony leaves both. The same assembled observatory chooses and runs the empty-bay test; it stays dark. The stipulated sensor-echo account fails. This is an ambitious fictional capability, not an assertion that present autonomous targeting systems already do it.

### Future memory — exchange creates something neither person previously held

Mira remembers an open gate and a welcome. Tovan remembers the same gathering from outside a barred gate. Collation preserves **OPEN / BARRED** under their separate names. After exchanging reconstructed first-person positions, Mira can inhabit exclusion without surrendering her own recollection, and Tovan can inhabit the welcome without adopting it as his memory.

They then design a new gathering reached from both the courtyard and alley. The garden hosts that new event and records a joint episode: **“We arrived by two paths.”** A shared present is created without voting either incompatible past out of existence. The old `collective-self-model → plural-identity` certification tail is replaced by the concrete invitation and shared act. This fiction does not require uploading or settle consciousness.

### Future city — a rule changes the living installation

The first observation is a schedule, not a vague resource-flow token: **12 units produced; 8 distributed by day; 4 stored against night demand of 6**. The observatory tests 6 by day / 6 stored. No food appears from nowhere. The water ledger likewise has **80 returned + 20 fresh = 100**, with 20 lost.

The winter comparison is **full wall A12+B4** versus **folded wall A10+B7**. Choral experience makes the asymmetry felt: A loses two harvest units while B gains three and regains daylight. The revisable agreement changes the panel position and A’s schedule to 5 by day / 5 at night.

Then the recycling partner diverts three units into its own repair. The city visibly changes to **A7+B7**, with the former promise no longer achievable. It keeps repair running and activates the agreed equal-share fallback: seven units each. Output does not magically return to 17. The final living assembly preserves its partners and has changed its actual operation; scarcity remains a problem to investigate, not a final-utopia label.

- **5 session-provenance tests:** fresh and prepared sessions capture source hashes; resealed scenario, runtime or player changes are rejected without writing the old session; a setup lacking complete provenance is not adopted.
- **50 randomized full routes**, including reversed drags, finish in 258–260 combinations. Every non-opening chapter still requires at least two earlier-created outputs on every route.
- **Frozen-era build and 129 aggregate tests pass**, using the preserved v2 compatibility fixture with only its candidate replaced. Local fixture: 10 active worlds / 997 recipes; the existing bundle-size warning remains.
- **Separate read-only current-policy check:** five routes pass against the reviewed event-local engine, with its exact hashes recorded in `current-policy-compatibility.json`. This does not replace the frozen trial runtime or claim a full latest-integration build.
- The independent reviewer’s arithmetic/branch rechecks, including exact reviewed snapshots, live beside the original audit. Their scope is deliberately narrow; they do not certify the futures, native layout or enjoyment.

`evaluation.json`, `case-values.json`, `test-results.log`, `aggregate-test.log`, `build.log` and `verification-summary.json` provide the corresponding evidence. `CASE_PLAN.md` contains author-visible combinations and should not be sent to a source-blind observer.
