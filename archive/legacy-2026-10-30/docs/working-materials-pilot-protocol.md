## Question and prior evidence

Test whether a finite collection combining familiar assembly, functional transformations, actual forms and record histories supplies a worthwhile longer campaign. Learning, clear progress, comfortable familiar construction and useful compression can all contribute; neither a new insight nor a difficult tradeoff is required at every action.

V1's fresh player completed twelve goals in 65 selected combines and valued parts of that shorter experience. V2 offers thirty goals on ten boards. Authored witnesses are 165 ordinary / 160 complete / 132 saved / 169 alternate-order interventions; prior independent broad-assembly controls are 156 / 148 / 135. These are explicit feasible upper bounds, not optima or player-fun findings. The independent lens refresh must pass on the final frozen engine before recruitment. Its evidence is retained separately, not substituted into the older review.

The short routes have already been investigated: broad reusable assemblies compress much familiar construction, while actual material, form, observation, copy and route events still require contextual work. Preserve that compression. It supplies a reason to expect another short result, not permission to force players onto inefficient routes. The named unresolved question for pilot A is how a fresh player experiences the full expanded collection, especially the later transformations and purposes. Its diagnostic value remains legitimate even if it cannot establish the target.

Use the previously declared **180–240 individually selected gameplay interventions** interpretation of about 200. No player receives that interval or an action budget. Do not change the threshold after play or manufacture length through quotas, extra recipes, splitting journeys, disabling copies or post-completion work.

## Sequence and stopping gates

1. Refresh independent lens, public-command and full-campaign controls. Freeze immutable source/package hashes. Parent publishes those files and confirms the remote commit before player creation.
2. Run **A first**, fresh with no history inheritance. Preserve actual public observations/previews, selected actions, failures, checkpoints and final report. Independently verify deterministic capture and endpoint before deciding the next gate.
3. If A exposes a broken interface, disclosure defect, unmistakable padding or a full-campaign short solution, preserve A as a diagnostic result and suspend B/C replication. Investigate the concrete finding before any newly versioned test. A shorter valid completion is not a player failure. Do not recruit until a favorable report appears. A short route already known from controls is a prior warning; a fresh short completion settles that session's scale honestly.
4. If A is viable for the target (without concrete gate failures), run B with identical version/order and no A exposure, then C with the frozen free-choice assignment. Complete the planned viable cohort before a provisional success claim. Early satisfaction remains valid for C and early stopping remains valid for all.
5. An independent skeptical reviewer, not implementer, coordinator or player, audits all actually run sessions. If A stops the cohort gate, review A and explicitly label B/C unrun. Reports do not establish human pleasure, human duration or statistical population preference.
