# One possible journey through the estuary collection

26 October 2026. **Bounded paper witness, not executable evidence or a fresh pilot.** It accompanies the immutable architecture draft without changing it or the live-host gate. The aim is to see whether ideas can change actual state across purposes. A simple capable final world is a legitimate result. This document deliberately exposes where the proposed collection is still short or relies on unimplemented laws.

## What is carried, and what is only proposed

A single world retains: the observatory's method and apparatus; two continuing relatives; a responding grove; a shared room; a translated performance; a learned house; and a distant community. The following trajectory uses six projects. It does not assign a minimum number of operations to any project.

- **Implemented in separate kernels:** temporal stimulus/control distinctions, actual branching episodes and taught learning, role succession, physical arrangement, demonstrated control replay. These are not yet one compatible engine. A paper arrow between them is not an integration test.
- **New proposed laws here:** a minimal trace-bearing grove process; a temporal score with an actual discriminable replay; purpose-driven room configurations; delivery epochs and delayed institutional messages. Their precise toy forms below make the consequences checkable on paper, not historically or scientifically validated.
- **Not needed on this route:** personal prediction, a compulsory refusal conflict, every original future idea, a universal governance regime, or simultaneous maintenance of every possible future. Their omission is a valid compression, not missing required play.

## Proposed tiny law set for the unimplemented substrate

These public toy laws are intentionally small. If they only support a brief demonstration, that is the result.

**Grove process.** A fictional grove has `shoots ∈ {open,closed}`, `afterEffect ∈ {on,off}`, and an ordered actual event trace. A warm pulse sets afterEffect on; a cold reset sets it off. Darkness closes shoots only if afterEffect is off. Returning to ordinary light opens shoots. Events append actual trace entries; copying an archive does not append a second ecological event. A whole stimulus sequence enacts its declared steps automatically. This is a fictional causal system, not a model of real forest restoration. A later full forest would need genuinely richer changes; this toy is deliberately not described as one.

**Translated score.** A source sequence maps warm pulse→rising pressure, cold reset→release, darkness→dark interval. Replaying the score emits that ordered event sequence for a receiving actor. The receiving actor can later reproduce its learned order when prompted. Changing tempo preserves ordering; reversing it changes the response. Source history remains source history: receiving a score does not make its visitor the person who lived the original episode. This is a symbolic performance, not a rendered sensory experience or a proven account of embodiment.

**Room.** One floor has cells `(0,0)` through `(2,2)`. Occupying bodies cannot overlap; bodies can be moved out or replaced without a material cost. A teaching performance needs an actual source at `(1,0)`, an unobstructed central path through `(1,1)` to a seated visitor at `(1,2)`. A private retreat needs an enclosing partition across the middle row and an accessible sleeping place on its far side; this does not work simultaneously with that open central path. A present workshop needs a working table in a side column and an open entrance. These are purpose-driven coarse room laws proposed for this paper only, not a claim that the current physical engine evaluates these uses. Sound, privacy and full habitation are not secretly simulated.

**Delivery.** A message sent in epoch `n` becomes available in epoch `n+1` after a whole agreed local activity. It cannot authorize an earlier action. A role successor can receive messages addressed to that continuing role; a personal invitation remains personal. A shared event scheduled for epoch `n+2` can therefore be agreed without instantaneous remote consent. The player selects a whole activity/plan, not repeated wait clicks. This deterministic one-epoch law is a toy, not an account of relativity or actual communication networks.

## What the shortest capable world teaches us

This route deliberately uses a small number of capable constructions many times. The pulse method solves explanation, useful response and restoration; the Causal Portrait becomes interpretation and counterfactual art; a learned sequence passes through divergent relatives, visitors and distant communities; a single room is reconfigured rather than rebuilt; a whole method serves the learned host. These are concrete cross-purpose dependencies on paper.

However, the route also exposes serious compression:

1. The proposed grove is basically the earlier persistence model with traces. It is **not yet a substantively new ecology**. Calling it Witness Forests does not solve that problem.
2. The score is an ordered symbolic encoding. It establishes a different output, but not sensory wonder or a challenging translation space. The visitor may do nothing more interesting than reproduce a known sequence.
3. The room admits a simple movable-partition solution and unrestricted replacement. That is valid, potentially pleasant construction, and very short. Its inheritance semantics add no deep dilemma by themselves.
4. The distant event is solved by a willing schedule and role-addressed delivery. A universal optional treaty remains strong. Prediction and protected refusal are not necessary, so forcing their use would add checklist work.
5. The learning host may still be a tiny macro installation. Actual control coupling matters mechanically, but its experienced value remains pending.

Therefore this paper route **does not justify implementing the whole collection or claiming a long game**. It shows several reusable objects and new possible lives, but not enough evidence that each proposed world law provides sustained useful construction. A simple end state is allowed to be good; it need not be sabotaged to preserve future tasks.

## Normal versus complete catalogue, without a false count

Normal introduces method composition, kinship/reunion, translation, reversible rooms, teaching/succession and bounded cooperation as they become useful. Complete catalogue makes these capabilities available from the start, while actual histories, apparatus, scores and enacted agreements still must exist. Both conditions may use the same short trajectory. Do not count each illustrative table row as one engine action or add ancestor recipes merely to lengthen normal.

The complete-catalogue control is especially revealing here: it retains the real acting/teaching/arranging but may remove nearly all conceptual surprise. That could still leave a worthwhile toy world; conversely normal discovery could be its strongest pleasure. Only an actual implementation and full fresh experience can distinguish them.

## Bounded conclusion for selection

Retain the architecture as a coherent content vocabulary with explicit cross-purpose roles. Preserve the short route and saturation policies. After the current host pilot, the strongest distinct new hypothesis is likely **experience translation with consequences for what another actor can do**, rather than another authorization table or another plant truth-table variant. That is a selection suggestion, not implementation approval. It would need a concrete expressive construction space and actual reuse in two different lived projects before recruiting anyone. If that cannot be specified without labels doing the work, choose a different hypothesis rather than expanding the catalogue.
