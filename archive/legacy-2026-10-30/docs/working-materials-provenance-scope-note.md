# Frozen Working Materials: bounded provenance scope check

26 October 2026. Coordinator source-exposed counterexample, not player evidence, an exhaustive audit, or a repair. **Existing engine/UI remain unchanged** at engine SHA-256 `944e095d14693e9a300bebf7a107e3a33c991739ab9e518e3cb07a269f6110aa`.

## Result

The new Retained Project's physical-copy carrier fallback has **not been shown to exploit an existing Working Materials archive goal**. Existing archive physical-copy predicates require the seeded subject `survey-A` and `archive-a`; genuine seeded records retain their immutable `originKey` ahead of the carrier fallback. Public observations instead create `place:ID` subjects. This bounded inspection is not an impossibility proof for all compositions.

A **related, concrete frozen Optics identity gap** does exist: `wrapped-instrument` can be awarded after the original blank survey camera receives a different camera's observation and is carried to wet-station, without the original camera ever observing. A replayed route takes **12 ordinary-catalogue combines**, or9 with the complete named catalogue. `supporting-window` correctly remains unearned, so this does not solve the full board or campaign.

The exact public wrapped-instrument condition asks for the actual original camera, a beacon record from survey-window, and a flexible boundary enclosing its own lens/plate. Unlike supporting-window, its record predicate lacks `fromInitial:'survey-camera'`. The requirement text says the original instrument retains “its real beacon observation”; if that means its own witnessed observation, the predicate is too broad. If borrowed observations are intended, the goal should explicitly say so in a future version. This is **not** evidence that the new physical-copy fallback directly awards the old archive goals: no physical copying is used in this optics counterexample.

## Actual route and evidence

[`probe.mjs`](../results/retained-project-preplay/old-materials-provenance-scope/probe.mjs) runs against unchanged old modules, never imports or modifies the new candidate. Both [`ordinary.json`](../results/retained-project-preplay/old-materials-provenance-scope/ordinary.json) and [`complete.json`](../results/retained-project-preplay/old-materials-provenance-scope/complete.json) preserve every action, exact preview/transition equality, before/after hashes, final state/records/goals and complete replay.

1. At survey-window create a replacement camera, have it observe beacon, and optically relay that genuine record into the original blank camera.
2. Construct a clear rigid host and put the original inside. Supporting-window stays false: the earlier observation snapshot has only the replacement lens and no enclosing surface. The engine correctly does not rewrite it retroactively.
3. Put that host inside a flexible clear shell, attach a cart and carry the assembly to wet-station. The actual original lens/plate are flexibly enclosed, but the only record remains the replacement's. Wrapped-instrument becomes true.

The ordinary route actually constructs its solar lead/heater, fuses sand, constructs shell and cart; complete mode starts with those legal names. No injected state, solver or source mutation is used to execute either route. Record `record:76` identifies replacement camera `o:72` and lens `p:74` with no original-origin field, while original survey camera is merely its recipient. An exploratory ordinary variant initially omitted the cable needed for real heat and failed transformation; this was corrected in the retained executable route, not represented as a successful action. `probe.json` preserves the first complete-mode exploratory result; named ordinary/complete files are the final reproducible evidence.
