## Additional lawful alternative, not a shorter count

The reversible F assembly can unify the two drive paths:

1. Place S exposed and F at the wind pad.
2. Connect S.electric→F.electric and F.shaft→U.shaft.
3. Attach Q, connect L→U.in, and connect U.out separately to G and B.

This is again **eight selections**. In sun, electrical input to F's coupled reversible motor supplies its shaft; in wind, the actual fan drives that same shaft. This follows the stated electric-to-shaft relation and accessible work ports; it does not add a generator recipe, mode-switch action or new source. The final interface sheet should make F.electric's input/output capability explicit so readers do not mistake a generative port description for output-only behavior.

I do not claim a five-selection lower route by attaching Q directly to L or a receiver: only U's matching membrane seat is supplied. Nor may G be used as an output junction without such a port. Property-equivalent alternatives should remain legal when specified, but an audit must not manufacture their interfaces.

## Head, cycle and quality counterchecks

- At p=0, a driven pump receiving H=0 outputs4. Feeding that output back to the **same** pump yields max(4,4)=4, not8. Repetition cannot increase it indefinitely.
- Receiving H=5 at p=0 preserves5, not9. This is an **absolute height-bound fictional pump model**, not a realistic additive pressure-gain claim. That distinction should remain public.
- A pump moved to p=3 cannot accept the only available H=0 inlet, even if it has work. Power does not create water or satisfy the inlet requirement.
- A distinct driven pump at p=3 receiving a genuine H=4 proof can output7 if its placement and connections are lawful. That is a permitted staged design, not a same-pump exploit or a required ladder for this screen.
- A motor/generator/shaft loop with no external sun or wind input remains inactive under the least-source-grounded closure. Copies and reversible conversions cannot invent work.
- Omitting Q in the L route can produce sufficient head but still carries grit. Pumping does not purify. Conversely, Q without drive produces clean head0, which serves the lowered but not the original elevated receivers.
- I's height is not a free always-active clean source: actual compatible work and heater/ice contact must first establish its water output.
- An electrical edge cannot terminate directly at an incompatible shaft port merely because work is desired. The disclosed actual converter supplies the relation.

The attached finite tables are elementary paper proofs and arithmetic, not executed mechanical tests.

## Small clarifications before any executable contract

**Water proofs should explicitly be source-grounded finite paths or a least closure**, just as work is. “Carries its real source” should mean an actual derivation from active L or heated I, not a source label attached to an unseeded circulating stream. The proposed routes already have genuine source paths; this is a boundary clarification rather than a counterexample to them.

**Mixed quality needs a rule if multiple streams later share an inlet.** Does a clean and gritty feed mix, remain separate, or require a selected path? No volume simulation is needed, but an existential clean-path proof must not silently stand in for a clean mixed output if mixing is intended. Current routes avoid this ambiguity.

**Confirm the supplied matching Q and atomic attachment convention.** The scene calls it a known reusable spare and expressly grants one attachment into U. Keep that convention, or explicitly state actual loose-part availability and placement. Do not add a separate fabrication/fit operation after counting eight.

**Keep the geometry abstraction honest.** Exposure is determined by staging versus the named solar/wind pads; abstract links have no length loss and mechanical links are declared work-port connections. This does not yet demonstrate realistic shaft alignment, wind capture, routing clearance or an optical ray model. Any later geometric restriction is a new law to disclose and review, not something these paper counts already verified.

## Useful intermediates and whole-mission limits

The local relationships are real within the proposed logic: an existing membrane changes stream quality; the pump changes reachable elevation; a coupled converter changes the usable work interface; actual heating activates a high clean source. These effects justify a paper investigation without an industrial bootstrap or a compulsory salvage itinerary.

Yet all equipment starts known and usable, the membrane already fits, and all successful routes may keep the suit unit intact. The screen therefore establishes neither that disassembly/lining/forming is needed nor that those transformations are enjoyable. That is acceptable: intact use should not be disabled to stage-manage improvisation. More varied repurposing would require additional useful situations, not arbitrary faults or incompatible fittings manufactured solely to force steps.

G and B have the same local clean-water requirement apart from elevation. Calling one growing and one work does not make this a tested pair of rich working environments; their other conditions are expressly outside the screen. There is no full habitat, contact, expedition or self-sustaining-base result. The six-selection lower-site solution and static complementary-source answers demonstrate how inexpensive this local abstraction can be, not that a broader reusable ontology must be short or boring.

**Keep:** intact equipment, genuine source/quality/head relations, direct conversion and passive alternatives, clear counters and immediate completion. **Clarify:** water-proof grounding, mixed quality and exact port/attachment conventions. **Reject:** counting row propagation, forcing high-site identity, adding a pump stack, withholding working gear, or inventing a fitting/disassembly quota to enlarge the episode. Whether this is appreciated recognizable making or routine setup remains unmeasured.
