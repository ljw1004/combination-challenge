# Mars: from a distress signal to a place that works

26 October 2026. **Prospective whole-game synthesis; no new engine, assignment, player or long-game result.** Existing delivered Mars UI/content and all frozen research versions remain unchanged. The bounded live-host integration is still a separate experiment awaiting publication/play. Its result may remove, rather than add, the optional operating-host idea below.

## Decision

Develop one complete **survival → communications → mobility → self-sustaining base** paper campaign for adversarial compression before another implementation. Its unifying question is: **can the place you made keep supporting life, contact and useful work when you are elsewhere?** The response should be visible constructions and actual services, not a narrative chapter list or a sequence of archive certificates.

A larger reusable ontology is appropriate if its combinations create different capabilities. It is not appropriate to multiply obligatory recipe unlocks, specimen collections, copies, ports or journeys to reach a number. About 200 individually selected interventions remains the desired scale; this document offers a workload hypothesis, not evidence that a lawful full solution needs, or a player wants, that many actions. A cheap successful whole-base design stays successful.

### Evidence, separated

- **Human feedback, relayed by the coordinating parent:** Lucian said the staged crash/orbiter interaction's concept and storytelling worked well. That interaction used beacon/contact → relay → water → garden and staged visible locations/combinations. This supports brief understandable motivations and tangible progress. It does **not** establish long-game enjoyment, a 200-action preference, or endorsement of this new campaign's laws. Preserve visible partner/result discovery; do not replace it with a global output shopping list.
- **Agent experience:** Retained Project completed in 76 accepted actions with specific appreciation of actual reused creations and objections to transport/setup. Geometric Materials completed in 47 (33 station +14 conservatory), with optional shared clay/relief/archive and shelter/specimen arrangements appreciated; the second site's specimen and wet-copy work was explicitly repetitive. Its actual common-center arrangement mattered, but was small. Those counts cannot be added to make this campaign.
- **Mechanical controls:** repaired geometry admits 40/40/34 ordinary/complete/saved whole-campaign routes. Authored longer routes are not lower bounds. Earlier Mars/Working Materials routes and agent stopping reports already show that a large list of goals can become repeated service setup. A new fiction alone does not repair that.
- **Pending evidence:** live-host teaching uses real apparatus and two automatic physical transitions in short complete controls (39 normal /37 complete selected interventions). No fresh appreciation result is available at this writing. Learning/mandate themes are optional candidates, not ingredients to force into every Mars base.

## The visible place and finite ending

Use one connected landscape with three retained regions, not seven reset tutorial boards:

1. **Crash basin:** damaged shelter, an exposed contact beacon, a shaded work area, a dry basin and the first garden footprint.
2. **Ridge and ice seam:** inspectable sunlight directions, an occluded communications path, an actual water-producing site and terrain with exposed versus sheltered approaches.
3. **Far terrace:** a useful base footprint and a route back; different daylight/protection geometry makes moving a finished camp or serving it remotely a real alternative.

Exact coordinates, shapes, contacts and source rows must be fixed in the next paper specification. These names are not yet a playable map. No site should demand a named object when ordinary properties suffice. Nothing requires the original crashed equipment, original garden or original vehicle to survive if replacements fulfill the purpose.

Show the whole endpoint from the beginning, with short local motivations rather than dialogue:

> Establish a living base, maintain contact from its inhabited/work locations, make a usable journey between basin and terrace, and leave the resulting water, growth and work services dependable under every disclosed operating condition.

The avatar's arrival/departure changes where service is needed, not how many food, oxygen or fuel units remain. There is no death clock while planning. A completed unopposed journey resolves in one selected action; no tile moves or waits are counted. Self-sustaining means functional closed-loop or source-supported operation, **not production quotas, battery charge, stored supplies or elapsed survival time**. This is fictional game logic, not real survival engineering.

## Complete arc: different purposes for a shared vocabulary

The order below is a recommended presentation, not a required construction order. A player may build the ending first, copy assemblies, prepare outside the site, replace installations, or use a different regional base.

| Purpose and brief motivation | What the player actually creates/changes | How earlier capabilities matter differently | Lawful alternative / likely collapse |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Be found.** “The orbiter cannot see this hollow.” | Make a visible powered signal/contact installation and connect it to an actual receiver path. A first useful shelter/work surface can be simple. | Light is first a signaling resource, not only plant illumination. Visible operands produce an immediate understandable result. | An exposed mast or outside relay may solve this cheaply. Keep it; the opening may be comfortable learning. |
| **2. Make water useful.** “The ice is reachable; the garden is dry.” | Couple actual warmth to the seam, convey its output, separate the disclosed contaminant if present, and make a growing protected bed. | A reflector/thermal surface that exposed the beacon can deliver warmth; a porous interface later made durable can keep serving or be replaced. | Duplicate collectors and an always-open clean branch may solve everything statically. Do not impose a water budget to stop them. |
| **3. Keep service under changed exposure.** “The ridge lights the other face; dust crosses the basin.” | Adapt supply and enclosure/light paths to all public east/west and clear/dust rows, with actual protection of what needs it. | A transparent boundary protects while transmitting; a movable opaque one gives shade, but may block signal or growth. Shared supports/clearance can make arrangements interact. | Saturate with outside collectors and transparent covers. If this is fully sufficient, it is a valid robust design, not an excuse for arbitrary breakage. |
| **4. Make contact useful at a distance.** “Being found is not the same as staying connected from the workshop.” | Establish actual reachable signal paths from occupied/work locations around terrain; combine endpoints, relay/reflective boundaries and power sources. | The first beacon becomes one part of a network; a shelter, water apparatus or later vehicle may occlude a previously useful path. | Copy fixed relays everywhere. A spatially consistent universal network is legal. Record whether the problem is arranging it or merely repeating a relay recipe. |
| **5. Reach and use the terrace.** “A better base is across the broken approach.” | Make a supported crossing or a genuinely fitting movable construction, relocate or reproduce useful apparatus, and obtain actual service at the destination. | Rigid extent and hinge-subtree geometry now affect movement/placement, not a target silhouette. Existing modular designs reduce setup. | Build remotely, use multiple vehicles, replace the garden or bridge the route instead of folding a camp. No original-cargo identity requirement or arbitrary capacity cap. |
| **6. Make a productive home.** “The terrace should support more than arrival.” | Arrange growing space, dry/thermal work space and service access so their physical boundaries and sources coexist. Use transformations on actual materials to adapt working surfaces and exposed interfaces. | One roof can shelter multiple uses; one wet source may be helpful for growth and incompatible with a dry working surface. What was temporary becomes durable where its function requires it. | Independent modules on the open apron may eliminate conflicts. Keep them. The footprint must matter through useful service locations, not a newly imposed object limit. |
| **7. Leave it working.** “You can visit the ridge without undoing home.” | Verify the connected installation across the disclosed operating conditions and actual departure/return situation; fix real interactions or choose a robust static answer. Optional taught operation may run actual already-built controls if that experiment earns its place. | Earlier construction is now infrastructure. A good design can make this a short satisfying ending rather than a maintenance shift. | A single robust prefab may already satisfy the ending. Recognize completion immediately. Do not require a storm, a failure, separate proof clicks or a ceremonial handover. |

No extra archive, specimen or camera-copy task is included merely because the existing engine already has its predicate. Observation may be instrumental to a genuine survey or communication purpose; “record the next place again” is not itself the arc. Physical history is useful when it explains an object's present role, not when an original-ID test forces sentimental reuse.

## Shared ontology: what exists, what would be new

**Already supported in some frozen version, but not jointly validated:** inspectable partner/results; inexhaustible known designs and saved assemblies; real source provenance; clear/opaque/porous material distinctions; water/heat/light/power service predicates; local fitting and transformation; retained boards; literal geometric support/collision/optical paths; dimension-preserving hinges; actual record/copy distinction. Working Materials exposure rows and ice/purification are not automatically features of Geometric Materials. Combining these mechanisms requires an explicit new-version core and differential revalidation, not mixing saved state files or summing counts.

**Smallest genuinely new functional contribution to specify:** a **live communications path** through actual endpoints/relays and disclosed terrain occlusion, with source-supported operation and present receiver reachability. It must make distant contact a different purpose from collecting camera observations. Do not implement realistic radio equations, signal-strength budgets, per-tile cable laying or bandwidth economics. Fix a small consistent visible path law and permit alternative chains/placement. The existing optical predicate is an analogy, not validation of this new service.

**Conditional second addition:** actual whole-journey movement through disclosed clearance/support, if ordinary placement plus copies does not already express the route purpose. Geometry need not simulate every wheel step. A new movement law must affect reachable useful configurations and permit bridges/alternate vehicles. If it only adds attach-cart/fold/move/unfold chores, reject it.

**Do not add by default:** pressure/oxygen chemistry, fuel inventories, production rates, weather randomness, opponents, NPC dialogue trees, a compulsory programming language, civic permission ledgers or mandatory lineage. There is no requirement to import all previous experiments. The pending learner may become an optional way to operate real infrastructure, not the reason every resident request exists.

## Where around 200 decisions might come from—and why that is unproved

The strongest plausible source is **a larger connected set of useful things to make and arrange**, with familiar construction becoming easier as capability transfers. It is not 200 dilemmas, 200 unique recipes, or 200 confirmations of a designer route. A clear first construction and a repeated useful connection may be worthwhile; the pilot's unwanted repetition still has to be addressed in actual play.

A speculative first-visit workload **budget**, not yet even a counted route estimate, for the **whole new campaign** is:

| Actual selection class | Design workload hypothesis, not a quota |
|---|---:|
| Discover/build useful canonical and player-designed composites across the distinct purposes | 65–85 |
| Select actual placements, joins, divisions and meaningful reconfigurations | 35–50 |
| Connect real service interfaces and live communication paths | 20–30 |
| Transform, fit or repurpose actual working surfaces | 15–25 |
| Choose consequential whole journeys and operating changes | 10–20 |
| **Total hypothetical range** | **145–210** |

Independent design criticism is decisive here: an all-condition camp/network may absorb purposes 1–4 and 6–7, while remote construction may eliminate the terrace journey. The 65–85 construction and 10–20 journey/operation allocations have no exact witness yet. They are not evidence supporting implementation.

This deliberately does **not** promise a 180–240 result: its lower range is short, and the upper end depends on unbuilt content and individually chosen construction rather than cheap saved designs. Even the 65–85 construction estimate needs an explicit useful-capability list; it must not become an instruction to invent 85 compulsory nouns. A comfortable first-time player might make more revisions or alternatives, but inefficiency cannot be the plan for missing length. The current evidence does not justify a stronger numerical claim.

For plausibility, the next complete specification must expose enough distinct useful construction to support roughly 200 without requiring it. If exact normal/complete/saved witnesses collapse below the target band, retain the short result and reassess the **overall campaign scope**, not silently pad it. A larger genuinely distinct scenario can be added only with a new purpose and actual end-to-end test; no arithmetic addition of old independent demos.

## Required whole-campaign screen before source

Write **one exact map, source/exposure table, recipe/property sheet, endpoint and complete route**, not isolated local sketches. Then independently attempt:

1. **Ending first / universal camp:** build an all-condition transparent, supplied, connected installation immediately. Remove any supposed phase progression that adds no functional work.
2. **Unlimited duplicates:** collectors, relays, gardens and workshops at every useful site; independent remote installations instead of carrying one privileged object.
3. **Complete catalogue / learned prefab:** every design available but no actual achievements/history, then one reusable rich assembly. Preserve all speedups.
4. **Open-apron modularity:** avoid cramped interactions through lawful external supports and separate modules. If the task depends on a site, specify the actual source/contact/view relation, not an occupancy cap.
5. **Static robustness versus operation:** a fixed design that works in all disclosed rows versus a useful movable/controlled alternative. Do not demand the controller if static wins.
6. **Journey removal:** if remote construction and communication satisfy the actual purpose, the nominal expedition cannot be forced by a “must visit” certificate. Either accept the alternative or choose a different honestly movement-dependent purpose.
7. **Setup compression:** remove repeated carts, tubes, mounting, proof records and relay hops that do not change a functional decision. Bulk known assemblies remain direct capabilities.
8. **Effect removal:** remove the alleged contribution of a dynamic change while preserving resolution; if the same endpoint still holds, do not count the change as demonstrated depth.

Report actual full route counts and operation classes, not just a narrative effort estimate. A long authored route does not defeat a short alternative. The previous dynamic-waterway review's universal-network critique applies here too.

## Evaluation and stop conditions

Only after a complete paper route and independent compression review support implementation should the new whole core/content be built. Then require deterministic mechanical/public-preview tests, independent full ordinary/complete/saved witnesses, immutable publication and a fresh complete-campaign assignment under the long-play protocol. Earlier staged-human praise, the 76/47 agent plays and the pending hosting pilot remain separate evidence.

Capture actual whole play, including comfortable familiar steps, valid early satisfaction, shorter completion, repeated execution, interface limitations and the player's reason for stopping. A new UI may eventually present this through staged places and directly visible pieces; no current UI changes are authorized by this document. Fresh logic testing must not treat unseen polish as part of the experience.

**Keep** constructions that players value making or sharing, concise visible purposes, and lawful shortcuts. **Change** repeated late work whose only defense is that it increments a checklist. **Reject** a long-game claim if the full outcome is another short network, a small controller wrapped in discovery prerequisites, or a long sequence of transport/setup chores. A worthwhile short game is still worth retaining—but the owner's approximately-200 goal remains open.

## Coordination and next bounded decision

The independent future-ideas lead recommends teaching only as optional dependable operation: it can replay demonstrated real controls while a creator pursues another role, but the present integration has not yet shown concurrent survival work or experiential value. Intellectual future-lives themes remain a separate optional direction, not the Mars backbone.

The next paper specification is authorized only as a bounded attempt to **falsify or concretize this complete arc**, not as an automatic path to a new engine. It must give the terrace a real useful relationship or admit that mobility is optional/dispensable. A motivating story does not make a compulsory journey lawful by itself. If a universal connected camp fulfills the proposed endpoint, preserve that answer and report the arc as a simpler construction game.
