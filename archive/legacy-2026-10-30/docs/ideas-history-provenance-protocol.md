## Hypothesis and scope

An idea can be more than its coverage: the same apparent conclusion can carry copied, independent, or original evidence. Combining accounts with explicit provenance and different institutional purposes may make constructing and reusing explanations satisfying. The opening offers simple progress; later boards combine faithful transmission, scope and corroboration. Historical inspiration is documented separately, and all game laws are invented.

This is a **six-board diagnostic**, not a claim of about 200 decisions. A developer witness completes it in 26 accepted combines; this is neither a minimum nor a player result.
