# The world becomes a tool

**Design proposal; no implementation or long-play claim.** Frontier sustained an architectural project through36 combines, not180–240 decisions. Its strongest continuation repurposed actual history: teach a passage, make it a boundary, lose a causeway, build another living bridge. The elective player preferred a hearth and one boundary coda. Preserve both endings. More hostile countering would not address this result.

**Mechanism: live embedding.** A familiar whole creation becomes an operand in another spatial relationship. Its interior continues running; its actual entrances, exits, travel delays, terrain and scars determine its larger behavior. Nothing is replaced by a summary statistic, earned key or reset level. The larger map is inspectable from the beginning. Nesting expands what the player can combine.

## Four reusable laws

1. **Grasp a whole.** Any connected creation can be selected with its boundary ports exposed. Combining moves or connects that actual creation while preserving its identity and internal state. Its design is inexhaustible; copies retain provenance but have separate identities and declared starting phases. Ports require actual exits; an isolated loop initially has none.
2. **Ground means overlap.** A+B on ground joins their footprints at chosen cells. Shared cells are genuinely shared: traffic, terrain and closures affect both. Shared cells take the union of exits and closures; incompatible terrain cannot overlap.
3. **Channel means succession.** A+B at a channel joins a chosen output of A to an input of B. A front traverses their real interiors in sequence. Direction, type changes, blocked edges and elapsed beats transfer literally. Travel delay comes from the route, never a replacement power statistic.
4. **Basin means feedback.** A+B at a basin joins selected outputs back into inputs of both. Existing movement/contact laws resolve the returning traffic. Relative phase and internal route lengths now matter. Opening a port changes an old place, including any previously valued boundary; feedback is an actual route into the retained world.

Every pair, port eligibility and functional forecast stays public. Place changes the operation—overlap, succession or reciprocal coupling—not which secret recipe the author accepts.

## One cross-context chain

Retain the player's four-cell Home heart and a six-cell Other heart, with their existing types and scars. Home's clockwise passage has a four-beat period; Other's has six. The player's established ambition is to preserve both while sharing a crossing.

Add a physical branch to Home without cutting its return, then combine it with the old causeway **at a channel**. Its circulation delivers fronts to the causeway; inherited turns and closures determine which entrance works. The old hearth becomes a transport process without ceasing to be the hearth.

Combine that living crossing with Other **at a basin**. The returns meet at different phases: some complete the shared passage; some collide or enter an old heart through its former boundary. Reuse an already built detour as a serial delay, or change which real ports are joined. This changes arrival relationships rather than buying more throughput. Finally, **ground overlap** can make the resulting two-heart construction the crossing itself, with its distinct interiors still inspectable.

The next construction is wanted because it lets the same valued creations do something together. It is optional; a player who prefers their earlier separation may stop. Reject the hypothesis if this relationship supplies no intention.

## Competing direction and next experiment

A continuing co-maker could instead retain successful relative action traces from the player's actual constructions, then reuse them under matching public terrain conditions. A learned hearth could build a return through the old causeway. This changes another agent's capabilities through teaching, rather than embedding a world. It risks copy spam, opaque selection or another counter routine; learned templates and their application must be visible.

**Recommend live embedding first:** two retained hearts, one causeway, three catalytic places; no new vocabulary catalogue. Test whether the player voluntarily turns a whole creation into a useful process and changes an old port for that purpose.

**Attack:** copy everything, connect everything, or keep isolated perfect hearts. Isolation remains valid. If one generic detour or universal insulation preserves every desired behavior, or the second use is merely permission paperwork, reject the expanding-agency hypothesis. Do not rescue it with upkeep, compulsory chapters or a move minimum.
