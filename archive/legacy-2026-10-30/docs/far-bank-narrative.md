The central promise is: **something you made for one purpose reaches someone with a different ambition**. The resulting work can travel onward and eventually return with an intention its first maker did not assign. The first valley remains a place in that story, not a completed board discarded at a chapter boundary.

## The spine is a creation

Rill receives an actual creation from the valley and makes the **Answering Garden**: leaves keep the old veins, but turn toward another light. She reads Ilex’s first mark as a possibility of return. This is her use of the mark, rather than a retcon of its origin.

Sen wants the answer to reach someone particular. His **Homeward Mirror** distinguishes a source from a descendant and a quotation from an author. That makes exact origins useful, and makes their audience consequential again.

Tal sees linked replies as the beginnings of a common person. The **Grove That Says We** can undertake something its contributors cannot each truthfully call only theirs. A continuing office separates the life of that purpose from the membership of the people currently voicing it.

Ada wants the shared work to reach beyond its makers’ known circle. Her **Lighthouse for the Unknown** is a welcome for someone she cannot name in advance. An invitation and every private address behind the invitation need not be the same thing.

Neri wants a door out of the common work that does not require denying the common past. The **Walking Orchard** retains its lineage while its later fruit ceases to speak automatically for the old wall.

Eno offers protected continuation in **The Unwritten Night**. A small light may be visible without illuminating every route by which it arrived. A chosen contribution does not purchase its contributor’s whole history.

The changed work returns to Ilex, Mneme and the Judge. Ilex becomes a recipient of a descendant of his own act. Mneme must distinguish preserving a singular origin from requiring every continuation to disclose it. The Judge must recognize a grant when its holder asks something its giver finds inconvenient. Closure can include an exchange, a continuing common speaker, distinct reciprocal lives, a protected return or an acknowledged separation.

## What the durable grant enables

The core designer proposes rights enforceable against later unilateral edits by the creator. A right to transform can let Rill safely begin a work of her own. An exit can make staying a real choice. Refuge can outlast the current keeper’s kindness. A constitution can allow its own holders to revise their common purpose rather than preserve the first creator’s intention indefinitely.

These are capabilities for making a world, not permissions to collect. A right needs an actual consequence for who can construct, revise, expose, represent or leave. Multiple grants can conflict over one work and its descendants. The model must test whether an unlimited collection of copies bypasses every claimed conflict, and whether a sincere invitation already provides every useful guarantee. The story cannot establish those distinctions by calling one choice more philosophical.

## Five changes within each relationship

Each receiver has authored material for **inherit**, **reply**, **speaker**, **custody** and **succession**. Those are five changes of ambition, not a prescribed series of acknowledgement actions. Their concrete meanings differ: Sen distinguishes particular voices, Tal makes a common speaker, Ada opens reach, Neri departs, and Eno protects a beginning. Later stages should operate on earlier made things, not award a new status after five configuration fields match.

The stage data supplies a displayed intention and question, an enacted response and a counterclaim. The model decides whether an actual changed work, office, constitution or relationship warrants them. A counterclaim should identify a recipient’s substantive purpose, not simply demand the next missing checkbox. An achieved relationship can remain responsive to relevant later changes; it must not devolve into perpetual demands either.

The exact future personal passages belong to committed events. The functional recipe, right, adoption condition, refusal policy and consequences remain inspectable. Withholding a character’s exact future words does not justify hiding eligibility or world laws.

## Reporting actual effects

The new module owns no propagation, adoption or covenant state. Its generic event prose uses core-supplied literal detail and guarded factual flags. An unchanged event returns literal detail without manufacturing another revelation. Intentions are explicitly not achievements. A statement that Mneme wants protected custody cannot prove that outgoing testimony stopped; the earlier review of Confluence exposed precisely that mismatch.

The six work images are authorial descriptions the core may embody. The existence of a lighthouse paragraph does not establish a functioning public invitation. Before freezing, integration should identify the actual work or transformation behind each image and remove unsupported physical claims.

## What remains to test

The content now has enough causal material for another constructed campaign; this does not establish 180–240 wanted opportunities. The first diagnostic should follow a real exported work through adoption, a reply, a changed common speaker, a conflict over custody or departure, and a changed return to the retained valley. Track ambitions and decision episodes, not just the number of accepted combinations.

At valid endings, an elective player may be satisfied and stop. If it continues, record which displayed question it actually adopted and what it tried to make. The mandated long condition can expose the middle and homecoming but cannot turn required continuation into appetite. A six-person cast and thirty authored stage responses are a content budget; the actual run must show whether the world develops or merely asks the same paperwork question repeatedly.
