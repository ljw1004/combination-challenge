## Decision

**Ready for a small frozen diagnostic play, with severe scope limits.** The model now does something V2 did not: a constructed method changes the predictions of explicit mechanisms. An uncooled light pulse exposes persistence; adding a thermostat distinguishes light from heat while removing the immediate-versus-retained heat distinction. Those consequences follow executable public laws, not method names or lineage certificates.

Nevertheless, the entire method space has only **six states**, and a fixed **five-combine** construction solves the single inquiry. This is a tutorial-sized method-design puzzle, not a large intellectual-history game or evidence for the roughly-200-action target. Do not inflate it by requiring every control, adding intermediate acknowledgments, or concealing already visible forecasts.

## Exact finite-space challenge

A method consists of two properties: steady versus pulsed illumination, and warmth free versus held cool versus held warm. Every method-transforming operation stays within those six states. Controls replace the relevant property; they do not accumulate a list of increasingly strong safeguards.

I enumerated every single method and all 15 unordered pairs of distinct methods:

- No single method distinguishes all three explanations.
- Exactly **four pairs** distinguish all three. Every winning pair consists of the **uncooled pulse** and **any thermostated method** (steady/pulsed, warm/cool).
- An ordinary steady uncooled comparison contributes no discrimination.
- No collection consisting exclusively of thermostated methods separates immediate heat from retained heat: the clamp overrides both mechanisms.

A fixed legal solution is:

1. Lamp test + pulse.
2. Resulting method + record opening.
3. Original lamp test + keep cool.
4. Resulting method + record opening.
5. Combine the two result objects.

The existing reuse witness instead cools the pulse itself. Both have five accepted combines. A warm clamp is equally valid. The fixed route is a real baseline, not a policy to ban because it is short.

**Five is also a structural lower bound under the current object grammar:** every winning pair contains two noninitial methods, requiring at least two method-transforming combinations. Both need result objects, requiring two readout combinations, and the goal requires one combined result, requiring one join. There are no initial result objects or single-method winners. This is a claim about this small grammar, not human decision complexity or an optimum under imagined future operations.

## Rich observation does not conceal a shorter result

The public `inspect` operation includes light, warmth and leaf-opening arrays, not merely the opening diary shown in compact descriptions. I therefore also compared the **entire inspectable trajectory** for all six methods.

Even treating every displayed variable as an observable, no single method separates all three models. Under free warmth, the direct-light and immediate-heat models have identical light/warmth/opening trajectories. Under a clamp, the two heat models have identical trajectories. Thus the goal is not being preserved by hiding a useful thermometer or a moment of the trial.

The laws, exact predictions and full readout are inspectable before commitment. Inspection and preview do not create method or result objects, mutate state, or advance the completion predicate. The player can understand the answer from public information before making it. This is a **construction goal**, not a hidden-answer identification experiment; `Record leaf opening` commits a chosen forecast as an operand and reveals no secret real-world result. Do not later describe the player as discovering which explanation actually governs the glasshouse: none is selected.

“Retained” means the explicitly coded **previous-moment window** (`lamp now OR lamp in the previous moment`), not indefinitely stored warmth. On the three-moment pulse this yields closed → open → open. Avoid describing a longer retention law than the model contains. “Starting cold” is a pre-control condition; a warm clamp overrides it at the first recorded moment, as the public forecasts show.

## What is new, and what remains unestablished

Compared with V2, the distinction being constructed concerns explanatory mechanisms and the effects of interventions, not certification of source ancestry. That is a genuine changed hypothesis.

But the pulse is an available transformation, not a freely programmed schedule; rivals are supplied rather than invented by the player; and the inquiry is a small finite classification of prediction signatures. There are no acting schools, discovery history, branching research programmes, or unknown empirical observations. Any claim of intellectual resonance, satisfaction, or desirable further play must come from the fresh play, not these labels.

The prospective question should be narrow: **Does composing a pulse with a control/readout, and recognizing why the control erases one distinction while revealing another, feel like making a useful investigative idea?** Keep any appreciation of clear enactment; no requirement that all five selections be novel dilemmas.
