## Important semantics

- A transformed leaf has explicit rigidity, optical, permeability and recording traits. Its immutable input/source provenance grants no ancestor capabilities.
- Actual leaf IDs, initial component origins and genuine impression/fit/copy events survive permitted physical changes. Blank design copies keep geometry but clear actual origin/history.
- Records are associated with actual carrier parts. Appending a blank plate does not transfer a wet sheet's record. Optical relay or physical copying is an actual event.
- A faithful observation records the actual lens and view-boundary IDs plus surface snapshots. A goal claiming observation through a surface cannot be earned by adding that surface afterward.
- Causal local growth checks refer to the actual illuminated tray and seed inside the checked boundary, not an unrelated working greenhouse in the same larger object.
- Explicit `achievement` cards retain lifecycle milestones; all `current` services must hold simultaneously at campaign completion. Historical achievement remains separately visible.
- Normal CLI commits expose concise changes, while full state, public observation and exact forecast evidence remain in the append-only capture. Full goals, rules and objects remain explicitly inspectable.
