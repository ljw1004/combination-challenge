# Mars Workshop: independent mechanical and scale review

26 October 2026, final confirmation 15:44 UTC. **Reviewer-only evidence, not player onboarding, a blind play, or an enjoyment finding.** The reviewer read the proposal, revision-9 doctrine, design contract, evaluation protocol and implementation. The reviewer did not implement the engine/content/adapter or author their primary witnesses. This review supplied two consistency bug reports that the implementation owner repaired; verification of those repairs is therefore not independent discovery by a new reviewer.

## Result first

**An explicit reusable-whole-assembly campaign completes all twelve jobs in 75 ordinary-catalogue combines, 69 complete-catalogue combines, or 56 combines with the previously made assembly available.** At each ending **all twelve goals are currently true**, not merely historically earned. These are legal, sampled, privileged **upper bounds**, not shortest-path proofs or player decision counts.

The approximately-200 target is not established. The short construction is a successful construction and must remain legal. The evidence does not show that clear progress or short campaigns are unfun, nor that a fresh player could not appreciate a longer path. It does show that catalogue size and twelve job names cannot support the proposed length by themselves.

## What the universal attack actually establishes

One actual assembly contains separately arranged light-transmitting, dark-protected and air-exposed parts, plus illumination/heating, contact, observation and movement capabilities. Copies of the same saved design supply nearly all newly placed structures. Its appearance/name does not change for each goal. Exact construction/actions are retained in the evidence, not inserted into the public guide.

This defeats the simplest intended opposition between “dry support,” “dark sample,” “protected garden,” “warm room,” “camera” and “mobile span”: the public aggregate laws permit those capabilities to coexist in one construction. The relevant condition can then be obtained from location, external contacts, a recorded event or movement.

However, this is **not** a proof that one placement at every marked target wins the campaign, nor that every scenario has identical laws. The executed policy still selects real sources and contacts, covers opposing light rows, observes from two actual viewpoints, makes specimen contact, preserves original-object identity, leaves a physical crossing, chooses a low route, and relays actual records. Copying does not manufacture these events. These contextual differences are real, but their executed workload is much smaller than the prospective 12–16 contextual combines per job.

The witness deliberately leaves useful copying intact. It does not invoke an output-building command, script inside the game, record cloning, cross-map physical teleportation, an ever-achieved loophole or an illegal fit. A route with explicit waypoints remains one whole-object trip. An ordinary frame-bearing object may serve as a span; no special bridge token is demanded.

The implemented geometry is a bounded whole-root folding law, not a detailed geometry simulator. Internal functional contact is mostly structural within an assembly, rather than a preserved graph of independently routed internal wires. Those public simplifications help explain the compression. They are not defects to “fix” merely to protect an action budget.

No unresolved mechanical failure remains in these sampled traces on the final reviewed hash. This is not a blanket correctness claim. In particular, arbitrary nested assemblies, all aim/pose combinations, all route histories and all malformed inputs were not exhaustively tested by this reviewer.

## Public forecast and capture disclosure

The initially reviewed CLI preview returned compact goals/shapes but no predicted per-row physical effects. The adapter owner added an explicit opt-in inspection argument rather than forcing large default dumps.

Actual CLI validation confirmed:

- Compact `preview ID ACTION_JSON` stays compact.
- `preview ID ACTION_JSON world` exposes predicted phase effects and source provenance, and its inspection exactly matched `inspect world` after committing that action.
- An object-targeted forecast includes its real part tree and per-row sources; its predicted full-state digest matched the committed final state.
- Place-targeted forecast is available as well; detailed world inspection remains the comprehensive route.
- A synthetic capture with one operator and one player actor override verified and counted the two separately. These actor labels test the harness; **they are not a player session or experience evidence**.

The tested opt-in path resolves the identified functional-forecast disclosure gap without requiring a full world dump on every action. Complete catalogue, rules, actual parts, exact contexts and source/record history remain queryable. No preferred next move was required for these checks.

## Decision and next discriminating question

**Keep the legal short campaign and reusable construction; do not present this as a demonstrated about-200-decision game.** No fresh play was conducted by this source-exposed reviewer, so comfort, boredom, appreciation and sustained fun remain unknown here. Mechanical success is not a substitute for those reports.

The next design iteration needs a falsifiable source of additional useful construction or contextual work, not more unlock labels, manual rebuilding, forbidden whole designs, finite stocks, hidden recipes, forced duplicate prerequisites or a move minimum. A new candidate should face this same reusable-assembly attack before an about-200 replication claim. If this version is played for its shorter scope, preserve that scope honestly and keep any genuine positive experience evidence without promoting it to the longer target.
