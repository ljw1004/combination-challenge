# A Promise With Two Futures

Standalone logical experiment `ideas-history-futures-1`. The people, civic rules and responses are invented; this is not legal advice or a model of real human motivation.

Mara and Lio have diverged from one remembered life but retain one inherited civic identity. Mara keeps a garden-care promise. Lio wants to join an expedition without automatically inheriting that promise. Ibis would willingly take over care. Later, the relatives can share new experiences and make a common garden decision while retaining their own personal choices.

The three public goals concern **continuity, partial reunion, and purpose-specific authority**. Different legal arrangements can satisfy them. A federation can retain one civic identity while giving each member their own personal decisions; kinship can recognize separate civic persons who later establish a shared garden voice. Discovery does not automatically adopt a doctrine everywhere.

```sh
node experiments/ideas-history-futures-play.ts init my-future
node experiments/ideas-history-futures-play.ts inspect my-future recipes
node experiments/ideas-history-futures-play.ts offers my-future consent
node experiments/ideas-history-futures-play.ts preview my-future '{"a":"consent","b":"federalism"}'
node experiments/ideas-history-futures-play.ts combine my-future '{"a":"consent","b":"federalism"}'
node experiments/ideas-history-futures-play.ts inspect my-future world
node experiments/ideas-history-futures-play.ts report my-future '{"text":"Your own experience"}'
node experiments/ideas-history-futures-play.ts finish my-future
```

That example is an available idea combination, not a required route. `inspect laws` gives all public rules; `offers` lists every legal pair and exact resulting responses. Inputs remain available. A refused care offer does not bind the actor. A consent-based merger is legal once the disclosed conditions hold, and can later be reopened without deleting people or memories.

## What a combination changes

- **Ideas + ideas** discover reusable practices. They do not themselves alter the family's institutions.
- **A civic compact + an authority mode** creates a template for individual assent or a common voice.
- **A template + a scope** creates a covenant for garden, personal or all decisions. Either authority mode can govern either scope; there is no secret special case requiring one named blueprint.
- **A covenant + the family** adopts that authority for exactly its scope. Overlapping scopes are replaced; unrelated scopes remain. Explicit covenants override the identity's default authority. Release covenants restore that default.
- **A care offer + an actor** immediately evaluates the actor's public willingness and the residents' acceptance. There is no extra confirmation click.
- **A reunion practice + the family** shares new knowledge, not decision powers. That knowledge enables an actual later garden decision.

After a combine, public actor policies settle into current tasks and responses. Mara or Ibis keeps the gate open; a personally authorized Lio joins the expedition; individual survey refusals or a joint acceptance are enacted; a knowledgeable authorized garden voice accepts the new plan. These responses are automatic consequences, not extra decisions to count. There are no finite stocks, points, timers, periodic gate-closing chores or action minimums.

The fixture is deliberately limited. Garden access begins valid and remains valid under the lawful caretaker transitions; do not claim an opening rescue that never happened. Most charter proposals are consented to by the actors under a disclosed fixture premise. Care willingness and the knowledge condition for merger supply distinct responses, not a rich autonomous psychology. Whether this still feels like a permission ledger is a live test question, not answered by the future-themed names.
