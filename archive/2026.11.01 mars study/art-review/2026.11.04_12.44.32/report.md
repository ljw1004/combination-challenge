## Family and size verdict

The family shares neutral exposed metal, modest surface wear, simple seams, restrained hand markings and coherent light. The material continuity makes the locally fabricated bodies clear. Their smooth formed shapes use the accepted fabrication premise without introducing an ornate workshop. The small valve, electrical module, leads, heater/sensor and trap remain subordinate. Exact donor provenance cannot be inferred from gray surfaces alone; the named donors and construction prose properly supply that information.

At the proposed sizes, the canister has a clean bottle silhouette and a visible small valve; the cell remains a distinct squat closed vessel with two outlets and an attached electrical module; the reactor remains a long tube on two rests with a visibly separate catch jar. The small lettering is supporting information, not a reliable substitute for the game's names. No recognition rate is claimed. The water cell is the heaviest of these three silhouettes, while the narrow canister leaves useful working space. These proportions are suitable pilot anchors; actual installed scale and the repeated canisters still need joint inspection.

The requested essentials are either visible or appropriately internal. The only required shape change is the trap inlet. Resolve the heater/sensor lead reading while preparing connected states. Later furnace, water, fuel and transfer plates require fresh review of their actual revised images, scene scale, shared-module continuity and retained parts; this pilot does not approve them in advance.
