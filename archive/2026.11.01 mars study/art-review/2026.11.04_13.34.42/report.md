## Scope and exposure

Reviewer: fresh Codex agent `austere_pilot_recognition`. During the original first-interpretation stage, the reviewer saw only neutral samples 1–3, separately presented in one tool response on a static warm plain background. Each PNG canvas was 230 × 220 pixels and was viewed at its supplied proposed gameplay size without enlargement. No manuscript, design brief, source files, answer key or other review was consulted. Visible markings in the artwork remained available.

The same reviewer then revisited unchanged samples 2 and 3 at the same size after receiving ordinary interaction context. For sample 2, the astronaut had made iron and would attach clean water and electrical power to the body, then collect from its two outlets using separate empty cylinders. For sample 3, filled hydrogen and carbon dioxide containers would connect to separate feeds, with another empty canister brought to the output. No target name or internal construction was supplied. This was a second exposure within the original assignment, not another blind trial.

## Final outcome

| Sample | Unaided physical family and exact-function limit | Outcome after ordinary context |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | High-confidence gas cylinder/cartridge recognition from its rounded metal body and projecting fitting. Hydrogen identification depended on the visible H₂ marking; contents were not inferred from silhouette alone. | No additional context stage was needed or conducted. |
| 2 | The metal canister, H₂/O₂ fittings and wired meter supported an electrochemical-apparatus reading. The initial moderate-confidence fuel-cell interpretation remained ambiguous with an electrolyzer: the picture did not establish process direction. | Clean water, electrical input and separate gas collection resolved the function as a water electrolyzer with high confidence. Internal construction remained unspecified. |
| 3 | Moderate-confidence mounted pressure-vessel/valve-assembly recognition. Its chamber, retaining strap, supports and plumbing did not identify a unique purpose; burner or propulsion use remained plausible. | Hydrogen and carbon dioxide feeds established a reaction-vessel role with high confidence. Methane production was a moderate-confidence inference from the reactants, not visible information. Separate feeds and output could not be confidently traced in the picture, and internal mechanism remained unknown. |

This pilot supports unaided cylinder recognition for sample 1, context recovery of process direction for sample 2, and context recovery of a broad reactor role for sample 3. It does not establish unaided exact-function recognition for samples 2–3. No production keep/change/reject decision, style verdict, live-game usability result or review of subsequent assets was part of this assignment. These are model interpretations, not human recognition-speed or preference measurements.
