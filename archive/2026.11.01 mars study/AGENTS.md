# Working on Mars

Read the active [Mars design brief](../../stories/mars/design-brief.md) for every story or artwork assignment in this study. It owns accepted intent, including experimental iterations; follow its [revision requirements](../../stories/mars/design-brief.md#revision-and-artwork-requirements). Proposed departures belong in working notes until adopted. Do not replace the active brief with an old iteration's copy.

`mars-v12/` and `illustrated-baseline/` are preserved references. Future manuscript and runtime-art changes belong in `stories/mars/`; production sources and working hypotheses remain in this study. The [production entry point](production/README.md) distinguishes historical packages and commands from current work. Check actual script inputs and output paths before use; old exporters can overwrite a preserved manuscript or the live story.

Follow the shared [iteration process](../../docs/story-authoring.md#develop-a-story), [graphics methods](../../docs/icon-guide.md) and [retention policy](../../docs/engineering.md#verification-records). Trial generations, rejected candidates, review sheets and intermediate output go under ignored `runs/`, with only deliberately selected production material promoted into the study.
