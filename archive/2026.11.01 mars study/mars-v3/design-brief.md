# Mars v3 — A Place That Can Endure

This brief owns this draft’s intent. The [manuscript](mars.md) owns the playable work; the parent [story-design guidance](../README.md#story-direction) records the user’s direction. The [game’s philosophy](../../../README.md#philosophy), [authoring guide](../../../docs/story-authoring.md), [format specification](../../../docs/story-format-spec.md) and [UX](../../../docs/ux.md) govern the shared framework. This is a candidate for user evaluation, not an accepted replacement for the illustrated Mars.

## The player and the promise

You are the only person on the surface, commissioned to establish a locally supplied research station, investigate the nearby ice and bring samples back. Complete pressure, flight, life-support and precision laboratory equipment were sent ahead. The landing compromises the external solar installation, not every system at once. You restore a dependable beginning, then make the surroundings, supplies and connections that turn delivered equipment into a place you can live and work.

The hook is ownership through useful making: the water under the dust becomes a drink, a garden and a way home. The material that sustains the home also holds an extraordinary past. A tiny, long-dead articulated body is the discovery, never a companion, a living pet or a reason to collect photographs.

There are three continuing threads: the home and garden; reading the ice and the old landscape; and making the return journey possible. They meet in a darkness crisis that tests what the player has already made. Geography stays simple: the station and its nearby resource bank/channel, one field exposure reached by the same rover, and the adjacent return pad. Individual workshop fixtures and laboratory stations are receiving locations within those places, not separate expeditions.

## What combining means

Make and adapt physical things through a small set of recognizable operations: salvage, shape, separate, heat, insulate, grow, examine and preserve. Imported precision equipment does substantial work; local feedstocks and reused packing supply much of the bulk. The player should see why both inputs belong together before reading the result.

A stock of metal, mesh, insulation or clean jars can support several constructions. A unique planted trough, occupied sample case or core cannot quietly produce another copy of itself. The same two crop troughs survive the storm and yield the eventual harvest. The same body-bearing core and its field case progress through observation, storage and backup cooling before a contained transfer into the flight carrier. A small ordinary end section can be sacrificed while the body and untouched neighboring ice remain cold.

Different receiving fixtures distinguish forming a bench, a tray and a case from the same stock. Follow the [authoring guidance](../../../docs/story-authoring.md#make-the-action-understandable) on using contextual two-item recipes sparingly. The two retained contextual pairs have a physical reason: reaction inside a reactor, and transfer between two containers inside a cold enclosure. Use the shared hotspot limits to keep most chapters within two hotspots. The two exceptions have three distinct jobs: charging/radio/window work, and the two electrolyzer outlets plus atmospheric capture.

Completed equipment can become part of the place. The recovered solar rack becomes the next chapter’s charging socket; the recirculating nutrient reservoir supplies a later nursery through its tap; the built growing nook later receives the two threatened crops. These hotspots represent earlier constructions, not newly supplied copies. Earlier goals establish the construction before its receiving surface is introduced.

The original scale target was approximately 200 worthwhile combinations. The current revision has 153 combinations on its complete route after the user requested removing unnecessary processing and comparison work. It is shorter than that original target; any future expansion must add substantial story-driven making. A small crop harvest and the body reveal may be short; a useful construction can take longer. Elapsed time is an accepted genre abstraction. Convenient cues establish a field season and crop growth without timers, waits or resource quantities.

## Narrative progression

| Passage | Work the player performs | Reason to want the next passage |
|---|---|---|
| Landing and first water | Recover the array; restore contact and a warm window; sample and treat local ice | Make the place dependable, and understand the strange band inside useful water |
| A working home | Test local ceramic material; build protection, furniture and growing troughs; recycle water | Feel the station becoming yours, with something alive in it |
| A way back | Operate electrolysis, the methane reaction, water recovery and separate cryogenic stores | Start a seasonal process with an established purpose |
| The old landscape | Examine channel grains and a fresh vein; identify gypsum; inspect a fragile ridged fragment freed from the band | The fragment came apart: seek a whole one inside unbroken ice |
| One field expedition | Make a refrigerated case; cross a short drift; take the protected band core | Bring the unknown thing home still inside its ice |
| Something folded | Make an optical window, backlight the speck and examine it under the cold microscope; warm one sacrificed adjacent section | Discover the body, then learn how to bring it home without losing its surroundings |
| Darkness | Shed industrial loads; shelter both troughs; use passive watering, a small pump and insulated refrigeration | Keep the place and discovery alive with earlier knowledge |
| Sunlight and departure | Restore reserves; renew the nursery; harvest potatoes; finish fuel transfer; store equipment and pack real samples | Leave a useful place and bring the actual find home |

Chapter introductions establish situation or motivation in one or two sentences. The decisive events happen through combinations and short resulting observations. Earth messages are delayed; they can give a human response without supervising a seconds-long emergency. No passive crewmate needs rescue and no other person becomes an inventory object.

## A small fiction ledger

1. **Mature future expedition hardware.** The mission has complete, compatible pressure systems, a rover, a solar workshop, water treatment, instruments, a regenerative electrical reserve, an integrated propellant plant and qualified return hardware. This is an equipment premise, not a claim that a lone person can fabricate precision machinery from regolith. Manufacturing rates, total launch mass and exact storage capacities are abstracted.
2. **A favorable surveyed site and process.** Accessible layered ice, nearby water-altered rocks and local material that can be processed into useful non-pressure ceramics occur together. The furnace recipe also yields a washed, tested porous growing aggregate. This particular combination of geology and practical performance is fictional; local trials make the assumptions visible.
3. **An extraordinary preserved organism.** Ancient life produced a millimetre-scale, partly mineralized articulated body, exceptionally preserved inside the sampled layer. The fragile ridged fragment and the later body’s anatomy make it extraordinary in the fiction. They do not establish a real Martian organism, a terrestrial taxon, an absolute age, pristine DNA or freedom from every possible contamination route.

## Scientific boundaries and sources

| Topic | What the game teaches and what it assumes |
|---|---|
| Accessible ice | Orbital measurements can identify promising subsurface ice, with limits on depth and spatial resolution. The exact accessible bank is invented. Keep the heated resource borehole distinct from the clean scientific exposure. [SWIM overview](https://swim.psi.edu/about.php?section=overview). |
| Local ceramics | Concentrated-sunlight sintering of lunar simulant is a demonstrated concept; that does not qualify this Mars feedstock, its strength or a pressure vessel. Blocks, equipment feet and removable service covers are non-pressure construction around an imported pressure cabin. [ESA solar-brick demonstration](https://www.esa.int/Enabling_Support/Space_Engineering_Technology/Printing_bricks_from_moondust_using_the_Sun_s_heat). |
| Water | A cloth separates suspended particles, not dissolved salts. The complete supplied processor performs the stages needed for its declared use. The story does not teach that clear or merely heated water is potable. Martian contaminants are a reason for deliberate treatment, not extra paperwork. [NASA perchlorate research](https://www.nasa.gov/general/detoxifying-mars/). |
| Crops | Controlled root aeration, imported nutrients, light and a pressurized cabin support the crops. The small garden supplements stored food. Local granules support roots; they do not provide a complete diet for plants. The departure plan leaves clean equipment and cool seed stock, rather than promising an unattended farm. [NASA deep-space crop research](https://www.nasa.gov/science-research/nasa-plant-researchers-explore-question-of-deep-space-food-crops/), [NASA growing systems](https://www.nasa.gov/exploration-research-and-technology/growing-plants-in-space/). |
| Propellant | Water electrolysis supplies hydrogen and oxygen. Hydrogen reacts with captured carbon dioxide to make methane and water; the product water returns to the process. External energy and additional local water remain necessary. Methane and oxygen stay in separate compatible systems. [NASA demonstration plant](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19970026777). |
| Cryogenic shutdown | The qualified stores retain some liquid while separately venting boil-off outdoors during the storm. Restarted production replaces those losses before departure. Insulation does not guarantee indefinite storage without cooling. [NASA cryogenic-storage explanation](https://www.nasa.gov/isam/robotic-refueling-mission-3/). |
| Scientific inference | One mineral examination identifies gypsum in a cross-cutting vein and supplies a clue to past fluid movement. Its method is Raman spectroscopy, but the player sees the plain name “Mineral microscope.” A separate lens examination finds an unknown ridged fragment; intact sampling and microscopy reveal the body. No reference-core, mineral-matching, polarization or handling-control study gates that discovery. Detailed characterization belongs to the receiving laboratory. [NASA vein interpretation](https://science.nasa.gov/resource/signs-of-hydrated-calcium-sulfates-in-martian-rocks/), [JPL instrument roles](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/news/press_kits/mars_2020/landing/mission/science/). |
| Frozen preservation | A body-bearing core stays near −45 °C in supplied active refrigeration. A sacrificed adjacent section shows changes at −30 °C in this fictional sample; the −40 °C phase-change cartridges provide transfer margin. These are not universal Martian-brine temperatures or a fully qualified preservation limit; the carriers retain a conservative margin. Other soluble salts, rather than gypsum alone, account for the low-temperature liquid. Active refrigeration must condition the cartridges again; warm and cold stores remain separate. Liner access opens only inside the contained cold enclosure. [NASA cold-stowage systems](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20110010968). |
| Storm | Airborne dust reduces sunlight. Cleaning settled panel dust becomes useful after the sky clears; thin Martian air does not justify cinematic hurricane damage. The existing reserve keeps the planned mission safe, while the player adapts the expanded garden and frozen archive. Thermal storage buffers a transition rather than sustaining an indefinite storm. [NASA dust-storm explanation](https://www.nasa.gov/solar-system/the-fact-and-fiction-of-martian-dust-storms/). |

These sources support processes and boundaries, not the feasibility of the complete fictional installation. No claimed engineering certification or quantified human survival model is implied.

## Presentation and evaluation

The science follows a direct dramatic line: examine something, discover a feature that matters, and act on it. Separate samples remain separate. Sequential goals can guide examining one and then another without constructing a comparison object. Retain a scientific operation only when its observed result changes the question or the practical next step.


Use text items on a neutral surface while testing the story. Future artwork must make fixed fixtures part of the station, preserve proportional size and silhouette, and visibly follow the same crops, case and core. The existing [Mars artwork study](../README.md) and [icon guide](../../../docs/icon-guide.md) remain the reference; this draft does not generate or commit new illustrative artwork.

The experience hypothesis is that earlier making earns later attachment and capability. Reject passages that feel like equipment assembly delaying the interesting work, repetitive sample-handling certification, or a sequence solved without attention to purpose. Examine the distinction between intelligibility through partner hints and actual scientific or practical inference. The [project working notes](../working-notes.md) retain iterations and concise findings. The shared [evaluation guide](../../../docs/fun-and-evaluation.md) defines reviews and playtests; the [standard authoring workflow](../../../docs/story-authoring.md#check-and-preview-a-draft) covers shared lint, analysis and preview commands.
