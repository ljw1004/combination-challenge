# Review assignments

Follow the [evaluation method](../../../docs/fun-and-evaluation.md). Distinguish source-informed editorial analysis, sequential blind reading, player-only logical interaction, browser checks and human experience. Do not pool them into a single score.

## Story director

Review cause and effect, motivation, narrative clarity, emotional changes, the contribution of each playable operation and the necessity of new supplies. Challenge convenient failures and convenient solutions. Offer concrete cuts or rewrites. Revisit the draft after fresh-reader findings; do not treat the original outline as fixed.

## Sequential blind reader

Start without the authoring brief, intended twist, future chapters, recipe graph or previous answers. Read one supplied scene at a time. Before reading the next scene, record what the protagonist wants, what you expect to happen, your desire to continue (1–5, with reasons), clarity/motivation (1–5), and anything that feels forced. Reveal subsequent material only after recording that response. These ratings describe an agent's reaction, not a validated enjoyment scale. End with the strongest and weakest beats and a concrete counterexplanation for apparent success.

## Composition and authenticity editor

Judge each recipe forwards: what each input contributes, why someone here would try it, what actually changes, whether the result follows, and whether a physical or narrative premise is missing. Examine the recipe in its chapter and across reuse. Separate credible abstraction, explicit speculative-fiction premises and unsupported real-world claims. Use authoritative primary sources for disputed facts; avoid turning validation and handling details into filler steps. Propose removal where explanation cannot repair a weak operation.

## Player-only trial

Use only the supplied `npm run play` session's normal observations, readings, visible goals and partner hints. Do not read story source, authoring documents, recipe lists, checkpoints, other agents' answers or evaluator internals. Make predictions before meaningful discoveries, explain selected actions sparsely, and report actual confusion, optional curiosity and satisfied stopping. Completion is not a requirement. Supply the complete move schema and the criterion-map report format from the [player protocol](../../../README.md#logical-playtesting) before starting; coordinates are placement metadata, not a hidden puzzle. Report the experience criteria with concrete events where possible. Preserve the session until `npm run evaluate -- SESSION` has replayed it, then retain only concise conclusions and discard temporary artifacts.

## Technical and browser checks

Verify story lint and legal continuation before exposing a draft to fresh players. Use independent legal witnesses for coverage, never as player instructions. Check beginnings, busy intermediate states, important revelations and the ending at phone widths with real drags, ordinary advancement and saved-state restoration. Count newly supplied identities, meaningful reused objects, accepted combinations, placements, rejected attempts and administrative actions separately. Run the repository's required tests and production build. Record failures and limits alongside passes.
