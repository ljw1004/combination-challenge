# Austerity review — emergency artwork

4 November 2026. Fresh informed art-direction audit of all **23 `emergency-*.png` source images** in [survival/sources](../survival/sources/), viewed individually at source resolution and together. Read the [manuscript](../../../mars-v12/mars.md), [brief](../../../mars-v12/design-brief.md), both science consultations and [icon guide](../../../../../docs/icon-guide.md). This is component/provenance review, not blind recognition, phone-scale verification or hardware qualification. It incorporates the latest user direction and the coordinating agent's subsequent science/horticulture decisions. The manuscript and owning brief must adopt the resulting changes together.

**Verdict: replace the machinery family and strip unnecessary construction from the cave and garden before expanding the art.** The current pictures repeatedly convert “small recovered parts” into bespoke machines, furniture and fittings. A distressed finish does not make these things simple to fabricate. The game's educational purpose is better served by visibly understandable reuse and a few plain local parts.

## Finite material and equipment families

These are provenance constraints for the artist, not a new player inventory economy. Every added object must have an original mission job or be visibly made by a simple operation from the established stock. “Laboratory spares” alone is insufficient justification for a newly drawn assembly.

The real process distinction remains: magnetic concentration sorts mineral grains; solar heat plus electrical current enables iron extraction; divided water electrolysis supplies separate gases; methane production takes hydrogen and carbon dioxide. The compact adapted equipment, useful local iron and successful small vessel manufacture are bounded story premises. Removing ornament must not remove necessary separation, control, containment or service compatibility. Resolve those functions in the science consultation, not by drawing another unexplained box.

## Minimal art brief for the next pilot

Produce only a **plain gas canister, divided water cell and methane chamber** first. Review the delivered components before producing state sheets or scene variants.

1. Show quiet, useful forms in the established cream/fabric/red-landscape palette. Local iron is plain, serviceable and minimally joined. Slight handwork is visible; exaggerated dents, grime and rust are not substitutes for simplicity.
2. Canister: one plain formed body, one compact reclaimed service head, directly applied gas marking. No handle cage, analog gauge, brass wheel, plaque, foot ring or decorative collar. The small service head must be recognizable as carried precision hardware; the body must dominate.
3. Water cell: one plain locally formed divided body, two separate required outlets, necessary electrical contacts and compact allocated internals/control. Existing water bag and cable provide the two gameplay fittings. No table, IV stand, fabricated mounting feet, industrial flange ring or new reservoir. Use the engineering-approved setting and support; do not hide an outdoor-water problem inside a decorative enclosure.
4. Methane chamber: one plain local reaction body on simple ceramic rests, separate hydrogen/CO₂ connections, necessary product/water routes, and only the allocated small process/control parts. No oxygen feed, vented badge, brass trim, ornamental feet or supplied processor core.
5. For every visible component, the artist must state **original source, present function, simplest joint**. Delete anything without an answer. Required hidden functions must also have a finite source; hiding the machinery is not an austerity pass.
6. Approve the three actual images for component restraint and source credibility, then examine them at gameplay size. Expand the same parts into the furnace/gas/fuel/transfer scenes only after that review. Do not reinterpret an approved connector, body or control unit while generating the next sheet.

Keep the useful actions: extracting iron, making local vessels, separating gases, supplying the reaction, carrying distinct propellants, growing food and keeping it warm. Remove the manufacturing burden that currently disguises those actions. This review authorizes no claim that the present source art or integrated game has passed the replacement brief.
