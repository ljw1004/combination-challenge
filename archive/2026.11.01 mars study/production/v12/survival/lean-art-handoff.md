# Fuel artwork production notes

4 November 2026. Built-in imagegen supplied the fuel, controls, cold-flask and supper masters. The source author's inspections were production checks, not independent reviews. [Selection](selection.json) and [layout](layout.json) identify the current inputs and precise geometry; superseded approximate extraction bounds are not authoritative.

Generated source repairs added the processor's ceramic footing, reduced the controls to three selectors on a small plate, and used thin folding-handle cookware. Actual transparent cold-flask and food images were checked on light and dark grounds; hidden RGB colours under zero alpha did not justify modifying the generated alpha.

Mechanical extraction repaired clipped feeds and the winch hook, restored the methane neck in front of the industrial array, and kept original feet and pads. The same upright catch-rock pixels are reused at smaller opening/station scale. `prompts/lean-geometry-pixel-reuse.json` records exact donors, masks and destinations for both derivatives. No painting was synthesized during extraction.

Independent review subsequently rejected the right-hand pipe as an apparent oxygen output from the methane reactor. The revised feed approaches from offscreen right, physically separate from the reactor, in both fuel scenes. The former adjacent-service-block interpretation is superseded. A later review also rejected foreground plant movement between construction and transfer; the transfer export now reuses the completed plant geometry and changes only the lander support and transfer lines. The [current verification record](feedback-verification.md) and linked fresh reviews own the final outcomes.

Local cages and guards remain non-pressure structures around qualified supplied flasks. Reused forms do not establish cryogenic capacity, oxygen cleanliness, structural qualification or a complete ascent mass budget; those boundaries belong in the [design brief](../../../mars-v12/design-brief.md).
