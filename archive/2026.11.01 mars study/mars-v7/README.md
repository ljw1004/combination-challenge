The [completed full chapter campaign](../playtests/2026.11.03_00.41.35/report.md) followed all three director rounds and their revisions. It includes three fresh players and independent grading for every chapter, including every changed chapter. This is logical playtesting, with no visual or artwork work.

Structural checks complete **93/93 combinations**, with **128 item identities / 35 primitives** and **29 chapters including the ending**. Starting boards average **3.3 items**, maximum **7**. Lint has no errors and two reviewed mixed-fate warnings for the water line and lettuce trough. These counts are structural evidence.
