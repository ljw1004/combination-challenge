# Mars: A Place That Can Endure

A solo expedition to make a home, read the ice, and bring something extraordinary back.

> NOTE: Text-first v6 draft. Plausible future equipment, a surveyed site with accessible ice and usable ceramic feedstock, and the preserved organism are fictional premises. See design-brief.md for scientific boundaries. Each crop and core follows a single physical history. Material stocks represent repeated useful quantities without resource accounting.
