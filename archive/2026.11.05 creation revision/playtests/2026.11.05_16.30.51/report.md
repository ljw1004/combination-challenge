Play style: --fast

**Definitions**
- Worthwhile: counts actions supported by meaningful decision-making or a valued story payoff or reason to keep playing, counting each action once. Show the average credited actions / average total gameplay actions per player, not an average percentage.
- Fun: a narrower count of meaningful decisions: worthwhile deliberation or intentional experimentation. Measured by --detailed, including inspections and unsuccessful well-formed attempts; --fast reports it as not measured. Fast menu-assisted diagnostic ratios are not comparable.
- Hooks: the players' reported desire to continue: "high" means specific eagerness, "medium" means mild or conditional interest, and "low" means indifference or reluctance

## Chapter 11: Insects in the forest

Worthwhile: 3.7/4 worthwhile decisions (average across 3 players)

Fun: not measured (--fast)

Hook: medium

Strongest moments: Insects + Air → Winged insects was the shared standout: all three players valued learning that insect and bird wings evolved separately and selected this result as most worthwhile. Winged insects + First forest → Canopy life gave flight a concrete ecological purpose. Players 1 and 2 explicitly found this satisfying; Player 3 described a clear but predictable conclusion.

Weakest moments: Insects + Air compressed wing evolution into a pairing Player 1 found somewhat arbitrary and Player 2 called a broad shortcut. The explanation supplied value afterward without making the action a substantive inference. Winged insects + First forest was an obvious final placement in a prescribed sequence. Players 1 and 3 selected it as least worthwhile, although Player 1 also found it satisfying and Player 2 valued its ecological payoff.

## Chapter 12: Vertebrates move onto land

Worthwhile: 5.3/7 worthwhile decisions (average across 3 players)

Fun: not measured (--fast)

Hook: medium

Strongest moments: Limbed vertebrates + Membranes → Shelled egg combined a meaningful functional choice with an appreciated explanation of embryo protection, water retention and gas exchange. Players 1 and 2 named it most worthwhile; Player 3 also explicitly valued the explanation. Air-breathing fish + Roots → Limbed vertebrates gave all three a valued distinction between habitat and evolutionary cause despite being a sole-option action. Both still valued the resulting explanations; Player 1 did not identify these recipes as weaknesses. These are conceptual limitations, not interface friction.

## Chapter 20: Surviving an ice age

Worthwhile: 6/9 worthwhile decisions (average across 3 players)

Fun: not measured (--fast)

Hook: medium/high (2 medium, 1 high)

Strongest moments: Varied survivors at the advancing ice creates cold-adapted populations: all three players identify the variation-to-selection sequence as most worthwhile, and all graders credit the application as meaningful. The earlier creation of varied survivors also receives its own supported appreciation for establishing that differences precede severe cold. River migration reaching the oasis creates a sheltered refuge: all three records support a payoff from making migration concrete through water, food and cover. Connecting that refuge with cold-adapted populations adds explicit satisfaction for Player 1 and valued clarification for Player 3, but remains a less rewarding abstract summary for Player 2.

Weakest moments: Desert + Water creates an oasis without explanation: all three players identify this predictable, sole-option link as least engaging. The later sheltered-oasis explanation repairs its purpose but does not retroactively reward the initial creation. Sheltered oasis + Cold-adapted populations summarizes survival rather than depicting a direct interaction. All three notice the abstraction; Players 1 and 3 still value its payoff, whereas Player 2 emphasizes the diminishing choice in the forced finish. This is representational and menu-structure weakness, not recorded operational friction.
