## Continuity and scope

Variation was a required goal in c07 and is explicitly re-presented in every later chapter that uses it. Each contextual use preserves it. No new primitive, habitat token, preparation step or engine feature is introduced. Recipe count and chapter goals are unchanged. The island no longer has two fixed ingredients competing to be dragged: its population is movable and Variation is the receiver.

The marine coast and woodland are established by earlier required passages; the ice scene follows the recovered ecosystem. Volcanic island was optional in the earlier geology chapter, so the initial island scene must not imply that a particular populated island has already been made. The sea-channel composition establishes geographic separation while leaving the Island life creation meaningful.

Recipes remain global. A player who races ahead can use another Variation recipe at any of its fixed appearances. The current habitat illustrates where the process matters in the intended chapter; it is not an exclusive geographical gate. Result readings therefore describe the relevant lineage and process rather than falsely naming the currently visible ground as its sole origin.

## Scientific boundaries

- Arthropod ancestry belongs in marine environments, not a forest. Fossil research supports swimming and bottom-associated early arthropod forms while leaving details of their origins contested. The shallows are a visual simplification, not a claim for a particular tidepool as the birthplace. [Legg et al., 2012](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3497099/).
- The woodland margin represents opportunities and differing conditions for terrestrial lineages. Do not state that dry ground directly invented the amniotic egg or that the first amniotes necessarily laid shelled eggs: reproductive origins remain debated. Preserve the manuscript's limited claim about what such eggs allow. [Jiang et al., 2023](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41559-023-02074-0).
- Island birds can diverge through genetic processes and culturally transmitted songs. Keep both in the Different songs reading; the visual motif must not present all learned song differences as genetic mutations. [Grant and Grant, 2008](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2606742/).
- The foothill stage exposes differences already present; the advancing-ice operation represents selection over generations. Research on hare coat-colour variation illustrates how standing variation and gene flow can contribute to climate-related adaptation, but does not directly substantiate this fictional population's coat-thickness history. [Giska et al., 2019](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6883779/).

The habitat choices are authorial illustrations informed by these sources, not reconstructions of one documented historical sequence. Logical checks and later artwork reviews must be recorded separately.
