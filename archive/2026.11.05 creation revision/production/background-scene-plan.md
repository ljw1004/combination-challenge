# Creation background production inventory

Working production plan, 5 November 2026. The [single active brief](../../../stories/creation/design-brief.md) owns adopted intent; the [icon guide](../../../docs/icon-guide.md#register-changing-scenery) owns registration and review methods. This inventory maps the current [study manuscript](../creation.md) to selected paintings. Current verification outcomes are recorded separately; the inventory alone is not artwork acceptance.

The user's selected reference family is Ravilious and the [four-scene study](<../inspiration/eric ravilious - ideas in his style.png>), with more force in elemental waters, volcanic heat and ice. The [Pillars study](<../inspiration/eric ravilious - pillars of creation in his style.png>) supplies the opening's subject and atmosphere. The [Pluscarden reference](<../inspiration/pluscarden abbey.jpg>) informs the final composition within the same watercolour treatment. Opening, the two-combination campfire scene and ending may be full compositions; other playable backgrounds need uncluttered areas for movable icons. The current brief keeps the Moon exclusively in its movable icon: no background in this inventory includes a Moon.

## Opening state construction

Use one shared outer nebula painting. The selected production starts from the open cavity and generates a local pre-opening infill, then registers that change with nested extraction masks. The outer pillars and all pixels outside the changing region remain identical. The cavity's edge should describe displaced and thinned dust: swept rims, tapered wisps, oblique depth and uneven luminous edges. A perfect dark disk with a uniform boundary would look pasted on.

Use the opened view to register all three transparent world states. Initially the small centered sphere is mostly rocky, with modest glowing cracks. Creating Moon through the asteroid impact changes that same hotspot to a distorted molten world shifted about half the normal sphere’s diameter right and a quarter diameter down. Keep its silhouette clear of the lower-right cloud tendril. Applying Moon replaces it with the centered spherical molten world. Keep the same artwork anchor throughout, with a receiving rectangle covering both visible positions. None of these states has an axis, loop or ring. Moon stays a movable icon. The exact crops, shared padding and scene-derived cloud occlusion are recorded in scene-state-layout.json.

The actual opening permits multiple orders: Stars and Asteroids come from the deep; the Sun may be made before the world; Light can be made before or after stabilization. Do not bake all these movable creations into every successive state. The backdrop and registered world/cavity layers must work in the legal partial states, leaving the surrounding deep, cavity and current world separately receivable. Keep the Moon out of every background and world state, and avoid introducing oceans or green continents before chapter 2's cooling.

## Cave continuity and changing light

Choose a small set of physical anchors in the first deep-interior painting: one distinctive rock seam, the dry floor and the painting-wall plane. Keep those anchors in all four interior states. The chamber is enclosed: no entrance, outside view or daylight. Torch/firelight supplies the illumination that reveals the art. Show the animals first, then add stars without replacing or moving the animal. An extant animal belongs here, not a resurrected giant browser or hybrid beast.

Generate full scenes in sequence before extracting anything. Firelight changes broad surfaces, so a tiny cutout around the flame will not suffice. Test either a registered complete-state layer with a smaller independent receiving hitbox, or a carefully bounded opaque cave replacement that covers every changed wall and floor surface. Use the existing renderer and the icon guide's methods; select the simpler artifact structure after checking actual display scale. A transparent receiving rectangle alone cannot enact lighting or painting changes.

The exterior returns to the same coastal opening established in the earlier landscape paintings. Preserve its banded pale ochre rock, broad arched dry upper shelter and darker sea-level recess, with the gathering's campfire on dry ground. The interior can share those rock bands and pale seams without showing its entrance. Its completed paintings remain deeper inside and need not be visible from outside. The waterline must remain safely below the inhabited shelf; the scene must not replace the coastal cave with an unrelated mountain cavern.

## Variation and other receiving regions

Variation receivers are marine shallows in chapter 11, the wet-to-dry woodland margin in chapter 12, separated coast in chapter 16 and vegetated foothills in chapter 20. Their shared pencil DNA emblem identifies the recurring concept while the actual painted habitat is the receiver; follow the current brief and Variation production metadata. Final positions depend on the paintings; the current manuscript's percentages are not artwork constraints.

The ice's Variation region must remain available before Advancing ice exists, and the glacier replacement must not cover it. Do not depict cold as creating a deliberately useful mutation. The island scene similarly establishes separation without pre-populating a colony that the player still has to make. Other than these requested concept contexts, preserve the existing distinction between movable large symbols and actual landscape receivers.

## Checks before scene acceptance

- Compare the same-landscape families together: creation/world; coast/cave; fern/flowering/new forests; inhabited/impact/regrowth/ice/retreat; cave interior/exterior.
- Check every changing state in its actual composite and in legal partial orders. Inspect hidden prerequisites as well as finished beauty: no painted animal before painting, no low advancing ice before its creation, no Moon in any background, and no prematurely inhabited island.
- At playing size, confirm the landscape receivers are reachable and distinct, state transitions replace the same physical place, and ordinary scenes leave useful icon space without a detached blank apron.
- Inspect portrait crops, name badges, icon contrast, actual drags and reloads in the browser. The full-frame opening and ending are deliberate exceptions to the ordinary quiet-foreground composition.
- Keep selected masters, exact requests and registration records in this production directory. Keep trial sheets, captures and individual reviewer packets under ignored `runs/`; retain concise independent review outcomes with their version and coverage.
