# Settlement design brief

This records the story’s established visual and geographic intent for future edits. Shared interaction behaviour belongs in [UX](../../docs/ux.md), and reusable illustration methods in the [icon guide](../../docs/icon-guide.md).

Use rough, rustic coloured woodcuts with generous natural blank space in the backgrounds. Keep the opening orchard, cooking clearing, shelter and house on the home bank; the footbridge crosses away from that ground. Preserve this geography so later visits remain coherent.

Soften every background with **22% warm cream (`#fffaf0`)**, baked into the artwork once. Preserve the woodcut detail without blur or additional colour grading, and keep item and hotspot artwork at its original strength. This gives the icons more separation from the scenery while retaining the integrated landscape and Water's opening position in the river. Apply the treatment to untreated source artwork; never fade an already finished background again. The [UX artwork contract](../../docs/ux.md#item-artwork) governs rendering.

The catalogue icon pairs a timber cottage with a complete apple tree standing in front of it. Show the tree's trunk rooted in the foreground and keep its branches visibly separate from the roof; the tree must not appear to grow out of the house.
