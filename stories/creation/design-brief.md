# Creation

## Intent

Play a creator beginning with Earth, Air, Fire and Water. The scale is stars, oceans, continents, living worlds and minds. The pleasure is making something astonishing and discovering what it does next. The dramatic question is **Can you make a world that has a future of its own?**

Creation is an original elemental myth informed by natural processes, not a retelling of a religious text or a literal account of cosmology. Earth is stone, Air is wind, Fire is flame and Water is water: their pictures and ordinary descriptions must support the combinations. They are the four elemental sources; technical item identities may distinguish their presentations. The elements may already exist when play begins. The opening is one creation scene with all four elements present: boundless Waters of the deep is a receiving landscape, while Earth, Air and Fire are movable. Air opens a Void in the waters. Within that opening, Earth gathers into a molten world and Fire into a Sun. In the deep, Earth and Fire scatter into wandering bodies and stars; a great wandering body striking the world makes its Moon. The Sun remains movable, and shining it upon the world makes Light. Subsequent water cools the world, raises a steam cloud and returns as rain. This is an invented setting, not an interpretation or adaptation of a particular scripture. Invent a mythology that follows from those tangible elements; do not disguise an atomic or stellar history by relabelling them as matter, motion, energy and change. A combination can compress immense distances, many generations or a whole natural process. Its reading must distinguish that imaginative act from any factual claim it teaches. Life's origin and consciousness must not be presented as solved recipes.

The audience is a curious older child or adult. After the invented cosmogony reaches a concrete planet, preserve recognizable relationships and approximate historical realism through the ages of the Earth. That transition lets familiar natural history carry the later combinations; the myth need not keep inventing its own rules. Offer useful ideas about water and rock cycles, ecosystems, disturbance, adaptation, shared knowledge and independence. Keep the causal contribution of both ingredients visible. No construction chores, resource accounting or settlement progression. Human activity matters at the end as evidence of an independent world; it does not become a technology tree.

## Voice and arc

Address the creator in plain, clear English. The game is figuring out combinations, not deciphering enigmatic goals or poetic hints. State what the player is trying to make or change; explain what each result is and how it arose. Let scale, physical consequences, pictures and discoveries supply wonder. Remove gnomic pronouncements, vague promises, personified natural processes and praise from the narrator. Do not hide necessary information to manufacture mystery. Avoid a punitive deity, doctrinal claims and a foreordained march toward humans. Extinction and suffering matter; restoration cannot undo a lost lineage. Cooperation and resilience should be enacted through combinations and explained directly.

Move from unruly elements to a sky full of lights, a restless world and ocean, then life that changes its own conditions, ecosystems with competing needs, catastrophe and renewal, and finally creatures that remember, question and create. The ending fulfills rather than cancels the player's creative power: a world carries that power onward.

Establish the world's axial tilt during the initial creation, so the later seasons have an earlier cause. The glancing impact tips this world's rotation; its new Moon then steadies the world. Represent this as a changed landscape world in chapter one, keeping that introductory scene grounded in things. The Moon moderates changes in an existing tilt rather than creating it or fixing its angle forever. Introduce the separate Steady axial tilt symbol when the later seasons chapter uses it with the Sun. As with the movable world and water, this later technical primitive represents something already established in the fiction. Use the concrete names Stars and Asteroids throughout. In the water cycle, let sediment and the river's transport build the delta, with slowing flow and deposition stated in the result.

Fire entering the waters of the deep makes stars. This is an accepted mythic gesture whose grandeur and material character should be expressed through the artwork. The primordial waters can suggest a dense cosmic nebula without claiming literal astrophysics. Keep human prose spare once the images communicate the setting. Supply text-only evaluations with separate visual context when needed, identifying provisional imagery as an authored premise; do not make the human story carry extra explanation solely for readers who cannot see its pictures.

Aim for about 200 substantial combinations. Long, connected ancestry is a defining pleasure: an early creation can matter again far into the story. Preserve that scale and cumulative wonder. Comparisons with other stories describe structure; shorter chains are not a quality target. Remove an action only when its own contribution or payoff is weak. Follow a few recurring threads across chapters: light, water, stone, life, memory. Chapters should usually bring 4–8 symbols into focus, with at most two visible goal chains. The cave chapter deliberately has three independent strands—painting, song and teaching—visible from arrival, so no strand implies a prerequisite for another. Preserve optional discoveries when they supply real wonder or a useful contrast. Wonders accompany discoveries that need interpretation or change the story; simple intermediate actions need not interrupt play.

Treat the icons as representations of phenomena, populations and capabilities. Reusing an ancestral population does not recreate a particular dead individual. Major changes still have real consequences: the great browsing lineage ends, its traces remain, and later communities are different. Establish small mammal ancestors before the impact; keep insects, birds and mammals on distinct branches. Introduce inherited variation with early reproduction and reuse it in later divergence.

Let the player race ahead by combining. Chapter order is a narrative invitation, not an era lock. Use actual causal dependencies where an event needs something to exist, and brief `[If skipped]` passages where skipping a chapter would otherwise lose an essential transition. Large circles signify pivotal creations and remain movable. Fix an item only when its landscape or apparatus supplies a meaningful receiving context. Such places may naturally gate an operation; they must first be created from the four elements through actual recipes. Island life can receive Variation where separated birds live; Advancing ice can receive already varied survivors where cold changes which descendants persist. Keep these places present after their result appears. The sea cave is hollowed by waves long before its later visitors make pictures and music there; preserve its geography and dry upper walls across those appearances. Its later receiving surface belongs to the actual painted landscape.

Move postglacial forest recovery before the learning, language and music chapters. Name its result **New forests**: these provide food, shelter and living space for the later communities. Spreading trees are no longer the final achievement. The final acts are a community testing a prediction against the shore, painting a remembered animal and adding stars, learning a song from one another, and bringing their shared singing together with wonder. **Shared song + Wonder → Song of creation** is the culmination, as an ordinary pair of movable icons. Keep each musical step distinct: recalling a sound, joining voices, and expressing wonder at the world. Learning from others happens within Shared song. Keep **Shared discovery + Story → Teaching** as a goal of the preceding chapter, alongside painting and making a song: passing on understanding is a substantial achievement in its own right. Do not make it another prerequisite token for the final song, and remove New question and Remembered song as separate preparation steps.

The last playable chapter is **A day of rest**. Painting, making a song and teaching belong to the preceding chapter, where the work is done. The already-created **Social group** becomes the gathered tribe in the final landscape. Drag Song onto the people to make Shared song; then combine the Shared song and Wonder icons anywhere on the board. The second action has no hotspot requirement. These are two simple gestures, not a difficult final puzzle. The gathering holds the creation of Shared song until the last chapter, so the culmination still happens there. The completed paintings remain part of the tribe's surroundings, while the final artwork evokes the scale of Song of creation.

The song embodies the singers' understanding of creation and their place within it. That understanding is limited and can develop as they learn more; the player was present at the beginning and knows what happened. Leave that difference implicit in the ending. Do not describe the singers as inventing an account, add a separate creation-story ingredient, unpack their beliefs or certify an answer. Their version of a song of creation is enough. Keep the final reading short and let the final artwork carry the scale.

Use Memory directly once in the song branch: Echo plus Memory makes a repeatable song. Shared song also inherits Memory through Social group's learned warnings. The investigation and paintings have their own remembered experience; they remain worthwhile earlier creations without every one becoming a literal ingredient in the final song. The ending needs no abstract certificate of perfection or independence.

## Visual intent

Develop artwork only after the story and combinations survive repeated logical reviews. Use circular symbolic icons throughout; they are windows onto phenomena, not literal objects on a workbench. Choose and pilot a coherent visual language suited to cosmic and ecological scale. Backgrounds should show the world's progression while leaving calm working space for the symbols. The same planet, moon and principal coastline must remain recognizable through later states. No buildings or settlements as the dominant image.

The [authoring guide](../../docs/story-authoring.md), [evaluation guide](../../docs/fun-and-evaluation.md) and [icon guide](../../docs/icon-guide.md) govern development. This single active brief governs the manuscript and any subsequent study iterations.

### Adopted visual language: the painted celestial atlas

Use luminous opaque gouache with selective fine ink contours: lapis-blue grounds, jade and turquoise living forms, ochre minerals, restrained coral energy and warm ivory light. Subjects have strong silhouettes and a few identifying structures; fine paint texture supplies pleasure at larger sizes without carrying essential distinctions. Narrow ivory-gold rims unify the circular windows. Avoid glossy game coins, generic flat glyphs, photorealism, modern laboratory props and literal religious personifications.

Every inventory image is a symbolic illustrated token, including populations, places and mental capacities. Backgrounds show a world at the current stage; dragging a Moon or a song is a creative gesture, not a physical object installation. Pivotal creations use the same visual language at a larger size without becoming fixed. Standard exports are 256 square pixels (64px at the 390px reference viewport); larger exports are 384 pixels (96px). The primordial waters, opened void and molten ground are actual landscape receivers during creation. All three remain in that scene. Ordinary Water and a large movable Rocky world are separate primitive identities introduced in chapter two: the player understands them as the same water and world, now represented at a different scale. Both world identities display the name Rocky world and retain recognizable geography. No conversion operation or engine unfixing feature is needed. Cooling the movable world transforms it into Stone crust. The cave's fixed receiving surface is registered to its painted dry inner wall, with room for two ingredients and their ordinary name badges on short phone browsers. Sea cave, Lit cave, Painted cave and Painted cave with stars are successive states of the entire cave, with one landscape hotspot replacing its predecessor at the same centre. None is a small fixed picture icon. Pigment plus Memory at the lit cave is the story's only two-ingredient contextual operation. Adding Stars to the animal painting is a single-ingredient hotspot action; preserve the animal as the stars appear. Remove the imagined beast and the separate sky-painting branch. The completed wall remains visible during the day of rest. Do not add a permanent second legend or labels inside pictures.

Variation uses a warm-ivory naturalist's lineage diagram, with turquoise related cells, lapis branching connectors and a changed coral/ochre inherited mark. Its different visual treatment identifies a continuing process. Echo that branching motif in related birds and mammals at its landscape contexts, without implying that an environment deliberately creates a needed mutation. Those appearances are integrated scenery, with ordinary hotspot feedback where interactive.

Recover existing paintings where possible. Use exact, centered circular frames and opaque interiors; remove neighboring atlas fragments and uneven hand-painted perimeter artifacts. New paintings use full-bleed square sources with their essential subjects inside the eventual circle, followed by deterministic cropping and framing. The production record states any accepted source-aspect correction or edge coverage.

Related states must differ in structure: dividing cells have a constricted waist, partnered cells contain distinct smaller organisms; a first fern forest, fallen trunks and open pioneer woodland are different communities, not recolours. Celestial pictures use different mass/arrangement, and biological wings retain insect, bird and mammal anatomy. Songs use a human voice and a painted sound ribbon; shared singing joins several voices. The final Song of creation brings the world's earlier creations into the musical image. These abstract conventions depend partly on the game's names and readings. Depict people in warm varied earth tones with simplified faces; do not make one real-world culture the destination of creation.

The final landscape should evoke Song of creation, drawing the earlier sky, waters, land and living world into one composition around human song. The supplied Pluscarden embroidery is a reference for linked scenes, braided visual continuity and scale. Develop an original composition within the painted celestial atlas style; do not copy its panels, lettering or doctrinal figures. Artwork stays paused while these combinations are being tested.

The recurring coast has a hooked ochre headland, one arched wave-cut cave, teal water and a pale dry shelf and upper wall inside. The paired-crater pale Moon and non-Earth world geography remain recognizable. Preserve those landmarks through forest, loss and recovery. Great browsers are long-necked sauropod-like animals; their tracks pair broad oval hind prints with smaller D-shaped foreprints. Their lineage never returns alive after the impact. The cave's later art shows an extant animal and stars, not a hybrid beast or human memories of extinct giants.

For the late human scenes, move the view into a sheltered cave with a recognizable opening and dry interior walls, rather than leaving the paintings on an austere exposed coastal cliff. The earlier cave-making history remains; the new view need not foreground the sea. Bring Fire into the Sea cave to replace it with Lit cave, then replace that with Painted cave and finally Painted cave with stars. Every state can receive a Call to make Echo, so painting and song remain independent. Exactly one cave hotspot is present at a time; do not retain a separate acoustic cave alongside the changing painting. On the day of rest, show the tribe around a campfire in front of that same recognizable opening, with the painting visible behind them as part of their shared culture. Social group is the actual gathering in this landscape, not a fixed circular token. The new artwork must not be treated as already produced or reviewed.

Paint dramatic landmarks mainly in the upper part of portrait backgrounds and leave a calm, medium-light working field below. Calm space must be part of the artwork, not a CSS tint. Use 780×1688 runtime backgrounds, preserving painted proportions when extracting atlases. Images can compress scale poetically; anatomy, lineage, dry painting surfaces and the story's before/after states remain coherent.
