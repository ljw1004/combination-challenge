# No Fixed Address

**Development status, 5 November 2026:** human play feedback found this version difficult because many small icons represented subtle distinctions. Its approach is set aside for the [active reconsideration](../../projects/cyberpunk-study/README.md). The sections below describe the published version, not constraints on the new concepts: its plot, AI curriculum, item count and circular-token direction are all open to replacement. Proposed alternatives remain in the study until a new direction is adopted.

## Purpose and player promise

A cyberpunk combination story about repairing a city that has decided your neighborhood is unprofitable. The player is **June**, a repairer in **Latch**, a densely inhabited district beneath the Meridian company's elevated transit line. **Moth** is a discarded municipal assistant running on a small, visibly repaired orange service terminal. **Eli**, June's brother, works the neighborhood clinic night shift. People speak in introductions and discoveries; people are never inventory ingredients.

The emotional question is whether a place can remain a home when its essential services become revocable subscriptions. The practical question is what it takes to make a useful AI accountable, limited and repairable. Intelligence is valuable when it helps people act and can be corrected; neither a confident answer nor a sympathetic voice establishes truth, authority or consciousness. The story must also enjoy invention, music, warm food and beautiful broken things. Political meaning should emerge through work and consequences, not speeches after every gesture.

Target curious teenagers and adults. Assume no coding background. Teach transferable distinctions through action: model versus evidence, missing data versus absence, training versus testing, source text versus instructions, signatures versus truth, authorization versus capability, prediction versus policy, redundancy versus independence, and shared maintenance versus a heroic one-time fix. Use concrete devices and circumstances rather than a parade of abstract claim tokens.

## Scope and development

The story has 219 authored combinations in 24 playable chapters plus an ending; the reference completion route takes 171 combinations. Many alternatives accept sensible assembly orders rather than adding required gestures. Keep varied short constructions, investigation, physical repair, optional pleasures and later payoffs. Count is never a reason to keep filler. Accept independent assembly operations in either order where the fiction permits, converging to one final device. Two independent working threads are an available rhythm, not a template; vary chapter length and structure according to the work. Use at most two simultaneous fixed targets and ordinary two-input combinations primarily; no invented resource accounting or timed failure. Narrated clock pressure is dramatic, never a hidden mechanical timer.

## Story spine

Meridian's forthcoming dawn migration expires Latch's delivery and utility credentials because its payment records show too little revenue. This is an intentional commercial rule, not an AI spontaneously becoming evil. The assistant first reveals the omission, then proves fallible too. June and neighbors secure immediate needs, establish the exclusion, and build a modest local alternative using equipment they own or are authorized to service. They cannot repair the entire city in a night. No magic hack defeats corporate power; local power, cooling, communications, human agreements and maintenance all matter. The ending earns one district's continued operation and an uncertain connection to its neighbors.

Four movements of six chapters:

1. **Find the hole.** A rejected delivery address; recover Moth from municipal e-waste; restore low-voltage workshop/market lighting; map an accessible path; discover omitted requests; make the assistant listen to actual local evidence. Primary payoff: an address can be missing from a database while a neighborhood is plainly alive.
2. **Make the claim answerable.** Clinic cooling and reserve power; test recommendations on unseen local cases; reveal the distribution policy behind ostensibly neutral routes; inspect the migration notice; demonstrate a locally operated door/network. End with three durable handoff identities: `grounded-moth` (Moth with source display), `local-network` (a portable local network controller), `expiry-proof` (the authenticated migration notice and affected-service list). All three are established by required work in this half and therefore exist before the second half.
3. **Build a way to stay.** Establish the neighborhood's control over its own equipment; reject a proposed dependency on recording private conversations; build a consent-based request/appeal route; prepare independent communications; restore the pump service with competent local operators; rehearse loss of the corporate uplink. The practical work changes the stakes; no new villain or unrelated disaster.
4. **Keep it alive.** Connect an adjacent block without recreating Meridian's gatekeeping; resist instructions hidden in a supplied maintenance document; give maintenance to more than one person; let Moth express uncertainty about its own continuity without deciding real-world machine consciousness; survive the scheduled credential expiry; reopen the workshop in daylight. Recall specific objects and people from the opening. End with tomorrow's work, warm food and a repaired place, not total revolution.

Working chapter titles (authors may improve locally while preserving movement): Customer Not Found; A Voice in the Parts Bin; Lights Below the Map; The Long Way Home; A Map with a Hole; What the Machine Heard; The Blue Room; Borrowed Minutes; Test It on a Tuesday; Who Gets the Last Van?; The Kindly Update; A Door That Still Opens; Read the Small Print; The Price of Silence; Speak for Yourself; Offline Doesn't Mean Alone; The Pump Room; The Unplugging; The Other Side of the Fence; A Poisoned Gift; The Night Shift; A Name of Its Own; Dawn Without Permission; Open Tomorrow.

## Voice, causality and allowances

Present-tense first-person June, economical, dryly funny, attentive to materials and people. Introductions normally one or two sentences. Created-item discoveries normally one to three sentences; reserve longer ones for earned reversals. Dialogue sounds like people at work. Moth's practical voice may be funny and warm, but it cannot make physical changes or grant rights by saying so. Human decisions remain human decisions. No generic hacker skulls, omniscient AI, kidnapped human minds, miracle microchips, instant model training or claim that retrieval alone guarantees truth.

Use existing repair equipment, donor appliances, compatible modules, authenticated records and voluntary local contributions with stated provenance. Work is a fiction and complete operations abstract routine assembly and elapsed time. Mains, refrigeration, potable-water and clinical systems are handled through isolated service modules and qualified operators; do not turn the game into hazardous step-by-step practical advice. The clinic story concerns reliable cooling and delivery, not diagnosis or medication instructions. Official signatures establish who issued a statement, not that it is fair or true. Holdout cases must remain distinct from tuning examples. New batteries store existing energy; a mesh is not itself a power source or an internet connection.

Every item identity must remain physically and narratively consistent. Future primitives stay in their own chapter. All global recipes must be safe if performed whenever their ingredients are available. Avoid hotspots for portable records and no unnecessary place/collect bureaucracy. Important cross-chapter carry is explicit through required earlier goals. Optional work must remain genuinely optional and must not appear completed later if skipped. Each investigation must change the actual job; successive discoveries cannot merely restate exclusion. Seed later pressures before their resolution, and carry specific residents' ordinary projects through the story so its warmth is earned.

## Visual language

**Iconic presentation:** circular tokens represent objects, classes of supplies, records, capabilities and states of larger systems. They are a visual notation for the work, not literal equipment floating through a painted room. Backgrounds establish place and mood; their depicted furniture is decorative, not an undisclosed interaction target. Equipment states use ordinary draggable tokens. The fixed dawn migration monitor gives the two post-migration service checks their actual time and place; it is a stable circular hotspot, never a changing installed assembly. There are no registered scene replacements, transparent scenery hotspots or compound assembly overlays. This choice supports readable, flexible combinations while retaining causal continuity in the story. Later appearances of an important system must retain its defining features; symbolic presentation does not excuse missing prerequisites or contradictory outcomes.

**Night-shift printshop:** circular repair-sticker icons, strongly drawn silhouettes, inky aubergine/navy grounds, cyan light, warm amber practical lamps, restrained vermilion, visible screenprint/risograph grain. Equipment looks used, specific and fixable. Moth retains the same orange terminal, chipped corner, tiny antenna and simple monochrome display throughout its states. Records visibly differ by their physical form and diagram; exact semantic content still uses names/readings.

Atmospheric portrait backgrounds show the workshop, covered market/underpass, clinic service room, records alcove, pump room and rooftops, with two consistent dawn variants and a title cover. Leave a quiet lower working area for movable circular icons. Movement from wet midnight to muted dawn carries progress. The 202 drawings cover 331 item identities: neutral hardware and record forms can be shared when their exact configuration, evidence or test outcome is supplied by names/readings; visible fitted components must persist. Portable circles are authored at 256×256 for a nominal 64px display, and the fixed migration monitor at 384×384 for 96px. Transparent margins surround opaque navy grounds and cream rims. Background exports are 780×1688. Circular graphics are the user's explicit requirement.

Recognition is contextual. The images do not independently establish battery charge, thermal contents, authorization, a passed test or a specific data finding. Use the existing item names and discoveries for those distinctions; do not imply that the AI recognition checks establish human recognition speed, enjoyment or learning.
