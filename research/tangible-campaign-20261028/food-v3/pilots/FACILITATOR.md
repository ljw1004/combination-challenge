# Five bounded, uncoached v3 pilot segments

These are **prepared**, not already independent human trials. No pleasure, recognition, accessibility or instruction-free success is claimed. `pilot-player.ts` uses the exact candidate runtime and actual canonical-prefix checkpoints. It does not replace missing creations with caches. Its automatic next-stage action models the controller transition, not the browser's animation or pointer behavior.

Give a participant only `PLAYER-INSTRUCTIONS.txt`, a segment name and the command output. Do not expose this file or the graph. For a model-agent pilot, record that it is a model-agent logical pilot, not a human playtest. A full-history agent already aware of the graph is not fresh.

| Segment | Starts at | Stops | Question |
|---|---|---|---|
| `pastry-supper` | Pastry workshop, chapter 7 | After saucepan supper, before sugar kitchen | Does the earlier butter/flour work help the cook discover a different hot-pan use, or does placing the receiving token merely create friction? |
| `cake-travel` | Station early shift, chapter 19 | Actual Twinkie ending | Does the crew lunch and moulded batch prepare a useful finale rather than another display-cake quota? |
| `provisions-to-train` | Preserving works, chapter 18 | Actual Twinkie ending | Does the sealed provision have a recognisable later purpose? Does the batch carry naturally into filling and wrapping? Is the 15-object station arrival clear? |
| `custard-chocolate` | Custard kitchen, chapter 11 | Before quick bread, chapter 15 | Which transformations change expectations, and does the participant want to continue after this dessert section? No assumption that 42 steps are either enjoyable or excessive. |
| `orchard-inn` | Cider maker's lunch, chapter 3 | After rainy-inn supper | Do juice, bread, butter and toast feel genuinely useful across a change of predicament? Is vinegar an intelligible change of purpose, not a spoiled drink? |

Record the full `pilots/sessions/SESSION.json`, initial view, attempted actions (including rejected directions), hint/inspection requests, backtracks and free-form feedback. Do not force optional recipe coverage or require a guessed combination to match the author's witness. The intended stopping conditions are the end of the selected segment, voluntary withdrawal, or a blocker that the existing interface cannot resolve without author coaching.

Afterward ask only:
1. What changed between the two kitchens?
2. Which earlier-made thing helped you, and why?
3. Which step felt least necessary or most surprising?
4. Would you want another chapter right now?

**Keep** a transition only if the participant can explain its useful connection without the facilitator supplying it. **Revise** if the idea lands but placement/wording obscures it. **Shorten or remove** a repetitive chapter rather than adding a reward token or a quota. Compare the long candidate with the preserved short starter; do not retire the starter merely because the long route is solvable.
