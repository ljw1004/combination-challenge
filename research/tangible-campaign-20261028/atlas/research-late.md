## Best usable findings

1. **Medieval optics really does flow into Kepler.** His 1604 optics work explicitly supplements Witelo. In 1611 he explained lens combinations after Galileo's telescopic discoveries. Reusing an earlier ray diagram, translated optics page and image-plane construction is not metaphorical glue. Practice and theory influenced one another; avoid “the theory inevitably produced the telescope.”
2. **Printing and anatomy genuinely meet in a particular object.** Vesalius sent anatomical woodblocks from Venice to Basel in 1542; he helped correct the 1543 Fabrica there. A player-created corrected proof, figure key, woodblock and anatomical discrepancy can jointly produce an inspectable illustrated edition. Do not imply perspective alone caused anatomy.
3. **The strongest later braid is geology + political economy + natural history.** Lyell supplied geological perspectives; Malthus's population argument was an identifiable stimulus to Darwin. Wallace must appear as an independent contributor. Lyell initially opposed transmutation: an inherited work can be used against its author's conclusions.
4. **Formal logic and electrical machinery have an unusually literal reunion.** Boole's algebra mapped onto open/closed relay circuits in Shannon's work. This is ideal reuse of an early logic output after many chapters, but the intervening Boolean algebra step is necessary: Greek syllogisms must not magically become digital circuits.
5. **Snow is an evidence-combination chapter, not a map epiphany.** Household records + water-supplier identification + death counts + denominators yield a comparison. The suppliers' customers were intermixed; the circumstance was not a randomized experiment. A diagram alone does not show causation or demonstrate that pump-handle removal ended the outbreak.

## Source cards, checked

### optics-kepler: historical transmission, strong
- [Museo Galileo: Kepler's Optics](https://brunelleschi.imss.fi.it/genscheda.asp?appl=MAM&chiave=900026&lingua=ENG&xsl=sezione)
- [University of Oklahoma primary-book exhibit: Dioptrice, 1611](https://galileo.ou.edu/exhibits/optics-lenses.html)

Supports the explicit Witelo → 1604 Kepler relationship and the later lens/telescope work. Telescope configurations involve tradeoffs, not a universally better instrument. Use dated chapters 1604–1611 after medieval Latin optics.

### anatomy-print: historically specific cross-craft production, strong
- [Washington University Becker Medical Library: Vesalius in Italy and writing the Fabrica](https://beckerexhibits.wustl.edu/legacy-exhibits/vesalius/italy.html)
- [Metropolitan Museum: Fabrica object](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/358129)

The book united dissection, textual correction, artists, woodcutters and printing. The Met object is the **1555** edition; do not label its exact object as 1543. Becker documents the first edition's publication and movement of its woodblocks. The abridged Epitome also matters: circulation depended on format, cost and readership.

### probability-letters: primary text, strong
- [Fermat and Pascal on probability, 1654 letters, University of York transcription](https://www.york.ac.uk/depts/maths/histstat/pascal.htm)
- [Royal Holloway: Fermat correspondence guide](https://www.gtfp.cs.rhul.ac.uk/pulskamp/Fermat/fermat.html)

The interrupted-game division was not invented in 1654; earlier authors discussed it. The correspondence includes disagreement, misunderstandings, correction and different successful methods. Preserve that structure. A fair split follows from an explicitly fair toy game model, not from a generic “probability” token. An enumerated outcome tree can later feed statistical or communication exercises; that latter reuse is pedagogical, not proof of a direct intellectual debt by every later figure.

### lyell-darwin: documented influence and disagreement, strong
- [University of Edinburgh: Charles Lyell](https://lyell.ed.ac.uk/about)
- [Darwin Correspondence Project: Charles Lyell](https://darwin-editorial.darwinproject.ac.uk/charles-lyell)
- [Darwin Correspondence Project: writing Origin](https://darwinproject.ac.uk/writing-origin)

Lyell influenced Darwin's geological outlook; Darwin also persuaded Lyell on specific geology. Their relationship was not one-way acceptance: Lyell remained troubled by human origins. Retain a “contested explanation” output rather than equating publication with universal acceptance.

### malthus-darwin-wallace: actual cross-domain synthesis, strong
- [University of Utah rare-book exhibition: Evolution of Darwin](https://www.lib.utah.edu/collections/rarebooks/exhibits/past/evolutionofdarwin.php)
- [Darwin Correspondence Project: Origin](https://darwin-editorial.darwinproject.ac.uk/letters/darwins-works-letters/origin)

Utah documents Malthus's population writing among Darwin's influences and Lyell's book on the voyage. Darwin Correspondence documents Wallace's 1858 essay and the joint presentation. Do not reuse the exhibition's quotation uncritically as Darwin's own exact wording without tracing its original; paraphrase the influence. A population-pressure mechanism is not a moral endorsement of poverty, hierarchy or competition.

### snow-households: primary historical evidence hosted by university, strong
- [UCLA: the Grand Experiment](https://epi-snow.ph.ucla.edu/Stream3_GrandExperiment_d.html)

Contains quotations from Snow's 1855 book and the water-company comparisons. Household supplies could differ along the same street. Supplier identity was checked with receipts and water evidence; comparing counts requires denominators. Keep medical-treatment content out: this is historical inference. The page explicitly says there is no indication Snow and Faraday associated; their shared concern for the Thames must **not** become an invented direct influence edge.

### shannon-switches: primary thesis record, strong concept; date caution
- [MIT repository: A symbolic analysis of relay and switching circuits](https://dspace.mit.edu/entities/publication/2dffdeb7-2862-4575-8a8e-b29fb59319e5)
- [MIT-hosted history thesis via NYU: Claude Shannon](https://cs.nyu.edu/~amos/download/images/f/f3/Guizzo_ClaudeShannon.pdf)

The conceptual connection is Boolean logic ↔ switches; the secondary historical thesis supplies narrative detail. MIT catalogue reports **1940** for this record, whereas standard histories date thesis submission to 1937 and the publication to 1938. Label a chapter “1930s–1940s” unless the parent verifies the actual document title/date page. Avoid exclusive invention claims: parallel switching-theory work existed.

### ostrom: primary retrospective, strong but no forced Shannon genealogy
- [Ostrom Nobel lecture, 2009: Beyond Markets and States](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/economic-sciences/2009/ostrom/lecture/)

Useful final return to institutions and collective decisions. The chapter should compare cases and revise a rule under monitoring rather than create “perfect governance.” This is not the inevitable endpoint of earlier mathematics or a documented direct inheritance from Shannon. The lecture's title explicitly resists the markets/states binary.

## Structure recommendation

Prefer a **single chronological route with recurring threads**, not rigid alternation. Make printing, geometry, counts, argument and observation reappear when the history gives them real work. Do not alternate science/humanities merely to alternate: it risks the old disconnected anthology with a new pattern. A chapter can reconnect two branches after a long interval; that is the desirable surprise. Explicit dates and place labels do chronology work without lengthy transition prose.
