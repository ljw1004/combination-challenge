# Atlas of Questions: connected-history research candidate

## Useful result

**A new, executable 258-combination campaign across 26 dated historical chapters and three explicitly speculative future chapters**, beginning before Athens and continuing through a self-questioning observatory, a choral memory garden and an evolving metabolic city. Every one of the 28 later chapters must actually use **at least two earlier player-created outputs**. The same tokens return from older areas; no prerequisite creations are silently supplied in caches. The engine, narrow A+B interaction and current-scene carry policy are unchanged.

This is a **research candidate, not a published replacement or a human fun result**. Use `scenario.json`; do not copy `compat-worktree` into production. The new ID is `atlas-connected-questions-v1-research`. Existing Atlas identities, source, saves and direct routes remain untouched.

### The first coherent playable arc

`opening-arc.json` is a separately playable **43-combination / five-chapter** slice with the same beginning, not a checkpoint with invented prerequisites:

1. **Babylon, c. 700–500 BCE:** marks + quantities + units make a reckoning table; dated observations make an interval table and watching calendar. An omen interpretation remains beside the event record. Religious interpretation is not mechanically deleted to obtain observation.
2. **Greek argument, c. 400–330 BCE:** the actual sky record and watching calendar test the difference between a valid inference, an overbroad premise, and rival causes that predict the same event. The player makes a counterexample and a still-open question.
3. **Alexandria, c. 300–150 BCE:** the argument becomes useful in the two-circle equilateral construction. A proof roll joins the reasoning to a visibly disputed textual witness. A diagram is not automatically a proof; an editorial restoration is not certainty.
4. **Baghdad, c. 800–850 CE:** the earlier proof roll meets Syriac/Arabic witnesses; Indian positional calculation enters as an independent tributary. A worked account and square completion give a checked algebra method. Arabic scholars contribute developments, not just preservation.
5. **Cairo, c. 1020 CE:** the Arabic mathematical argument helps make a ray model. An aperture image in the dark room and a deliberately moved lamp constrain it. The old open question exposes what the demonstration does not settle about vision.

This arc is genuinely connected as a game. Its Babylon-to-Greek-dispute edge is explicitly a **pedagogical comparison**, not a fabricated claim that Aristotle read our invented tablet. The later astronomical chapter also makes the original cycle table do work again.

## Diagnosis of the old Atlas

The recovered fork's actual authored-route audit finds **12 disconnected chapters out of 22 post-opening chapters**. A first quick prerequisite walk found 13 because it retained only one producer per output and missed anatomy's route through `field-note`; that shortcut is not the correct baseline. The fork audit records actual combinations and is the retained baseline.

The deeper problems were an undated, fully packaged modern test protocol placed before Athens, independent chapter supply kits, chronology drift (for example comparative Asian printing after photography), and transitions that explained connections the player did not enact. Reordering or adding prose would not repair this.

The replacement does not manufacture an early universal “scientific method.” Observation, deductive argument, mathematics, instrument tests, natural history, statistical comparison and institutional analysis develop in particular settings, retain limits, and sometimes contradict inherited claims.

## Why one chronological route, not rigid alternation

A single unbroken invention chain falsely implies necessary progress. Fixed science/humanities or East/West alternation merely puts a pattern on the disconnected-vignette problem. This candidate instead uses **one chronological walk with recurring branches**:

- numerical records → models → navigation;
- proof/textual comparison → translation → print → inspectable anatomy;
- optics → perspective → telescope → photography → microscopy;
- chance → population comparison → Malthus, Snow and communication;
- argument → algebraic logic → relay switching;
- geology + population pressure + natural history → Darwin/Wallace.

The reunion matters mechanically. For example, Boolean rules and an electromagnetic record are both necessary in the switching workshop. The population pamphlet and geological record rejoin the natural-history branch. No extra track selector, dependency-graph HUD or carried-items paragraph has been added.

## What is historical and what is game design

`connections.json` distinguishes **19 bounded historical-tradition/application edges** from **65 pedagogical reuse edges and 12 speculative-synthesis edges**. The latter are real gameplay dependencies, not evidence of a named thinker's direct intellectual debt. Source annotations support particular bounded historical claims; they do not turn every invented two-input recipe into a historical event.

Strong historical anchors include the Greek/Syriac/Arabic and Arabic/Latin textual traditions, Latin optical reception and Kepler, printing and Vesalius, Malthus and Darwin, and Boole with Shannon's circuit analysis. The early research file documents the actual 1482 Latin Euclid object and the care needed around perspective's uncertain detailed genealogy. Sources are in `scenario.json`, `research-early.md`, and `research-late.md`.

The scope is a **selected history of representations and inquiry**, not a complete global history of thought. Indian arithmetic is substantive, not assigned Greek origins. Asian printing is acknowledged as earlier and diverse, but is not given a forced Greek-to-China ingredient chain. This remains disproportionately European after the medieval chapters. A broader next version should add a researched continuing non-European intellectual strand, not a token name-swap chapter.

The prior BBC official-feed work is reused: `episode-index.csv` contains 1,116 feed entries, not 1,116 listened-to episodes. Official Translation Movement metadata was inspected in this pass; Probability and Euclid direct pages had access limits. Episode titles and lexical connections are research leads, **not proof of influence**.

## The future is a new capability, not an administrative checklist

All three future scenes are **undated fiction**, not 2050/2100 forecasts. Their new inventions are not validated by the historical sources.

- **Self-questioning observatory — 9 combinations.** Rival biological predictions meet a Boolean experiment lattice and an optical observation chamber. A surprising bloom in darkness defeats the first model. The same apparatus then chooses a more discriminating next trial: the new capability is a laboratory that can ask again, not a generic “AI + science” badge.
- **Choral memory — 9 combinations.** The photographic archive becomes a revisitable courtyard; conflicting recollections remain alternate paths. A speculative encoding and viewpoint exchange let people inhabit each other's accounts, creating a shared “we” with no single original. Neither vivid experience nor perfectly copied data settles consciousness or personal identity. The new artifact is a many-voiced memory garden, not a claim of successful mind uploading.
- **Metabolic city — 10 combinations.** Complementary living partners, a water/nutrient loop and the learning observatory form a responsive district. Its productive wall shades a neighbor. The choral memory makes that conflict experiential; a seasonal commons rule becomes part of the living infrastructure. A changed symbiont reopens both ecology and allocation. The end is an evolving city, not a flawless utopia.

The sources bound inherited principles: [NASA/JPL's AEGIS](https://ai.jpl.nasa.gov/public/projects/aegis/) demonstrates bounded autonomous targeting, not this fictional laboratory; [personal-identity scholarship](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/identity-personal/) articulates unresolved identity questions, not uploading feasibility; [NIST's framework](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/5-sec-core/) supports ongoing model evaluation, not certification of the imagined city. These source relationships are separately labelled `speculative_synthesis` in the graph.

- `chapters.json` / `CAMPAIGN_PLAN.md`: chronology, objectives, recipes and earlier-made inputs.
