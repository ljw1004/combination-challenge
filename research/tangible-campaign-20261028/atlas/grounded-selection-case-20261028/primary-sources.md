# Primary-source verification — chapter 22

Verified 2026-10-28 through the primary-text transcriptions below. These sources support historical reasoning and framing, **not the invented numerical dossier**. No historical passage is quoted in the patch.

## Darwin: imagined predator/prey case, retained abilities, accumulation

[Charles Darwin, *On the Origin of Species*, first edition (1859), chapter IV, pp.90–91 and 95–96](https://darwin-online.org.uk/converted/published/1859_Origin_F373/1859_Origin_F373.html)

- At pp.90–91 Darwin explicitly presents imaginary illustrations. His wolf case changes the relative availability of fleet prey during food stress and conditions an advantage in speed/slimness on retaining other needed abilities. He connects an advantageous variation with survival, offspring and transmission.
- At pp.95–96 he compares cumulative biological change with geological processes operating over long periods.
- **Adaptation boundary:** our exact 10/10 adults, ten same-category pairs, four pups each, 15/5 recruits, deterministic one-generation transmission, non-overlapping censuses and causal recruitment premise are invented. Darwin did not report this population, dataset or inheritance rule. The game neither reconstructs all of his theory nor asserts that all adaptation is increasing speed.

## Wallace: domestic reversion does not establish fixed natural bounds

[Alfred Russel Wallace, *On the Tendency of Varieties to depart indefinitely from the Original Type* (1858), pp.53–62, in the joint Darwin–Wallace publication](https://darwin-online.org.uk/converted/published/1858_species_F350.html)

- The opening at pp.53–54 identifies an argument from domestic reversion to the permanent boundedness of natural varieties and contests that extension.
- The discussion/conclusion at pp.60–62 distinguishes domestic from natural conditions and argues for possible continuing departure under conditions permitting persistence.
- The introductory letter at pp.45–46 records independent reasoning and the joint presentation. The game retains that acknowledgment while giving Wallace a substantive argument rather than a biographical token.
- **Adaptation boundary:** the game's one-generation shift is not a demonstration of indefinite divergence or a refutation of every possible later return. Wallace supplies no 10/10 or 15/5 wolf counts. The patch does not adopt the essay's caricature of Lamarck or its language of inevitable advancement.

## Modern teaching limits, not period quotations

The distinction between a stipulated causal model and a small observational sample, the explicit drift/migration/measurement alternatives, and the instructions to seek independent inheritance and recruitment evidence are authored explanatory limits. They are not attributed as verbatim statements by Darwin or Wallace. Nothing in this dossier licenses a human moral ranking, eugenic inference or institutional policy.
