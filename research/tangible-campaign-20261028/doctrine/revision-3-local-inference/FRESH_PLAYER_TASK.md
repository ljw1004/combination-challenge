# Fresh-player task

Play the opening desk of this prototype using only its player interface. Do not open scenario JSON, implementation, authored routes, research notes, prior play reports or tests.

Work in `/workspace/shared/combination-campaign-research-20261028/doctrine/revision-3-local-inference`. Choose a new session name and begin:

```
node play.mjs YOUR_SESSION look
```

Available commands: `look`; `read ITEM_ID`; `partners ITEM_ID`; `combine FIRST_ID SECOND_ID`; `undo`; `stop YOUR_REASON`. Prefix every command with `node play.mjs YOUR_SESSION`.

Before attempting a combination, briefly state what you expect it to mean. Read manuscripts or use partner highlighting whenever you wish. Stop whenever you want, or when the next desk opens. There is no required number of combinations, successes or completed goals. Do not ask for recipe solutions to turn a stalled session into a successful one.

Report what felt understandable, surprising, compelling, arbitrary or tiring, what you thought the story was saying, and why you continued or stopped. Preserve unsuccessful attempts. Identify this as simulated play, not human evidence.
