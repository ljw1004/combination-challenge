## Two approaches compared

1. **Associative inference, with preserved references (selected):** real premises meet directly, and the output says what the conjunction adds. A named manuscript keeps its cited leaves through the existing incorporate/preserve grammar. There is no separate “attach citation” action.
2. **Named slotted manuscript:** familiar construction grammar could represent a developing argument, but current empty slots do not show argumentative roles. A source-per-slot implementation would mainly expose a checklist while retaining arbitrary prerequisites. It merits a future experiment only if fitting a component changes a recognisable argument, not merely completes a bibliography.

## Freeze and fresh trial

`pilot-integrity.json` locks candidate, local model/schema/lifecycle, player, checkpoint preparer, test, authoring manifest, builder, quote ledger and assignment. The CLI and preparer reject changed bytes or a changed Node version before loading the runtime. Session metadata binds both candidate and entire manifest digests. This freezes the logical pilot, not the whole operating system or browser UI.

Coordinator creates a new session with `node prepare-second-desk.mjs SESSION`. It starts from the earned chapter-2 checkpoint and separately records seven authored baseline actions. The fresh evaluator receives only `FRESH_PLAYER_TASK.md`, session name, and player commands. It may stop whenever it wants. No quota, recipe reveal, rescue, or forced completion. Trial predictions are recorded before attempts; failures and voluntary stops stay in evidence.

Primary evaluation question: do actual premises and visibly parallel goals support reasoning, or does this still feel like matching the author’s phrases? Fresh play remains pending until its own session closes. The next decision must follow that outcome, not the authored successes.
