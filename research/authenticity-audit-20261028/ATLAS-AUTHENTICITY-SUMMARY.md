## Highest-priority rework: four underlying problems, six HIGH rows

### 1. The printing chapter combines three incompatible actual documents

HIGH recipe IDs: `make-illustrated-form`, `make-detected-error`, `make-checked-edition`.

The concrete payload chain is:

1. `merchant-manual.reading.text`: the cloth calculation **18 ÷ 3 = 6 units per length; five lengths cost 30**. This is copied to `circulating-arithmetic`, then `make-set-page` produces `set-page` (label **Set arithmetic page**).
2. `illustration-method.description` and reading preserve the **E/S courtyard projection**, with post and jamb depths 4/8 and shifted intersections 1/1½. `make-diagram-block` engraves that plate into `diagram-block`.
3. `make-illustrated-form` combines those two and says text and diagram correspond, but no relation between the cloth unit-price text and courtyard diagram is authored.
4. `swapped-label.reading.text` says the trial page exchanges **A and B** while the explanation is unchanged. The established courtyard uses E/S and post/jamb, not an authored A/B pair; the arithmetic page does not establish that geometry either.
5. `make-checked-edition` checks the resulting quire against `proof-roll`: **the equilateral triangle AC=AB=BC with vertex C restored**—a third document rather than a witness to the actual printed contents.

**Minimal coherent repair:** choose one actual page. Easiest is typeset the existing triangle proof roll and engrave the matching triangle diagram, then check against that same source. Keep the perspective method's legitimate later uses, including anatomical illustrations. Do not simply rename the generic outputs. Also make the label error consequential: an A/B swap on a symmetric equilateral triangle need not itself change the theorem, so specify an actually inconsistent reference or radius label.

Direct chain consumers: `make-inked-form`, `make-corrected-proof`, and the printing objective `printing-checked-edition`. `checked-edition` then feeds `make-printed-anatomy`, `make-circulation-book`, `make-comparable-bills`, `make-mathematical-treatise`, `make-electromagnetic-record`, and `make-descent-book`; preserve their reusable-reference role when repairing its identity.

This finding is based on internal payload mismatch, not a claim that historical printing or mixed-subject books were impossible.

### 2. Orbital predictions appear without an authored orbital hypothesis

HIGH: `make-orbital-table`: `tycho-table` + `algebra-method` → `orbital-table`.

- `tycho-table.reading.text` describes recorded pre-telescopic positions and the obligation to answer discrepancies; it supplies **no candidate orbit or predicted positions**.
- `algebra-method.description`/reading supplies a **positive-length completing-square operation**, followed by general equality/units discipline.
- `orbital-table.description` says calculated positions are compared with observations, preserving discrepancies instead of hiding them in a perfect circle. It has no additional reading. Neither an actual candidate prediction nor the calculation yielding one has been authored.

**Repair:** supply one concrete candidate orbit/prediction table and compare its dated positions with the recorded ones. The older algebra can remain when it performs an explicit calculation. This does not demand reconstructing all Keplerian mathematics; it asks that the two things being compared actually exist.

Affected consumers: `make-predictive-sky-record`, `make-orbital-geometry`; downstream interpolation and Moon-test branches should retain their already clear, numerically correct new dossiers.

**Repair:** add a specific overclaim and a contrary geological observation, or pair the sequence with explicit rate/observation limits. Do not manufacture a refutation merely by consulting an old successful counterexample.

Affected consumer: `make-geological-record`, then later deep-time/evolution references.

### 4. The first logic chapter corrects an error that was never supplied

Exact evidence:

## Important MEDIUM findings, not falsely escalated to physical impossibility

- **`make-receiver-design`:** `equivalent-circuit` is retained as a reference, not physically transformed. Its full description/reading explicitly describes the permit function `(A OR B) AND NOT C`, contact counts, and static eight-row equivalence. `receiver-design` has no reading and says only that a logical receiver can execute decoding. General Boolean-design transfer is plausible, so this is MEDIUM, not a claim the author literally wires a door circuit as a decoder. Ground the transfer with majority `M=(a AND b) OR (a AND c) OR (b AND c)` and a prefix-tree parsing state, or call the output a decoder specification rather than a completed receiver design. MIT's [source-code notes](https://web.mit.edu/6.02/www/s2012/handouts/3.pdf) distinguish the actual code mapping/tree traversal.
- Several mandatory old-item dependencies provide only generic discipline: the particular equilateral proof before perspective/orbital geometry; the particular completing-square result before rates; the gravitational Moon calculation before a geological time question or a field question. These are often repairable by making the genuinely reusable method visible and showing its new operation. They are not all historically false.
- A finished mathematical `checked-edition` or `printed-quire` repeatedly stands silently for a new publishing process. Because lifecycles retain it as a reference, these are MEDIUM missing-method/labeling findings, not literal magical paper transformations. State the inherited layout/proofing method or retain actual printing supplies.
- **`make-framed-photograph`:** the result is viewpoint/exclusion annotation, not a physical frame. A label such as **Situated photograph** would avoid the same kind of mental-model trap as “glazed bottle.”
- **`make-supplier-rates`:** the label correctly uses deaths per 100 homes, but 8%/1% in the description is ambiguous. The receipts reading identifies affected households, so a household proportion may be intended. Specify distinct affected households if percentages are used; otherwise retain deaths per 100 households. Do not turn that denominator into person-level risk. The authored Graunt reading itself already warns against that substitution; Snow's [primary later account](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/66507/66507-h/66507-h.htm) distinguishes houses from estimated inhabitants.
- **`make-metabolic-imager`:** explicitly speculative and otherwise coherent, but the sensor description says absorption while its label/reading detect emitted glow. Align the observable; no need to demand a real biological protocol.
