Commands after initialization:
- view SESSION: see the current place, things, invitation and goals.
- hint SESSION ITEM_ID: see compatible partner IDs and useful settings. Outputs are not revealed in advance.
- act SESSION '{"type":"combine","a":"ITEM_ID","b":"OTHER_ITEM_ID"}': combine two things. A place operand uses @PLACE_ID as b.
- act SESSION '{"type":"place","item":"ITEM_ID","location":"PLACE_ID"}': put a thing at a location, including a setting for a later combination.
- act SESSION '{"type":"move","item":"ITEM_ID","x":100,"y":150}': move a thing to open space.
- act SESSION '{"type":"advance"}': move on after current goals are met.
- act SESSION '{"type":"visit","area":"AREA_ID","carry":["ITEM_ID"]}': return to a known area with actual selected things from your current area.
- undo SESSION: undo your last accepted action.
- stop SESSION 'your report' or finish SESSION 'your report': end the session. Finish is appropriate if you reached its ending; stopping early is equally valid.
