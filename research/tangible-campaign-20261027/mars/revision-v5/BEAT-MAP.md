## Cause and payoff

1. The familiar landing recovery, habitat, rover and outcrop opening is preserved. The same observed terrain earns a weather-route payoff.
2. The reviewed lighter weather/relay sequence finds the missing crew; repair or tow brings them to the prepared refuge. No claim that v4 is fresh-play validated.
3. **New camp beat:** rescued people gain a shared lit workbench and send a personal recording home. The radio retains its identity when put on a stand. Comfortable obvious progress is intentional.
4. **New thermal beat:** a controlled bare-versus-insulated wall-sample comparison supports fitting an actual removable thermal screen. The chamber is a meaningful catalyst, not a decorative rectangle.
5. Waterworks and greenhouse provide a stable camp and a meal. The water is explicitly process water, not a safety verdict from a conductivity reading. Growth time is narrated.
6. Workshop work prepares the vehicle for the next leg. A largely intact scout shows the terrain; **new ground-check beat** makes the distinction between a picture and a traversable surface concrete. The replacement wheel is now a motivated workshop goal, not an unneeded side recipe.
7. Geological sampling asks what the place used to be. Earlier core observations and later retained samples earn a return-cargo payoff.
8. The oxygen plant services an actual vehicle module. Cabin readiness no longer consists of assembling every internal wire or producing certificates.
9. Timing and observations establish the rendezvous. A stable slotted return capsule keeps samples and records together, then departure closes the rescue-and-exploration story.

## Places and story assumptions to challenge

- Mars remains an expedition through durable places. Current-scene packing collects known equipment from visited sites with provenance; no magical later primitive grants.
- The expedition is large enough to have pre-positioned engineered sites and stored instruments. This is an explicit fiction, not a lone astronaut manufacturing industrial capability from rocks. The sheer convenience/density of these stores still needs independent narrative challenge.
- Thermal workshop and greenhouse are camp facilities. The engine still depicts one generic area per work scene; it does not model distances or plumbing. Collecting a whole water-system token for greenhouse use is an abstraction that needs UI/story treatment, not a new portable/nonportable rule by default.
- The protected spare controller still has no later functional payoff in this draft. Its local dust-protection motivation survives the v4 pilot, but a future revision should either give it an authentic matched-hardware use or remove that obligation—not arbitrarily wire it into an unrelated instrument.
- The homebound sequence still represents changes in the flight vehicle as outputs. A later review should decide which are genuinely satisfying combinations and which should instead be narrated travel; removing them must reduce reported length, not spawn substitute badges.
