# Concrete next play slices

These are candidates for fresh play, not declarations of fun. Comfortable, predictable making is welcome. The objection is to arbitrary operations and renamed paperwork, not to every linear chain.

## Mars: Weather → Relay → Rescue

Begin from a legally replayed outcrop checkpoint. Restore an observation station and protect existing equipment; recover an actual distress message using the relay; then repair or tow the crawler and provide a refuge. This gives three different jobs with one clear developing situation. Earlier maps and gear matter. Keep machine identity through component slots. End with relief, not an extra acceptance-document chain.

## Ideas: Optics → Perspective, plus Chance separately

Optics can make shadow and projected image tangible. Perspective can reuse the observer/image relation in a recognizable courtyard painting. Discovery descriptions should show an actual example, not merely a new method label. Separately, the interrupted game provides a particular dispute about a stake rather than an abstract task to produce 'good reasoning'. Supply legal checkpoint inputs; do not claim the isolated slice validates all 220 draft recipes.

## Elements: Crossing → Mill → Bakery

Build a route and transport; harness the stream to prepare grain; feed the journey using that work and the newly encountered local cellar. The shared gathering provides continuity. Test whether the return of wood, tools and grain feels satisfying rather than administratively repetitive. The later Harbor → Letters sequence can contrast anticipation of arriving guests.

## New worlds: Food, Restoration, Shoreline

- Food: recognizable physical transformations through cultures and pastry; distinct local ovens prevent accidental return chores.
- Restoration: keep the same map's identity through documentation and protection, ending in a visible provenance story.
- Shoreline: plan, install and observe. Treat a season report as evidence of observations, not automatic proof the intervention succeeded.

Other minis remain useful speculative comparisons. Document-heavy intellectual scenarios are not disqualified, but their conceptual operations need to earn their names. A brush can write an interpretation; it cannot be the missing reasoning that makes the interpretation substantive.
