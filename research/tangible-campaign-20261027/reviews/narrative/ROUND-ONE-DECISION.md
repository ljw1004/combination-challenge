## Interpretation discipline

Simple, predictable, linear construction is not automatically padding. The user explicitly enjoys comfortable progress and small local challenges. Reject arbitrary renamed states, unsupported material operations and chores caused by accidental old-location dependencies. Retain understandable assembly, recurring tools, a reason to care about the next place, and visible results.

Fresh source-blind trials can reveal confusion and engagement hypotheses, but are not human fun evaluation. The user's actual playtest remains the important checkpoint. Historical, theological, ecological and technical plausibility need separate source/domain review.
