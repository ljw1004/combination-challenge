# Frozen v1 authenticity and semantic review

Reviewed 2026-10-27. Scope: all seven frozen scenario JSONs, including premises, items, recipes, stage motivations/caches, source metadata and notes. No frozen files changed. Independent source checks are below. This is a content review, not evidence of human enjoyment or a requirement for 200-step campaigns.

## Useful result

**No critical false-history, universal-religion, or actionable dangerous-procedure blocker found.** The specific Benedictine case is accurately and honestly scoped. Aristotle's four explanatory roles are also sound. Food is the strongest tangible A+B set. Chinese-thought and literature have the biggest *semantic* weakness: the player manufactures named documents without seeing the ideas or verses supposedly transformed. That is a reason to revise or deliberately test these minis, not to reject the domains.

## Severity-ranked actionable findings

### P1 — High impact on the promised experience, not a safety or factual emergency

1. **Chinese-thought: comparison has no actual compared claims.** `compassion-notes + brush = mencius-reading`, `ritual-notes + music-example = cultivation-reading`, and the later `comparison-table + counterexample = open-question` identify containers or operations, not their contents. The JSON never says what the counterexample or teacher response is. Consequently the promised preserved disagreement cannot be inspected; a player can match names without understanding either position. **Next revision:** put one short original paraphrase on each reading, a specific challenging case on the counterexample, and the unresolved question on the final manuscript. Distinguish the cultivation of incipient compassion from shaping habits through deliberate practice, without implying Mencius rejects cultivation or ritual. Keep the fictional school and no-fusion framing. This finding is directly visible in the JSON; philosophical context is checked in [SEP Mencius](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/mencius/) and [SEP Xunzi](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/xunzi/).

2. **Literature: the poem's changes are asserted, not perceptible.** `station-poem + line-break = enjambed-poem` has a basically correct description, but there is no actual poem, line-break placement, returning phrase, or revised text anywhere in the frozen data. `refrain-poem + reading-aloud = revised-poem` therefore promises a change with no observable cause. **Next revision:** supply a tiny original two-to-four-line poem and show the before/after lineation and the particular refrain. The player should recognize what changed without an explanatory lecture. Not every break is enjambment: syntactic continuation across the line is the relevant feature. [Poetry Foundation definition](https://www.poetryfoundation.org/education/glossary/Enjambment), [Academy of American Poets](https://poets.org/glossary/enjambment).

### P2 — Moderate clarity/authenticity improvements

3. **Restoration: specify that the flood-surviving map is already dry and stabilized.** The premise says salvage, and the first speaker says it survived the flood, but no visible story statement distinguishes a historically flood-damaged dry map from a currently wet one before enclosure/display. The explicit non-instructional disclaimer and conservator boundary substantially mitigate this; do not characterize the current game as dangerous treatment instructions. **Small repair:** “Our conservator has dried and stabilized this map; now document and house it.” Also describe measuring without putting a ruler on fragile media. CCI supports rigid supports, limited handling and suitable enclosures, while separately treating water emergencies; it does not validate a wet-map-to-folder sequence. [CCI paper care](https://www.canada.ca/en/conservation-institute/services/preventive-conservation/guidelines-collections/paper-objects.html).

4. **Chinese-thought: the opening motivation shifts Mencius's feeling into action.** “Why might someone reach out…” is plausible original fiction, not a severe misquotation, but 2A6's central claim concerns immediate alarm/compassion, not guaranteed rescue behavior. **Repair:** “Why might someone feel concern before thinking of a reward?” Keep the question hypothetical, as its recipe already does. The primary passage is quoted and identified as 2A6 in [SEP Mencius](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/mencius/). CText primary URLs returned 403 during this audit, so their full texts were not directly verified here.

5. **Shoreline: `planted-edge` switches ontological type at placement.** Its own description honestly says it is a portable project model until placement; therefore no scientific deception, but the player carries a model and apparently installs a coast. **Repair:** make the output a bounded “Planted coir module” throughout, with site installation represented by placement. Add a brief nursery-growth time cue to `native-seed + nursery-tray = marsh-plugs`; this is legitimate time compression, not instantaneous biology. NOAA supports native-vegetation approaches in estuaries, not these exact construction recipes. [NOAA living shorelines](https://oceanservice.noaa.gov/facts/living-shoreline.html).

6. **Causation and theology: distinguish sound abstraction from satisfying combination.** Four `*-card -> *-exhibit` recipes and the theology permission chain are conceptually defensible but may play as labelled worksheet completion. Unlike Chinese-thought, theology supplies the actual distinction, condition and outcome in recipe descriptions; that is a meaningful strength. **Test, do not automatically rewrite:** ask players to explain why the superior's permission does not apply to all brothers, and why a purpose explains a boat without physically changing its hull. For causation, visible exhibit captions should identify timber, hull structure/pattern, builder's skilled making and ferrying people. The plan is a representation of form, not literally the whole formal cause. [Aristotle Physics II.3](https://classics.mit.edu/Aristotle/physics.2.ii.html), [Rule of Benedict 53](https://pressbooks.palni.org/ruleofbenedict/chapter/rb-53/).

### P3 — Minor polish / explicitly acceptable compression

7. **Food branding:** `twinkie` is qualified as Twinkie-inspired in its recipe but the stage says the endpoint “is a Twinkie.” Prefer “Twinkie-style snack cake” in the visible item/stage as well, to align with the deliberate non-formula abstraction. No commercial-formula authenticity established by this audit.

8. **Food culture specificity:** the yogurt recipe correctly says a *suitable* bacterial culture, distinct from bread yeast. “Yogurt starter” would be a clearer item label than generic “Lactic culture.” Heating, incubation and time are compressed, not a complete procedure. No need to turn the game into a cooking lesson. [WSU Extension, Yogurt made simple](https://pubs.extension.wsu.edu/product/yogurt-made-simple/).

9. **Literature print-shop compression:** the inked-form/booklet recipe omits pressing and prints after folding. Fine as a small fictional craft abstraction, but a recipe description can mention the printer pressing the inked type onto sheets before folding/binding. Do not require extra checklist nodes merely for procedural realism. [FSU Special Collections printing methods](https://guides.lib.fsu.edu/c.php?g=1037582&p=7523112).

10. **Source hygiene:** NLM's supplied four-humors URL now exposes a redirect stub; use the [current NLM exhibition](https://www.nlm.nih.gov/exhibition/shakespeare-and-the-four-humors/index.html). CText and lowercase Poetry Foundation URL were inaccessible to the web reader, but the latter's capitalized indexed entry and Poets.org independently verify the term. Broken reader access is not evidence of a false claim.

## Recommended interpretation of human tryouts

Run these as small contrasts, not graduation tests for entire domains. Food should establish the tangible transformation baseline. Theology and causation can test whether a clear role/condition or explanation feels like discovery. Chinese-thought and literature need visible semantic content to test more than token-chain tolerance. Restoration and shoreline can test preservation/planning rather than power-up progression. Ask “What changed, and why did these two things belong together?” No source review can certify fun, and no additional universal-faith survey is required for this explicitly local Benedictine fiction.
