# New-world trials — frozen v1

These seven executable scenarios are small falsifiable proposals, not seven endorsed full games. Parent owns wiring into the common UI and publication.

## Try first
Food is the most immediately tangible. Restoration tests preservation rather than power escalation. Theology now poses a concrete Benedictine hospitality/fasting distinction grounded in Rule 53 rather than an abstract ladder of synonyms. Shoreline tests an intervention followed by observation, but its ending currently cannot respond differently to evidence.

Literature, Chinese thought and Causation remain deliberately risky small trials: their document/exhibit chains may feel like homework. Keep them short and discard or radically remake them if that is what human play shows.

## Evidence and limitations
Each scenario embeds primary/authoritative sources and explicit fiction/caveat notes. Sources support stated concepts, not the invented recipes or proof of game quality. Source research is not an expert endorsement. No real food preparation, conservation treatment, coastal engineering or medical advice is intended.

All seven pass shared-engine validation and a complete author-aware legal route. Each JSON has chapter checkpoints containing exact replay actions. `*-route-evidence.json` records the complete route, metrics and limits. `route-summary.json` summarizes the routes. These are not source-blind playtests or UI tests. No user/human fun score exists yet. Optional branches are not required by those route counts. No simultaneous same-location multiple-object goals remain. Food has local baking equipment rather than accidental returns to a finished area. Restoration explicitly retires incorporated map versions.

`frozen-manifest.json` records version1 hashes and candid per-trial assessments. Do not edit scenario files while a frozen trial runs. `build_candidates.py` regenerates drafts without checkpoints; run `validate-routes.ts` afterward to regenerate checkpoints. The validator loads only the seven named scenario files, not its evidence/manifest JSON.
