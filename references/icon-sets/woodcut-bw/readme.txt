## Wood engraving

Woodcut / linocut / relief printing. Bold carved shapes, irregular marks, strong contrast, visible handmade texture. More rustic and evocative than modern flat icons. A particularly good visual language for primitive materials, huts, trees, fire, stone tools, and early crafts.

This collection developed into monochrome wood engraving after the William Blake discussion: broken contours, fine hatching, modest observed objects and no enclosing badge. The original inspiration.png is also shared by the coloured woodcut collection.
