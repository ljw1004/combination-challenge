## Diderot

Copperplate engraving / encyclopedic illustration. The world of Diderot's Encyclopédie (1751 onward). Delicate black lines, cross-hatching, precise objects, almost scientific presentation. Particularly apt for tools, machines, architecture, and the development of technology. Diderot's great project was to document human knowledge, including the previously undervalued mechanical arts and trades. That's extraordinarily close to what our game is about. I'd simplify the engravings, perhaps introduce a few muted colours, and retain their characteristic precision.

(c) Lucian Wischik
