## Japanese Edo

Japanese Edo-period woodblock prints. Look up ukiyo-e household objects or dōgu-zukushi. Collections of everyday objects drawn with confident outlines, flattened perspective, and restrained colours. I find this particularly compelling for your game: each object looks individually interesting. What particularly appeals to me here is that a household object can have its own personality. The shapes aren't reduced to sterile symbols, but neither are they extravagantly modelled. It would allow a clay pot, lantern, pickaxe, roof tile, and window to coexist beautifully.
