Make Something Worthwhile

Cookbook

Write a cookbook. Make doughs, sauces, fillings, pastries and cakes.

Cosmic: A Sea Beneath the Moon

Bring particles, starlight, stone and ice together. Follow the making of a world until rain finds a shore beneath the Moon.

Creation

Begin with Earth, Air, Fire and Water. Kindle stars, raise oceans and bring a living world into being—then discover what it can make without you.

No Fixed Address

The city says your neighborhood no longer exists. A repair shop, a discarded AI and a few stubborn neighbors disagree. Make something that will still work in the morning.

History of ideas

Follow questions across centuries: what explains an eclipse, what makes a life good, and when should a rule change? Combine evidence, arguments and inventions to discover how ideas meet—and what each encounter makes possible.

Mission to Mars

Your lander crashes badly onto the surface. Can you patch things up and complete your science mission?

Settlement: A River Worth Returning To

Starting from four storybook elements, travel from an orchard to a coastal gathering in rustic coloured woodcuts. Make useful things for people you meet. The world remembers what you leave behind.

Theology: Disputed Questions

A manuscript desk, not a test of faith. Follow a wound, a failed promise, bread, a hungry household and a request for prayer through changing arguments. Build what each writer is claiming, test it against a case, and leave real disagreements open. New chapters reuse distinctions you have made. Each item has a short explanation you can revisit.
