# Event-local lifecycle correction — 28 October 2026

## Rule

Only the accepted combination/installation may retire inputs declared transformed/incorporated by **that recipe**. The existing `needed` check still protects future useful inputs and current/future objectives at that event. An outside copy or qualitative supply deliberately left then remains physical until a later recipe explicitly consumes it. Discovering an alternative output, an unrelated last use, moving, visiting, advancing, or loading never retroactively absorbs it into an old product.

This is not a finite-resource simulation or blanket retention. Immediate replacements, consumed ingredients, and installations still retire their actual inputs when no remaining use protects them. Finished leftovers remain in their current areas. Scene-local carry, not deletion, controls the next chapter's contents.

Declared construction contents are copied into the new owner's visible components at the event, even when an outside source remains. Creation placement uses the resulting component height. The original source keeps its fitted slots and position. A later modification of that separate source must not appear retroactively in the earlier assembly. Direct slot labels are not duplicated as component labels.

## Why the old green suite was insufficient

The previous runtime deliberately reconciled all earlier incorporations after every action and on restore. The previous final-use regression required that policy. It allowed Seed oil to delete its explicitly retained Millstone into an old Grain mill, and Porridge + Compote to delete untouched Bread into an old Picnic basket. Neither current action visibly explained those removals. The older audit and generic “retain a supply only while another authored use needs it” reasons described that superseded policy; the future-use safeguard is now evaluated **at the recipe event**, not continuously afterward.

Preservation was also guarded by retirement. A retained source could keep its own fitted parts while the new assembly omitted them. That omission is not proof that the original source's slot data was destroyed. Optional Mars radio installation and synthetic fitted chassis tests cover both sides of this distinction.

## Save compatibility and deliberate limits

- The outer save schema remains `version: 1`. `lifecycleVersion: 2` marks event-local decisions and construction snapshots. Marked saves restore without reinterpretation, including their undo history.
- Unmarked valid old saves load without resets, token deletion, token resurrection, changed events, or changed metrics. Already absent incorporated inputs remain absent. Existing outside copies remain present at their recorded coordinates.
- The only migration is additive **visible construction metadata** recovery. It requires adjacent retained snapshots that show a preserved recipe becoming used, takes the source's actual pre-action fitted parts, and follows the recorded output/owner lineage. It also covers installations.
- A currently retained source that was upgraded later is not evidence of what an earlier assembly contained. Recovery never uses its later state, item-definition possibilities, or fabricated default parts.
- If the relevant pre-action snapshot has fallen outside the saved history (normally the last 100 accepted actions), missing old components remain unknown. No parts are invented. A migrated save is marked so recovery is idempotent.
- **Changed legacy soup expectation:** an unmarked pre-lifecycle save that physically contains both the obsolete prepared pouch and warmed soup keeps that ambiguous old copy. Generic graph exhaustion is no longer used to “clean” it. New play still transforms the pouch immediately, and existing old progress/next recipes remain usable. A future narrowly identified historical repair would need its own provenance and tests.

## Coverage

`event-local.test.ts` covers current-recipe-only retirement on every existing authored route; actual Elements Millstone, Bread and River map; Mars retained Solar charger and Pressure patch; explicit later consumption; advance/move/visit and restore/history persistence; fitted chassis and actual optional radio installation in both directions; old output and installation recovery; truncated-history limits; immediate fitted replacement position and non-resurrection. The old last-use test was revised rather than quietly removed; the old soup test now records the explicit migration limit.

Required integration checks remain `npm run build` and `node --test tests/tangible-campaign.test.ts`. The isolated review also compares old/new route actions and local clutter, including current research candidates. Those research runs do not make candidates released campaigns or constitute native/human playtesting.
