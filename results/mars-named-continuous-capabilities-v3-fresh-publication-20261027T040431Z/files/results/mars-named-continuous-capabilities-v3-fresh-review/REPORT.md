## Display exposure and limits

`player-display-disclosure.md` is material, not incidental. The player reports separate serial commands, choosing each after its displayed result. Captured timestamps support serial execution; the raw command log alone cannot prove what tool text was cognitively inspected or every filesystem action. Source isolation was instruction-only; no independent access sandbox or full tool-transcript audit exists.

No future/source-only information was introduced by these transforms.

The final `next` display was token-truncated, though the player reports seeing its successful finish. Independent raw replay verifies completion regardless. **Raw capture was not filtered or truncated:** every full output is preserved and replayed exactly. Consequently favorable catalogue-legibility or low-search-burden reports describe a self-filtered JSON interface, not the full list and certainly not a scrolling graphical board. Their self-report of mechanical repetition remains useful but is not a measured human response.
