## Immediate Mars findings

1. **Sheltered ice hides the apparatus.** Existing recipe uses solar charger electricity to power a fixed enclosed melt head; electricity is not itself heat. The label does not communicate the melt head. A heater route needs an explicit, surfaced power/coupling premise. Retain the heater and converge on existing meltwater. Do not invent unsupported exposed-ice melting physics.
2. **Cover-first germination is a natural order.** Accept cover before or after watering/sprouting while still requiring water and the narrated warm germination interval. Converge on existing propagator for later nursery use; do not duplicate the tray/cover or instantly sprout by adding a lid. New-state lifecycle handling is necessary because current conservative retention can leave parallel unique objects.

These are being handled by the separate Mars author. Other Mars order permutations stay second-batch only.
