# From the full available abstracts to 29 research directions

**Coverage:** every one of the 1,099 distinct available RSS abstracts (227,290 words) was processed in whole-text feature/topic analysis. I separately close-read the substantive text of 56 abstracts, including every selected lead, and read all ten clearly labelled source-based supplements for RSS gaps. This is not a claim to have listened to 1,109 episodes, or to have found ten missing BBC abstracts.

Cleanup of all temporary source and derived feature files was verified at 17:59:18 UTC; this deliverable retains no BBC abstract compendium.

The earlier catalogue pass covered all 1,114 listed records. The temporary BBC source descriptions and document-level feature text are not part of this deliverable; only original analysis, metadata and links remain.

## What changed after reading actual abstracts

- **Added Automata:** a model that looks alive is an excellent playable question about the difference between imitation and explanation.
- **Added Malthusianism and Kropotkin:** a documented crossing from population economics into evolutionary thought, followed by a live dispute over competition and cooperation. This is much stronger continuity than inserting an old note into an unrelated chapter.
- **Added William Morris:** technological futures should face inherited questions about work, beauty, ownership and freedom, not merely end with more powerful machines.
- **Added Walter Benjamin:** photography now has a substantive route into criticism of media and power, beyond “pictures become more realistic”.
- **Changed Glass:** the abstract directs attention to successful craft preceding complete explanation, not just an inventory of glass instruments.
- **Changed Renaissance Magic:** manuscript authority, translation and textual dating matter, alongside experiments and optical wonders.
- **Sharpened Borges:** creative translation and invented scholarship return to earlier manuscript practices; he is not simply a final “infinite library” namecheck.
- **Rejected inherited simplifications:** the Needham abstract’s one-way progress framing, a competition-only caricature of Darwin, and the claim that pragmatism means there is no truth should not become the game’s voice. Abstracts are leads, not final authorities.

Four broad overlapping provisional leads—Optics, Origins of Infectious Disease, Complexity and Scepticism—leave the selected episode list. Their relevant ideas are not forbidden from the game. Selection is a research direction, not a chapter roster.

## The 29 selected directions

### 1. [The Library at Nineveh](https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/b00b7r71) · 2008-05-15
**The archive has a politics.** What makes scattered observations a body of knowledge? Put sky records, omen interpretation, scribal selection and royal power on the same desk.
Possible connection: Archive -> comparison -> an argument about what survives.
[Independent topic source](https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/object/W_1883-0118-243). A physical royal astronomical/astrological report is documented; no claim that the whole collection was a modern public library.

### 2. [Cynicism](https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/p003k9js) · 2005-10-20
**A philosophy can be a way of living.** Diogenes suggests an action rather than another summary: remove a social dependency and discover the freedom—and cost—of needing less.
Possible connection: Socratic examination -> Cynic practice -> Stoic self-command; later luxury critique is a comparison unless sourced.
[Independent topic source](https://iep.utm.edu/cynics/). Anecdotes are transmitted literary evidence, not eyewitness transcripts.

### 3. [Aristotle's biology](https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/m0002cfd) · 2019-02-07
**Classification starts with living things.** Aristotle need not be only syllogisms. Compare animal structures and ask what a part does; later classification and evolution can change that explanation.
Possible connection: Observed likeness -> kinds -> function -> adaptation.
[Independent topic source](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/aristotle-biology/). Do not turn Aristotle into a modern evolutionary biologist or the first observer anywhere.

### 4. [The Music of the Spheres](https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/b00c1fct) · 2008-06-19
**Beauty can guide an explanation without proving it.** The attraction of a harmonious cosmos is a way to connect music, proportion and astronomy while keeping observations able to resist the beautiful model.
Possible connection: Musical order -> celestial order -> Kepler’s revision rather than arithmetic drills.
[Independent topic source](https://galileo.ou.edu/exhibits/music-of-the-spheres.html). Historical harmonic inquiry is not evidence that planets audibly sing.

### 5. [The Translation Movement](https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/b00dp4d8) · 2008-10-02
**Translation changes the questions.** A manuscript crossing languages can acquire new problems and arguments rather than simply being carried like a sealed package.
Possible connection: Greek texts + Syriac/Arabic scholarship -> original work -> Latin reception.
[Independent topic source](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/arabic-islamic-influence/). Separate Greek-to-Arabic and Arabic-to-Latin routes; do not credit a single building with every translation.

### 6. [The Needham Question](https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/p0038x9m) · 2006-10-19
**Question the shape of the history itself.** This is a design safeguard: ask what is hidden by a story that measures every tradition against a supposedly inevitable European endpoint.
Possible connection: Paper, printing and other technical routes should disrupt a one-way ladder.
[Independent topic source](https://www.nri.cam.ac.uk/about/). The public abstract uses a strongly teleological framing; the game will not inherit its claim that Chinese inventions made little social difference. This lead is a research counterpoint, not an automatic chapter or geographic quota.

### 7. [The Science of Glass](https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/b05w456c) · 2015-05-28
**Materials can reorganize knowledge.** A working craft can outrun a complete theory. Glass links lenses and instruments, but also asks whether useful making requires a finished explanation of the material.
Possible connection: Craft -> lens -> distant/near worlds; many returns rather than a glass chapter quota.
[Independent topic source](https://catalogue.museogalileo.it/multimedia/LensmakingBis.html). The abstract changed the lead: theory lagging technology is as important as optical applications. Glass must not be described as an ordinary liquid flowing slowly at room temperature.

### 8. [The Encyclopédie](https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/p0038x93) · 2006-10-26
**Knowledge can be rearranged for readers.** The encyclopedic project offers a desk full of crafts and arguments with cross-references, and a live question about who gets to name the branches of knowledge.
Possible connection: Workshop knowledge + print -> public reference work -> contested classifications.
[Independent topic source](https://encyclopedie.uchicago.edu/). The complete historical source is available through an academic edition; game text will be authored, not bulk copied.

### 9. [Baconian Science](https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/b00jdb6c) · 2009-04-02
**What did Francis Bacon actually change?.** Make the player notice a negative instance, isolate a variable, and organize collaborative inquiry. This is a substantive answer to the user’s question.
Possible connection: Natural histories -> eliminative comparisons -> experiments -> cooperative research.
[Independent topic source](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/francis-bacon/). Bacon neither invented all experiment nor supplied the unique modern scientific method.

### 10. [Renaissance Magic](https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/p004y28n) · 2004-06-17
**Magic and experiment need not be opposites.** A prestigious ancient-looking manuscript can redirect a Renaissance reader’s attention. Wonder, translation and the later dating of texts make a better tension than a simple magic-versus-science contest.
Possible connection: Platonism -> Hermetic reading and natural magic; philology can alter the authority of a manuscript without making every idea in it disappear.
[Independent topic source](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/della-porta/). Hermetic transmission and dating still require specific sources before implementation; Della Porta is a related independently researched practice, not the same episode claim.

### 11. [The Invention of Photography](https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/b07j699g) · 2016-07-07
**An image can become a trace.** A projected image plus a light-sensitive surface is an immediately intelligible synthesis. Its later effect on art and evidence need not be another measuring exercise.
Possible connection: Camera obscura -> fixed photograph -> questions of framing, realism and art.
[Independent topic source](https://www.hrc.utexas.edu/niepce-heliograph/). Photography has multiple inventors and processes; a fixed image is not automatically an objective interpretation.

### 12. [The Observatory at Jaipur](https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/b00hmpdd) · 2009-02-19
**The sky is not a single European workshop.** Jaipur offers an active meeting of astronomical traditions and instruments, not an exotic interruption. Architecture itself can become an observing instrument.
Possible connection: Translated tables + local observations + monumental instruments -> correction.
[Independent topic source](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1338). Verify the particular texts and people before asserting a direct transmission route.

### 13. [Longitude](https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/m000vyn6) · 2021-05-13
**A clock becomes a position.** Reference time and local noon make longitude conceptually graspable. Mechanical timekeepers and lunar observations remain alternative systems with different requirements.
Possible connection: Astronomy + clocks + sea trials -> navigation; trade and coercion are not erased.
[Independent topic source](https://www.rmg.co.uk/stories/time/harrisons-clocks-longitude-problem). No numerical longitude problem; no simple lone-genius versus foolish-establishment myth.

### 14. [Humboldt](https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/p003c1c2) · 2006-09-28
**A collection becomes a connected landscape.** Humboldt makes a different synthesis from taxonomy: altitude, climate and plant distribution can become a patterned whole.
Possible connection: Specimen labels -> environmental relations -> ecology.
[Independent topic source](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41559-019-0980-5). Recognize collaborators and local knowledge; do not present one European as discovering that nature is connected.

### 15. [Linnaeus](https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/m001l291) · 2023-04-20
**Names help—and can carry prejudice.** Classification creates a powerful shared tool but invites questions about which distinctions are useful and which rankings are imposed.
Possible connection: Aristotelian kinds -> Linnaean naming -> evolutionary trees; human hierarchy remains criticizable.
[Independent topic source](https://www.linnean.org/learning/who-was-linnaeus/linnaeus-and-race). Do not repeat historical racial classifications as biological truths.

### 16. [The Anatomy of Melancholy](https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/b010y30m) · 2011-05-12
**The crowded desk can be the subject.** Burton is an antidote to sterile research summaries: inherited medicine, philosophy, literary examples and first-person experience gather in a deliberately sprawling book.
Possible connection: Ancient temperaments -> Renaissance reading -> later questions about mind and interpretation.
[Independent topic source](https://wellcomecollection.org/works/ztn635y7). Humoral explanations are historical positions, not present medical guidance.

### 17. [Relativity](https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/b02144gl) · 2013-06-06
**Agreement does not require one universal clock.** Relativity can be reconstructed through light signals and differently moving observers, without formulas or confusing relativity with relativism.
Possible connection: Operational time + light -> relative simultaneity -> spacetime.
[Independent topic source](https://www.einstein-online.info/en/relativity_space_time/). Observer-dependent measurements are constrained, not arbitrary opinions.

### 18. [Bergson and Time](https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/m0004s9w) · 2019-05-09
**Measured time and lived time are different questions.** Bergson opens a genuine disagreement that need not be settled by one discipline defeating the other. Memory and anticipation can transform duration.
Possible connection: Clock time versus experienced duration; comparison with fiction is a teaching juxtaposition unless documented.
[Independent topic source](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/bergson/). Do not use lived duration to refute the empirical content of relativity.

### 19. [Surrealism](https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/p00547l1) · 2001-11-15
**Ideas can be combined artistically.** Dream, chance and a familiar object in an impossible setting make an intelligible artistic operation. Freud, Marx and Dada are more fertile sources than “add art”.
Possible connection: Optical realism + dream association -> a surreal image; reason does not simply vanish.
[Independent topic source](https://www.metmuseum.org/essays/surrealism). Freudian inspiration is documented; art does not prove psychoanalytic theories.

### 20. [Jorge Luis Borges](https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/b0076182) · 2007-01-04
**A library can become a philosophical machine.** Borges permits a genuinely different kind of combination: identical words in a changed context, a library with every possible book, a map that defeats its purpose.
Possible connection: Earlier translation and textual criticism can return through creative translation, altered context, imaginary scholarship and possible worlds. These comparisons do not assert direct medieval-to-Borges influence.
[Independent topic source](https://www.poetryfoundation.org/poets/jorge-luis-borges). No quoted story text; no claim that Borges predicted quantum physics or the internet.

### 21. [Lévi-Strauss](https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/b01sjjxl) · 2013-05-23
**Relations can matter more than isolated objects.** Lévi-Strauss is a direction for investigating how categories and contrasts organize meaning, with the limitations of the outsider’s scheme left visible.
Possible connection: Classification -> relational structures -> contested universal patterns.
[Independent topic source](https://www.college-de-france.fr/en/chair/claude-levi-strauss-social-anthropology-statutory-chair). Specific structural claims and source communities need further primary-text verification.

### 22. [Nietzsche's Genealogy of Morality](https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/b087rt4z) · 2017-01-12
**Ask where a value came from.** Genealogy changes the question from whether a rule sounds timeless to how it acquired its authority and whom it serves.
Possible connection: Inherited moral categories -> historical formation -> later critiques of knowledge and power.
[Independent topic source](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/nietzsche-moral-political/). Do not sanitize Nietzsche’s elitism or make genealogy equivalent to “all morality is false”.

### 23. [The Antikythera Mechanism](https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/m0024x0g) · 2024-11-14
**A model can be built into matter.** The Antikythera mechanism makes a powerful return of the oldest sky records: recurring celestial events represented in a working arrangement of gears.
Possible connection: Recorded cycles -> mechanical model -> later clocks and computation, with gaps acknowledged.
[Independent topic source](https://antikythera-mechanism.namuseum.gr/en/what-is-the-mechanism-and-what-does-it-consist-of/). Do not invent an unbroken device lineage to modern computers.

### 24. [The Fable of the Bees](https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/m0000t3y) · 2018-10-25
**Private motives and public outcomes can diverge.** Mandeville creates a provocative counterweight to ancient virtue and later economic order. Keep prosperity, moral cost and distribution as separate questions.
Possible connection: Luxury and self-interest -> unintended social effects; Smith is not just Mandeville with a new label.
[Independent topic source](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/mandeville/). Attributed controversial thesis, not proof that greed is good.

### 25. [Automata](https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/b0bk1c4d) · 2018-09-20
**When a mechanism looks alive.** A clock can animate a figure without explaining life. Let a convincing imitation generate a new question rather than automatically award the player a theory of mind.
Possible connection: Ancient devices, Renaissance clocks, mechanical accounts of bodies, and future machines can return to the difference between representation and explanation.
[Independent topic source](https://www.metmuseum.org/exhibitions/listings/2019/making-marvels-science-splendor/art-in-motion). Actual automata and astronomical displays are documented. Any continuity to later computing needs a separate source; apparent digestion must not be presented as real digestion.

### 26. [Malthusianism](https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/b011zzh6) · 2011-06-23
**An argument travels from society into biology.** A population argument meeting observed variation is a real cross-disciplinary turn, not a decorative borrowed manuscript. Its political implications must not be smuggled back as natural law.
Possible connection: Malthus -> Darwin and Wallace; later mutual-aid arguments challenge a competition-only picture.
[Independent topic source](https://www.darwinproject.ac.uk/view/letters/DCP-LETT-13238). Wallace’s letter acknowledges both naturalists’ debt to Malthus. That does not validate every Malthusian policy or make evolution a moral prescription.

### 27. [William Morris](https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/b0b9w0vq) · 2018-07-05
**More production is not the only future.** A beautifully made object can conflict with miserable work. Morris lets the same industrial inheritance generate rival futures: more output, more leisure, or more control over making.
Possible connection: Craft -> division of labour -> artistic and socialist criticism -> speculative workshop choices.
[Independent topic source](https://morrisarchive.lib.uiowa.edu/items/show/1872). Use Morris’s actual argument about useful work; do not reduce him to nostalgia or a blanket rejection of machinery.

### 28. [Walter Benjamin](https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/m0014710) · 2022-02-10
**A reproduced image changes its social life.** The photographic branch need not end at a better picture. Copies that reach a crowd can change authority, access and political persuasion.
Possible connection: Print -> photograph -> reproducible art -> public interpretation and propaganda.
[Independent topic source](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/benjamin/). Benjamin’s arguments about reproduction are historical theory, not a universal rule that copies destroy artistic value.

### 29. [Peter Kropotkin](https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/m0014pfr) · 2022-02-24
**Cooperation belongs inside the argument about life.** Keep mutual aid beside competition instead of forcing an evolutionary winner. The combination can change what a future community is designed to support.
Possible connection: Natural selection -> disputed social readings -> mutual aid -> cooperative institutions as a speculative choice.
[Independent topic source](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/4341/pg4341.html). Kropotkin’s argument is not a disproof of Darwin. Darwin did not reduce all living relations to combat; avoid that caricature.

## Design consequences, not a chapter plan

The game should braid several kinds of knowledge across the same desk: argument, observation, instrument, manuscript, image, institution and way of life. Greek philosophy stays a rich continuing backbone, not a set of isolated portraits. Connections to Arabic scholarship, Chinese material technologies and Indian astronomy belong where documented exchanges actually change a later question. They do not require unfamiliar standalone school chapters or a geographic quota.

The full-length architecture will be chosen from these recurring problems and the user’s original brief. No one-to-one mapping from 29 episodes to chapters is intended. Astronomy and perspective remain conceptual and visual arguments. Future technology grows out of this inherited world—including its unresolved objections.
