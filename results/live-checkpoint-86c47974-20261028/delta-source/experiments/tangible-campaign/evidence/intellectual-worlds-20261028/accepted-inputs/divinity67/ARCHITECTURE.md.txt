# Divinity: disputed questions — conceptual rebuild

This is a new world, not a wording patch. The compact 12-desk candidate and all old identities remain untouched. No checkout, publication or browser/native-UI work is part of this package.

## Player contract

Each desk begins with a recognisable problem, a named historical voice and a stake. Visible cards contain the essential premise, not a jargon name whose explanation is hidden in Read. Read adds one or two short paragraphs, authorship and scope. Combining means applying a distinction, challenging an inference, testing a case, or discovering the exact point of disagreement. It does not mean endorsing a belief or proving a miracle.

Cards retain the 96px renderer and will be measured with the actual model. The target is compact but adequate heads (usually 5–10 words); difficult claims get more words selectively. Detailed scholarly caveats live in readings, while material qualifications (who believes it; fictional case; no proof; no agreement) remain visible.

## Spine

1. Augustine: a healed wound and a stolen meal. Damage is not extra matter; bodily suffering is not guilt. Creation's goodness is a stated premise, not inferred from the wound.
2. Augustine: someone knows a duty but repeatedly fails; one infant is ill and one well. Teaching, inward help, personal acts and inherited condition are different questions. Reuse the damage/wrongdoing distinction to decide whether health identifies who needs forgiveness.
3. A thirteenth-century dualist text: a good maker and a harmful world. Construct its strongest causal objection, then contrast Augustine without forcing a verdict.
4. Aquinas: fire helps one thing and harms another; what reason can show. Reuse the dualist challenge and Augustine's loss account; separate explaining a belief from demonstrating it.
5. Aquinas reopens Augustine on the Trinity: relationship is not kind of being; three humans are not a model of one God. Reuse the earned boundary of reasoning. Not a newly invented chronological conversation.
6. A separate Lyon ritual witness: hands, a book, a spiritual claim and a demanding pledge. Reuse the origins disagreement; bodily practice does not by itself settle cosmology.
7. Aquinas: an axe makes a bench, water washes a body; can either explain inward change? Reuse Augustine's inward help and Lyon's embodied rite. Dependent action is not a rival god.
8. Aquinas: bread still looks and tastes like bread. Reuse reason's boundary and dependent agency; distinguish what is claimed to change from what is observed. No sensory proof of transubstantiation.
9. Luther 1517: a household has one coin; a seller offers assurance. Reuse dependent agency to distinguish declaring forgiveness from controlling it. Feeding a neighbour is a genuine competing claim, not decoration.
10. Luther 1520: if acceptance is a gift, why help? Reuse the earlier hungry household and Augustine's inward help without making their questions identical.
11. Marburg 1529: the same supper words, a remembered friend and an unbelieving guest. Reuse Aquinas's changed-bread claim and Luther's gifted acceptance; distinguish shared words, incompatible accounts and limited agreement.
12. Trent 1547: a gift offered, accepted, then lived out. Reuse Augustine's assisted choice and the earned household service; distinguish response from price and later growth from initial earning. Supply Luther's explicit exclusion of works increasing holiness before God, rather than mistaking temporal priority for incompatibility.
13. Calvin 1559: ask a living friend to pray; can a dead saint hear? A panel and a labelled bone create separate authority and evidence questions. Reuse the earlier distinction between dependent agency and permission for a particular practice.
14. Trent 1563: reopen the same prayer, panel and fragment under the council's premises. Reuse Calvin's objection; separate asking for prayer, worship, authenticity and authorisation. Better evidence does not erase a doctrinal dispute.

## Reactive chapter contract

Every new stage authors `onAlreadyComplete: {mode:'skip'}` or a short earned-fact `bridge`. No bridge carries a future recipe or invents an event. Current ordinary goals remain visible if only partly complete. Checkpoints contain real accepted actions, never injected discoveries.

## Verification scope

Actual-model schema/lifecycle/continuity validation, exact accepted checkpoint replay in both operand directions and two widths, restore/undo/Read nonmutation, label layout metrics, original-file hashes. Independent newcomer logical readers receive only look/Read/normal highlight and may stop diagnostically. Their success is not human enjoyment or UI evidence. No browser/native UI testing.
