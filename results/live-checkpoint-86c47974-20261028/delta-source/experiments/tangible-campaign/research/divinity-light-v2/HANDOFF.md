**Not published or registered.** Root is the sole integrator/publisher. Candidate root: `/workspace/shared/divinity-light-guide-20261028`.

## What changed, and why

The original eight-desk guide remains byte-identical as a diagnostic draft. An independent **logical** reader voluntarily stopped midway through its third desk: the readings were understandable and interesting, but assembling supplied claims into summary labels did not motivate another five desks. This was not a stuck route or a failure of normal highlights. The reader also identified a real content mismatch: a methods notebook became a purported Gospel copy.

The user then clarified that curriculum is an idea source, not a coverage checklist, and that one engaging module is enough. This successor is one bounded revision, not an attempt to obtain an AI “fun certificate.” It is aimed at a bright, curious student, with accessible readings for the wider grades 8–12 context.

- **Hillside:** Mark and John both explicitly report an extraordinary feeding, with people filled and leftovers. Their emphases stay distinct. Passover and “sign” are explained, and Catholic historical testimony is named alongside literary reading.
- **Print room:** an explicitly fictional sweeping headline is challenged by Luther’s actual thesis 43. A carried Luke passage—not a method summary—is reproduced verbatim as the same classroom paraphrase on a proof sheet and printed page. Earlier panels fit into a real four-slot exhibit whose parts remain visible.

The discoveries rest on inspectable short passages. The “invitation to critics” is labeled as an interpretation, not the only possible reading. The comparison between Luther and Luke is not presented as documented direct influence. This module is not a whole Gospel course, Reformation account, theology curriculum, or world-religions survey; those broad ambitions were deliberately dropped.
