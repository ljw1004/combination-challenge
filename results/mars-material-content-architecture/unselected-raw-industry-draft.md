# A larger Mars material ontology: useful intermediates, not a longer checklist

26 October 2026. **Prospective content architecture and representative chain, not an engine, route-length promise or play result.** The earlier exact paper screen and its lawful compression remain intact. Delivered Mars UI/content is untouched.

## Why the last route was small

The short paper campaign supplied heaters, solar cells, signal emitters, signal receivers, wire and structural frames as starting capabilities. Only ten learned canonical designs appeared in its ordinary route. Shared supply, remote placement and a better beacon location then compressed the installation further. That says a great deal about the **small authored content set and high-level starting vocabulary**. It does not demonstrate that inexhaustible concepts, disclosed recipes or a large reusable ontology cannot support richer construction.

The right next question is not “how can we forbid that capable camp?” It is:

> What useful things could the player enjoy making on the way to a capable camp, when the intermediate materials and devices also create understandable alternatives?

Lucian explicitly welcomed a large reusable ontology. His favorable feedback on the staged crash/orbiter concept/storytelling supports visible motivation and progress, not a numerical duration claim. Agent reports support shared-purpose creations and criticize repeating finished supply/copy setups. Those observations favor **more distinct constructive possibilities**, not repeatedly rebuilding the same small library.

## Concrete mission

**Turn a landing site into a dependable surface workshop and living base, with a powered link to the orbiter and a useful higher terrace.** The useful terrace offers light/exposure or contact advantages rather than a required visit stamp. The low ice seam, exposed collector ridge, sheltered living court and dry workshop give the same materials different jobs.

The actual mission endpoints should be few and functional: living conditions, clean elevated water where it is used, a dry working installation, and present communication. Do not separately demand every intermediate, every transformation, every device or a catalogue percentage. The player may solve them with direct mechanical work, electrical distribution, passive geometry, independent modules, or replacement. A modest elegant solution is valid.

The substantial chain below develops **water lifting and useful power**, then branches to lighting, communication, material processing and mobility. It is not a claim that one pump must be built in every solution. Gravity-fed service, direct mechanical pumping, moving the use site, or an alternative source must remain legal when their actual properties suffice.

## Content architecture: shared families rather than isolated recipe islands

Each node exposes public properties, form and compatible processes before combination. Named canonical recipes are examples of useful arrangements. Equivalent property/geometry constructions count even when their names differ. Every discovered concept remains inexhaustible; saved assemblies bypass repeated fabrication. Creation remains actual operand combination, not selecting a finished goal and delegating its manufacture.

| Family | Representative capabilities | Distinct useful consequences / reuse |
|---|---|---|
| **Raw matter and contexts** | ore, sand, clay, mineral grains, fibers, water/ice; actual solar, wind and thermal source sites | Different starting properties and available transformations. “Heat” as a disclosed place/process is not an invisible free perpetual power unit. No finite deposits or extraction quantities. |
| **Material transformations** | softened clay, fired porous ceramic, glazed ceramic; formed metal, wire, foil; clear glass, ground glass; pulp, membrane, insulating mat | Porosity versus sealing, conduction versus insulation, faithful transmission versus diffusion, rigidity versus flexibility. The same material can change use instead of accumulating decorative labels. |
| **Forms and interfaces** | sheet, tube, cavity, channel, mesh, curved surface, contact face, axle, joint | Shape affects actual contact, exposure, containment or movement. These are not required silhouette badges. A matching alternative form/tool remains valid. |
| **Energy conversion** | reflective collector, rotor, magnetic rotor/coil, generator, lamp, heater, motor | Actual source-supported effects; generator and motor are different uses of related electromagnetic structure, not separate magical success tokens. Direct wind-driven work remains a legitimate bypass. |
| **Fluid work** | porous separator, condenser, watertight conduit, impeller/pump, valve, receiving basin | Different changes to stream quality, elevation or routing. A pump matters only where actual elevation/work makes passive delivery insufficient. No liters, stockpiles, throughput quotas or water consumption budget. |
| **Structures and motion** | frame, supported enclosure, hinged panel, wheel/axle, carriage, span, deployable cover | Actual extent, support and clearances; saved assemblies and replacement remain legal. A vehicle is useful where movement serves a purpose, not because every object needs a cart attachment. |
| **Optical/electrical communication** | clear boundary, shaped lens, reflective surface, source/receiver, contact, relay, insulated lead | Present paths and controllable source/receiver relationships. Do not turn every destination into another photographic archive task. |
| **Working environments** | protected growing space, dry/warm surface, transparent wet vessel, shaded service area | These are outcomes of arranged parts and sources. They are not awarded by a named “greenhouse” or “workshop” recipe. Shared roofs, heat and supports are welcome. |

This is a content allocation, **not a list of mandatory discoveries or a count target**. Some candidate nodes may merge when their functional differences are too weak. A clear glass sheet and a polished lens should not be distinguished by a hidden quality flag; if focusing/faithful imaging is proposed, its visible geometry and effect must be explicit. Conversely, not every intermediate needs a unique late-game goal to justify comfortable construction or curiosity.

## Representative substantial chain: from local materials to elevated clean service

This is one publicly inspectable dependency chain and its branches. It specifies intended functional consequences, not a fully validated physics specification or final fixed sequence. Exact source, tool and geometry laws remain to be screened. These are game abstractions, not real manufacturing instructions.

The stages are not fifteen required objectives. One chosen route could omit electrical conversion, use a recovered casing, begin with a clean source or avoid the elevated terrace. Another may build electrical distribution because it will also power lighting, communication and heat. A first-time player can appreciate familiar steps without discovering a new strategy at each stage; a fluent player can reuse complete designs without reenacting them.

### Branches that make intermediates worth having

- The **same formed metal** can become a frame, foil reflector, wire or axle. These forms should enter different visible arrangements rather than all grant the same generic “advanced” property.
- **Clear glass** can protect a growing space, contain wet material or form a lens. Form and exposure, not a mandatory recipe lineage, determine whether it performs those jobs.
- The **porous ceramic** useful early as a filter may later be lined for a watertight housing. Keeping a porous replacement is a legitimate choice, not a prescribed original-object story.
- A **rotor/axle** can drive the pump directly, turn a generator, or move an actual carriage. This is a real architecture choice only if the same disclosed mechanical coupling works in all three contexts.
- A **source-supported electrical installation** can power a heater, lamp, motor or communication emitter. Those devices must have different actual effects; “connect four named consumers” alone would repeat the old network problem.
- A **valve or hinge** may control an already useful system. Static robust arrangements remain valid and may make operation unnecessary. The pending taught-host experiment is at most an optional way to run demonstrated controls while the creator pursues another role.

## Existing mechanics versus genuine additions

The current games already support inspectable processes, actual local surface/form changes, saved assemblies, source provenance, geometric support/contact/occlusion, and useful shared constructions. They do **not** already support all of the chain above.

In particular, a coherent implementation would need to decide whether to add **mechanical work/coupling**, **height-sensitive fluid service**, and **live communication**. These are substantial new laws, not content rows falsely presented as existing engine behavior. Do not add pressure equations, resource rates, fuel tanks or realistic manufacturing chemistry. A minimal property-and-port model can be public and deterministic, but must be defined consistently before claiming feasibility.

Avoid implementing all three new systems at once simply because the diagram is attractive. The next architecture screen should pick the strongest actual mission dependency and demonstrate its complete alternatives on paper. If mechanical conversion adds only extra names before an already sufficient free water link, remove it or revise the actual water law openly; do not hide a pump requirement inside a goal predicate.

## What makes this a richer ontology rather than padding?

A capability earns a distinct node when it changes what can be physically made, connected, exposed, transformed or operated, or when it is an intelligible constructive intermediate whose creation is itself worthwhile to test. It does not need a dedicated required task. However:

- A three-step process with identical intermediate/final properties needs a reason beyond increasing the action count.
- New forms need actual geometric differences; new materials need observable property differences.
- Equivalent substitutions and direct source/work pathways cannot be banned to protect the intended chain.
- Some sophisticated components may be available as recovered objects or complete-catalogue capabilities. The normal condition must state its starting level clearly; do not remove supplied devices after observing a short route.
- A single canonical output may bundle a useful familiar internal arrangement after discovery. Learned capability must genuinely reduce future setup rather than merely unlock a longer repeated fabrication animation.
- A recipe graph must be shared across purposes. Independent disjoint trees labeled “communication,” “water” and “mobility” are not evidence of transfer.

A large library can create wonder and comfortable progress even when an expert knows a short solution. That proposition needs actual reports. A perfect-knowledge control is useful for identifying required work and available shortcuts, not for declaring first-time creation worthless.

## Next finite deliverable before another engine

Specify one complete content sheet for this mission: starting capabilities; every offered material/form/device's public effects; actual site/source layout; the chosen minimal new law; and all functional endpoints. Include the representative chain above only where its steps survive that sheet. Then derive:

1. One complete ordinary route using its useful intermediates, with every actual combination and consequence listed.
2. A complete-catalogue route, a direct-mechanical/passive alternative and a reusable-rich-design route.
3. A small causal audit: which discovered capabilities affect several later purposes, which are comfortable enactment, and which are merely redundant prerequisites? Do not demand novelty from every action.
4. The actual resulting route lengths **without a numerical promise**. Do not fill missing duration by adding repeated consumers, arbitrary surface visits, exclusive recipes or operations after valid completion.

Only a coherent whole content model that survives this audit should become a new engine. Mechanical tests and fresh whole-game play would then establish actual scale, pacing and appreciation. The old short results are retained as evidence and reusable ideas—not counted as chapters already played in the new version.
