# The Kitchen Caravan — player-facing narrative packet

Travel with a kitchen caravan from a roadside breakfast through an orchard, a rainy market and the railway kitchens. Keep cooking in each kitchen while its people need you, then take your useful preparations onward. The journey ends by sending a real supper aboard the departing train. This fictional collection is not a chronology of invention; time, proportions and safe handling are compressed, not taught. A staffed pantry cart and managed cold storage keep saved ingredients and preparations under the cooks’ care between stops; some of the breakfast juice is reserved for the journey.

Opening supplies: Grain, Millstone, Water, Apples, Fruit press.
