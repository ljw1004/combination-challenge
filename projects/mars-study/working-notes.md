## Initial alternatives

| Approach | Attraction | Failure to test |
|---|---|---|
| Repair a delivered base | Immediate stakes, clear physical purpose | A succession of broken widgets and replacement supplies; little ownership beyond restoring someone else's work. |
| Travel from scientific site to site | Discovery and changing questions | Disconnected scenes, repeated sample collection and equipment deliveries; the v1 inventory problem. |
| Build a home and working laboratory from local resources | Substantial making, visible growth, investment in a place | Industrial processing can become a recipe conveyor belt; the science might arrive too late. |
| Repurpose earlier constructions when the situation changes | Recognition, transfer of understanding, earned solutions | Forced second uses or false physical continuity can make it feel like an author's puzzle. |

Working synthesis: build a place capable of doing science, then let the science and a naturally developing difficulty change what that place must do. A damaged landing can give the opening urgency; the middle needs constructive calm and investigative curiosity, not repeated failures. Return capability should be established early so fuel manufacture has a purpose before the ending.

## Combination philosophy to test

The player learns what familiar materials and processes can do: separating, shaping, heating, insulating, growing and examining. Complete tools do substantial work. Useful products cross chapter boundaries and acquire further jobs. Imported precision machinery and pressure containment remain plausible mission equipment; local bulk materials provide mass, shelter and feedstocks. A material does not quietly change form between uses.

Prefer several distinguishable production and investigation threads over one long assembly ladder. Make the next question understandable from visible inputs and the scene's goal. Chapter introductions establish situation and motivation in zero to two sentences; the combinations perform the consequential work.

## Evaluation hypothesis and falsifiers

A player should feel that the home and laboratory are theirs because their earlier constructions make later survival, investigation and departure possible. The surprising find should arise from a question they were already pursuing. A later difficulty should make earlier knowledge useful rather than supply another repair checklist.

Revise if fresh readers cannot explain why the work matters, if players repeatedly call steps obvious administrative delay, if the discovery is carried mainly by prose, if ingredients contribute only through an author's hidden explanation, or if sustaining 200 operations requires a stream of near-identical preparations. Measure actual arrival and peak working sets, not just starting declarations. Mechanical completion is necessary and insufficient.

## Review and iteration record

The independent narrative review chose making an enduring place as the backbone, a short recovery opening, and one field expedition motivated by the first water samples. The science review supported a compact imported workshop using local bulk feedstocks. Both rejected an improvised pressure vessel, unrealistic wind damage and a complete fuel-production programme appearing only at the ending.

Adopted: sunlight loss during a dust storm, with planned life-support backup that must also protect the expanded garden and a larger intact frozen archive. Rejected for this draft: extraction-induced foundation settlement. Its timing would require a stronger invented mechanism, and it risks making a competent builder look negligent. The storm should test a place the player has enjoyed, not destroy it and require rebuilding.

Scientific boundaries identified before drafting: separate the industrial water well from intact scientific cores; filtering does not remove dissolved salts; local ceramic work provides protection and support rather than pressure containment; plants need imported nutrients, air, controlled conditions and weeks of growth; methane and oxygen require supplied processing and separate storage. The carrier uses conditioned subzero phase-change packs and active refrigeration. Shade and insulation reduce heat input, and cannot manufacture cold. A warm thermal store only buffers a transition.

### Reviewer independence: correction

I initially reused earlier players for supposedly blind later-scene probes, then gave additional scenes to two newly created readers. The user correctly rejected that workflow. Fresh-agent creation had initially been blocked, but that did not authorize substituting remembered context. Those reused follow-ups are excluded from fresh-review evidence. They cannot establish that a scene or result was understood by an uncontaminated reader.

At the user's request, [story-design.md](../../docs/fun-and-evaluation.md#reviewer-independence) now requires a new agent with no inherited history for every review assignment and revision, in every role. A combination case has exactly two separately asked questions: what the reviewer would do next and why, followed by what a specified valid action would produce. After those two answers that agent is finished. Different scenes and revised versions require new agents. Story-beat reviews receive only a neutral recap through that point. Requests use plain English and state the context supplied. The project review instructions were corrected at the time to link to that rule instead of prescribing sequential blind packets; reusable instructions now live in the [evaluation guide](../../docs/fun-and-evaluation.md#reviewer-independence).

The replacement reviews use dedicated agents for individual cases and separate new agents for narrative, continuity and authenticity. A minimal-context bank case suggested chipping ice with the hammer, so the manuscript now accepts that natural alternative as well as shoveling. Separating the dry bank from the exposed ice makes collecting dry material in a jar predictable. A recovery case separates the player's preference to clean the solar rack from their correct prediction that refilling the drained pipe primes the well-to-cabin line. These are local predictions, not a full fresh playthrough; the separate historical outcomes report is no longer retained.


The final independent continuity review identified that insulating fresh hose had lost the connection to the already-operating well line. Insulation now transforms the connected well line itself. It also questioned nesting troughs that still held their medium and fittings; each cleaned trough is now placed separately on the storage rack. The new narrative review supports the central discovery and storm payoffs but still finds a long gap between the early geological question and the ice investigation, and repeated nursery/maintenance work near departure. Those remain pacing concerns for user evaluation, not findings erased by passing checks. The fresh authenticity review found no required change within the declared premises.
