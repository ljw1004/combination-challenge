# Mars study working notes

These notes follow the evolving Mars project. The entries below begin with the v3 story-design work; later project work continues here.

## Starting brief — 2 November 2026

The user confirmed a solo surface expedition and plausible future engineering, with a handful of explicit fictional allowances where useful. Steps 2 and 3 of the [story-design process](README.md#story-direction) are the assignment: develop the combination philosophy and arc, independently challenge them, draft actual combinations, evaluate and iterate. The target is approximately 200 meaningful combinations along a full playthrough. User evaluation follows this development pass; agent approval cannot substitute for it.

The v3 deliverable is a separate, playable text-first manuscript and its design material in [mars-v3/](mars-v3/README.md). The overall Mars project remains active. Detailed review responses, intermediate drafts, captures and play records are disposable scratch in `runs/mars-v3/`; these notes retain decisions and concise findings.

## Initial alternatives

| Approach | Attraction | Failure to test |
|---|---|---|
| Repair a delivered base | Immediate stakes, clear physical purpose | A succession of broken widgets and replacement supplies; little ownership beyond restoring someone else's work. |
| Travel from scientific site to site | Discovery and changing questions | Disconnected scenes, repeated sample collection and equipment deliveries; the v1 inventory problem. |
| Build a home and working laboratory from local resources | Substantial making, visible growth, investment in a place | Industrial processing can become a recipe conveyor belt; the science might arrive too late. |
| Repurpose earlier constructions when the situation changes | Recognition, transfer of understanding, earned solutions | Forced second uses or false physical continuity can make it feel like an author's puzzle. |

Working synthesis: build a place capable of doing science, then let the science and a naturally developing difficulty change what that place must do. A damaged landing can give the opening urgency; the middle needs constructive calm and investigative curiosity, not repeated failures. Return capability should be established early so fuel manufacture has a purpose before the ending.

## Combination philosophy to test

The player learns what familiar materials and processes can do: separating, shaping, heating, insulating, growing and examining. Complete tools do substantial work. Useful products cross chapter boundaries and acquire further jobs. Imported precision machinery and pressure containment remain plausible mission equipment; local bulk materials provide mass, shelter and feedstocks. A material does not quietly change form between uses.

Prefer several distinguishable production and investigation threads over one long assembly ladder. Make the next question understandable from visible inputs and the scene's goal. Chapter introductions establish situation and motivation in zero to two sentences; the combinations perform the consequential work.

## Evaluation hypothesis and falsifiers

A player should feel that the home and laboratory are theirs because their earlier constructions make later survival, investigation and departure possible. The surprising find should arise from a question they were already pursuing. A later difficulty should make earlier knowledge useful rather than supply another repair checklist.

Revise if fresh readers cannot explain why the work matters, if players repeatedly call steps obvious administrative delay, if the discovery is carried mainly by prose, if ingredients contribute only through an author's hidden explanation, or if sustaining 200 operations requires a stream of near-identical preparations. Measure actual arrival and peak working sets, not just starting declarations. Mechanical completion is necessary and insufficient.

## Review and iteration record

The independent narrative review chose making an enduring place as the backbone, a short recovery opening, and one field expedition motivated by the first water samples. The science review supported a compact imported workshop using local bulk feedstocks. Both rejected an improvised pressure vessel, unrealistic wind damage and a complete fuel-production programme appearing only at the ending.

Adopted: sunlight loss during a dust storm, with planned life-support backup that must also protect the expanded garden and a larger intact frozen archive. Rejected for this draft: extraction-induced foundation settlement. Its timing would require a stronger invented mechanism, and it risks making a competent builder look negligent. The storm should test a place the player has enjoyed, not destroy it and require rebuilding.

Scientific boundaries identified before drafting: separate the industrial water well from intact scientific cores; filtering does not remove dissolved salts; local ceramic work provides protection and support rather than pressure containment; plants need imported nutrients, air, controlled conditions and weeks of growth; methane and oxygen require supplied processing and separate storage. The carrier uses conditioned subzero phase-change packs and active refrigeration. Shade and insulation reduce heat input, and cannot manufacture cold. A warm thermal store only buffers a transition.

### First full draft

The exploratory manuscript contains 201 explicit recipes across 36 playable chapters and an ending. Its source-aware legal route completes with 185 combinations. That establishes mechanical feasibility, not player experience. It also exposes a peak of 11 items in the crop emergency and numerous opportunity warnings, so this draft is not ready for playtesting.

Priority revisions before freezing: eliminate duplicate physical identities for crops, the rover, the cold case and the core sections; make useful preparations happen where their motivating situation exists; replace recipes whose extra material appears only in an author note; reduce the many small laboratory stations; move basic backup preparations earlier; and remove token-like commissioning/analysis chains. The discarded draft and detailed checks remain temporary. Independent editors are reviewing its actual operations, not just its outline.

### User steering during revision

The user emphasized receiving locations as a way to distinguish operations on the same input. Contextual two-item combinations are harder for players and should be rare and compelling. The draft’s 19 contextual pairs were reduced to four: the first methane reaction, a physical mineral comparison, the two-well freezing comparison, and cold transfer between the occupied field case and flight carrier. Routine sampling, sieving, shaping, optical examination, food preparation and reconditioning now use ordinary pairs or one item at an explicit location. [Story authoring](../../docs/story-authoring.md#make-the-action-understandable) owns this authoring guidance.

The user also corrected the review priority assigned to crop durations. Sensible timing is welcome, but elapsed time is an accepted abstraction and must not constrain good combinations or create waiting chores. Timing cues remain where convenient; they are not an acceptance gate. Physical identity, a meaningful contribution from each input, and the player’s motivation remain more consequential review questions.

The user proposed building an item in one chapter and representing it as a hotspot in a later chapter. Adopted in three places: the completed solar rack becomes the next chapter’s array charging socket; the recirculating reservoir later waters the nursery through its tap; the insulated growing nook later receives the two threatened troughs. The player’s construction is established by an earlier goal, and the later hotspot is explicitly the same object’s working surface. No engine change or automatic identity conversion was needed. The standing authoring decision is recorded in [story authoring](../../docs/story-authoring.md#make-the-action-understandable).

### Physical and structural redraft

The two detailed composition reviews agreed on the main defects: unique crop/case/core states were being reused as copies, identical input pairs could silently select unrelated fabrications, some recipes had real ingredients only in author comments, and too much post-discovery work was packaging. The revised manuscript keeps two distinct troughs, one two-core field case, a separate sacrificed ordinary end section, and one final flight transfer. Known salt and adjacent-ice experiments replace repetitive protection states. Material stocks have public descriptions; uniquely occupied assemblies follow transformation chains.

The propellant plant now includes separation and storage from the start, explicitly recycles reaction water, handles separate boil-off during the storm, and restarts production afterward. The specimen’s cold cartridges are reconditioned before flight. The lamp itself receives its hood rather than producing a powered light while another copy survives. The installed water line, not a spare hose, is winterized. Distinct receiving fixtures express which piece is being shaped.

### First player and blind reading

A fresh player used ordinary observations and partner hints, performed 30 combinations and chose to stop at the next chapter’s arrival. The fan settling, delayed voice from Earth, brown band in the ice and nearly tasteless first drink earned attention. Dissolved salts remaining after filtration supported a concrete learning observation. The player wanted to investigate the band next; a further infrastructure chapter reduced their desire to continue. Two voluntary early investigations also produced names without a sufficient observed result. This is a negative pacing finding, not a failed completion quota.

A separate reader received nine player-facing packets sequentially and recorded expectations before seeing the next. The home details, geological inference, articulated body, preservation of enclosing ice, two fans during the storm and final greens carried the strongest interest. Early procedural construction, the first propellant sequence, cold-case assembly and rebuilding a nursery were weaker. The storm read as effective care for earlier work, but mainly contingency execution rather than a difficult decision. This is reading evidence, not interaction evidence.

Adopted in response: move the nearby grain investigation directly after the first water supply, interleave the fresh rock analysis with the first floor construction, remove an unnecessary grain-sieving step, give the earlier voluntary observations readable results, avoid a goal that answers the opening ice question, give the completed window curtain a sensory payoff, show the outgoing discovery message, state the reference core’s observed contrast, and rename the potato trough after backup power is no longer its defining state.

The resulting manuscript has 202 authored and played-route combinations. The second fresh player performed 37 combinations and stopped voluntarily after following the vein clue. Geology immediately after drinking water renewed interest, but the optional polished sections offered no readable finding. This supports the change of transition without establishing sustained enjoyment. The final pass gives the grain comparison and polished sections actual observations, removes the contrived ice-versus-frost question already answered by item names, and replaces system-facing stock explanations with physical descriptions. A narrow-screen check also prompted the shorter name “Chemically mapped cores.” These final prose changes have mechanical/browser checks, not another fresh-player verdict.

### Reviewer independence: correction

I initially reused earlier players for supposedly blind later-scene probes, then gave additional scenes to two newly created readers. The user correctly rejected that workflow. Fresh-agent creation had initially been blocked, but that did not authorize substituting remembered context. Those reused follow-ups are excluded from fresh-review evidence. They cannot establish that a scene or result was understood by an uncontaminated reader.

At the user's request, [story-design.md](../../docs/fun-and-evaluation.md#reviewer-independence) now requires a new agent with no inherited history for every review assignment and revision, in every role. A combination case has exactly two separately asked questions: what the reviewer would do next and why, followed by what a specified valid action would produce. After those two answers that agent is finished. Different scenes and revised versions require new agents. Story-beat reviews receive only a neutral recap through that point. Requests use plain English and state the context supplied. The project review instructions were corrected at the time to link to that rule instead of prescribing sequential blind packets; reusable instructions now live in the [evaluation guide](../../docs/fun-and-evaluation.md#reviewer-independence).

The replacement reviews use dedicated agents for individual cases and separate new agents for narrative, continuity and authenticity. A minimal-context bank case suggested chipping ice with the hammer, so the manuscript now accepts that natural alternative as well as shoveling. Separating the dry bank from the exposed ice makes collecting dry material in a jar predictable. A recovery case separates the player's preference to clean the solar rack from their correct prediction that refilling the drained pipe primes the well-to-cabin line. These are local predictions, not a full fresh playthrough; the separate historical outcomes report is no longer retained.


The final independent continuity review identified that insulating fresh hose had lost the connection to the already-operating well line. Insulation now transforms the connected well line itself. It also questioned nesting troughs that still held their medium and fittings; each cleaned trough is now placed separately on the storage rack. The new narrative review supports the central discovery and storm payoffs but still finds a long gap between the early geological question and the ice investigation, and repeated nursery/maintenance work near departure. Those remain pacing concerns for user evaluation, not findings erased by passing checks. The fresh authenticity review found no required change within the declared premises.

Final structure: 153 combinations on the full route, 154 authored recipes because either shovel or hammer can collect exposed ice, 200 item identities, and 40 chapters including the ending. There are 61 hotspot appearances, a maximum of three, with only two chapters exceeding two. The complete 320 px browser route passes. The historical outcomes distinguished these dedicated fresh cases from excluded reused-reader follow-ups and the earlier 202-combination handoff; that separate report is no longer retained.

### Chapter 1 model comparison — 2 November 2026

The user expanded the chapter protocol to eight players across four model families and clarified that each record needs two entirely fresh graders, making sixteen gradings. All 24 assignments used new agents without inherited conversation. The comparison recorded every timing and the frozen instructions; those separate reports are no longer retained, and detailed records were disposable verification material.

All records were usable under both graders. Terra and Luna took approximately the same time, but Luna wrote substantially more and followed an interface path unavailable in the browser: performing combinations with the opening message still open. This is a test-interface defect, not disobedience by the player. Both grading models noticed the resulting late cards and found the same small observational error in one Luna record. They agreed on 45 of 48 rubric labels; the differences concerned how to label limited choices and a temporary pacing problem. Astra High was faster on each pair, with the final pair effectively tied.

For further work, Terra Medium is the provisional player baseline and Astra High the grader. Luna merits a fresh comparison after the interface repair. Actual cost and resemblance to a less deliberative human remain unmeasured. The chapter's strongest payoff is the restored cabin fan; the weakest passage is processing material before its support purpose is clear. Earlier motivation and reducing a processing step are competing revisions to test, not changes adopted from this trial. The story and shared runtime remained frozen throughout.
