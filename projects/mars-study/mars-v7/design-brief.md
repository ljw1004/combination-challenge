# Mars v7 — A Place That Can Endure

This brief owns this draft’s intent. The [manuscript](mars.md) owns the playable work; the parent [story-design guidance](../README.md#story-direction) records the user’s direction. The [game’s philosophy](../../../README.md#philosophy), [authoring guide](../../../docs/story-authoring.md), [format specification](../../../docs/story-format-spec.md) and [UX](../../../docs/ux.md) govern the shared framework. This is a candidate for user evaluation, not an accepted replacement for the illustrated Mars.

## The player and the promise

You are the only person on the surface, commissioned to establish a locally supplied research station, investigate the nearby ice and bring samples back. Complete pressure, flight, life-support and precision laboratory equipment were sent ahead. The landing compromises the external solar installation, not every system at once. You restore a dependable beginning, then make the surroundings, supplies and connections that turn delivered equipment into a place you can live and work.

The hook is ownership through useful making: the water under the dust becomes a drink, a garden and a way home. The material that sustains the home also holds an extraordinary past. A tiny, long-dead articulated body is the discovery, never a companion, a living pet or a reason to collect photographs.

There are three continuing threads: the home and garden; investigating what the water carries; and bringing the discovery home. A dust storm tests the garden and interrupts fuel production. Sample storage remains reliable and does not become a storm substory. Geography stays simple: the station and its nearby resource bank/channel, one field exposure reached by the same rover, and the adjacent return pad. Individual workshop fixtures and laboratory stations are receiving locations within those places, not separate expeditions.

## What combining means

Make and adapt physical things through a small set of recognizable operations: salvage, shape, separate, heat, insulate, grow, examine and preserve. Imported precision equipment does substantial work; local feedstocks and reused packing supply much of the bulk. The player should see why both inputs belong together before reading the result.

A stock of metal, mesh, insulation or clean jars can support several constructions. A unique planted trough, occupied sample case or core cannot quietly produce another copy of itself. The same two crop troughs survive the storm and yield the eventual harvest. The first collapsed body stays sealed on its own membrane. An untouched core from the unheated margin of the known water source stays in the laboratory freezer. A second, remote core travels in the field case and reveals a whole body. Both sealed cores move into separate cradles of one real flight carrier; the original membrane travels in its own specimen locker. There is no comparison token, warming experiment or cold-storage crisis.

Build around actual components and supports: metal around shaped root-bed liners or a refrigeration insert, a bench on fired bricks, and coarse mesh around the growing potato bed. Shared hand tools do ordinary shaping and joining. Avoid a specialized former, mold or jig named after each desired product. Fine screening mesh and coarse cargo mesh have different physical jobs; a new material name must represent a real difference. Follow the [authoring guidance](../../../docs/story-authoring.md#make-the-action-understandable) on using contextual two-item recipes sparingly. The retained contextual pair reacts two gases inside the reactor. Final core handling occurs entirely within the cold-transfer enclosure and uses ordinary item combinations to fill two real carrier cradles. Use the shared hotspot limits to keep most chapters within two hotspots. The two exceptions have three distinct jobs: charging/radio/window work, and the two electrolyzer outlets plus atmospheric capture.

Completed equipment can become part of the place. The recovered solar rack becomes the next chapter’s charging socket; the recirculating nutrient reservoir supplies a later nursery through its tap; the built growing nook later receives the two threatened crops from their normal sunny shelf. The shelter under the bench is a distinct space, prepared before the storm. These hotspots represent earlier constructions, not newly supplied copies. Earlier goals establish the construction before its receiving surface is introduced.

The original scale target was approximately 200 worthwhile combinations. Tightening the story takes priority over reaching that count; any expansion must add substantial story-driven making. The [draft README](README.md) records the current measured structure. A small crop harvest and the body reveal may be short; a useful construction can take longer. Elapsed time is an accepted genre abstraction. Convenient cues establish a field season and crop growth without timers, waits or resource quantities.

## Narrative progression

| Passage | Work the player performs | Reason to want the next passage |
|---|---|---|
| Shelter, water, planting | Recover the array; restore contact and a warm window; establish treated water; make and plant root beds | Meet needs in a clear order and give the crops time to grow |
| A working home | Build protection and furniture; recycle water; eat the first leaves and store warmth | Feel the station becoming yours, with something alive in it |
| What the water carries | Clear and reset the clogged raw-water inlet; retain its flushed material and examine it under the microscope | A collapsed, long-dead body changes the mission; Earth requests untouched ice from the known source and a remote exposure |
| The ground we know | Core the unheated margin of the water bank and store that liner in the laboratory freezer | Preserve a known-source specimen before the longer expedition |
| A way back | Earth expedites return; commission the supplied demonstration/contingency plant for electrolysis, methane, water recovery and separate cryogenic stores | Production can continue while the rover retrieves untouched ice from the sheltered exposure |
| One field expedition | Make a refrigerated case; build a short supported rover crossing; take the protected band core | Bring the unknown thing home still inside its ice |
| Something folded | Make an optical window and examine a whole body in undisturbed ice | See the posture and surroundings the intake residue lost; keep both actual cores for the receiving laboratory |
| Darkness | An explicit Earth storm warning prompts industrial shutdown and garden protection; adapt watering and lighting | Care for the living home with earlier making and knowledge |
| Sunlight and departure | Restore production and cooling reserves; harvest potatoes; fill the flight tanks; transfer the real sample and take a fresh meal | Leave Mars and bring the actual find home |

Chapter openings show observed circumstances or delayed radio exchanges. Mara, NASA surface support, reacts to reports, relays the science team’s request and names the dust-storm threat explicitly; she does not narrate future discoveries or dictate every recipe. The decisive events happen through combinations and short resulting observations. Shelter, water and sowing precede sustained investigation. A blocked raw-water strainer makes maintenance necessary. The complete service operation restores flow and retains the flushed residue; that residue goes directly to the microscope after particle separation. It does not contaminate the treated supply. Earth then asks for untouched ice first from the known source, then from a farther promising exposure. The geology hammer remains a practical material-processing tool, without a separate local geology substory. Earth’s response motivates both the longer rover trip and the fuel plant, after the discovery. Radio delay prevents real-time supervision of a seconds-long emergency. No passive crewmate needs rescue and no other person becomes an inventory object.

Departure is a direct conclusion. Harvest, flight fuel and the actual cold transfer matter; winterizing, cleaning and stowing the station, seed storage and provision for a future occupant do not form a playable substory. The garden earns its place through the astronaut's season and meals. The planned year-long surface program originally ends with scheduled descent delivery of ascent propellant from an orbital depot to the surface station. The astronaut takes down the year-long survey calendar when the early-return order arrives. An approved earlier ascent and rendezvous can instead use the supplied local-propellant plant, which takes further months to fill the tanks. This is a future mission-logistics premise, not a claim that interplanetary transfer windows can be chosen freely.

## A small fiction ledger

1. **Mature future expedition hardware.** The mission has complete, compatible pressure systems, a rover, a solar workshop, water treatment, instruments, a regenerative electrical reserve, an integrated propellant plant and qualified return hardware. This is an equipment premise, not a claim that a lone person can fabricate precision machinery from regolith. Manufacturing rates, total launch mass and exact storage capacities are abstracted.
2. **A favorable surveyed site and process.** Accessible layered ice, local material that can be processed into useful non-pressure ceramics occur together. The same non-pressure ceramic bricks can be crushed, washed and tested for root support; this surveyed feedstock’s suitability is fictional. This particular combination of geology and practical performance is fictional; local trials make the assumptions visible.
3. **An extraordinary preserved organism.** Ancient life produced a millimetre-scale, partly mineralized articulated body, exceptionally preserved inside the sampled layer. The collapsed microscopic body on the first membrane and the second articulated body preserved in an untouched core make it extraordinary in the fiction. They do not establish a real Martian organism, a terrestrial taxon, an absolute age, pristine DNA or freedom from every possible contamination route.

## Scientific boundaries and sources

| Topic | What the game teaches and what it assumes |
|---|---|
| Accessible ice | Orbital measurements can identify promising subsurface ice, with limits on depth and spatial resolution. The exact accessible bank is invented. Keep the heated resource borehole distinct from the clean scientific exposure. [SWIM overview](https://swim.psi.edu/about.php?section=overview). |
| Local ceramics | Concentrated-sunlight sintering of lunar simulant is a demonstrated concept; that does not qualify this Mars feedstock, its strength or a pressure vessel. Blocks, equipment feet and removable service covers are non-pressure construction around an imported pressure cabin. [ESA solar-brick demonstration](https://www.esa.int/Enabling_Support/Space_Engineering_Technology/Printing_bricks_from_moondust_using_the_Sun_s_heat). |
| Water | A cloth separates suspended particles, not dissolved salts. The complete supplied processor performs the stages needed for its declared use. The story does not teach that clear or merely heated water is potable. Martian contaminants are a reason for deliberate treatment, not extra paperwork. [NASA perchlorate research](https://www.nasa.gov/general/detoxifying-mars/). |
| Crops | Controlled root aeration, imported nutrients, light and a pressurized cabin support the crops. The small garden supplements stored food. Local granules support roots; they do not provide a complete diet for plants. A cover shades developing potato tubers while leaving foliage exposed; the supplied shallow lettuce bed can use passive wicking, while the deeper potato module retains powered aeration. These are properties of the chosen systems, not universal species rules. [NASA deep-space crop research](https://www.nasa.gov/science-research/nasa-plant-researchers-explore-question-of-deep-space-food-crops/), [NASA growing systems](https://www.nasa.gov/exploration-research-and-technology/growing-plants-in-space/), [Iowa State: tuber light exposure](https://yardandgarden.extension.iastate.edu/faq/why-are-some-my-potato-tubers-green), [Nebraska: passive hydroponics](https://extensionpubs.unl.edu/publication/g2358/2023/html/view), [Oklahoma State: hydroponic media including brick shards](https://extension.okstate.edu/fact-sheets/hydroponics). The latter supports that terrestrial material class, not untreated Martian brick safety. |
| Propellant | Water electrolysis supplies hydrogen and oxygen. The plant automatically polishes its treated-water feed to its electrolyzer’s requirements. Hydrogen reacts with captured carbon dioxide to make methane and water; the product water returns to the process. External energy and additional local water remain necessary. Methane and oxygen stay in separate compatible systems. [NASA demonstration plant](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19970026777). |
| Cryogenic shutdown | The qualified stores retain some liquid while separately venting boil-off outdoors during the storm. Restarted production replaces those losses before departure. Insulation does not guarantee indefinite storage without cooling. [NASA cryogenic-storage explanation](https://www.nasa.gov/isam/robotic-refueling-mission-3/). |
| Scientific inference | Microscopy of intake residue reveals a collapsed long-dead body. Earth requests untouched cores from the unheated margin of that known source and a farther exposure in the same broad mapped deposit; orbital data cannot resolve an individual fine band. The later core preserves another body and its undisturbed surroundings. Anatomy motivates exceptional attention without proving age, native origin, DNA or a terrestrial taxon. Further characterization belongs to the receiving laboratory. |
| Frozen preservation | A body-bearing core stays near −45 °C in supplied active refrigeration. The −40 °C phase-change cartridges provide transfer margin. These are fictional equipment setpoints, not universal Martian-brine temperatures or a demonstrated preservation limit. No destructive warming test is required. Active refrigeration conditions the transfer buffers; warm and cold stores remain separate. Liner access opens only inside the contained cold enclosure. [NASA cold-stowage systems](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20110010968). |
| Storm | Airborne dust reduces sunlight. Cleaning settled panel dust becomes useful after the sky clears; thin Martian air does not justify cinematic hurricane damage. The existing reserve keeps the planned mission and qualified sample storage safe, while the player adapts the expanded garden. Thermal storage buffers a transition rather than sustaining an indefinite storm. [NASA dust-storm explanation](https://www.nasa.gov/solar-system/the-fact-and-fiction-of-martian-dust-storms/). |

These sources support processes and boundaries, not the feasibility of the complete fictional installation. No claimed engineering certification or quantified human survival model is implied.

## Presentation and evaluation

The science follows a direct dramatic line: repair the blocked water inlet, find the first body in its residue, recover untouched ice from two motivated locations, and bring all three specimens home. The original membrane and two frozen cores remain separate actual objects. Separate samples remain separate. Sequential goals can guide examining one and then another without constructing a comparison object. Retain a scientific operation only when its observed result changes the question or the practical next step.


Use text items on a neutral surface while testing the story. Future artwork must make fixed fixtures part of the station, preserve proportional size and silhouette, and visibly follow the same crops, case and core. The existing [Mars artwork study](../README.md) and [icon guide](../../../docs/icon-guide.md) remain the reference; this draft does not generate or commit new illustrative artwork.

The experience hypothesis is that earlier making earns later attachment and capability. Reject passages that feel like equipment assembly delaying the interesting work, repetitive sample-handling certification, or a sequence solved without attention to purpose. Examine the distinction between intelligibility through partner hints and actual scientific or practical inference. The [project working notes](../working-notes.md) retain iterations and concise findings. The shared [evaluation guide](../../../docs/fun-and-evaluation.md) defines reviews and playtests; the [standard authoring workflow](../../../docs/story-authoring.md#check-and-preview-a-draft) covers shared lint, analysis and preview commands.
