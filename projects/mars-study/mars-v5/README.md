Shelter, water and planting come first. Brown inlet material leads to a local sampling trip and a body under the microscope. NASA’s delayed response asks for untouched ice and expedites return; the supplied fuel plant runs while the rover makes the harder field trip. A dust storm tests the garden, then the harvest and actual specimens travel home. Metal construction uses real components instead of specialized formers.
