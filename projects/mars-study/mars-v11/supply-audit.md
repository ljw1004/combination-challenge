# Mars v11 — supply and location audit

The current manuscript has **45 primitive item identities: 44 Earth-origin and one Mars-origin**. Earth-origin includes reused cargo and containers, ordinary materials, seeds and necessary precision machinery. Blocks, iron, supports and many handling tools are made descendants, so they are not additional primitive supplies. This is an inventory-design count, not a launch-mass estimate.

The budget is at most 50. A plural material or component supply represents multiple real compatible pieces; a unique spacecraft, crop, specimen or assembled machine cannot be copied. An integrated factory unit must be a coherent physical assembly, not a collection of unrelated conveniences.

**30 of the 45 primitives occur in one combination or slot operation.** Every such item is identified by the count column above and has its justification in the final column. A single assembly operation can establish a machine used throughout the story through its later working surfaces. The final arming-plug procedure is an intentional emotional exception: fitting the real plug and pressing START lets the player initiate ascent. The panel’s one explicit recipe is additional to its four slot operations. Routine accessories removed after this audit are recorded below. Counts include authored combinations and implicit compound-slot operations, not quantities or launch mass. Established supplies also serve bundled routine assembly: steel and cable at the solar extension, spare control blocks and a distinct test cylinder at the reactor, ceramic under the replacement leg, and fresh cloth at the arrays. These uses remain physical in the result prose even when they are not separate inventory gestures.
