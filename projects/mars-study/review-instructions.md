## Recognition

Prepare unnamed, shuffled sheets from the delivered transparent PNGs at one quarter of their intrinsic dimensions, on a neutral ground, with neutral identifiers. Keep the answer key separate. Ask a reviewer who has not seen the targets for the best interpretation, confidence, alternatives and visible evidence. Accept a recognizable physical family separately from an exact material, preparation state or inventory role. Do not treat an inference about a scientific conclusion as something a picture can establish.
