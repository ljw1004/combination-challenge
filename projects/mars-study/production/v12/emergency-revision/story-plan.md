# Emergency revision — story plan

Implemented story plan for the user's 32-point response to the 26-chapter v12 draft, reconciled with the subsequent material, water and austerity directions on 4 November 2026. The revised sequence has **24 chapters**: move electrolysis after discovery and metal-making, merge the two fuel-building chapters, remove heat storage, retain the merged harvest/supper scene, and end over the departing base. The latest austerity pass removes the cave baffle and potato collar, and restricts equipment to specifically sourced useful parts. This records the implemented graph, not artwork approval. Adopted lasting premises belong in the [v12 design brief](../../../mars-v12/design-brief.md); check outcomes belong in this revision's [README](README.md).

Use the [water and garden consultation](science-water-garden.md) and [metal and fuel consultation](science-metal-fuel.md) for mechanisms and evidence limits. The user permits modest water-treatment fiction and straightforward iron-canister strength for the game. **The material is Iron recovered from the red ground, with no char, carbon adjustment or alloying.** Native meteorites and large salvaged steel stock were rejected. The only early steel supply is the small saved pin used as the electromagnet's core.

### Metal, electrolysis and methane

NASA's early-return request comes after the intact outline and establishes why machinery and propellant now matter. Metal precedes electrolysis, so remove every dependency on an already-operating hydrogen supply from metal-making.

The material begins as **iron-bearing red regolith**. The saved `steel-pin` plus winding-compatible power cable makes the separator electromagnet, which concentrates minerals rather than making metal. Adapt the same solar furnace with `laboratory-electrodes`, then fit `power-cable`; only the resulting `powered-reduction-furnace` accepts the concentrate to produce **Iron** (`iron-stock`). Sunlight melts the mineral bath; current removes oxygen. The two bounded advances are a compact capable furnace and a selected feed/process producing workable iron. There is no previous hydrogen, imported carbon/flux, crop-derived char, alloying or steel-making. Precious biological carbon remains conserved. The user accepts simple iron-canister fabrication and sufficient strength, without player testing or qualification chores.

`iron-stock` at `salvaged-cell-internals` makes the compact divided `water-electrolysis-cell`; its water and power slots commission it. Iron plus `hydrogen-valve` or `oxygen-valve` makes the two physically distinct empty cylinders, which fill at the same fully commissioned cell. Small recovered fittings resolve the one-result grammar without turning internal chemistry into a puzzle. Filled `hydrogen-cylinder` and `oxygen-cylinder` are portable. Keep oxygen alive for ascent; do not install it into a fixed fuel-plant compound.

Use the [finite donor allocation](science-metal-fuel.md#finite-precision-parts-explicit-donor-assignments), also adopted in the brief. The furnace's mineral experiment supplies the high-temperature insert; the regenerative-power experiment supplies one unused water-electrolyzer insert; the gas analyzer's retired carbon-analysis channel supplies its methanizer catalyst, heater/sensor and sample-drying fittings. One repair controller and temperature/pressure service readout moves between scheduled batches. The atmospheric branch supplies separated pressurized CO₂; allocated gas-service spares supply four dedicated valve/connector sets. No active breathing hardware, emergency oxygen, microscope or specimen cooling is cannibalized. Useful pressure and throughput are explicit mission premises, not hidden compressor boxes.

Every part must be compact original equipment, a finite repurposed component or the minimum local construction. No ornamental retro-industrial hardware. Show plain Iron canisters with rounded ends, simple formed seams, one short grey reused valve and a hand-marked gas identity. Remove handles, brass, individual gauges, bolted nameplates and foot rings. The cell uses cleared ground, a flat rock or its packed lab tray; the plain fuel chamber uses simple ceramic or rock rests. The furnace receiver is refractory containment, not an appliance housing. No manufactured outdoor table, decorative legs or duplicate control cabinets.

The merged fuel scene has no extra array, brick platform, frame-only crafting or supplied “processor core” primitive. The compound `fuel-assembly` accepts four independent fittings: Iron (fabricating its central chamber), hydrogen cylinder, CO₂ cylinder and power cable. Iron plus `carbon-dioxide-fitting` makes the empty CO₂ cylinder, filled at the existing scrubber's atmospheric branch. Iron plus `methane-fitting` makes the separate methane receiver. Catalyst and small process internals are included in chamber fabrication. **Oxygen never enters the methane reactor.**

Methane collection must require the completed feed/power/chamber state through a qualified recipe operand, rather than `next:` goal wording alone. Its portable methane-cylinder result and the existing oxygen-cylinder result survive to filling the lander. The air-derived CO₂ source, recovered process water, heat rejection and prolonged production are bundled mechanisms, with the engineering consultation owning their limits. The original array has an explicit load/time assumption; removing the expansion does not create free power.

Fuel transfer needs two fixed lander ports and two actual carried cylinders. ### One basket, one merged harvest/supper scene

Retain two fixed targets: the mature three-bed assembly and its actual preparation sink. Retain the earlier explicit merged-scene decision. Three independent crop targets plus the sink would violate the four-hotspot limit, so this revision deliberately trades harvest order freedom for a clear sequence using the same basket:

```text
Lined basket @ Mature beds → Potato harvest in basket
Potato harvest in basket [preserve] @ Mature beds → Lettuce harvest in basket
Lettuce harvest in basket [preserve] @ Mature beds → Soybean harvest in basket
Soybean harvest in basket [transform] @ Sink → Washed and soaked beans
Potato harvest in basket + Washed and soaked beans → Cooked stew
Cooked stew + Lettuce harvest in basket → Stew and lettuce supper
```

Each continued harvest unloads the previous crop onto the clean counter and reuses the same basket; the retained crop token represents that set-aside load. Use the same unmistakable cloth-lined net sling in all three harvest images, with the readings stating the unloading clearly. The crops are never combined into an abstract harvest bundle.

**Beans are gathered last.** Their only downstream use is washing at the sink, so this operation can actually retire the raw harvested-bean identity in every legal order. Potatoes and lettuce remain available for cooking/serving. There is no bean-to-stew bypass, and `next:` alone is not the enforcement mechanism. Keep the rendezvous hold, thoroughly cooked stew, fresh lettuce and the astronaut's supper recording.

The finale shows the original ascent cabin 30–60 metres above the recognizable completed base, with the lower stage/repair left behind, the cave mouth, original array and ice-bank geography coherent. It is a departure scene rather than an orbital docking picture. Adjust final prose to that moment; avoid saying “Docked” against a launch illustration. The protected specimens and the work left below supply the closing reward.
