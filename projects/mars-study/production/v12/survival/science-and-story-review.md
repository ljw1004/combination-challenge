## Growing and food

**Keep direct metered drippers in porous medium.** Moisture-sensor control can deliver small pulses as demand changes: [Nemali and van Iersel's experiment](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.scienta.2006.07.009) maintained different substrate moisture levels over a prolonged trial with little or no water wastage. This was a terrestrial bedding-plant experiment, not proof of sealed Mars potato/soybean/lettuce beds. Use supplied sensing, pulse valves, purified feed and a tested nutrient programme as explicit future assumptions; keep roots in damp, air-containing pores rather than standing water. Draw a feed connection, small lines and dropper tips, without a collection basin or return plumbing.

Matching water supply to uptake does not itself balance salts. [Fujimaki and colleagues](https://doi.org/10.14976/jals.28.S_115) found salt accumulation requiring leaching in a different capillary-fed tomato system using saline water. That result illustrates the limit, not a requirement to add a drain token here. The story assumes suitable feed purity, monitored finite crop cycles and routine medium servicing. Compatible cultivars, enough root volume and nutrient supply including soybean nitrogen remain declared premises; shared plumbing need not mean one unchanging dose for every crop.

Keep the large rear-wall sink for medium preparation and the single bean-soaking action, with ordinary cleaning between jobs and wastewater separate from crop feed. Soaked soybeans still need thorough cooking; [University of Arkansas soybean preparation guidance](https://www.uaex.uada.edu/farm-ranch/special-programs/Education_in_Agriculture/Docs/access%202019%20_3%20Cooking%20Soybeans.pdf) distinguishes those operations. The final food combination bundles cooking and lettuce washing. The same basket and clean food cloth handle harvest; no separate washing, cooking, knife, colander or service sequence is needed. This is an action abstraction, not a claim that food preparation is unnecessary.

## Materials, fuel and heat

**Keep reduction and steel refining distinct inside the same supplied solar furnace.** [HYBRIT's hydrogen reduction](https://www.hybritdevelopment.se/en/a-fossil-free-development/direct-reduction-hydrogen-pilotscale/) makes sponge iron and water; its [subsequent melting stage](https://www.hybritdevelopment.se/en/a-fossil-free-development/smelting-in-eaf/) is a different process. [DOE's high-flux solar furnace](https://www.energy.gov/cmei/h2awsm/high-flux-solar-furnace) demonstrates high temperature over a small target, not useful Mars steel throughput. The story therefore declares controlled atmosphere, sufficient metal-processing temperature, carbon/flux, crucibles/molds, handling/finishing and material checks. Favorable feedstock, compact output and qualified properties remain fictional mission premises. The local steel serves modest sheet construction and a short support, not arbitrary precision manufacture.

**Show an actual factory reactor and actual factory cryogenic stores.** A [NASA Sabatier system study](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20180004697) treats the reaction within a complete process plant; [NASA integrated processing work](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20130009664) also couples reaction, separation and water recovery. The module must contain thermal management, separation, methane drying and recycle rather than appearing from a catalyst or hose. With water-derived hydrogen, ideal net chemistry is `CO2 + 2H2O + energy → CH4 + 2O2`; product-water recovery reduces demand but does not close the net water balance.

[NASA cryogenic-fluid work](https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/cryogenicfluidmanagement-factsheet.pdf) concerns storage, transfer and control of ultra-cold fluids. Each supplied store retains its own qualified vessel, refrigerator, insulation, vent and compatible gas path. The common **Hose** identity denotes dedicated suitable stock, never one methane-contaminated line reused for oxygen. Plant capacity, power, heat rejection and early-return logistics remain future assumptions. The separate local open water tank is a low-temperature, room-pressure buffer with a sealed coolant coil: useful supplemental heat, with losses, not a source of energy or cooking heat.

## Ground, structures and specimens

**Keep the rover grounded.** A shallow erosion channel and short loose ascent make slipping/rollback legible. [Opportunity's difficult climb](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/news/rover-takes-on-steepest-slope-ever-tried-on-mars/) demonstrates severe slip, while its [nearby ridge ascent](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/news/opportunity-mars-rover-goes-six-wheeling-up-a-ridge/) shows that angle alone is no universal threshold. [JPL's Axel work](https://robotics.jpl.nasa.gov/how-we-do-it/systems/the-axel-rover/) provides a tethered-mobility precedent, not qualification of this crewed procedure. Draw the continuous rover route, firm astronaut access shelf, sound bedrock horn, straight pull and rounded crest. Site stability, anchoring, winch/tow ratings and remote low-speed control are assumed surveyed/equipment capabilities.

The shovel clears only modest loose grit and small stones on a naturally near-level cave floor or shallow exterior pad. Pressure fabric needs supplied rated seams/restraint, airlock and services despite its thin appearance. The small rollable lander rock bears at a sound low contact; later a low ceramic footing and short steel compression support replace it under controlled service lifting. Actual loads, contact bearing and material suitability are mission assumptions, not established by a plausible drawing.

**A thin liner contains ice; it does not refrigerate it.** [Observed Martian ice scarps](https://www.usgs.gov/publications/exposed-subsurface-ice-sheets-martian-mid-latitudes) expose layered deposits but actively retreat by sublimation. The low non-overhanging sampled margin is an invented surveyed site. The [NSF Ice Core Facility](https://icecores.org/about) uses refrigerated examination and storage, supporting an uninterrupted cold chain: sealed corer liner → rover cold cradle/transfer buffer → laboratory refrigeration → enclosed cold-microscope stage → storage → flight cradle. A clear optical liner, suitable ice clarity/inclusion position and compact qualified transfer are future-instrument assumptions. No case construction, window-making, slicing or melting is necessary in this story. The whole articulated body is fictional evidence; morphology does not establish age, terrestrial taxonomy, chemistry or native Martian origin. Keep membrane, source core and remote core distinct.

## Editorial consequences and pending checks

Complete operations can bundle compatible connections, ordinary checks and routine handling while leaving the real receiver visible. The adopted order is pressure before light, farm before discovery, return engineering before harvest/supper, and specimen loading after the meal. Late suitable ore, growing crops ready for tending, dust-covered arrays, the repair-stage footing site and mature harvest crops represent prerequisites through physical availability/state; they do not add extra player actions. These gates also keep later installed structures from appearing as portable results on earlier boards.

Artwork must still demonstrate these distinctions at playing size, and semantic/browser verification must check the integrated manuscript and independent orders. These consultations make no claim that that work is complete or that human recognition, pacing and enjoyment have been established.
