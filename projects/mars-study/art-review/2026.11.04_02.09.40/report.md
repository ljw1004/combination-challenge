# Survival scene artwork: independent informed review

Outcome-record time: **2026-11-04 02:09:40 PST**. The review-start time was not captured; the directory timestamp identifies this outcome record, not the start of the review.

## Scope and conditions

A fresh independent AI reviewer inspected the current survival scene deliverables using the [icon guide](../../../../docs/icon-guide.md). At the start of the assignment, the reviewer had not seen these proof sheets, made the artwork, or read previous review answers. Scene titles, state names and the physical requirements below were supplied, so this was an informed art-direction audit, not a blind recognition test.

Inputs were all 24 current JPG sheets in the temporary `runs/mars-survival/proofs/` directory. Each sheet presented scenes at their intended **390 px width**: background alone, every individual fixed appearance, then all declared appearances in declaration order. Coverage was **93 individual fixed-appearance views**, plus 24 backgrounds and 24 all-appearance diagnostics, for 141 panels. Mutually exclusive states in the final diagnostic panel were not treated as legal gameplay states.

Exact selected scene sources and hashes are recorded in [selection.json](../../production/v12/survival/selection.json); source bounds, registration and target regions are recorded in [registration.json](../../production/v12/survival/registration.json). The title/state scope below identifies the reviewed outputs. Only the dashboard registration entry was consulted to resolve the dashboard-only target requirement; no source masters were needed. Temporary sheets and raw review answers are not retained with this report. This audit does not cover the separately checked new core portable.

The assignment was to identify concrete meaningful defects in whole silhouettes, scene fit, replacement coverage, complete cable/hose routes, retained construction, stable camera and independent fittings. Specific requirements included habitat inflation before power; a small supporting rock; persistent paved/padded cave flooring and a near-flat tent before inflation and lighting; direct drippers and a large rear sink; a far-bank grounded winch with complete rope/hook; the same furnace without a press; manufactured reactor and cryotanks; an opaque open-top steel tank with a separate coil circuit and continuous wall tubes; a modest lander repair; two independent transfer hoses; a retained dish; and a dashboard-only target with a protruding plug.

## Outcome

**Keep all 24 scenes. No concrete blocking art defect was identified in the inspected deliverables.** No essential silhouette clipping, exposed unfinished forms or meaningful registration jumps were apparent.

The cave tent visibly starts near-flat, satisfying the roughly quarter-height intent, then becomes an unlit inflated enclosure before receiving lights. The prepared floor remains visible as smooth padding through these states. The solar/scrubber cables and gully winch rope are complete at scene scale, including the winch hook. Later outdoor scenes retain the dish where the habitat is visible. The heat-buffer tank reads as opaque steel with an open top; the coil and its two wall-connected tubes remain distinct from the tank's contents. Independent methane and oxygen transfer hoses coexist without erasing one another. The dashboard plug projects visibly from its socket; its registered replacement region is confined to the dashboard, leaving the windows stable.
