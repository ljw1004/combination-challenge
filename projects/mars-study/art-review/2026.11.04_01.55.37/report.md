## Outcome and limits

- **Clear unaided physical recognition:** the winch, metal bars/sheet, shovel, lined basket, rock and open utility rover were recognizable from their construction. Potatoes, leafy greens and the metal pot of chunky food also read well, including their combined arrangements. Steel's exact alloy and the rock's composition were not established by appearance.
- **Recognizable forms with unresolved identity:** figure 01 read as a folded flexible silver sheet or blanket, without establishing its pressure-envelope role. Figures 06, 13 and 16 showed small yellow beans/seeds, but did not uniquely establish soybeans. Figure 09 read as tank-based industrial equipment, and figure 18 as a cylindrical mechanical cartridge; neither revealed its exact technical function. Figure 15 strongly suggested a hose/cable coil, with irrigation plausible before context.
- **Context recovered useful readings:** crop context made the yellow seeds credible soybeans; growing-bed context made the coil credible drip tubing. Shelter use suited the flexible sheet. The vessel, pipework and controls supported propellant-processing hardware, and the plug's housing and end fitting suited a cockpit socket. Exact reaction chemistry, material performance and launch function still depend on text. The basket weave suggested wicker unaided, but a stiff cargo-mesh basket with a cloth liner remained plausible and did not visibly contradict the context.
- **Superseded core remained weak:** figure 17 showed a pale layered block with a darker middle band. Layered-ice-cliff context supported a preserved sample, but ice material and a drilled cylindrical form remained unclear; no distinct round core end was visible. This finding applies only to the thin-lined 78 px version. It supplies no verdict on the later cylindrical replacement.
- **Geometry:** no clipped or convincingly detached pieces were observed. The winch fittings, drip-line projections and cartridge contacts appeared connected. The rover nearly filled its canvas but remained intact.
