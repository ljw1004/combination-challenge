## Dramatic promise and order

A solo astronaut lands badly beside a delivered starter habitat. The same arrival cabin can return to orbit, but its grounded lower support needs repair. The player restores shelter and water, constructs the cavern farm, plants potatoes, soybeans and lettuce, tastes the first fresh leaves, and tends the developing staple crops in one continuous farm passage. **The substantial farm must be established before the discovery.** A later change to an immediate-return mission would remove the motive to start that construction. The discovery need not occur in the first quarter; a midpoint discovery is acceptable if the preceding making earns its space.

Brown material in the raw-water intake reveals a long-dead articulated body. Earth requests untouched ice and redirects the mission toward returning it. The local core preserves a reference to the water source; the farther exposed face permits choosing interior ice within a visible layer, away from station disturbance. The remote expedition immediately follows the reference core. Its second core reveals a complete body in its surroundings before the workshop and local materials become a fuel installation. A storm tests the established farm. The astronaut then completes the known support repair and loads fuel. The actual specimens remain secure in laboratory cooling through the celebratory meal, then board immediately before departure.

**The celebration meal is two chapters, after engineering and fueling:** build a cooker using the furnace's separate clean heating connection; then harvest, prepare and serve **potato and soybean stew with fresh lettuce**. A departure hold for rendezvous alignment gives the prepared astronaut a reason to pause. It is a logical mission condition, not a countdown or calendar. After the meal, an exterior loading chapter transfers the actual membrane and two cores. Inside the same cabin, a deliberately simple final operation uses a carried **Ascent arming plug** at the **Ascent console**. The player chooses the complete launch procedure: fit the plug, confirm ARMED, then deliberately press the existing START control. Plug insertion alone does not start the engine or power the already operating cabin. Removable spacecraft arming plugs have real precedent, but this crew-accessible Mars-ascent enable socket is an explicit fictional adaptation. It is not an actual NASA ignition key. Ascent is the observed result; rendezvous and handoff follow aboard the orbiter, still in space. There is no flight meal box, station winterizing, seed storage, cleanup or preparation for a future occupant.

Water connects survival, cultivation, discovery and fuel. The same crops and spacecraft make the storm and departure consequential. An outgoing observation and a delayed reply can provide warmth without radio tokens or a passive companion. Mara's recorded messages convey purpose and human reactions, not a list of recipes. The first emergency is handled locally; Earth never directs a seconds-long rescue.

## Time and gameplay scope

The player advances through completed operations and changed physical conditions. Do not impose calendar markers, crop-growth waits, fuel-production durations or resource accounting. Real growth, soaking, cooking and production are implicit in their complete operations. Results show the readiness that matters—dry pods, soaked beans, tender food, collected propellant—without requiring the player to imagine a schedule. The only explicit departure hold is the user-requested rationale for the celebratory meal; no waiting action is authored.

Approximately 200 worthwhile combinations is a length hypothesis, not a quota. A stronger short operation should remain short. User direction sets a **maximum of 50 primitive item identities**. Stocks and genuine integrated assemblies can reduce incidental supplies, but must not become bags of unrelated hidden capabilities. The current inventory is audited separately; the count describes game identities, not launch mass or a complete engineering bill of materials.

Imported precision equipment remains imported. Ordinary supports, covers, trays, handles, housings, simple heat exchangers and workshop tools should use the established material stocks where physically sensible. Do not add a special Earth item solely to make one recipe possible. Do not invent a named former or mold for every product.

## Mission and farm premises

The mission establishes a partly self-supplying research station for later expeditions. It deliberately allocates payload to reusable cultivation and manufacturing equipment rather than every planned meal. This is a mission choice, not a claim that a greenhouse necessarily saves mass during its first programme. Stored food bridges commissioning and interruptions and supplements the crops. The thirty-square-metre growing room supplies a substantial share of food under the stated optimized conditions, not a complete diet by itself.

Precursor robots surveyed an accessible cavern near suitable shallow layered ice. That particular favorable site is fictional. The excavator clears loose infill and grades ground; it does not tunnel through hard basalt. The cave provides shielding, not breathable air. Its imported deployable pressure enclosure is one real assembly with attached airlock and service spine containing air handling and regulated heating. A dedicated bank of breathing-gas cylinders fills the habitat and farm without transferring air out of the small cabin. The player constructs the floor, padding and lighting, then makes a sheltered berth using the existing cabin mattress before fitting the growing beds. The enclosure’s heater already warms that dry alcove; sleeping does not depend on later process-heat recovery. Bed structures, liners, drains, aerated reservoir and root medium follow.

The three crops share the environment, lighting, nutrient system and tested porous medium. Chosen compatible cultivars are an explicit horticultural premise. Ordinary mineral nutrient provision, including soybean nitrogen, is assumed as part of the delivered growing system. It needs no separate item or mixing action; watering the unwatered beds starts the shared loop. The potato root zone is deep and dark beneath illuminated foliage. Soybean stems receive support. Lettuce occupies a shallow tray with a clean fabric wick. Their different tasks sit on one common preparation sequence.

The initial pressure fill uses stored breathing gas. Once power returns, one atmospheric processor replenishes oxygen through carbon-dioxide capture/compression followed by an oxygen-making electrochemical stage. A separately controlled takeoff upstream of the oxygen cell later supplies carbon dioxide to the fuel plant; its gas never mixes with the carbon-monoxide exhaust. Selective capture, scalable capacity and compatible takeoff are explicit future-machine assumptions. The oxygen branch keeps operating during ordinary industrial use. During the storm the hot processor shuts down; stored breathing gas and the running scrubber bridge the interruption. This integrates two uses of the same machine rather than adding a second atmospheric unit.

The initial array is sized for the farm and baseline station. Factory expansion modules later support industry. The delivered reserve is specified for essential air handling, reduced electric heat, laboratory refrigeration and reduced farm operation through the forecast regional storm. It does not maintain full production. The fixed crop beds remain in the cavern. A locally made screen and reservoir cover reduce losses while the established pump changes mode. The water buffer supplements heating; it neither makes energy nor reaches cooking temperatures. Sample cooling is already essential and never becomes an emergency substory.

The original fuel cargo cannot support the revised return plan. The modular materials/life-support equipment provides a reviewed local-production contingency, not a finished fuel plant. The available orbital vehicle and approved trajectory are explicit future logistics. Producing surface ascent propellant does not remove orbital-transfer constraints.

## Material and equipment boundaries

Steel means known low-carbon steel sheet and lengths from expendable shipping braces. Their grade and form do not silently change between supports, a magnetic core, thermal panels and the specified grounded brace. The service tool performs ordinary cutting, joining and tube forming with its supplied heads. Clean cloth, foil, felt, mesh, hose, electrical conductor and liner sheet are reusable material stocks, not unique objects cloned between recipes. Different fresh pieces are used for food and industrial work.

The furnace's imported core incorporates its receiver, tracking base and temperature controller. Mirror petals remain imported optical surfaces. The press forms supported powder blanks; the furnace then sinters them into non-pressure ceramic. A made electromagnet separates the assayed magnetic fraction of the regolith. The imported retort reduces that concentrate under controlled heat and hydrogen, leaving porous iron; the heated press consolidates and shapes it. The compact machinery, exact feedstock and useful material properties are declared future/site assumptions. Ordinary red dust is not pure iron, a catalyst or ready growing soil.

The electrolysis core includes dedicated outlet separation and drying; its rack has separate dedicated hydrogen and oxygen receivers. Its ordinary controls stop when a buffer is full. Gas cylinders are compatible, oxygen-clean factory containers, each assigned its own gas and keyed connection. The hydrogen outlet feeds the retort; the oxygen outlet later feeds its distinct cryogenic store. Other vessels collect captured carbon dioxide and the contained commissioning batch. No unique bottle is used simultaneously for different gases.

The retort's mineral liner is exchanged for a clean compatible Sabatier cartridge. Standardized factory valve/control blocks serve the distinct installations. A test precedes the completed purification and return circuit. The actual reactor joins the supplied condenser/separator/dryer/recycle core; its cooled water return then joins treatment. That complete assembly—not an emptied test bottle alone—supplies purified methane. Ordinary mounting, spare control blocks and the separate test cylinder are included with the real installation; no unique vessel is duplicated. The hydrogen connection seats the retort at the already constructed furnace, and the water-return connection uses the established treatment port directly.

Qualified coolant tubing and locally formed metal panels provide the designed heat-rejection surface. The declared workshop design includes adequate thermal contact, radiating surface and area for the Mars setting; this does not rely on strong atmospheric convection. The player makes a vented tank from the existing steel sheets and fits a separate tubing coil inside it. Insulation and water complete the heat store in the pressurized farm service alcove. Sealed coolant lines pass through the enclosure’s service spine; the tank contains non-potable low-temperature water at room pressure. The room-facing insulation can fold back after the outdoor coil valves close, releasing stored warmth directly from the tank wall into the room. It is a finite supplement to electric heating, with no unexplained room-heating loop. No dedicated imported drum is needed.

Two qualified cryogenic vessels and two factory refrigerator units form a prepared equipment batch. Separate members are assigned to methane and oxygen. Each has its own pressure/vacuum boundary, operating connections, cooling, vent and transfer route; common controls do not mix propellants. Local material supplies outer shells and insulation only. Cryogenic capacity, compatibility, throughput and the overall energy balance are declared future-system assumptions, not a validated present-day mission design.

## Continuity and departure

The lander's grounded lower support is diagnosed and stabilized at the opening. Its upper cabin, tanks, avionics and engine mounts survive. The made braced leg and footing restore the lower support; ordinary lifting, cribbing and load transfer are bundled with installation using the spacecraft’s service equipment. No separate jack primitive, engine or pressure-tank fabrication is needed. Known ceramic blocks and steel form the footing and brace together after the ground is prepared. The actual foot, brace and lower stage stay on Mars when the original upper crew cabin departs.

The original membrane remains sealed in its holder. The source core stays in its liner and freezer cradle. The remote core stays in its liner/case through the optical examination; only the external optical face is prepared. The field refrigeration insert includes its qualified transfer buffer. Both actual core liners enter separate flight-carrier cradles, with their surrounding ice, within a portable qualified cold-transfer enclosure beside the lander. The original membrane transforms into its flight-secured state in its own locker. This can happen before or after core loading; neither result claims all specimens are aboard. Food and scientific containment remain separate. The body is exceptional fictional evidence, not a demonstrated age, terrestrial taxon, DNA sequence or native origin.

Recipe order must respect physical prerequisites. Either the wrapped or unwrapped actual water line can feed treatment, without leaving two persistent copies. The recovered-water circuit requires its separator. The station service panel commands separate installed controllers for power, the well and the fuel plant. Shutdown power remains available while line drainage, cooling and isolation finish; the panel does not contain mixed process fluids. The initial Station solar supply changes its display name to Dust-coated solar supply at the post-storm chapter. Cleaning its removable panel sections at the workshop service bench transforms that original identifier into a distinct restored Station solar supply. The old identity retires. Cleaning uses fresh cloth around a smooth steel handle; no new brush is imported. The compound service panel represents operating switches for the reserve, actual well line and fuel fab, not storing machinery inside a cabinet. All three shutdown states and restored solar power are required for its restart state. The automatic sequence warms, checks and primes the water line before it opens process feeds. The same qualified panel state controls the final separate propellant transfer. This deliberate diagrammatic use of compound slots is text-only here; switch-overlay graphics remain postponed. Potato and soybean planting goals latch separately so an early bed extension retires the original form. Harvest-ready crop surfaces appear only at the harvest passage; earlier tools cannot cut down the soybean stand before it survives the storm. Goal `next:` alone does not enforce those constraints.

The final cooker uses a made conductive jacket around the imported food pressure vessel. The existing furnace receiver supplies controlled high-temperature heat through a separate clean connection. The closed pot returns through the airlock before opening and is resealed for reheating outside. Mature soybeans are soaked and fully cooked before potatoes finish in the same vessel. A few washed leaves from the actual living lettuce tray accompany the stew. Loading and heating the beans are one complete operation; adding potatoes and reheating are another. Two chapters accomplish this one named meal; departure contains no food-packing task.

## Research anchors and limits

- [NASA excavation robot](https://www.nasa.gov/infrastructure-pilot-excavator/) and [candidate Martian lava tubes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.13156): loose-material robotics and shelter potential. The accessible surveyed cavern/ice site is invented.
- [NASA lunar-module propulsion reference](https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/static/history/alsj/LM09_Main_Propulsion_ppMP1-22.pdf) and [operations handbook](https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/static/history/alsj/lm10handbookvol1.pdf): armed engine control and a manual START pushbutton support the final control metaphor; the Mars vehicle remains fictional.
- [NASA plume–surface interaction](https://www.nasa.gov/directorates/stmd/plume-surface-interaction-psi/) and [lunar-module architecture](https://www.nasa.gov/history/apollos-lunar-module-bridged-technological-leap-to-the-moon/): ejecta and staged ascent motivate the fiction, not a certified Mars vehicle design.
- [NASA ice-drilling assessment](https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2015/06/mars_ice_drilling_assessment_v6_for_public_release.pdf): casing and sealed controlled-pressure extraction. Treatment remains a separate function.
- [NASA crop researcher interviewed by USDA](https://www.ars.usda.gov/oc/utm/growing-plants-in-space/), [NASA controlled crops](https://www.nasa.gov/exploration-research-and-technology/growing-plants-in-space/), [potato tuber light exposure](https://yardandgarden.extension.iastate.edu/faq/why-are-some-my-potato-tubers-green), [soybean preparation](https://www.uaex.uada.edu/farm-ranch/special-programs/Education_in_Agriculture/Docs/access%202019%20_3%20Cooking%20Soybeans.pdf): substantial area/light, nutrient supply and real food handling. These do not qualify arbitrary Martian regolith as growing medium.
- [ESA solar-brick demonstration](https://www.esa.int/Enabling_Support/Space_Engineering_Technology/Printing_bricks_from_moondust_using_the_Sun_s_heat) and [NASA materials processing](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20120016264): research precedents for non-pressure manufacture, with the story's compact equipment and material properties explicitly fictional.
- [NASA MOXIE](https://www.nasa.gov/space-technology-mission-directorate/tdm/mars-oxygen-in-situ-resource-utilization-experiment-moxie/) and [its electrochemical unit](https://science.nasa.gov/resource/moxie-twin-during-testing/): carbon-dioxide processing can produce oxygen; the story’s dual-purpose selective-capture installation and capacity are an extrapolation.
- [ESA closed-loop life support](https://www.esa.int/Science_Exploration/Human_and_Robotic_Exploration/Research/Advanced_Closed_Loop_System) and [NASA gas separation](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20110005536): chemistry and purification beyond condensation. The fuel-scale plant and cryogenic system remain supplied future capabilities.

The field case explicitly receives its steel shell before reflective insulation; its shell, shock mount, insulation and exposed heat-rejection face have distinct jobs. The three planting goals remain separately latched and are all visible, one for each crop. An unpurified methane test cylinder names its remaining need before it meets the completed purification assembly. “Fuel-fab area” and “Fuel-fab footing” denote industrial space and foundations, avoiding confusion with the growing beds.

Arming-plug research: [ESA’s Envisat account](https://www.esa.int/Applications/Observing_the_Earth/Last_Contact) documents physically replacing safe plugs with arming plugs for satellite mechanisms that actuate later. [NASA’s safety-analysis example, §3.2](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20150000903/downloads/20150000903.pdf) separates actuator-power arming from subsequent enabling/firing commands. These support removable electrical interlocks, not the exact fictional crew-console arrangement. The Apollo manual linked above supports a separate deliberate START command.
