# Mars v10 — narrative script

This is the current arc rendered in [mars.md](mars.md). The [design brief](design-brief.md) owns its physical premises and [working notes](../working-notes.md#iteration-10) retain the successive director findings. The script below incorporates the user's current direction, including the substantial farm before discovery, logical rather than calendar progression, fewer primitive supplies and a two-chapter celebration meal before ascent.

## The story we are telling

An astronaut arrives to establish a self-supplying research station. A hard landing has damaged the starter habitat and one grounded spacecraft support. By making useful things from the delivered equipment and local material, the astronaut earns a functioning home and a substantial cave farm.

Only after that farm is established does the raw-water intake reveal a preserved body. Earth asks for untouched cores and redirects the mission toward bringing them home. The player uses the same workshop, water and material knowledge to construct the return installation. The home they built becomes something to protect through a storm and finally something to leave.

The discovery can arrive around the middle of the story. It must not remove the reason for building the farm before that construction happens. First lettuce, storm survival and the final substantial meal have different jobs. The farm is shared infrastructure with three distinct crops, not three repeated preparation tutorials.

There are no elapsed-time instructions, calendar jumps, fuel-filling waits or resource counters. An operation ends with its physical result ready. Recorded Earth messages preserve the communication premise without simulating transmission latency. The final orbital-alignment hold gives an otherwise prepared astronaut a reason for the celebratory meal, without adding a waiting action.

## Editorial constraints

Keep the real body, two distinct cores, same rooted crop stands, same water line and same spacecraft physically accountable. Material stocks can be reused; unique objects cannot quietly duplicate. Receiving locations must be natural terrain, delivered apparatus or a surface of something already built.

The primitive budget is at most fifty. Standardized compatible components and true integrated factory assemblies are permitted; unrelated capabilities may not be hidden in an all-purpose kit merely to lower a count. Simple supports, covers and handling tools should be made from the known stocks. Precision pressure boundaries, electronics, catalysts and refrigeration remain explicitly imported.

The plot does not need a repeated assay or an extra accident to sustain interest. Specific human reactions from Earth and distinct physical construction achievements carry the return work. The three crop roles and the named meal provide its quieter counterweight. Chapter-specific fast tests and a whole-story detailed campaign will test those hypotheses; this script is not evidence of human enjoyment.
