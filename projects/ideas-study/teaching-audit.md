# Ideas: teaching and presentation audit

The [design brief](../../stories/ideas/design-brief.md) owns the adopted intent: an interesting, educational journey through interacting ideas, with discoveries worth pursuing even when they have no later descendant. Length is settled. This audit concerns the current manuscript's teaching, intelligibility, hooks and intellectual actions; it is not a new history-of-everything syllabus or a claim of measured learning.

## Scope and findings

Read all 57 playable chapters and the ending, including the 164 distinct result cards, primitive explanations, goals and combinations. The strongest teaching pattern is a concrete case that changes what can be concluded. The recurring weakness was a general result whose example or essential premise remained in an optional ingredient reading. Some connections also relied on a broad resemblance between inquiries instead of a useful contribution from the carried item.

The revision gives every result a motivating question, enough of its inputs to explain the operation, a reasoned outcome and an italic local-title restatement. Paragraphs separate those functions. Historical orientation identifies people or communities, places and source periods, while comparisons and speculative scenes remain explicit. Direct reasoning replaces commentary about performing reasoning. Most primitive readings were already short explanations and remain so; they do not acquire artificial syntheses.

Simplification means making the reasoning easier to follow, not minimizing the word count. Essential examples have moved into result cards; questions, paragraph breaks and closing titles add text. Repeated qualifications, vague referents and unnecessary narration have been removed or replaced with specific limits. The main expanded cases are the statue, practical judgment, gear ratios, translation distinctions, map coordinates, public criticism, plant geography and DNA copying.

Two recipe inputs change: the Earth estimate uses reasoning from premises rather than the unrelated equilateral-triangle theorem, and Burton's comparison uses two kinds of description rather than an inherited equivocation lesson. Four supplies now expose concrete work: equatorial coordinates, a weaver's objection, two-slope plant records and complementary DNA pairing. The triangle proof and melancholy encounter retain independent value. Several titles now state the actual result rather than an abstract editorial summary.

Independent manuscript, source, card-only and title-only reviews prompted further repairs: a reported H4 accuracy result, the missing Avicenna premise, Ptolemy's apparent backward motion, Mandeville's vanity-to-trade connection, a felt detail for melancholy, explicit cholera comparisons and simpler technical vocabulary. The Earth calculation now identifies local verticals and a common observation date; Lamarck's animal mechanism is distinguished from his account of plants. The 1801 constitution's restriction on cultivators changing residence, yeast's sugar conversion and PKU's food-to-development connection are stated in the results themselves. Names and closing titles stay local on Newton's desk, with attribution when the result is carried onward.

The user's further clarification made plain English an explicit requirement in the brief. A further pass replaced compressed abstract labels with people, actions and reasons: “subsistence” becomes “food supply,” and “care-continuity duty” becomes arranging a safe route for the carers. A fresh reader of cards from 17 selected chapters identified eight remaining barriers. The adopted changes explain the logical symbols and halting argument, substance and properties, nominalism, Kant's observation order, Locke's invented agreement and the PKU example. These were wording reviews, not evidence that people have learned the ideas.

## Boundaries and next evidence

The manuscript offers introductory reconstructions, not complete theories. The Euclidean case proves one construction; the orbit case is qualitative; field equations, Avicenna's full metaphysics, Mendelian segregation and gene expression are not derived in full. The present scope should be judged by the explanations it actually promises. Regional and disciplinary representation is selective, with substantial concentration in European intellectual history and natural science; this audit does not certify global balance.

Presentation can make an operation intelligible and rewarding without giving the player a substantive choice. Compatibility hints remain normal information. The revised explicit inputs permit predictions and calculations in several places, but the frequent prescribed sequence still needs player evaluation. Human retention and transfer require learners to explain and use distinctions on new cases, followed by delayed checks where appropriate; agent source review cannot supply that evidence.
