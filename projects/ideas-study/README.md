# Ideas study

Continuing development of [History of ideas](../../stories/ideas/ideas.md), guided by the game's [educational purpose](../../README.md#philosophy) and the story's [design brief](../../stories/ideas/design-brief.md). Follow the interplay of ideas from ancient times to modern, including discoveries worth experiencing without a later descendant. Length is settled; current priorities are playability, enjoyment, intelligibility, hooks and teaching coverage.

Playtests follow the shared [evaluation method](../../docs/fun-and-evaluation.md#chapter-fun-playtests). Retained runs contain accepted player responses, independent assessments, chapter syntheses and the mechanically assembled report. These are local chapter assessments, not continuous whole-story player journeys, browser tests, human enjoyment measurements or factual authenticity reviews.

## Teaching and presentation

The [whole-story teaching audit](teaching-audit.md) maps every chapter's intended learning, connections or independent value, and adopted repairs. All 164 distinct result cards now contain a motivating question, their essential premises, a reasoned result and an italic restatement. New concrete cases strengthen translation, coordinates, public criticism, plant geography and DNA copying. Historical orientation and direct reasoning follow the shared authoring guidance; autobiographical first-person narration is unnecessary.

The [current outcomes](working-notes.md#teaching-and-presentation-audit) distinguish manuscript and source review from player evidence. The full `--fast` runs below concern the earlier shortening revisions, not the subsequently rewritten cards.

## Shorter journey

The current manuscript has **57 playable chapters**, down from 85, and a **164-combination completion route**, down from 272. The completed shortening revision had 67.7% of the baseline source length and 72.8% of its automatic route prose; those text ratios do not describe the subsequent teaching revision. Counts describe structure and text, not human duration. Repeated preparations and conclusions have been folded; selected calculations, observations, interpretations and institutional cases now carry explicit evidence and consequences. Desk clearing stays implicit.

The [working notes](working-notes.md) record selection decisions, every chapter's scores, strongest/weakest patterns, independent source-review findings, successive changes and remaining questions. Verification follows the user's request to work entirely in logical game space.
