## First revision pass: concrete prototypes

### 18: supply the calculation and the model test

The current operands “Unknown shares can be related to known shares” and “Fresh observations trouble inherited sky models” state the kind of result but omit the evidence. All three players noticed the missing specifics. Replace the first with a small, explicitly authored merchant problem: for example, three equal shares plus two remaining units total eleven. Combining the recorded quantities with the available method should show why each share is three. This uses the existing combination grammar; it does not require a new numeric-input interface.

For the sky branch, select and verify a particular period-appropriate criticism or mismatch before writing the replacement. Do not borrow the later telescope/Venus case into this earlier setting. If a suitable case cannot be supported, narrow “Where does the old sky model fail?” and state the evidence limit rather than inventing a failure. Keep the useful theme that inherited knowledge is actively examined.

**Falsifier:** fresh players still receive only the labels “unknown share” and “model read critically,” or cannot explain what the supplied quantities or observation contribute. More positive adjectives or a higher percentage without that explanation would not count as success.

### 34: make the replication question answerable

The existing recipe combines the rubbed/untouched-wood comparison with printed instructions, while the goal asks whether another workshop finds the same result. The current output says that the comparison can travel; it does not report a second trial. Replace that final input or extend the case with an explicitly illustrative second workshop record, so the required combination compares actual outcomes and relevant conditions. Carry the inspectable method forward into 81. If the intended scope is only preparing a shareable procedure, change the goal accordingly and assess whether that smaller payoff is sufficient.

Keep the matched-wood control, which every player valued. For the optical branch, reveal a specific part of the arrangement that makes the effect inspectable rather than repeating the inverted-image explanation from 20. Do not turn routine setup into several new compulsory actions.

**Falsifier:** the revised chapter still completes a replication goal without an outcome, or players describe both branches as another statement that methods should be shared.

### 75: give the low hook a concrete human consequence

This chapter has 5/6 credited actions and **three low hooks**. Its two recipes move from a common-concept item to relational kinship, and from cross-referenced knowledge to institutional classification. Players found the bridge to institutional control unconvincing and the relationships unworked.

Replace those general statements with a small relation diagram and a clearly labelled illustrative institutional case file. **Falsifier:** players still cannot identify who is affected, what the classification changes, or a specific question they want to pursue. Another abstract qualification with high Worthwhile and low hook would show that the narrative problem remains.

## Work on the surrounding arcs

The manuscript frequently resets with a cleared worktable and an instruction to compare ideas. Together with 65 chapters lacking a high hook, this motivates an arc hypothesis: several transitions need a more concrete reason for the next inquiry.
