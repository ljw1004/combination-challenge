# Song of creation: ending choices

The [single active brief](../../stories/creation/design-brief.md) owns the adopted intent. The song embodies the singers’ understanding of creation and their place within it. The player witnessed the beginning; the singers understand part of their world and can learn more. The final reading does not need to explain that difference or describe an invented creation account.

## Selected sequence

The penultimate chapter does the work: lighting the cave, painting, making a song and teaching what the community has learned.

```text
Fire @ Sea cave → Lit cave
Call @ any current cave state → Echo
Echo + Memory → Song

Shared discovery + Story → Teaching
```

Painting, Song and Teaching are three independent goal strands, all visible on arrival. Only the lighting-to-painting sequence uses successive goals. Teaching is a goal alongside the paintings and Song. It is worth making in its own right: people pass on both a discovery and how to investigate it. It need not become another ingredient preparing the final music.

The last playable chapter is **A day of rest**. The already-created Social group becomes the tribe gathered around a campfire outside the painted cave. It is a natural landscape hotspot. Two simple drags complete the ending:

```text
Song @ Social group → Shared song
Shared song + Wonder → Song of creation
```

The tribe supplies the shared singing in the first action. Shared song and Wonder then combine as ordinary movable icons anywhere on the board, with no hotspot or placement requirement. Social group is fixed only in this final scene, so Shared song cannot be made earlier and the culmination still arrives here.

The final reading remains: “They sing about the world and their place in it, as they understand it.” The final image will carry the scale. This is an intentional release after the work, not a chapter that needs extra puzzles or preparatory tokens.

## Routes considered

| Final route | Contribution and tradeoff |
| --- | --- |
| **Shared song + Wonder** — selected | A community’s music expresses its amazement and curiosity about the world. Wonder already grew from looking at the stars; no new abstraction has to be manufactured. Shared singing remains a distinct achievement. |
| Song + Shared discovery | Gives voice to the community’s learned experience, with direct ancestry from its tide investigation. It risks making the ending instructional and narrowing creation to one investigated phenomenon. |
| Song + Painted sky | Joins the two art branches in a concrete place, but suggests a song about stars more strongly than the whole of creation. Do not enlarge it by inventing a separate creation-account token. |
| Song + Wonder, without Shared song | The leanest route. It loses the useful change from one singer to several listening and coordinating their voices; that step is worth keeping. |

The earlier Teaching → New question / Remembered song route was mechanically coherent, but the user found the final chapter administrative and New question forced. The final combination now uses the existing Wonder. Teaching moves to the preceding chapter; New question and Remembered song are removed. The earlier Changed song and Children’s chorus also remain removed. An intermediate four-action music finale was superseded by the day-of-rest direction. The gathering gives the first gesture a natural place; the user's subsequent simplification makes the last gesture an ordinary pair.

## Memory accounting

Memory is used **once directly in the music branch**, in Echo + Memory → Song. Shared song also inherits Memory through Social group, which descends from learned warnings. Wonder does not add another Memory branch. Learning the song from another singer is part of the shared-singing action and description.

The whole manuscript has five direct Memory recipes: Learned path, Shared warning, Story, Painted cave and Song. Teaching still inherits Memory through Story and the shared discovery’s language/calendar ancestry. These are ingredient counts, not claims that memory vanishes when its token is transformed or that a player must recreate it five times.

The original musical branch used Memory twice: once to recall the echo and again to turn Chorus into Remembered song. Removing the latter step avoids repeating recall merely to prolong the ending.

## The cave changes as it is painted

One landscape hotspot passes through four states, each replacing its predecessor at the same centre:

```text
Fire @ Sea cave → Lit cave
Pigment + Memory @ Lit cave → Painted cave
Stars @ Painted cave → Painted cave with stars
```

The animal stays on the wall when the stars are added. All four states can receive Call to make Echo; the player may follow painting, music and teaching in any order. These alternative echo recipes preserve the same acoustic possibility through the cave's changes, rather than adding new tasks. There is only one two-ingredient contextual recipe in the whole manuscript and only one cave hotspot present at a time. Do not keep a separate acoustic cave or show its paintings as fixed circular icons.

The completed cave has no later ingredient use apart from Echo if it has not yet been made. On the day of rest, it is scenery and the gathered Social group is the sole hotspot.

All states now use the same landscape receiver, with provisional artwork and coordinates. Later production will show a sheltered cave interior before and after lighting, then add the animal and stars on the same wall. The tribe gathers around a campfire outside its recognizable opening in the final scene, with the painting behind them. The old exposed-cliff artwork is not acceptance of this new setting. No engine change is needed.

## Forests establish the setting earlier

The seed sequence now follows the retreating ice, before learning and language:

```text
Seeds + Seasons → Dormant seeds
Dormant seeds + Seed dispersal → Scattered seeds
Scattered seeds + Soil → Young grove
Young grove + World after the ice → New forests
```

New forests names the concrete result; its description explains food, shelter and living spaces for the tree dwellers in the next chapter. Growth still requires suitable soil, moisture and warmth. No forest task follows Song of creation.
