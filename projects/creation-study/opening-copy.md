# Human prose and text-only visual context

The [active brief](../../stories/creation/design-brief.md) governs the language. Goals and readings use plain, clear English: players work out combinations, not enigmatic clues. The [manuscript](creation.md) is the current human copy. Images may later make some visual description unnecessary, but do not remove useful explanations of causes, physical processes or scientific limits merely to make the prose shorter.

Fire entering the waters of the deep makes Stars. This is an accepted creative choice. The painted nebula conveys the surrounding medium's scale and character; a literal reflection mechanism is not required. Stars and Asteroids are the player-facing names. The world steadied by its Moon is a landscape state in the opening; the separate Steady axial tilt symbol appears with seasons later.

[Opening AI guidance](opening-ai-guidance.txt) supplies additional visual context for text-only opening trials. It describes the intended visible premise without revealing unperformed combinations or their results. The runner freezes its exact text with `--guidance-file` and supplies it equally to players and graders. The earlier trials used provisional art, so their reports remain evidence about an authored premise, not the finished pictures. Earlier trials retain their original guidance and verdicts.

The integrated opening shows an irregular dark cavity in displaced nebular dust and a small mottled molten world without a painted Moon. Extra context for later text-only trials must describe this visible setting. Keep evaluation instructions and explanations needed only by text-based reviewers out of the human story. Recognition and continuity reviews must inspect the actual pictures separately.
