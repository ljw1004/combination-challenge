# Human prose and text-only visual context

The [active brief](../../stories/creation/design-brief.md) governs the language. Goals and readings use plain, clear English: players work out combinations, not enigmatic clues. The [manuscript](creation.md) is the current human copy. Images may later make some visual description unnecessary, but do not remove useful explanations of causes, physical processes or scientific limits merely to make the prose shorter.

Fire entering the waters of the deep makes Stars. This is an accepted creative choice. The artwork will convey the surrounding medium's scale and character; a literal reflection mechanism is not required. Stars and Asteroids are the player-facing names. The world steadied by its Moon is a landscape state in the opening; the separate Steady axial tilt symbol appears with seasons later.

[Opening AI guidance](opening-ai-guidance.txt) supplies additional visual context for text-only opening trials. It describes the intended visible premise without revealing unperformed combinations or their results. The runner freezes its exact text with `--guidance-file` and supplies it equally to players and graders. While the images are provisional, reports must call this an authored premise, not evidence that finished artwork communicates it. Earlier trials retain their original guidance and verdicts.

Once artwork is integrated, revise the extra context to describe what is actually visible. Keep evaluation instructions and explanations needed only by text-based reviewers out of the human story. Recognition and continuity reviews must inspect the actual pictures separately.
