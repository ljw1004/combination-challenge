This story is an educational combination game for curious high-school and adult readers. Its purpose is enjoyable making and discovery that leaves players with useful understanding of how ideas work.

Visual context for this text-only opening prototype: the first scene is an invented elemental creation myth. Its landscape receiver, Waters of the deep, is a vast primordial cosmic medium, rather than an ordinary terrestrial pond or a vessel of drinking water. The painting shows dense blue-grey and warm ochre/coral nebular dust with immense pillars and curling filaments. Element icons represent a creator's gestures at this enormous scale. The later earthly water, weather and living landscapes become more concrete.

This is textual context for artwork that is now integrated. A text-only trial cannot establish visual recognition or quality; those require separate inspection of the actual images. Judge the actions and readings you actually encounter; no unperformed recipes or results are supplied here.
