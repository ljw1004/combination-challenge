**Definitions**
- Worthwhile: counts actions supported by meaningful decision-making or a valued story payoff or reason to keep playing, counting each action once. Show the average credited actions / average total gameplay actions per player, not an average percentage.
- Fun: a narrower count of meaningful decisions: worthwhile deliberation or intentional experimentation. Measured by --detailed, including inspections and unsuccessful well-formed attempts; --fast reports it as not measured. Fast menu-assisted diagnostic ratios are not comparable.
- Hooks: the players' reported desire to continue: "high" means specific eagerness, "medium" means mild or conditional interest, and "low" means indifference or reluctance

## Chapter 4: Moving plates and continents

Worthwhile: 4.7/6 worthwhile decisions (average across 3 players)

Fun: not measured (--fast)

Hook: medium/high (1 medium, 2 high)

Strongest moments: Volcano + Ocean → Hydrothermal vent was the shared strongest payoff: all three linked volcanic heat and seawater chemistry to life without sunlight, named it most worthwhile, and connected it to continuation interest despite routine execution. ## Chapter 10: Decomposition and forest renewal

Worthwhile: 6.7/9 worthwhile decisions (average across 3 players)

Fun: not measured (--fast)

Hook: medium

Strongest moments: Returning dead-wood nutrients to growing plants: fungal digestion supplied a specifically useful mechanism for all three, and Returned nutrients + Roots delivered explicit satisfaction for Players 1 and 2. Player 3 preferred the decomposition explanation and found some intermediate execution repetitive. Renewing forest + Clouds → Forest rain: all three singled out transpiration contributing to rain far downwind as an interesting or useful extension beyond decomposition. Weakest moments: Rainfall + Air → Storm: all three initially lacked a clear connection to the goals; Players 1 and 2 identified the opening as least engaging or slow. The result repaired its relevance, but the sole-option menu offered little agency. Decomposition + Soil followed by Returned nutrients + Roots: Player 3 explicitly found these separate actions repetitive once the pathway was clear. Players 1 and 2 instead valued the sequence and its growth payoff, so this pacing weakness was not shared unanimously.

## Chapter 16: Bird migration and island life

Worthwhile: 3.3/7 worthwhile decisions (average across 3 players)

Fun: not measured (--fast)

Hook: medium

Strongest moments: Island life at Variation → Different songs was the shared strongest discovery: all three players singled out inherited variation versus learned traditions as interesting, surprising and most worthwhile. The payoff came from the result rather than the forced choice. Lava + Ocean → Volcanic island provided a smaller explanatory payoff across the records by clarifying that many eruptions build an island. Player 2 specifically tied this informative construction to willingness to continue.

Weakest moments: Different songs + Food web → Many-voiced world felt abstract to all three: songs did not explain how birds receive ecological support. Player 2 also wanted greater specificity about migrants. Player 1's satisfying closing imagery partly offset, but did not erase, this conceptual weakness. Earth + Fire → Lava was a predictable, sole-option intermediate that Players 1 and 3 identified as least worthwhile or less engaging despite its useful explanation. This weakness concerns constrained execution rather than a protocol problem; Player 2 instead selected the opening mountain crossing as weakest.
