# The Travelling Harbour — Cyberpunk study

**The Travelling Harbour** is an illustrated cyberspace combination game: build a ship, put it in a bottle, give it a harbour, then turn the enclosing relationships inside out. Its creations keep developing through borrowed movement, independent copies, a garden, a play and a travelling night regatta. Sera, an AI curator, accompanies the exhibition.

The completed development pass has **186 combinations, 215 illustrated identities and 27 playable chapters plus an ending**. The legal reference route takes 94 combinations; alternatives include different creatures, places to sit, room arrangements and assembly orders. It remains an unpublished study for user review.

With `npm run dev -- --port 4182`, [play locally](http://127.0.0.1:4182/make-something.html?story=projects/cyberpunk-study/harbour.md) or open the [art catalogue](http://127.0.0.1:4182/projects/cyberpunk-study/production/gallery.html). The [manuscript](harbour.md), [single active brief](design-brief.md), [development outcomes](development.md) and [art production record](production/README.md) are the current deliverables.

Repeated fresh fast playthroughs, a complete detailed playthrough, targeted detailed testing and independent story/continuity reviews shaped the game before artwork. Subsequent visual reviews addressed recognition, ancestry, route geometry and phone readability. Final replay and artwork checks pass; all 81 sampled chapter-entry layouts across three screen sizes keep their objects exposed and clear of goals. Bounded mechanical exploration exercised every recipe, but does not prove every possible history. These are AI and mechanical checks, with no human enjoyment claim.

## Background

The user played **No Fixed Address** and found the many small circular illustrations and subtle technical distinctions hard to follow. On 5 November 2026 they asked to set that approach aside and brainstorm a futuristic story whose narrative unfolds through the continuing lives of things the player combines and creates.

The [unreleased manuscript](cyberpunk.md), its artwork under `assets/` and its original brief returned from `stories/cyberpunk` because the story is not ready for release. Its old plot, AI curriculum, circular-token system and approximately 200-combination scope do not constrain these explicitly proposed alternatives. The [first development archive](<../../archive/2026.11.05 cyberpunk study/README.md>) describes the earlier work and its evidence limits; successful AI checks did not establish that its human experience worked.

The historical baseline can still be previewed at `/make-something.html?story=projects/cyberpunk-study/cyberpunk.md`. This project is excluded from the published catalogue and hosted files. The former published URL's browser saves and drafts remain under that URL; they are not migrated to either study preview.

[Exploration questions](exploration.md) define this exercise. [Iteration outcomes](iterations.md) preserve the candidates, questions, independent findings and decisions. Develop two or three substantially different candidates in every round, carrying lessons forward through different solutions. Reviewers are fresh for every assignment. Concepts and narrative traces are design deliverables; raw reviewer packets/replies are disposable under `runs/cyberpunk-rethink/`.

## Concept-stage result

[Four rounds of brainstorming and review](iterations.md) produced these [current treatments](round-4.md), each with its own rules, story beats, continuing creations, representative recipes and remaining risks:

| Candidate | What combination means | Continuing life | Present judgment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **The Harbour in the Bottle** | Reverse the containing relationships in a digital world. | Small ship → bottled beacon serving a harbour → ship carrying that same bottled harbour. | Preferred compact story prototype; strongest ending image. |
| **Borrowed Moves** | Copy and compose behavior independently of its body. | Toy's route → useful carrier → longer route → copied pattern or whole carrier serving illumination. | Strongest basis for a longer construction game; test the actual route diagrams. |
| **The Kite With Two Histories** | Fork a creation and merge its independently developed descendants. | One light sculpture → two local compositions → one new descendant, with both earlier works surviving. | Expressive alternative; its two lives need more narrative weight before expansion. |

The reviewers' strongest distinction is between a memorable transformation and a reusable game grammar. A currently supplies the first most convincingly; B supplies the second. Neither has human play evidence yet. The earlier length target is not a reason to pad either one.

These treatments led to the small playable sequences, expanded drafts and artwork trials recorded in [development](development.md). The concept-stage judgments above describe the alternatives before those tests; they are not a separate active plan.
