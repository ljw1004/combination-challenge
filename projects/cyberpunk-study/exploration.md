# What can combining mean?

## Starting evidence and scope

The user's actual play experience is the reason to reopen the premise. Small illustrations carried too many objects and conceptually subtle states. Enlarging them could help recognition, but does not by itself make the narrative belong to the combination medium. The working hypothesis is that the earlier plot relied too much on successive jobs and explanatory discoveries; a replacement should give the things we create a continuing, consequential life.

This is a hypothesis to challenge, not a claim that every part of the earlier game failed. Set its story aside. Futuristic, cyberspace and cyberpunk premises are available; an AI protagonist, the earlier cast, 219 recipes and circular artwork are not requirements for this exercise. We are developing story concepts and concrete combination sequences, not commissioning art or filling a quota.

The user subsequently highlighted cyberspace as a source of combinations impossible in the physical world. Explore copying, linking, nesting and inherited behavior as possible world rules; do not equate cyberspace with ordinary crafting under neon decoration.

## Questions for every candidate

1. What does combining mean here—physical making, transformation, cultivation, composition, copying, joining places, or something else? What makes the two inputs useful before the result is explained?
2. Which few creations will the player recognize and care about? Trace at least one from its first making through a later *different use*, with earlier contributions still necessary. Carrying a token unused or renaming a status is insufficient.
3. What next situation exists because of the previous creation? Can a story beat be removed without changing what the player makes or can do? If so, why is it there?
4. Is there a reason to anticipate the next result beyond completing a repair checklist? What changes in the meaning of an earlier object by the ending?
5. What would the player handle on an actual board? Prefer a small, distinguishable vocabulary and visible whole-object changes. Required premises must survive skipped optional readings. No artwork-recognition claim can be made from prose alone.
6. Can the idea work with authored binary combinations, reuse/transformations, chapters and fixed receiving places? Do not quietly depend on autonomous simulation, numeric resource management, timed events, branching conversations, automatic decomposition into multiple outputs, or new engine features. People and relationships are not components to install.
7. What is the most plausible failure? What example would distinguish a compelling construction story from an attractive synopsis?

## Iteration method

Each round contains two or three materially different solutions, not cosmetic variants of a favorite. Begin with independent divergent brainstorming; select a comparable trio of concrete treatments. Informed reviewers see only the current candidates and standing guidance, without prior criticism, favored verdicts or old drafts. Ask different questions: medium/causal structure; narrative desire and continuity; later, forward understanding of a particular visible combination.

A local blind combination probe follows the [two-question method](../../docs/fun-and-evaluation.md#blind-combination-and-story-beat-readings): record the spontaneous next action first, then separately ask the predicted effect of a specified combination. One fresh reader per case; no result or author explanation beforehand. These are reading probes, not engine playtests or evidence of human enjoyment.

Keep concrete rejected ideas and adopted lessons in concise iteration outcomes. Preserve the best alternatives through the final comparison. A concept can be ready for a small playable prototype when its defining operation, continuing creation, causal arc and difficult example hold together under fresh scrutiny. That is a narrower conclusion than claiming a complete game works for players.
