# Cosmic creation study

A storyboarding project about raw creation, with the proposed arc **a star’s material becoming a sea**: early particles and gas, stars and their scattered material, a solar disk, rocky worlds and icy bodies, Earth and the Moon, then a descent to rain collecting in a sea.

[brainstorming.txt](brainstorming.txt) preserves the supplied conversation writeup, including the original alternatives and the later seven-beat proposal. It is the starting material for this study, not an implemented manuscript. The later proposal ends at the sea and leaves life for a possible continuation; the earlier ideas remain available for consideration.

The next design question is whether the major transformations support enough satisfying player combinations. Develop proposed items, locations and small paper boards; review factual authenticity and player intuition separately, and seek worthwhile acts of creation without padding the story with chemistry chores. Follow the [authoring guide](../../docs/story-authoring.md) and [evaluation method](../../docs/fun-and-evaluation.md).

The [backlog](../backlog.md#story-ideas-worth-retaining) tracks this proposal. [Working notes](../../notes.md#1-november-2026--cosmic-story-brainstorming-recovered) record the supplied material’s provenance and the limits of its reported reviews and combination counts.
