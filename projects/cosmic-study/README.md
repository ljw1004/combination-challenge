## Working method

First write approximately 1,000-word stories that work as literature. The cosmos itself should carry attention, in the spirit of Carl Sagan's accessible cosmic narratives; human-scale framing is not the goal. Things coming together should steadily change the situation; no recipe quota, atom inventory or catalogue of cosmic epochs should dictate the arc. Then investigate what the player could meaningfully combine.

At **every substantive creative iteration**, develop two or three materially different candidates. Each new set must use the previous round's learning but solve the problems in different ways. Renaming the same plot or polishing one favourite three ways does not count. Compare narrative premise, viewpoint, structure, stakes, ending and the nature of the player's eventual participation. Carry this branching method into conceptual combination design.

Candidate prose is a design deliverable, retained here. Temporary reviewer packets and individual responses belong in `runs/cosmic-rethink/`; retain only instructions, input identities, concise findings, decisions and evidence limits. Every reviewer is fresh and receives no earlier drafts, verdicts or author's preferred winner. Reviewers are told that sustained implication of combining is the intended literary approach, and must distinguish a successful literary encounter from an understandable game action.

These are experiments, not three approvals or a commitment to any endpoint. The study remains active through narrative selection, conceptual design and eventual playable evaluation. No new artwork is warranted by prose review alone.

The first round explored human or hand-scale framing too strongly. The user clarified that compelling narrative should come from the cosmos itself. These drafts remain visible as the actual alternatives explored; subsequent rounds must solve that requirement through genuinely different cosmic narratives, rather than merely deleting their human endings.
