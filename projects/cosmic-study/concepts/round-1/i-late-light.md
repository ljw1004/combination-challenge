# I — Reconstructing the possibility of a late star

Concept candidate for [What the Darkness Kept](../../narrative/round-3/i-late-light.md). Proposed scope: **four conceptual combinations**. This is explicitly an argument reconstructed from a specified cosmic history, not material transformation disguised as cards.

**Player action and question.** Begin with the small red star in the dark galaxy. The player connects facts about the earlier pair until the new light has a causal account. The question is **what preserved its fuel, and what finally made that fuel usable?** No astronomer, instrument or document-making task intervenes. The visible cosmos accompanies statements about its history. Combining statements asserts a relation; it does not cause a merger.

**Complete chain.**

1. Return to the pair's formation. **Both bodies formed rich in hydrogen + Neither sustains ordinary hydrogen fusion → Hydrogen can survive unused in the pair.** The first premise supplies the fuel's origin; the second supplies the reason it need not be spent as stellar fuel. The result is conditional on the supplied history in which the bodies remain intact and separate. Automatic narration carries the pair through the galaxy's bright generations. The brief youthful burning of a rare form of hydrogen does not become another ingredient.
2. Turn to the pair's arrangement. **Two bodies bound in an orbit + Gravitational waves carry orbital energy away → The pair's orbit shrinks.** The first premise contributes the bound, circling configuration; the second identifies an actual loss of orbital energy. Their conjunction explains why separation need not persist. “Time” adds nothing as a card. The supplied scenario has an initial orbit whose decay permits contact only after the bright stellar populations have faded; this chronology is a stated scenario condition, not something the two statements numerically prove.
3. **Hydrogen can survive unused in the pair + The pair's orbit shrinks → Preserved fuel can meet in a late merger.** The first result contributes material history; the second contributes a route to ending the separation. The proposed case retains the prose's nearly equal pair and unstable contact. Those assumptions are stated when the bodies first appear; they are not invented by this conclusion. The reading describes distortion, mass exchange and the turbulent merger, including escaped gas. It does not imply that every contacting pair merges this way.
4. **Preserved fuel can meet in a late merger + Enough mass remains in a hot body → A new hydrogen-burning star is possible.** The first input supplies the otherwise unexplained old fuel; the second supplies the changed physical conditions for sustained reactions. “Enough” is explicitly relative to sustaining hydrogen fusion in this specified remnant, not merely weighing more than either progenitor. The conclusion distinguishes continuing nuclear light from a temporary collision flash. The ending returns to the red star and its long future.

**Material, context and elapsed time.** There are no movable material tokens and no process stations. Movable statements concern the same two brown dwarfs throughout; the output of each join is another statement. Galaxy aging, radiation from the orbit, the unstable contact and the remnant's settling are narrated physical events. They do not become collectible powers. The five supplied premises enter only where needed; earlier conclusions are reused when the two explanatory branches meet.

**Vocabulary and cost.** Brown dwarf, hydrogen fusion and gravitational wave are the necessary new concepts. Electron crowding explains the initial failure to ignite in prose, without another primitive. This candidate gives up the original making premise completely: the player constructs an explanation, while nature makes the star. That is a substantive change of mode, not a way to claim four material recipes where the narrative supplies one merger.

**Main risk and falsifier.** The gestures may merely assemble sentences whose relationships the narration has already answered. Reject this mode if the joins only feel like matching words, or if the resulting explanation makes all brown-dwarf mergers inevitable future stars. No extra measurements or apparatus are proposed to manufacture additional work.
