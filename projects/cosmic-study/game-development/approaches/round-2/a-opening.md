# Three opening solutions for the ensemble prototype

Authoring alternatives, 6 November 2026. These address the risks identified during architecture work: orbital renaming cannot sustain a chapter, physical ingredients must stay distinct from processes, and the young disk and luminous stars must have actual ancestry in the dark lane.

1. **Begin at the first crossing, with unlike material side by side.** The orange-star stream meets the other galaxy's stellar outskirts; the lane cloud meets crossing gas. One remains stars on another path, while the other becomes a compressed cloud. Loose gas and an outer cloud establish two additional, materially different consequences. The following scenes follow identified portions of that cloud into stars and one separate stellar system. This puts the central question into the first gestures and needs no inventory of gravity, heat or elapsed time.
2. **Begin inside the dark lane and let the galaxy arrive.** Start with cold gas and dusty inflows gathering into a quiet cloud. Introduce the approaching galaxy only after the player has formed an attachment to that place, then disrupt its familiar arrangements. This could strengthen loss and recognition, but it postpones the exceptional premise and risks an opening of routine cloud growth followed by more cloud growth. It deserves a separate playable comparison if the selected opening feels emotionally thin.
3. **Begin with the old star beyond the spiral, then reconstruct its lost surroundings.** The first scene shows an intact orange star against a broken galactic shape. Subsequent chapters rewind to the lane and reveal why stars and gas reached different fates. This provides an immediate mystery and a quieter voice. It also adds chronology management before players understand the materials, and must avoid supplying the old stream's later state as a primitive that bypasses its real construction.

**Chosen for this prototype: 1.** The initial comparison is short; subsequent work develops gas, stars and their surroundings rather than repeatedly renaming the old stream. Two separately followed inflows inside the same established lane create the nursery and the selected young system. The actual derived young stars then illuminate and disperse gas. Their bright behavior is not supplied as unrelated scenery.
