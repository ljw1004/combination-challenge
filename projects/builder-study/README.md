# Builder study

An original construction story, **A Fair in the Air**, for a player of about ten. The [active design brief](design-brief.md) owns intent and the current hypotheses. Development begins with playable construction and repeated independent evaluation; production artwork follows acceptance of the story.

Requested outcome: about 200 worthwhile combinations, repeated fast playtests, detailed playtesting, original diagrams and garden scenes with assembly continuity, promotion to `stories/builder`, commit and GitHub push.

The research, manuscript, production source and concise evaluation outcomes live here during development. Disposable sessions, screenshots and reviewer packets belong in `runs/builder-study/`. Retained playtest outputs follow the standing [evaluation guide](../../docs/fun-and-evaluation.md).

Current working manuscript: [builder-v11.md](builder-v11.md), 181 combinations with alternate experimental orders and 30 chapters including the ending. Earlier numbered inputs remain temporarily while their evaluations are resolved; they are not separate active briefs. [Evaluation outcomes](evaluation.md) records accepted changes and limits. The [geometry sources](art/README.md) are in mechanical prototype/review, before the final artwork expansion. Animation is deferred to a separate phase.
