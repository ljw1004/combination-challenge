# Builder evaluation outcomes

Development began on 6 November 2026. The [design brief](design-brief.md) owns the current intent; the [standing evaluation guide](../../docs/fun-and-evaluation.md) owns the methods and rubrics.

## Experiment design

The initial full-story screens use one fresh player per playable chapter, followed by an independent grader and fresh chapter synthesis. This is an explicitly reduced screening design for identifying revisions, not the normal three-player acceptance sample. Selected later passages use three players. Every revised assignment uses new participants.

Exact player guidance: “This is an original construction story designed for a curious player of about ten. Report your own experience candidly; do not impersonate a child. Consider whether the things you make and the mechanisms you learn are worth the player's time.”

Players: Terra Medium. Independent graders and chapter synthesis: Astra High. The runner freezes the manuscript, shared runtime, actual checkpoint history, rubrics and guidance before dispatch. Reports distinguish fast-mode hook evidence from detailed-mode meaningful-decision counts. Source-unexposed chapter tests do not establish enjoyment by real children or cumulative attachment in one continuous playthrough.

## First draft and revision decisions

The first complete draft had 189 combinations and 26 chapters including its ending. Shared analysis completed all 189 actions; starts averaged 8.2 objects with a maximum of nine. Full story lint found zero errors and one `fixed-analysis-limit` warning at the existing 20,000-state bound. This is incomplete exhaustive fixed-state verification, not a pass for all orders.

The first fast screen completed all 25 playable chapters with accepted closing reports, frozen replay, independent grading and synthesis. Its retained [report](playtests/2026.11.06_20.06.51/report.md) records mixed evidence, including a low hook for the whale-skin chapter and repeated criticism of release-only actions, repetitive fittings and unexplained assembly order. The successful trials are chapter-local evidence; they do not establish a continuous player's attachment.

Three fresh informed reviewers separately assessed narrative/necessity, early mechanisms, and later feasibility. The premise and distinct toys were retained. Adopted revisions removed repeated rubbing faults and most separate bench-release operations, supplied articulated dragon links, clarified the pinball lane/corner and crane delivery, shortened later hardware assembly, and reserved the complete airborne show for the guests. Pneumatic and animation projects add distinct worthwhile making. Power-changeover, test-load removal, lift balance, tethering and emergency-stop descriptions were corrected. Reviewers' source-level plausibility judgments remain separate from geometry validation.

A second informed story review accepted the standalone toys but requested earlier operation, social payoffs and further compression. The third draft moves the first powered wave and rover-wheel test into their construction chapters, makes bare intermediate results explain concrete contributions, completes Kit's promised turn and Jo's response, and gives the final accessory control its own causal action. The remaining 182 operations retain recognizable component construction; their usefulness is being tested rather than inferred from their count. A fresh physical review accepted the pneumatic/animation premises and identified two small control omissions, addressed by explicit stop resets and a supplied accessory extension/master-switch path.

## Further editorial and mechanical revisions

The second full fast screen completed all 29 playable chapters with closing reports, replay, independent grading and synthesis: [report](playtests/2026.11.06_20.19.34/report.md). New pneumatic and animation constructions supplied distinct observations, while mounting-only results remained weak. The final show had a high hook in this one-player screen; that is not a population estimate.

A fresh fourth-draft editorial review retained the premise, learning mechanisms and social ending, but identified excessive subdivision in three constructions, an impossible rover drawing sequence and a promise of a playable cam reversal absent from the recipes. Draft five combines routine tube/eyecap, boom/pin and wave support/motor mounting work, supplies paper before driving the rover, explicitly releases its test stops, and removes the unavailable reversal promise. It has 178 combinations, all on the completion witness. The count remains subordinate to worthwhile making.

Independent review of the first dragon model rejected it despite correct analytic linkage endpoints: actual rod bores faced perpendicular to their pins, the spring crossed its bracket, and service/retention hardware was incomplete. Repairs must be checked on actual geometry by a new reviewer. Whale layout work also exposed a projector-aiming wedge that would misalign its drive belt; draft five moves an adjustable screen instead. These findings support retaining editable canonical geometry and reviewing meshes, not trusting an attractive render or endpoint equations alone.

## Detailed play and order revision

The full third-draft detailed screen completed 29/29 playable chapters, with closing reports, replay, independent grades and synthesis: [report](playtests/2026.11.06_20.27.32/report.md). Its evidence distinguishes low deliberation during familiar assembly from worthwhile mechanism explanations and finished-toy satisfaction. It also identifies consequential arbitrary-order friction: receiver before caster, spring before the cam test, lens before the cloth, decoration before the replacement-cam test, and module mounting before whale skin/fins.

A new informed gameplay review retained the constructions and proposed targeted order changes. Draft seven uses the existing compound-slot semantics for independently mounted rover components; allows spring-first, pawl-first and lens-first experimental routes; supplies the dragon's scales, wave soundbox, target score wheel and switched lamp lead as explicit prepared components; and installs the lift module while the whale frame is open. It has 180 combinations, with 173 actions in the selected completion witness; the additional routes are alternatives, not compulsory padding. Fast semantic lint has no errors. Its mixed-fate warning for the rover deck is intentional: fitting preserves the deck, while completing the guarded rover transforms it.

A fresh informed editorial reviewer accepted draft seven for illustration development, subject to geometry validation and local hardware clarifications. Draft eight specifies the motor-pulley insertion path, supplies the launcher's adjustable stop explicitly, and keeps the rover on its test supports until the drawing operation releases it. These are physical-continuity clarifications, not new recipes.

The fifth-draft third fast screen completed all 29 playable chapters plus the ending, with no interrupted assignments: [report](playtests/2026.11.06_20.44.33/report.md). Seventh-draft focused fast and three-player detailed checks are underway. A diagnostic response was not added to any player record. The adapter now identifies forbidden event types in errors so technical failures can be distinguished from attempted source/tool access. The focused seventh-draft runs finished with four fast-assignment failures and three detailed-assignment failures; completed chapter reports remain separate from the excluded assignments. The [eighth-draft fast replacement batch](playtests/2026.11.06_21.01.34/report.md) completed all four chapters (11, 12, 17, 18), including independent grades and synthesis, with no interruption.
