# Active projects

This index describes live projects only. Each project keeps its briefs, research, plans and retained supporting material together throughout its development.

- [Cookbook study](cookbook-study/README.md): ongoing refinement of the cooking experience, cookbook narrative, icons, backgrounds and fixed stations. The study includes earlier candidates, source artwork, standalone PNGs and review evidence to support further revisions and new artwork.
- [Cosmic study](cosmic-study/README.md): storyboarding a raw-creation journey from early particles and stars to Earth, the Moon and a sea. Preserves the supplied brainstorming and explores worthwhile combinations between the major transformations.

The [editorial and research backlog](backlog.md) tracks unresolved work and ideas across the repository. Temporary downloads and disposable output belong in ignored scratch space such as `runs/`.
