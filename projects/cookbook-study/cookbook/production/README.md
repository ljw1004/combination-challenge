# Cook’s Pen production

The finished set is installed in [stories/cookbook](../../../../stories/cookbook/): 230 item identities, five fitted trolley layers, fixed stations and 35 backgrounds. This directory keeps the source material needed to continue the illustration work, not the details of each production run.

- [artwork](artwork/): fifteen ordinary item atlases, five targeted repair sheets and the trolley source. The early botanical ground and coloured equipment atlas are retained as past artistic directions.
- [catalogue](catalogue/catalogue.json): subjects, depictions, intended sizes and batch order.
- [generation](generation/): item/equipment briefs and the targeted repair instructions.
- [geometry](geometry.json), [item manifest](item-manifest.json) and [framing settings](tools/framing-overrides.json): current source extraction and display dimensions.
- [Acceptance decisions](reviews/decisions.md): the final recognition findings, repairs and remaining ambiguities.
- [Editorial approach](narrative/editorial-notes.md) and [original travelling manuscript](baseline/kitchen-caravan.md): the narrative premise and its predecessor.

Use [export instructions](tools/README.md) for new source drawings. The current manuscript is maintained once, in the story directory; production no longer keeps extra complete copies. Backgrounds and fixed equipment have their own linked collections in the [study index](../../README.md).
