# Cookbook outcomes

These summaries record the development decisions of 1 November 2026. Run inputs and outputs have been discarded. They are not replayable evidence or automatic validation of later changes; use the [instructions](verification.md) for new work.

| Work | Final outcome |
|---|---|
| Art direction | Fifty playable comparisons informed the choice. B1 Cook’s Pen was selected for personality; S1 tight fading halo was the familiar mobile-game runner-up. Watercolor and cut-paper were specifically admired. The curated collection retains the distinct directions and completed sheets rather than every variant’s assets. |
| Cookbook adaptation | All 36 chapters became sessions developing a cookbook. The mechanics, 230 identities, recipes, goal conditions and five trolley slots were preserved. The user reported playing from beginning to end, compelled to see more. |
| Recognition | Fresh model review led to targeted repairs of structure, wetness, components and cake layers. [Acceptance decisions](cookbook/production/reviews/decisions.md) record remaining ambiguity in powders, flavour/history, filled meringue nests and mixed roots/beans. |
| Backgrounds | Thirty-five culinary themes replaced repetitive botanical grounds. Contextual spreads replaced isolated decorative ingredients; shallow bottom bleed and faint upper motifs became the approved composition. Five pages use cloth. |
| Fixed stations | Pencil A won. Printed labels were removed, pairs separated and targets individually registered; the shelf gained padding. All 35 pages received the selected apparatus treatment. |
| Implementation | Full arrival and equipment-interaction checks passed, including reload and completion. The last implementation suite passed 142 Node and 92 Chromium tests, and the production build passed with existing warnings. |
| Rename | Kitchen became Cookbook in the story, route and study directory. Installed artwork stayed byte-identical, with unchanged mechanics. |

The user’s full playthrough is attributed feedback, not a controlled enjoyment trial. Recognition reviewers were models; browser checks used author-informed checkpoints and Chromium emulation. Native-device comfort, human recognition speed and fine ingredient distinctions remain open.

The later curation keeps about 308 MB of art, references and instructions. It removes duplicate exports, superseded chapter sets, per-item sample assets and iteration records. The original 35 final page PNGs and all installed story/artwork files remain unchanged.

Curation checks passed: 179 gallery artwork views, three/four-column and phone layouts, links and four live chapter previews. All 35 pages reconstructed byte-for-byte from the retained paintings and registration. A preview export exercised item extraction, repaired drawings and trolley layers without changing installed files. Current story/runtime files and all 304 installed artwork files are unchanged. Detailed check output was discarded.
