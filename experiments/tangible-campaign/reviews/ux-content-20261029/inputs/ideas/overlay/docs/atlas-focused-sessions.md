New identity: `atlas-focused-sessions-v6`. Predecessor: `atlas-selection-repairs-v5`, preserved unchanged.

## What changed

- The 34 dense chapters become 85 focused reading sessions. The first session retains its original chapter ID; subsequent IDs append `-session-2`, `-session-3`, or `-session-4`.
- Later sessions are **evening / next-morning work at the same worktable**, not a succession of invented topical alcoves or trips to new cities. They share the existing era/place artwork. A later session has no italic context heading; genuine original era/place arrivals can have one.
- All 530 item identities, 283 recipes and their input lifecycles, source references, and original objective IDs/output requirements remain. No new combination or procedural gate was added. The ordinary source inventory is partitioned exactly once and fully present at each session’s arrival.
- The persistent earlier panels retain their surviving objects and positions. Only useful actual work travels. The existing state-sensitive visit closure selects a prepared conclusion or its still-needed antecedents; there is no new runtime/schema machinery, hidden inventory, stock drip or retroactive consumption.
- Twenty-two new frontier objectives make an already-needed conclusion visible at the end of its earlier session. Existing intermediate/reveal dependencies are made session-local. An intermediate reused by another goal in the same session stays intermediate; a goal that now ends a session is terminal there.
- The old longitude route’s cross-session `after` references are removed only after its lunar determination and sea-tested clock have become compulsory completed goals in the preceding session. The original provisioned-route outcome is still required. The optional time-based longitude remains optional.
