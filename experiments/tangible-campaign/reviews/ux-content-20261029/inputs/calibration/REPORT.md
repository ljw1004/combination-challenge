## Readiness reassessment

The first editorial handoff was **not yet fully calibrated**. It rewrote entry cues, but its validation inventory missed several inherited completion popups. The actual composed Elements bakery still said “save a little summer for the gathering”; the orchard completion asked fruit to stay ripe forever. This successor corrects those surfaces and sets the Elements orchard **entry cue and motivation exactly** to:
