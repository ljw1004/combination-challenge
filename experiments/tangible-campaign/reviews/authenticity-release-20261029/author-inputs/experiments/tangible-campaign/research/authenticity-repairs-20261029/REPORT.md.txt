### Why the lamp repair was not forced

`camp-signal-lamp-bracket` incorporates and preserves `signal-lamp`; the engine conditionally retains a loose source while `signal-6-signal-beacon` still needs it. Later `camp-light` incorporates the bench lamp into the lit workbench. Existing `preserve` carries **visible component names**, not the component's conditional capabilities. Giving every bench/lit-workbench the directed-signal capability would falsely grant it to the solar/ordinary-lantern branches. A new variant just for the first mount would not solve the next incorporation. No shared-runtime change, false spare supply, disassembly chore, or hidden order requirement was introduced to hide this open issue.

## Research and limits

- [FAO, Principles of Oil Extraction](https://www.fao.org/4/X5043E/x5043E03.htm) distinguishes preparation/expression and explicitly describes traditional pressure from stones, levers and related equipment. This supports the mechanism family, **not** the efficiency/yield of the fictional wooden press. It is contrary evidence to an absolute impossibility claim about stone pressing.
- [FAO, Ghani technology](https://www.fao.org/4/T4660T/T4660T0B.htm) describes a stone/wood mortar, loaded mechanism and oil collection. The game press is **not claimed to be a historically exact ghani**; no animal, motor or ghani was silently supplied.
- [V&A, Richard Batterham: methods of making](https://www.vam.ac.uk/articles/richard-batterham-methods-of-making) distinguishes forming, unfired ware and firing. This supports the state distinction, not a complete machine implied by the generic Wheel token.
- [Met Office, Make your own rain gauge](https://weather.metoffice.gov.uk/learn-about/met-office-for-schools/other-content/other-resources/weather-station/rain-gauge) uses a transparent vessel and external scale, and addresses the bottom/geometry and repeated observation. **The candidate deliberately makes the narrower claim of relative fill level**, not the article's calibrated rainfall measurement.
- [Darwin, Origin of Species, 1861 primary text](https://darwin-online.org.uk/converted/published/1861_Origin_F381/1861_Origin_F381.html) separately connects variation, struggle and reproductive preservation. The candidate is an attributed teaching conjunction, not a new quotation or a proof of all evolutionary mechanisms.
- [Fitzwilliam Museum, torches](https://data.fitzmuseum.cam.ac.uk/id/terminology/term-68417) identifies fuel-bearing fiber and a portable support as ordinary torch forms. [Milwaukee Public Museum, oil lamps](https://www.mpm.edu/node/15505) identifies plant oils and fibrous wicks. Using those together here is a bounded material inference, not a primary historical attribution of this exact torch or a validated burn duration.

Accepted prior abstractions — ordinary cooking, writing, servicing, actual observations, the crossing/river/harbour map chain and the workshop chest family — are not redesigned because a blind pair score alone was low. Prior reports and their scores remain unchanged. The old scone-mixture naming suggestion is outside this batch and was not silently folded into it. This does **not** claim every adjacent pottery/food-storage or lighthouse-performance question is settled.
