# Atlas: full redesign method (28 October 2026)

## Scope and dependency

This is a new, separately identified full-length candidate, not a repair or replacement of the original Atlas. The original campaign, archived route and save identity must remain byte-identical. Target: 200–300 substantive combinations, broad intellectual history, a crowded research desk, and the sustained experience of making connections across time. No checkout, GitHub, publication or UI/browser changes are authorized in this isolated workstream.

**New user prerequisite:** another agent collects all available *In Our Time* episode abstracts. This agent must then independently mine the full collection and choose 5–30 interesting episodes as inspiration or directions for research. Episode choice does not imply chapter choice. A topic/chapter plan is not yet committed. Corpus mining, including coverage gaps and selection reasoning, precedes the plan.

## What changes from the previous research candidate

- Replace finicky arithmetic and microscopic preparatory steps with intelligible intellectual moves: a counterexample, a new explanatory model, a technique made possible by an earlier theory, a contradiction exposed, an artistic juxtaposition, a competing practice or an institution.
- Preserve astronomy and perspective, but use arguments understandable from labels. No invisible diagram, remembered formula or mental calculation is a prerequisite.
- Sources may be examined through optional Read; labels carry the playable distinction. Reading is not an extra task or hidden answer key.
- Normal partner highlighting is part of the game, not a playtest failure. AI-reader stops are diagnostic observations, never an authority to replace the user's brief.
- No repeated “premise + claim → report” machinery. A result must be an identifiable idea, object, model, practice, tension or possibility with a reason for its two inputs.
- No compulsory old-note toll. A previous creation must change what the new recipe can mean or do, not merely certify continuity.

## Braided continuity and historical honesty

Every chapter after the opening consumes, applies, challenges or materially reuses at least one earlier **created** item, preferably several, and frequently returns to older strands after a gap. A prior raw supply, ornamental carried manuscript, newly supplied duplicate, or narrative mention does not qualify.

Every cross-time edge is classified independently:

1. **Documented transmission:** identifiable text, translation, citation, institution or material route.
2. **Historical adaptation / criticism:** a later practitioner changes or contests an inherited idea.
3. **Modern teaching comparison:** a meaningful juxtaposition, explicitly not evidence of influence.
4. **Speculative construction:** future fiction, not a prediction or a historical assertion.

Chronological ordering does not imply inevitable improvement. Religious and natural accounts can coexist. Models may coexist when the evidence does not distinguish them. Counterarguments may remain live. Non-Western traditions are active contributors and interlocutors with actual connecting routes, not a decorative checklist. Contested macrohistorical explanations (including arguments popularized by *Guns, Germs, and Steel*) are attributed and tested against omitted mechanisms, not promoted to settled fact.

## Source protocol

The corpus is an inspiration index, not sufficient evidence for recipe claims. Follow selected leads to primary texts and authoritative research, museums, archives and scientific institutions. Record dates, URLs, claim scope and limitations. Do not invent quotations, attribute a fictional teaching example to an original source, or confuse an episode abstract's juxtaposition with demonstrated influence. Retain rights/coverage information supplied by the collector. Avoid copying abstract prose into the game.

## Mechanical contract

- Existing declarative recipe grammar; normal highlighting; no finite-scarcity puzzle.
- Explicit `retain`, `transform`, or `incorporate` reason for every object input.
- Transform a particular draft/material at its position; retain genuinely reusable sources, evidence and tools. No graph-exhaustion culling.
- `preserve` for any transformed/incorporated visible construction.
- Intermediate goals have explicit successors; final gold objects do not feed another goal in the same chapter.
- Carry only what the current scene needs; leave unused material in its prior scene, never delete it to tidy the desk.
- Locations are occasional catalysts, operands or meaningful placements, not universal gates.
- Author `onAlreadyComplete` explicitly for each stage: mostly `skip`, rare non-mechanical `bridge` for an essential story fact. Do not repeat earned work.
- Rare milestones reward pivotal discoveries without becoming one-per-chapter recaps.
- Checkpoints are exact replayed action prefixes, never synthetic inventories or unearned discoveries.

## Evidence and stopping condition

Deliver architecture and representative redesigned cases after corpus mining, then a complete candidate with source registry, inputs, recipes, motivations, objectives, lifecycles, chapter connection ledger, author route and earned checkpoints. Run schema/lifecycle/objective/construction checks, full-route replay, scene-local availability, prior-created continuity checks, source-reference validation, save/undo/reapply and multiple-order logical routes. Obtain independent narrative/gameplay and authenticity reviews. Address concrete findings without reverting to a miniature. Root integrates only the final pinned artifacts. No browser/native UI or human-enjoyment claim will be made.
