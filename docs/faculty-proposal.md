# Faculties from actual combinations

**Status:** designer invention after the completed Field, Imprints, Resonance and Frontier plays; selected for a small logical test, not demonstrated fun or a long game. [Frontier’s completed review](frontier-play-review.md) retains the appreciated ending and the failure to reach180–240 opportunities. No UI work is proposed.

## Four-law proposal

An enacted construction becomes another maker’s faculty. This is grounded in Imprints’ [demonstrated order-dependent memory](logical-imprints.md#executable-causal-chain); the following transfer and learning laws are inventions.

1. Every physical combination leaves an inexhaustible **trace**: its actual operands, primitive eligibility guard, relative place/link footprint and provenance. Traces reference source history; transferring one never invents history at its destination.
2. **Trace + Memory at an avatar’s place** teaches that avatar the traced operation. It can enact it in the same world, binding the footprint to compatible terrain and connections. Bindings, guards and consequences remain inspectable.
3. Physically combining two learned faculties creates an ordered procedure; the player chooses their order. Each operation runs only when its public guard currently holds. Material effects and intermediate arrivals occur normally between operations.
4. The maker travels existing paths toward the nearest compatible footprint, executes its procedure, then considers another. It rests when nothing can change. Executions leave ordinary world history and new traces.

The qualitative transition is from constructing terrain to **constructing an acting procedure**. Teaching transfers something the player actually did, including its spatial dependencies.

**Hand-run, two contexts.** The player has grown reeds at upstream B and fired downstream C. Those actual traces supply “root upstream” and “fire downstream” faculties.

- **Downstream soil:** firing is ineligible. Either ordering roots B; gentle water and seeds establish a descendant at C. The maker extends the familiar orchard’s living process.
- **Downstream clay receiving exact A:** fire-first C retains A; subsequently rooting B fragments future arrivals, but C keeps its earlier copy. Root-first B fragments A before C is fired, so C never acquires that exact copy. Both procedures finish with rooted B and fired C. Their different histories remain consequential.

Later broadcasting C can republish retained A, producing strong flow and changing downstream growth. The old crossing becomes a teacher and causal ancestor; the old record becomes an active source. This transfers Imprints’ existing distinction into a new actor’s capability, rather than unlocking an independent puzzle.

**Next intention:** decide what agency to give this maker, then respond to what its procedure changes in the inhabited world. Preserve exact memories while protecting a living corridor, or revise the procedure when those commitments conflict. Creating a procedure is another kind of combining with material consequences.

**Attacks:** harmless gardening plus isolation could satisfy every ambition; “fire everywhere first” might dominate both orders. Test these immediately. A universal procedure achieving every valued result means automation followed by closure. This engine does not yet justify180–240 opportunities; further faculties must create different relationships in this world, not more destinations.

## Independent design review before implementation

**Keep the player's combination concrete.** The constructed output is a faculty or procedure. Its operands must be an actual trace and Memory, or two learned faculties, with the place participating. A command such as “make a garden at C” would instead select a finished result and commission its construction. Maker activity is the consequence of creating its faculty, not a substitute for the player’s act. If most later play becomes selecting destinations and watching known macros, the intended interaction has weakened.

**Transfer behavior, not a permission key or a fabricated past.** Trace provenance may identify the old crossing, but provenance alone cannot earn causal transfer. The procedure must preserve the recorded primitive operation and its spatial dependency. Destination memories, routes and arrivals must be read from the actual destination. Channel/fork/basin catalysts would count only if channel succession, guarded branching or feedback actually change execution; merely enabling the same procedure would not establish the proposed qualitative change.

**Smallest functional test:** record one actual player combination that activates a broadcast, then physically combine that trace with Memory at the maker. Apply the learned operation in two recipient histories made by legal prefixes: identical fired, nonbroadcasting surfaces with current fragment arrivals, one having actually retained exact A earlier and one never having received it. The same taught operation must republish exact A only in the first history. The second must broadcast fragments, not inherit the teacher’s A. Connect an unrooted, unfired clay neighbor: exact republication should flood it; fragment-only flow should leave it passable. This avoids relying on a soil neighbor that may already have rooted autonomously. The changed walkability makes the causal transfer affect later behavior. Test this before adding procedure composition or a campaign.

**Bold experience hypothesis:** “I made a garden; can I make a gardener I trust around the home we share?” The interest would come from granting usable agency to a familiar counterpart, seeing its real effects on a valued old place, then wanting to teach or compose something different. A deterministic procedure is not evidence of consciousness or independently felt values. Adoption of this project remains to be tested; no request queue should supply it by default.
