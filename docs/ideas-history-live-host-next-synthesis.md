# After the live-host pilot: consequential named combinations

27 October 2026. **Prospective synthesis, not implementation authorization.** Preserve the frozen source, raw capture and independent review. The newer human preference for meaningful named binary combinations and portable creations takes precedence over silently carrying the old physical assembly grammar into Mars.

## What the actual result supports

The one fresh normal pilot completed in 44 accepted selections: 31 station and 13 later, with 34 manual physical transitions and one automatic transition separately counted. The player valued actual serviced material becoming a recorded artifact, and hosting as a changed later purpose for the same apparatus. Independent replay verifies those consequences. Its two taught absolute poses and current-view guard were legitimate; the relative half-turn was not required.

The player also described philosophical combinations as disclosed unlock/application steps, not conceptual discovery, and preferred keeping actual handover while shortening installation ceremony. Noncivic hosting would serve the same resident requests in the final state. Therefore this pilot supports a small construction-to-operation connection, not demonstrated value in civic governance, a larger ontology, human enjoyment or approximately 200 decisions.

The newer human feedback separately rejects sticky location assembly and generic composite bags. Positive agent remarks about this historical fixture do not override that feedback. Do not transplant its entire physical representation into the new Mars game.

## Next change to propose, not build yet

Make the **first meaningful construction both reveal a reusable idea and enact its concrete result**, rather than requiring a global unlock followed by a flag installation where neither selection itself expresses the new possibility.

For example, a future named-recipe grammar could expose:

- **Demonstrated View Method + Resident Mandate → Resident Viewing Practice.** The output is an inspectable executable practice with actual demonstrated controls, requested purpose, refusal scope and current physical bindings. It is not a bag containing arbitrary policies. A whole method is one operand; its original construction is not counted again.
- **Resident Viewing Practice + Working Observatory → Civic Observatory.** The actual existing apparatus becomes a taught successor service: requests route to it and invoke those real controls. Former human operators remain people with knowledge and legal manual alternatives. The output is a named creation whose operation can be inspected, not an extra complete flag. Discovery and first enactment can occur together; later known copies of the *practice* do not fabricate actual observatories or duplicate record history.

This proposal compresses the existing ceremony; it does **not** claim that renaming the same two settings creates new depth. Physical binding, actual request operation and continued human life must remain real. A static observatory can use an empty practice where genuinely sufficient. A direct human service still counts as service, even if it is a different chosen project. If the binary recipe only hides a row of preselected permission settings, reject that presentation rather than calling it a philosophical advance.

The same pattern is more promising across different lived possibilities:

- **Continuing Person + Fork Kinship → Divergent Family:** actually produce independent continuing lives with shared experience, not unlock an actor-duplication button.
- **Divergent Family + Returned Experience → Reunion Practice:** actually change what its members can do through a selected new experience, retaining distinct itineraries; do not merely award shared-memory tags.
- **Experienced Sequence + Sensory Translation → Embodied Score:** actually construct a new ordered performance that another actor can learn/use. No claim of embodiment is earned by a source label alone.

These are proposed meaningful typed outputs, not invitations to make every family, practice and institution a universal container. The public result must expose its behavior and constituents without generic “X + Y” labels. Alternative recipes and whole-method shortcuts remain legal. Known concepts are inexhaustible. Discovery can be comfortable and worthwhile without every move being a dilemma.

## Why this must be evaluated as a collection, not another tiny rescue pilot

The larger estuary architecture supplies candidate cross-purpose reuse: an explanatory method becomes a lived episode, a returned episode becomes a performance, a construction becomes a learned host, and the same performance becomes a shared event between communities. The paper trajectory shows both real possible reuse and severe compression. A simple final configuration is legitimate; it does not by itself invalidate the pleasure of learning a large catalogue.

Before implementing, select the actual semantics of one complete connected collection. Identify what is learned or changed by each named result, which later purpose uses it differently, and what is merely interface compression. Preserve normal versus complete-catalogue and universal optional/static/manual controls. Do not force a separate application of every prerequisite or add another isolated camera/family kernel to seek a better rating.

The promising genuinely new branch is experience translation that changes a recipient's usable abilities, but its current symbolic-order sketch is not yet sufficient evidence. It needs an understandable construction space and two different concrete later uses. This remains a design hypothesis pending synthesis with the human's preferred binary grammar; no additional source or player is authorized by this document.
