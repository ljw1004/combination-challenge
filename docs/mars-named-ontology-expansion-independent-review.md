# Independent design review: larger named Mars ontology

27 October 2026. Design review only, by the source-exposed independent reviewer, not a fresh player or a human playtest. No engine, UI, recipe, capture or existing evidence was changed. This reviews the proposed expansion separately from the bounded grammar checkpoint; it does not authorize expansion during that build.

## Decision

**Keep the larger shared content pool as a proposal; revise several names and specify one complete objective sequence before implementation.** It offers recognizable inventions beyond another collection of supply-network chores. Familiar, easy combinations can be worthwhile progress. There is no reason to make every intermediate indispensable, hide its compatibility, or compress every obvious recipe out of the game. Conversely, a larger table alone does not establish an enjoyable longer campaign or a numerical duration.

The governing human grammar is the new named binary model: one global token per kind, inputs preserved, no copies or generic composite bags, target-derived context, portable location products, freely movable goal placements and progressive objective-specific supplies. Earlier assembly-based agent praise does not validate this different interaction grammar. This review does not import geometric simulation requirements or real survival advice into it.

## Strong content worth retaining

- **Solar charger:** a recognizable invention with later uses in radio, light and vehicle branches. Reusing its single token can make earlier work feel valuable without a finite battery economy or repeated manufacture.
- **Filter pump, watering hose, drip irrigator and garden sprayer:** distinct conventional devices, not simply labels for arbitrary bags. They can support different watering intentions. None needs to be an obligatory upgrade after another already answers the objective.
- **Seed tray → protected growing:** understandable transformation and care. An ordinary watered tray and context-enabled sprouting make the habitat matter semantically. The portable output avoids a sticky accumulated garden container.
- **Pressure patch, thermal blanket, dust screen, air cleaner:** plausible salvage repurposing when component descriptions provide the ordinary intended materials. Rejecting water-filter-plus-fan is good discipline: mere word overlap is not compatibility.
- **Hoist, folding ramp, field camera and panorama:** useful expedition inventions with purposes different from maintaining a base. A panorama is a recognizable portable informational thing; it need not unlock an entire archive checklist to justify existing.
- **Final compact base:** reusing things already made can be a satisfying ending. Short finishing work is not evidence that the earlier making was pointless. Do not require recrafting merely to extend it.

These are prospective merits, not observed appreciation. Some recognition depends on descriptions or images that do not yet exist.

## Names and pair meanings needing revision

| Candidate | Concern | Bounded revision |
|---|---|---|
| Solar rover + radio → Survey rover | Radio adds communication, not an evident surveying instrument. Saying that no survey was performed does not itself explain the new surveying capability. | Prefer **Radio rover** / **Remote rover**, or explicitly establish sensing equipment in the rover definition. A later camera-based survey rover is another possible branch, not a compulsory added step. |
| Cold frame + sprouted tray → Greenhouse; frame + propagator → Greenhouse | A small cold frame containing a tray has not obviously become a person-sized greenhouse. Two routes suggest incompatible scales. | Choose a consistent miniature growing-house meaning, or use **Planted cold frame** and a separately justified greenhouse route. Do not generate size variants just to mandate more recipes. |
| Frame + toolkit → Workbench | A frame could be a trestle bench base, or a flimsy picture frame. Adding tools alone does not explain its work surface. | Give Frame a recognizable general-purpose modular-frame description compatible with cart, shelter and workbench, or choose a specific supporting component only where the distinction earns its complexity. |
| Lens + tube → Telescope; lens + lamp → Inspection lamp | These are acceptable named-game abstractions only if lens means a suitable optical component, not a claim that any glass disk yields a working instrument. | Describe suitable salvaged optics; a magnifying inspection lamp is especially legible. No optics simulator or compulsory extra eyepiece chain is needed. |
| Catch tray + clear cover → Solar still | The draft already recognizes the missing collection geometry. A covered tray can also simply be a propagator. | Make the tray/cover descriptions support a basin, condensation cover and collector; distinguish from growing cover by meaningful use, not hidden ontology guessing. |
| Winch + rope → Hoist; hoist + tripod → Tripod hoist | Good integrated functions, but a winch may already imply a cable. | State an accessible winch mechanism needing a lifting line. Accept recovered complete hoists when a scenario supplies one. |
| Sealed box + gloves → Glovebox | Recognizable if gloves mount into access ports, less so if ordinary gloves merely sit in a box. | Describe adaptable ports/gauntlets; do not substitute an anonymous equipment kit. |

Some power prefixes also need restraint. A solar radio, solar rover and solar heater can each be recognizable, but requiring every appliance to acquire the same prefix would repeat a manufacturing pattern rather than expand purpose. Existing powered devices remain useful. The signal beacon and relay station should answer visibly different requests (visual signaling versus radio communication), not interchangeable wording on otherwise identical mandatory slots.

## Catalysts: mostly meaningful, with specific boundaries

**Sunny ledge for a still or solar cooker** supplies the named energy condition; **warm habitat for germination** supplies the growing environment; **repair bench for a pressure patch** can supply holding/alignment tools. These are semantic settings rather than arbitrary unlock locations. Their usefulness must be communicated before action; no player should have to infer a secret place from failure.

The bench needs a precise description: “clamps hold fabric and sealant during patch-making” is enough for this abstraction. Do not imply that merely visiting the bench certifies a pressure-safe patch in real life. If an equivalent clamping device later appears, consider an alternative recipe or setting rather than preserving a sacred bench password. Not every setting needs multiple substitutes immediately.

Context comes from the actual target B. A loose canister dropped onto a seed tray in the habitat can sprout it; reversing the drag onto a canister elsewhere need not do so. Pair result commutativity is therefore conditional on the same context, not unconditional gesture equivalence. The content sheet should explain this without inventing a free context selector.

Under exact-context precedence, if Sprouted tray already exists, that contextual combination must not create a duplicate or silently fall back to an ordinary Watered seed tray unless the contract explicitly changes. The player can move the target out of the habitat to access the ordinary recipe. Check this interaction during authoring: optional branches should remain comprehensible, not appear to disappear for mysterious reasons. This is a consequence to clarify, not a demand to change the current model midbuild.

A warm environment's accelerated growth and a sunny location's cooking are declared game abstractions. They need no elapsed-time tax, hunger meter, pressure simulation or finite fuel stock.

## Objective and one-token consistency

The draft correctly forbids requiring the same kind in two simultaneous slots. A second independent constraint is **one visible slot per place**: two different required things cannot both remain at the same place either. “Contact, water, growing, shelter and useful work” therefore needs distinct explicit placement destinations, alternative eligibility at a shared destination, or clearly historical milestones. Do not disguise multi-object accumulation as a goal location.

Inputs remain, but dragging a placed input returns it loose. Thus retained ownership is not retained current placement. When using a slotted invention later, choosing it as the stationary target may preserve the old goal while dragging it as A may regress that goal. Both are lawful. Objectives must label history versus current truth and permit retrieval of the unique old token; avoid solving this with sticky placement or clones.

Each new objective needs a small exact declaration: introduced supplies and places, accepted result alternatives, current versus historical completion, and which existing kinds it can reuse. If it supplies a kind already made, reveal the same token rather than another instance. Global novelty also means alternative routes are choices of how to first invent something, not repeatable production. Do not advertise all route variants as separate necessary accomplishments.

The final food suggestion is not yet a complete chain: Growing potato planter and Potatoes are different named concepts, and the pool specifies no harvesting relation. Either supply potatoes openly for the cooking branch, end cultivation with the growing planter, or author one recognizable harvest interaction. Do not retroactively claim that the existing rows already transform cultivated plants into dinner. Similarly, pressure-patch discovery is not automatically a sealed repair performed; choose an honest placement objective or an explicit named repair pair.

## Which sequences are genuinely different?

- **Contact → home** provides the clearest spine and should retain its sparse opening. Protected growing and a useful workspace offer new purposes without requiring all lighting variants.
- **Expedition** is promising if a route actually calls for a cart/rover, portable illumination, viewing or lifting. “Reachable contact point” currently describes intent rather than a defined named-game condition. Specify a comprehensible destination request; do not quietly reintroduce traversal physics or demand a photo at every place.
- **Cold cultivation** can be a distinct compact alternative. Choose a small coherent approach such as protected growing plus optional warmth and watering. Requiring propagator, heated propagator, greenhouse and heated greenhouse in sequence would dilute that distinction.
- **Repair/inspection** can justify tools that the other missions do not. It needs a concrete object needing inspection, holding or repair. A list of tools placed for their own sake would be weak even if every name were plausible.
- **Dependable base** works best as consolidation, not another five-stage restaging of the same work. Previously useful outputs may satisfy it immediately.

These may be alternative scenarios or parts of one sequence. Neither status is presently fixed. A future whole-experience test must specify its actual offered sequence; do not add unrelated scenario action totals or infer a campaign from the breadth of the catalogue.

## Recommended next gate

Wait for the bounded grammar checkpoint observations. Then select one coherent expanded sequence and a few genuinely supported alternatives; revise the handful of weak names above. Write exact objective/supply/place declarations and inspect their dependency closure, global uniqueness, target-context behavior and simultaneous placement feasibility. This is a content-consistency screen, not a demand to eliminate every easy recipe or prove that every intermediate has a unique mandated objective.

Keep recognizable optional inventions where making them may be welcome. Exclude generic “base kit” composites and compulsory near-synonym variant ladders. Implement only under a separate freeze, then observe the complete fresh experience, including what players actually choose and say about making and reusing things. No numerical promise, fun conclusion or new engine authorization follows from this review.

## Final-draft reconciliation — 27 October 2026, 00:21 UTC

The initial critique above is preserved against its initial source hash. I inspected the subsequent expansion draft, SHA256 `371c7b1d1de0c33a6851906b72e1516dc25bb89c2041e66d9bf866c8ee52a4ee`. It now names the proposed vehicle **Radio rover**, explicitly distinguishes this from changing the checkpoint, states the independent one-slot conflict rule, and specifies context lookup before novelty suppression. Those changes address the corresponding naming and rule-clarity findings at the design level. The added growing-structure scale caution acknowledges, rather than yet resolves, the actual choice of names and scale.

The added **Growing potato planter + harvest basket at growing court → Potato harvest**, followed by a contextual cooking alternative yielding Baked potatoes, closes the previously missing *authored dependency* between cultivation and food. The earlier statement that no harvest relation was specified applies to the initial draft, not this revision. This is not validation that the new abstraction feels natural or supplies a genuinely meaningful catalyst. A basket and a growing planter have a recognizable harvesting relation; a particular court needs a disclosed cultivation condition or episode meaning, rather than becoming a compulsory address for an otherwise portable harvest. The draft explicitly labels the setting an abstract cultivation/harvest episode, not a hidden elapsed-time requirement. That qualification is welcome but remains a hypothesis to select and test, not evidence of experienced growth.

Target-derived semantics still apply: placing the basket at the court and dragging the planter onto it can produce the named harvest without any prior planter residence there. If that is intended shorthand for the episode, say so; do not later interpret it as evidence that the planter physically matured at that location. Retaining the growing planter and basket is consistent with inexhaustible concepts, and global novelty prevents repeat harvesting into duplicate Potato harvest tokens. There is no need to introduce depletion, a timer, mandatory tending repetitions or a second basket to make the branch honest. Supplied potatoes remain a legitimate simpler cooking alternative.

The final recommendation is unchanged: keep the expanded pool as prospective content, preserve comfortable useful making, and select a coherent exact sequence after checkpoint feedback. The revised text resolves several presentational gaps but does not establish implemented correctness, a longer enjoyable campaign or a numerical workload. Remaining component-definition and concrete objective-purpose questions above still apply. No source, engine or UI changes were made by this review.
