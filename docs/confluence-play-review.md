# Confluence: first long play and the next invention

Confluence 2 supports a short, coherent creation story. It did **not** sustain the requested 180–240 decision opportunities. The two actual plays and the source model remain frozen; a continuation is being built separately.

The [elective-interest player](../results/logical-players/fun-confluence-interest-1.json) stopped at its first checkpoint after 20 interventions. It valued its settlement: exact quiet memory, plural public living accounts and Ilex’s garden. It left several statuses unresolved deliberately, with no recorded further elective construction. The [mandated diagnostic](../results/logical-players/fun-confluence-long-1.json) made 42 interventions plus finish, organized into 12 player-annotated episodes. All five movements had unlocked by intervention 17. It stopped because further actions would mean permutations, prerequisite probing or deliberately breaking a completed project. Neither run measures human minutes.

## What produced a next intention

The strongest elective episode began with a mistake in service of a chosen ambition. Publication at the village carried publicity back to a meeting with a stored exact witness record. The meeting flooded. The player wanted to retain public living speech, so it changed the incoming relation to reeds, then made the meeting a grove, erasing the already stored local exact copy. The original archive survived. Transmission, storage and current exposure were different things; changing only the future route could not remove the past local copy.

This is a mechanism worth developing: an invention acquires a consequence elsewhere; the player keeps caring about the original intention and composes a response. The source of value was not that three recipes existed. It was what using them allowed this particular valley to become.

Reeds also changed their role across play: carrying life, translating testimony, separating exact memory from public circulation, then enabling a garden authored by Ilex. This supports the owner's hunch about familiar combining becoming meaningful in a different context. It does not excuse repeated status quotations or opaque prerequisite probing.

The long diagnostic found a second useful relation: binding memory guaranteed custody, but incoming authority then compelled Mneme. A reed connection blocked authority while retaining stored exact memory. Material topology had become an institutional boundary. Again, the same world mattered across different concerns.

## What exhausted the experience

Actors remembered exposure and compulsion, and some acted on their own. The Judge changed an institution; Ilex planted a garden. After the three advertised own acts, however, they had no next concrete ambition. Their remembered histories changed policies without generating an ongoing project. The player could inspect more configurations but had no reason to pursue them.

Binding promises also lacked an attractive competing benefit for these settlements. Freely invited actors and differentiated routes could supply the outcomes the players valued. More menus would not supply an interesting commitment. A next version should make a durable grant enable another author's independently desired act while genuinely constraining the grantor's later unilateral revision. It must test whether copies or free invitations bypass that distinction.

The [mechanics review](confluence-mechanics-review.md) separately checks public-law gaps. Player reports of meaningful agency must not silently establish effects the model does not implement. In particular, Mneme changing policy is not itself an enacted custody institution. Ilex's living-ground prerequisite and publicity backflow need clearer public laws in the next version. Frozen captures retain what players actually saw.

**Something you made reaches someone you did not make it for. What will they make of it?**

[The Far Bank proposal](confluence-continuation-proposal.md) carries an actual valley creation, its lineage and its rights into new relationships. Recipients transform it, seek a return address, constitute a shared speaker, offer access, depart and protect a continuation. The altered creation can return to Ilex, Mneme and the Judge. The proposed engine is a developing relationship with the same creation, not a procession of reset maps.

The next test must observe adopted projects and actual combinations. A production outline with many scenes is not the long game. Permit closure; if a player exhausts worthwhile intentions early again, record that and change the invention rather than requiring a turn quota. Compare mandated long diagnosis with a separately identified elective-interest condition.
