## Design limits and prospective interpretation

Construction → demonstration → enacted control has a real causal connection: the host operates selected actual apparatus through the ordinary validator; it cannot invent construction, inherit blueprint records, bypass blockers or claim old images as a current view. Methods are exact demonstrated macros, not a learned general policy. A changed object can require new demonstrations because operands are actual IDs; same-point replacements remain valid.

The common standpoint is now explicitly justified as looking around without moving the resident viewpoint, not merely comparing arbitrary fronts. Calibration takes a real current optical center; it does not demand a designer's square. That declared purpose should remain public and tested, not added after seeing a shortcut.

Governance is a weaker causal addition than physical hosting: a noncivic host already serves resident requests. Applying `unowned` changes the response to unrelated installer orders, while the full endpoint additionally requires the civic state. Thus resident-service completion alone cannot establish that the governance combination was useful or appreciated. Handover genuinely changes routing; the refusal policy has an actual counterfactual effect, but no refusal demonstration is required. Do not manufacture extra authority-conflict chores solely to prove the concept exists. A player may find this meaningful continuity or ceremonial administration; that remains unmeasured.

The shorter retained-site integration is a legitimate hypothesis after the previous player's repetition complaint. It is not already a successful experience, and 39 selections cannot be represented as 200 by adding automatic replay or prior campaigns. Public output/capture completeness, final source/assignment publication and fresh neutral play remain separate gates. No recruitment is authorized by this draft report.
