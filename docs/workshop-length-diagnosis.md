# Mars Workshop: why the first full campaign is short, and the next hypothesis

26 October 2026. **V1 mechanical diagnosis and prospective design options. No V1 code change, new play, enjoyment finding or remote write is made here.** The first fresh full-campaign pilot is still the appropriate qualitative next evidence. Preserve the short game and every valid shortcut.

## Useful result first

The proposed 171–219-combine scope did not survive implementation. The [ordinary witness](../results/mars-workshop-preplay/ordinary.json) completes all twelve jobs in **74 interventions**; [complete catalogue](../results/mars-workshop-preplay/complete.json) takes **69**, [saved assemblies](../results/mars-workshop-preplay/assembly-reuse.json) **60**, and [reverse order](../results/mars-workshop-preplay/alternate-order.json) **74**. These are actual authored action lists with exact replay, **best-found upper bounds, not proven minima or player evidence**. The [screen summary](../results/mars-workshop-preplay/summary.json) records the source hashes belonging to those runs.

The [independent broad-outpost review](mars-workshop-independent-review.md) records **75 ordinary / 69 complete / 56 with the previously built assembly**. All twelve goals are currently true at those endings. Its [archived replay verification](../results/mars-workshop-independent/2026-10-26T15-42-38-610Z-7f65108ec878/replay-verification.json) establishes exact replay of those traces. Do not merge that route into the author screen or call two source-exposed planning implementations independent human evidence.

**Recommendation:** preserve and pilot V1 as a short candidate. If the player finds the construction worth doing, prefer a **transformation-led larger collection** as the next substantive hypothesis, with a small explicit distinction between *assembling parts* and *transforming a material/form*. Test relative geometry as a competing mechanism, not as an automatic realism upgrade. Neither more recipes nor stricter fitting is yet evidence of 200 worthwhile decisions.

## 1. The actual workload, not the proposed arithmetic

The ordinary trace contains:

| Intervention class | Actual count |
| --- | ---: |
| Canonical/design synthesis | 5 |
| Contextual placement, attachment, interface or span | 46 |
| Reconfiguration | 5 |
| Whole-route travel | 6 |
| Observation, sampling or transmission | 12 |
| **Total** | **74** |

Map increments are **11 landing, 11 ice, 15 twin, 13 arch, 15 dust, 9 return**. Twelve goal cards therefore did not require twelve independent 12–16-combine constructions. The actual average is about **6.2 interventions per job**, with substantial sharing between the paired goals. Counting a single act twice because it helped two cards would be false length.

The complete catalogue removes exactly five syntheses, leaving the other 69 acts unchanged in the paired author route. Saved assemblies remove nine further contextual acts, leaving 60. This is useful reuse, not an exploit to prohibit.

The proposed budget made **two different mistakes**:

1. It treated most of the 27 canonical composites as likely discoveries. Only five were synthesized.
2. More importantly, it presumed far more contextual work than the six maps actually ask for. Even compelling players to make the other 22 canonical recipes would yield only 96 interventions along this route, still far below 200—and that compulsion would be the wrong game.

This is not mainly a missing-prerequisite problem. Do not “fix” ordinary mode by making the player reconstruct supplied objects or withholding directly reusable structures.

### Which canonical designs actually mattered?

Ordinary begins with the fifteen primitives and freely reusable designs for the supplied **greenhouse, camera and sample-case**. The witness additionally learns **solar-lead, solar-heater, sample-tray, clear-shell and cart**. Its final known canonical composite set has eight entries, including those three supplied ones.

Nineteen other canonical names remain unknown in that ordinary route: reflective-shell, mesh-screen, hinged-shell, sealed-passage, filtered-passage, folding-frame, solar-lamp, light-guide, directed-lamp, rootbed, lit-greenhouse, warm-greenhouse, solar-still, outlet-still, covered-cart, garden-cart, folding-span, shelter-cart and solar-camera.

Unknown names do **not** mean absent functions. A greenhouse already contains a rootbed; adding a lamp to an actual greenhouse does the work of a named lit-greenhouse without learning that catalogue entry; adding wheels to the existing greenhouse makes it mobile without the garden-cart recipe. These are intended consequences of actual part composition and inexhaustible designs. They are also why forty-two names are not forty-two functionally new capabilities.

## 2. Where the construction collapses

### A. Canonical outputs mostly abbreviate additive part trees

The engine's canonical recipe function builds part trees. Many functions are then inferred from the presence of familiar leaves: frame + wheels enables travel; a lens + plate enables observation; a sourced heater warms its assembly; a tube supplies a water interface. Generic attachment is legal and can assemble the same useful structure without going through each authored name.

That is coherent for an **assembly game**. It is weaker evidence for an **ontology of transformations**, in which combining two things produces a materially or functionally new thing. Making a jar and firing clay are not the same operation: the jar is an arrangement; firing changes what the material itself does. V1 mostly implements the former.

### B. Many component relations are deliberately coarse

- Any unfolded frame supports a displayed gap. The ordinary trace uses a bare frame as the spare span; a folding-span name is unnecessary.
- A hinge on the selected root can fold an arbitrarily elaborate whole root into the low/narrow category. It does not articulate particular connected pieces.
- Internal conduction and lamp interactions are structural; external interfaces are explicit. A useful collection of parts can therefore become a multipurpose device with little internal fitting.
- Power/water links need compatible endpoints and a public route, but do not require a sequence of intermediate geometrical contacts.
- More objects can share an anchor. V1 has no placement-occupancy puzzle.

These abstractions were disclosed design decisions. The game is not wrong because it has them. But the proposal overestimated the contextual construction they would support. Changing them is **new semantics for V2**, not a retrospective correction that makes V1's valid 74-action route invalid.

### C. Several goals reuse nearly the same solution

Ordinary actions 1–8 build a lit greenhouse and connected heated ice source. Actions 12–17 use almost the same arrangement on the next map, now with the useful concepts known. This can be comfortable familiar enactment if the player values it. It is not a second twelve-action invention.

Two opposed solar leads solve both twin-ridge power and the warm dust-valley support. A clear sample case already solves protected placement; the map's dark alcove supplies darkness without changing the case. The camera can take both viewpoints in one mobile protected assembly. These are sensible, efficient answers.

The return “passage remains traversable” clause adds little work: the model rejects oversized assemblies at the low/narrow passage already. The implementation lead explicitly identified this clause as largely guaranteed by placement legality. Do not credit it as a separate route-construction problem.

### D. Purpose and actual history prevent total collapse—but do not supply 200

Record events, sample contact and retained physical identity still matter. A copied blueprint cannot fabricate a viewpoint or move the stranded original instrument. Those laws produce the twelve observation events and six travels in the ordinary route.

The author's simple prefab placement attack achieves no goals, but that proves only that **that particular attack** did not connect sources or perform the required events. The independent multipurpose-outpost route demonstrates why “universal prefab failed” is not an impossibility result. The successful actual event work remains short.

## 3. Do not confuse consistency fixes with redesign

| Kind | Appropriate treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Preview differs from step; an inside operation bypasses the advertised boundary rule; a copied blueprint carries observations it never made; joining an actual object loses its retained physical identity; advertised fit is checked on placement but not after attachment | Correctness/disclosure issue. Fix through the normal reviewed pre-freeze process, rerun witnesses, preserve the earlier evidence/hash and report any count change. |
| A goal says two particular sample sites but accepts any sample; public window requirement does not match the evaluated subject/viewpoint; a travelled-low-route requirement checks only unrelated visits | Predicate/disclosure mismatch. Reconcile explicitly rather than quietly awarding the intended story. |
| Hinge folds its whole root; any frame spans a gap; a part anywhere in an assembly supplies a structural function; multiple objects can share an anchor; direct whole interfaces need no cable-by-cable placement | Disclosed V1 semantics. Keep in V1. Stricter variants need new versioned laws and a fresh test. They are not free length obtained by “bug fixes.” |
| Supplied actual designs become inexhaustibly known; generic assembly bypasses catalogue names; complete-catalogue or saved-assembly routes are shorter | Intended reuse. Preserve it. Do not restore a manufacturing tax, prohibit a useful whole, or force canonical chains. |
| Unused recipes or a short appreciated campaign | Content/scope result. No correctness defect is implied. Preserve any value while continuing toward the larger requested experience. |

## 4. Three substantive routes forward

### A. Transformation-led ontology — preferred first hypothesis

Add a distinction between:

- **assembly:** actual constituents remain with their capabilities and relationships;
- **transformation:** a material or form becomes a new functional state, with declared properties that need not include all input properties. Provenance is retained, but properties are not the union of the input leaves.

Examples are clear glass made from a raw mineral, a flexible waterproof lining made from fiber and binder, a fired form that retains an impression, or a reflective coating that stops being transmitting. A goal should care about the resulting property, shape or actual record—not require the recipe's name as a password.

Why this addresses the measured failure: it introduces something to learn and reuse **other than a convenient alias for already-functional primitives**. A pile containing sand, a heater and a frame is not thereby a clear rigid surface; a real transform must occur. That is not an arbitrary extra prerequisite on the old greenhouse if it introduces a new kind of material and new purposes for it. Existing film greenhouses must still work where film is sufficient.

Risk: an opaque recipe checklist can replace an additive checklist. Inspect all results and conditions; permit alternative constructions; never make every named result mandatory. The presence of more transformative verbs still does not establish enjoyable or lengthy play.

### B. Relative fitting and local hinges — strongest competing route

Preserve the existing 42 names but give parts small relative positions and exposed interfaces. A hinge rotates its attached subtree rather than compressing the entire assembly; a support has actual endpoints; a light source has an actual exposed location. Copies retain that arrangement and can still be placed whole. Whole routes and whole external links remain single actions.

Why this addresses the measured failure: a universal collection of useful parts is no longer automatically a universal fitting arrangement. A copy can be useful but need an actual change of orientation, hinge placement or exposed interface in a new context.

Risk: the game becomes packing/engineering work that the user did not ask to make obligatory. It can add burden without adding a valued construction. It also has higher implementation risk than a data-driven transformation result. Do not adopt it merely because it makes the witness longer.

A clean first contrast would reuse a garden, a span and a camera from V1, with **only** relative attachment/local-hinge laws changed. Compare complete-catalogue and saved-assembly routes as well as fresh play. Do not combine that test with a large new chemistry catalogue and call any improvement causal.

### C. A larger collection of finite problems — valid, conditional on value

An approximately 200-intervention game can consist of a collection of short levels. The owner explicitly permits distinct goals in a shared ontology and comfortable familiar progress. There is no requirement that each scenario introduce a new law or every action be a dilemma.

At V1's observed six-ish interventions per goal, something like **thirty-plus genuinely adopted goals**, not twelve labels, is a plausible content order of magnitude. This is a workload inference, not permission to multiply the same solved garden by thirty locations. The content must be specified and played: different desired object states, actual identities, placement relations, observation conditions or constructions. A changed name alone earns no novelty credit; familiar same-role enactment can still earn value through the player's report.

If the V1 pilot values the clear construction, a collection is a serious option. If it calls most of V1 unwanted setup, tripling that setup is unlikely to address the report. Wait for that evidence before choosing how much V1 to retain in the next full campaign.

## 5. One executable next hypothesis: “Working Materials”

**Prospective V2 hypothesis:** familiar things remain enjoyable to combine when some outputs are genuinely new materials/forms, and several different finite purposes make those outputs worth learning and reusing. This is not a claim that making the rules harder is better.

### Small engine delta

Add an explicitly typed recipe result:

```text
assembly recipe → existing actual part composition
transformation recipe → a new functional leaf/state + immutable input provenance
```

A transformation entry declares its public input classes, actual context condition, resulting properties and which source/form/record provenance survives. It may produce a new leaf while the reusable source/tool remains present. No ingredient quantity is decremented, no source is exhausted, and learned transformed concepts are inexhaustible thereafter.

Use **the same present/absent fields and actual records** first. Add only a few orthogonal material properties, such as `rigid/flexible`, `transmitting/diffusing/reflecting/opaque`, `porous/watertight`, and `temporary/durable record surface`. Properties belong to the actual relevant surface/part, not to an object-wide union. The goal can require one actual surface to be both supporting and transmitting; a supporting frame elsewhere in an opaque heap must not make the heap a transparent support.

Do not implement fluid volumes, chemistry quantities, heat equations, fuel, autonomous growth clocks or a generic simulator. Keep all process conditions visible. A transform can happen in one deliberate combine once its actual context exists; nobody waits ten empty beats for firing.

### First concrete transform set

Use a new content branch, initially four familiar raw capabilities—**sand, clay, fiber, binder**—with twelve transparent toy transformations. The final names/count are not a quota, and these are game laws rather than real fabrication instructions:

| Actual combination/context | Resulting distinction |
| --- | --- |
| Sand + a working heat source | Clear rigid material; does not retain heater power merely because heat made it. |
| Clay + a real water context | Moldable clay; no finite water stock. |
| Moldable clay + an actual form or recorded relief | A shaped/impressed soft form retaining that actual form's provenance. |
| Soft form + a working heat source | Rigid fired form retaining shape/impression; no longer freely moldable. |
| Fiber + a real water context | Pulp: a changed wet material, not a dry-fiber capability union. |
| Pulp + an actual mesh form | A sheet with the form's profile and declared transmission/record behavior. |
| Sheet + clear film | Protected laminate; still retains the actual sheet and any genuine record. |
| Clear rigid material + foil | A reflecting surface that no longer transmits through that surface. |
| Clear rigid material + mesh | A diffusing surface rather than a clear view; light passage and faithful imaging differ. |
| Fiber + binder | A flexible water-tight lining, without automatically becoming a rigid support. |
| Fired porous form + clear film | A water-tight lining on that form; it loses the formerly open passage through that surface. |
| A recording sheet + an actual recorded original | A genuine physical impression/copy with linked record provenance; a blank copied design does not invent that event. |

The “actual form” is a **small public profile**, such as an opening and two named contact edges, not a full mesh or continuous geometry system. It can initially be a finite tuple of exposed side/orientation/opening flags. A form-taking transformation copies the actual tuple; combining profiles may only use a few explicitly declared union/lining operations. This is a local form/data field, not the full relative-geometry alternative above.

These rules are deliberately not a gate inserted before the old lamp or greenhouse. New jobs need different properties or real transformed forms. Old constructions remain valid where they satisfy a goal. A transparent frame-and-film arrangement can still win a goal that only needs enclosure and light.

### First discriminating constructions, run as part of a full campaign

1. **One surface, two functions.** A lit viewing passage also needs an actual supporting surface at its displayed contact edges. A clear rigid cast, a suitably open supported arrangement, or another property-correct answer is valid. The point is qualified local properties, not requiring “glass.”
2. **Change a useful thing's role.** An actual soft impression is first used to fit a local opening, then a durable version of that same impressed form must preserve its identity while another actual surface stays porous. A fresh unobserving prefab does not copy the impression; a learned design may still make other blank copies freely.
3. **Light is not always an image.** Supply diffuse light to a growing place while retaining a faithful real observation through a different surface. A diffuser is useful without being a camera window. Existing open sightlines remain legal alternatives where the map offers them.
4. **Preserve without keeping everything unchanged.** Make a physical copy of an actual original, protect one copy against a disclosed wet/exposure row, and keep another actual surface open for filtering/contact. This combines record provenance with material change, not three identical deliveries.

Run these on the same headless interface and include them in the candidate's complete witness, rather than reporting another two-action slice as a scale result. The smallest **mechanism check** can use these four constructions, but the planned research deliverable below is the complete campaign/collection. If these four already collapse to property-label selection with no valued enactment, reject the transform hypothesis before writing the remaining content; then continue with the competing route, not stop the project.

## 6. A believable full-campaign scope, without a new arithmetic fiction

Plan a **thirty-job collection across about ten maps**: retain or revise V1's twelve purposes according to the fresh pilot, then add eighteen transformation/form purposes. The content is larger than one new tutorial, but the engine delta stays small. A prospective eighteen-job matrix is:

| Family | Three proposed distinct purposes—not three renamed target sites |
| --- | --- |
| Surfaces | A supporting clear view; a flexible water-tight wrap on an actual carried object; a porous surface that keeps a separate destination dry. |
| Light | Diffuse illumination versus a faithful image; a reflected path retaining actual record provenance; transmitting and blocking surfaces arranged to protect a dark original while making a public copy visible. |
| Forms | Take an actual irregular opening's form; retain that impression through firing; use positive and lined forms for different contact/passage roles. |
| Records | Preserve an actual impression through a changed material state; make two actual copies for incompatible exposure conditions; reuse a formerly blank structural surface as a record carrier without pretending its copies witnessed the event. |
| Living/contact | A new material supplies light without exposing the bed; separate wet growth and dry specimen work through real surface properties; make a protected movable enclosure whose actual contact surface still admits the required water effect. |
| Integration/reuse | Repurpose an actual earlier formed piece; bring actual transformed records/forms home through the existing mobility rules; combine a familiar growing place, a transformed viewing surface and protected records into one completed outpost. |

This matrix is **not** yet eighteen playable levels or evidence of variety. Before coding each map, specify its subjects, property checks, public exposure rows and at least two contemplated constructions where plausible. Remove any entry that is just “make the same laminate again.” Several clear similar actions within a larger project remain allowed; they are not automatically padding.

The scale hypothesis is grounded only weakly: the retained ordinary V1 route supplies 74 interventions; eighteen additional substantive jobs averaging about five to eight contextual interventions, plus zero to twelve actually useful new transformations, would put the collection around **164–230 interventions**. **Do not claim that range from this sentence.** Shared assemblies, combined goal completion and irrelevant branches can shrink it exactly as they did V1. The pre-play deliverable is an executed whole-collection witness ledger, in ordinary, complete and assembly-carryover conditions, replacing every estimate.

If the resulting good route is 120, call it 120. Do not fill a numerical gap with extra casts, smaller paths or required unused recipes. Retain a valued shorter collection and either add genuinely different purposes with known demand or pursue the relative-fit branch. The original user goal remains open until an actual played campaign near the desired scale receives positive agent evaluation.

## 7. Decision after the fresh V1 pilot

- **If the player values clear progress and the actual completed constructions:** retain those portions. Test Working Materials as an additional capability/purpose branch, with familiar enactment allowed and whole-assembly reuse intact. A larger varied collection may be enough; no need to turn every act into difficult geometry.
- **If the player values fitting, travel or observation but calls additive device assembly a checklist:** prefer the local-relative-fit contrast, retaining the appreciated actual-object roles. Do not make chemistry breadth the automatic answer.
- **If the player reports no worthwhile construction across V1:** do not commission thirty more similar goals. Implement the four-construction transformation discriminator or the competing geometry variant, obtain fresh play, and choose a new logic family if neither supplies value.
- **If the pilot is positive but short:** that is a successful short experience plus an unmet campaign-length goal, not grounds to reject the report or declare the overall project finished.

No choice here depends on unavailable human testing. Agent play remains the authorized empirical loop; its reports do not establish human enjoyment. Freeze new semantics and conditions, keep exact exposures and valid short routes, evaluate, publish the next actual result, and continue.
