# Living Works 2: a different goal in the same ontology

Prospective protocol, 26 October 2026, after all three Living Works 1 plays completed.

## Evidence motivating the test

All three players found a compact direct-sensing period-four solution to ordinary alternating watering. Those were valid successful constructions. No player needed the longer route operators. This leaves their value untested; unused branches are not intrinsically defects in a large ontology.

The new single level changes only the explicit goal: every cyclic wet run and every cyclic dry run for each grove lasts at least three beats, with no simultaneous watering/flooding and both groves living. Any valid component arrangement is accepted. No particular recipe is required. This is a public temporal construction constraint, not an action quota, inventory limit or time pressure.

## Conditions

- One experienced player from fun-living-sequence-1.
- One new player without prior source, model or playtest exposure.

Both receive the identical slow board and all 24 known concepts. Compare actual construction and transfer, not a causal estimate of human skill. The experienced participant's earlier exposure is explicitly recorded; neither run is called an uninterrupted campaign or elective continuation.

Only the public CLI may be used. Prior-player solutions and author witnesses are not included in public onboarding. The changed goal and every actual recipe/condition are public. The experienced player may use their own memory and public prior notes; the new player may not read them. Neither sees source or automated search results.

## What to inspect

Does an existing idea of sensing and feedback transfer, and what new route or timing inference is needed? Does a retained whole route help express the construction, or are direct components clearer? Does the new goal create worthwhile work rather than merely repeat the same construction at a larger parameter? A familiar method can still be valuable transfer; do not require a new paradigm or pretend it supplies one.

Record brief intentions before actions, predicted effects, actual cycle evidence, failed predictions or repairs, and which execution was routine or appreciated. Finish when the stated goal is achieved or no worthwhile progress remains. No optional-play quota; counts of commands and beats are not human minutes.

Old V1 source and all old captures remain unchanged. The direct period-four witness must still satisfy V1 and fail this stricter goal. Both whole-route and manual-component period-eight witnesses must pass, including cyclic first/last-run handling and exact physical-state replay. Reject unknown operands, preserve input, and compare one-step forecasts with actual steps. Freeze the new module and public CLI before play.
