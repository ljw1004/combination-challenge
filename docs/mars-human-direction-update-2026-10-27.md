# Mars direction update after human play

27 October 2026 UTC. This records a new user-directed model/UI scope, not a reinterpretation of earlier agent plays.

The owner rejected sticky location placement and automatic accumulation of multiple items into place-level composites. Mars now uses named binary combinations with three explicit location roles: a justified catalyst for A+B at P; an operand A+P producing a portable named thing; or a freely movable placement satisfying a goal. Combined results must be recognizable named things, not bags labeled by concatenated parts.

Subsequent human instructions simplify the board further: every non-location item has the same rounded-rectangle treatment; no actual/design/infinite-copy distinction; one globally existing thing of a kind suppresses further creation of that result; hover highlights only novel-result-compatible sources/partners/places and does not reveal result names. Inputs remain, the dragged source is moved away, and a genuinely new result appears nearby. Locations unfold only when an introduced objective needs their catalyst, operand or placement role.

The current shared contract is [Mars named things](mars-named-contract.md), with content in `src/mars-named-content.ts`. Builder owns the separate model/UI; the campaign researcher owns content/contract only; the full-Mars reviewer independently checks both. Old Working Materials remains separate rather than being retroactively assigned these rules.

The geometric/material network research, larger physical architecture, and local salvage/elevation paper screen remain preserved evidence and possible separate experiments. They are **not the preferred Mars logic** after this human direction and must not be silently imposed on the named binary model. Their action counts, mechanical passes and agent reports do not transfer to the new game. The live-host agent experiment also remains a separate previously frozen condition, not a test of the latest human interaction request.

The approximately200-decision research goal is still open. The newly supplied finite named content is deliberately not padded to match an old concept/checkpoint count. Its actual usability and appeal require fresh observation under the new rules.
