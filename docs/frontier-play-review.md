## What to retain

- A construction can become a causeway, boundary, memorial or replacement connection through actual use. A name or paragraph is not required to supply the sequence.
- A remembered direction can be a useful construction ingredient. The strongest episodes involved changing an old place's role, not merely selecting the winning local type.
- The counterpart's visible commitment supports informed intervention while its other active fronts still have consequences elsewhere.
- A player can value coexistence, a hearth or a terminated encounter without maximizing territory or completing the supplied invitation.

## What to change

The general circuit indicator is too easy to acquire through automatic forks, while broad branching becomes clutter relative to a small chosen project. Many direct type counters have an obvious answer. The final policy fails to account for closures, yielding repetition rather than a developing response. Making that policy harder to stop would not by itself supply another wanted ambition.

The mechanics audit also limits the proposed narrative. Amber remembers and prioritizes ownership of a footprint; it can destroy the original circuit's functioning topology itself. The long player explicitly corrected its final account: both lineages still have living circuits, but preserved remembered territory is different from preserving the original route. Some history fields also include installed orientation, not only actual travel. Future representations and laws should make these distinctions clear.

The evaluator's reconstructed transitions, captured-public references and checked developments remain separate. The short boundary has a verified one-step effect; that does not establish permanent peace. The long player's appreciation and understanding reports remain accounts, including their correction, rather than automatically checked beliefs.
