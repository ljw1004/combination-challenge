## Precision limits and primitive alternatives

The sheet is sufficiently specific for the above constructive paper argument, but is not yet a complete executable physical contract:

- It supplies unit footprints, wall height, ray height and canopy height, but not every body's vertical extent/material opacity or exact support coordinates. A unit-footprint body intended to occlude the low ray and clear perimeter-supported canopy are natural conventions used by both routes, not an independently verified 3D model. The attached arithmetic checks only distinct footprint interiors and named face contacts. Do not label them engine collision tests.
- A growing bed's light predicate should explicitly use the same horizontal source-ray convention, including selected-body exclusion, rather than relying on the collector paragraph by analogy.
- Bare grounded `solar-cell`, directly power-connected `heater`, primitive `signal-emitter` as an origin, and a bare `clay` working surface have insufficiently specified interfaces/function. “Canonical list is convenience” permits property-equivalent alternatives; it does not itself define all primitive functional predicates. If a powered bare emitter already originates signal, its supported-beacon discovery is unnecessary. If it lacks support or an origin interface, explain that property rather than requiring a recipe name. No additional exact savings are claimed from these ambiguities.
- Arbitrary joins need a clear interface/accounting rule. This route uses two real face joins and the explicitly shared warmth rule; it does not assume a magical union of remote parts or free propagation of power/water to everything in an assembly.
- No exact saved-rich-assembly count is justified yet. Its complete actual geometry, support, joining structure and internal/external connections must be specified; one may not call disconnected distant objects a one-piece design merely to reduce the count.

These are clarification requirements if a short prototype is later authorized, not invitations to add anti-shortcut exceptions. In particular, forbidding the separator heater from melting ice or warming its joined surfaces would retract the written useful-property law solely to rescue the longer witness.

## What the screen does—and does not—support

The baseline contains real spatial reasoning: the wall shadows the original garden, heater placement can obstruct light, and a workshop can obstruct a signal corridor. The compressed answer still requires real relationships: source-grounded water, face-contact warmth, sharing a roof without shading the garden, outward power sources, and aligned contact paths. A first-time player might value discovering and assembling that capable camp. Neither the short count nor expert compression establishes boredom.

However, the optional crash/home/terrace pads, unlimited abstract supply links and remote construction make the geography cheap. The endpoint does not require a journey, a relay network, distinct stages of survival, or operation through changing configurations. It is principally a static, source-supported camp with one origin serving two receivers. That honest short-game description is stronger than calling it a 200-action survival-to-mobility campaign.

The exact paper result does not transfer counts or praise from the staged human interaction, the 76/47-action agent plays, or the live-host experiment. Comfortable discovery can matter, but hypothetical extra mistakes, optional expeditions or unplayed elaborate designs cannot supply missing length. A later larger campaign would need additional genuinely useful purposes and an actual combined route—not repeated services added until the table looks long.

**Keep** this short capable-camp option, shared useful components, direct primitive/property alternatives once clarified, and immediate recognition of robust completion. **Reject** the 145–210 workload claim for this realization and any response that forces relay hops, separate heaters, repeated visits, original cargo, finite stocks or history stamps merely to defeat the counterexample. **Next decision:** either retain the paper result as a small possible prototype, or propose a genuinely broader purpose before spending implementation effort on the long-game goal. No engine, recruitment or enjoyment gate is granted here.
