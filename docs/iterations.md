# Iteration record

The experiential target is understandable invention with consequential commitment. Each cycle below preserves the previous rules. The first three are matched rule comparisons; the fourth responds to repeated blind-player evidence. The candidate screen preceded them and is exploratory across different models, not a controlled experience comparison.

## 0. Cheap comparison: reject shallow candidate implementations

**Hypothesis.** Giving an opponent a visible intention, or an environment a visible process, could turn combinations into useful choices.

**Test.** Three one-page sketches; standalone cheap flood and traveller simulations, seeds1–12, four policies,96 games. [Concepts and results](concepts.md).

**Observed.** Informed policies saturated both models. The traveller could be stopped through repeated mud or a fixed gate chain; the flood could be beaten with one derived drain. These are counterexamples to equating completion with a good design.

**Decision.** Reject those implementations. Select a richer environmental routing slice because a diversion can protect one asset while endangering another. Keep the reverse shooter separate.

## 1. Separate discovery → invention in place

**Hypothesis.** A turn spent discovering will produce useful anticipation between immediate relief and future power. Contradicted if policies bypass discovery or it mainly delays the first useful experiment.

**Change.** `separate` requires a later deployment; `field` permits the discovery action to create its first instance. Inputs stay known in both. Every other mechanical parameter is identical.

**Prediction.** Earlier practical effects, more capability-dependent plans, fewer setup losses. Risk: discovery becomes simply a stronger deployment and commitment loses tension.

**Observed.** Across36 scenario/seed cases per policy, separate greedy wins12, bounded solver32 with0 discoveries. Field greedy and solver each win36; solver averages4.28 discoveries. A blind separate player nevertheless discovers Grove and wins6 total remaining health; four field players win11. The latter comparison has one baseline player and cannot establish a population preference. See [playtest record](playtests.md).

**Counterevidence.** The planner's discovery preference is partly an evaluation artifact. Some field discoveries occur after survival is secured. Separate discovery is viable and clearly understood by its blind player.

**Decision.** Keep invention in place for earlier visible consequences. Attribute the result narrowly: it changes action economy and practical uptake. It does not establish that separate discovery is unfun. Next test: equalize the decision budget or compare concrete early tradeoffs with people.

## 2. Disposable reeds → persistent rooted reeds

**Hypothesis.** A living defence should remove repeated repair and remain a usable alternative to slower groves. Contradicted if persistence never matters or one living tool dominates every context.

**Change.** `rooted` reeds absorb1 each pulse indefinitely, except incoming4+ uproots them after their final absorption. `field` reeds have2 total capacity. No other rule changes.

**Prediction.** A reed plan trades lower throughput for immediate, lasting protection; replace fewer exhausted reed beds.

**Observed.** Scripted gameplay verifies renewable absorption versus depletion. The opening branch comparison supplies winning grove, channel and reed continuations with different immediate damage/final health. However greedy/goal traces are identical across all36 matched field/rooted cases. The fresh rooted player deploys reeds that absorb only once; replaying those actions with disposable reeds still wins at[6,6].

**Counterevidence.** The screen and blind runs do not test an experiential benefit of persistence. No observed repair burden was removed in these particular runs.

**Decision.** Retain rooted reeds as a consistent renewable alternative with explicit overwhelm feedback. Mark experience benefit unknown; this is a provisional design choice, not a successful enjoyment finding. Next test: repeat a moderate local pulse so persistence changes a publicly plausible plan without merely increasing total difficulty.

## 3. Immediate pressure → two preparation turns

**Hypothesis.** More room to invent may encourage curiosity without erasing anticipation. Contradicted if it mostly adds setup/unused inventions to an already solved situation.

**Change.** `breathing` adds two zero-rain preparation turns to rooted rules. Forecast and storm remain identical afterward.

**Prediction.** More viable development openings; fewer forced emergency actions. Risk: slow groves mature free of opportunity cost.

**Observed.** Both greedy and solver already win36/36 rooted cases and still win36/36. Solver mean discoveries increase4.00→5.36. In the breathing planner's193 development discoveries,105 have no observed practical use.108 occur after the first state where waiting already wins without further damage. All solver cases end with both assets at full health. These effects do not establish curiosity.

**Counterevidence.** A human might enjoy generous construction or optional expression. We have no reports about that. The solver's novelty bonus inflates its activity.

**Decision.** Reject the grace period for the default. Preserve it in the experiment selector. Keep zero-grace rooted rules and test optional optimization separately. Do not claim that tighter pressure is universally better.

## Return to original designer sources

After these screens and the first blind trace, reread Koster, Ma, Barth, Teikari and MDA. [Research revisit](research-revisit.md) preserves original interpretations and revised inferences. The resulting correction was to distinguish useful invention from activity: more recipes, more actions and higher win rates can coexist with an early-settled encounter.

## 4. Repeated waits → optional full forecast resolution

**Hypothesis.** Once every remaining publicly forecast pulse is harmless, manual Wait inputs add no survival decision. Removing those clicks can preserve the visible plan payoff.

**Change.** `settledForecast` simulates the entire remaining actual storm. When it causes zero damage and ends in a win, the UI/CLI offers optional accelerated resolution. It executes each normal transition and logs every response; the player can keep experimenting instead.

**Prediction.** Four or five settled waits can become one input, without a premature victory or missing replay consequences.

**Observed.** All seven blind sessions contain4–5 trailing waits. Browser tests execute safe resolution, confirm all remaining recorded actions are waits, validate the win and next encounter, and import an identical replay. Engine tests reject a forged acceleration before safety. This directly verifies the input reduction, not improved experience.

**Counterevidence.** It does not create new decisions after the plan settles. It can make a shallow encounter shorter rather than deeper. Human enjoyment, anticipation and satisfaction with the payoff are untested.

**Decision.** Keep optional acceleration. Revise this small slice before expanding its content. Next: one clearly announced timing/spatial change that changes which existing tool is useful, plus actual human inspection.

## Grader iteration: audit the instrument too

The independent evaluator found two false-positive routes in our initial grader. First, the existence of a branch file triggered choice support. This was replaced with measured viable alternatives while the more interpretive worthwhile-choice judgment remains unknown. Second, zero trailing waits hid solver deployments after survival was settled. Every run now records its earliest zero-damage safe completion and subsequent optional activity.

The grader also reports unused discoveries, keeps confident prediction errors despite eloquent annotations, distinguishes changed-context repeats from equivalent repairs, and always leaves human enjoyment unknown. Eight supplied-feature adversarial fixtures and real replay-level counterexamples are executable. The feature fixtures validate rubric logic, not automatic interpretation of arbitrary natural-language experiences.

The instrument changed from `evidence-1` to `evidence-2`. Game actions/outcomes and frozen rule comparisons are unchanged; current profiles are regenerated from those same raw captures. [grader-audit.json](../results/grader-audit.json) records the observed errors and corrections. The initial aggregate file was overwritten before this audit snapshot was added, so that record is explicitly a reconstruction, not a byte-for-byte preserved first output. Future iterations should snapshot both outputs before changing the grader.
