## Frozen scope and next boundary

Read `ideas-history-estuary-interaction-grammar.md`: several commands supply three or four bindings, and method authoring configures parameters. Thirty submissions are **not thirty binary combinations, physical clicks, or measured interesting decisions**. The public guide and prospective play protocol retain that distinction. No new CLI player is authorized by this freeze. No roughly-200-decision or human-enjoyment claim follows.

A successor adapter must offer concrete named things and meaningful place/context roles with an actual two-operand gesture for every normal action. Merely distributing configuration fields among named tokens, or splitting an unlock from an install, would not address the limitation. It may reuse this causal substrate without pretending to simulate all the fiction. All six projects must remain available; breadth must not be traded for a polished isolated example. Existing Mars source and deployed Site remain outside this change.
