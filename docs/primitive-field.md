# A field without authored scenes

`field-1` is a representation baseline over frozen Confluence 2, not a new mechanical game or a claimed long experience. Its public view contains places, links, avatars, capabilities and lasting changes. It exposes no chapter openings, dialogue, supplied personal questions or ending paragraphs.

This tests the owner's correction cheaply: can the primitive world carry a readable history without the authored passages? Known weaknesses in the underlying game remain, especially exhausted ambitions after one act per resident. A fresh play can evaluate this representation and a short experience; it cannot establish visual beauty or the requested 180–240 opportunities.

The projection also repairs information omissions in the earlier observer: exactness, publicity and storage are visible per speaker origin; previews show changed forms, institutions and avatar histories as well as carrier fields. The public laws name actual first-act conditions and distinguish Mneme's future testimony policy from an institution he never constructed. The underlying transitions are unchanged.

`fieldAvailability(state, {item, a, b, at})` supplies symmetric eligibility queries for a future light-up interaction. For example, dry marsh ground cannot yet support the growth pair; opening a water route enables it. Stone+Water creates a weir at a place but a conduit at a neighboring link. These are small existing examples of context participating in a combination. They do not prove that every future level needs three operands.

Play through the existing logical CLI:

```sh
node experiments/logical-play.ts init my-field field unfinished-valley 0 manual
node experiments/logical-play.ts rules my-field
node experiments/logical-play.ts actions my-field '{"at":"orchard"}'
node experiments/logical-play.ts act my-field '{"type":"combine","a":"seed","b":"water","at":"orchard"}'
```

No frontend is added. The live Bell/Mneme reader remains available as a historical experiment. New spatial primitive proposals are separate from this baseline.
