## Why a versioned update

Revision 8 already allowed understood execution and said that every combine need not be strategic. In practice our Mars summary still framed straightforward action too negatively. The generative theory and evaluator prompts leaned toward devising, unresolved questions and changing roles; they needed an explicit distinction between opening/orientation, comfortable progress and a later construction challenge. A version records that change without pretending the earlier tests asked the revised question.

The owner's correction is stronger than an exemption for insight followed by execution: making clear progress can itself be comfortable. It need not produce measurable learning, a later purpose or a separately meaningful decision in each action. Learning the mechanics can nevertheless contribute to early play. These are prospective experience hypotheses, not newly observed enjoyment.
