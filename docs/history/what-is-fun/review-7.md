# Revision 7: history in the primitives

Author's coherence and actionability review, after the owner's correction. This is a self-review, not an independent audit or a new playtest. Revision 6 remains archived unchanged; revision 7 applies prospectively.

## The correction

The owner still values sweep, beauty, curiosity and philosophical concepts. We overinterpreted those preferences as a request for authored dialogue, personal mysteries and chapter sequences. The owner now asks for little text narrative and for history emerging through the game's primitives. Their positive examples include an avatar, foe indicator or Tetris block, and especially a place in the terrain that can participate in combinations. Place, time and other simple context can help a history unfold.

Revision 7 changes the vehicle, not the intention–construction–response–renewal hypothesis. It rejects the prose-led Far Bank campaign as the current direction while preserving its artifacts. This is a direct preference correction, not evidence that its unfinished, unplayed campaign would be unenjoyable.

## What changes operationally

| Before | Revision 7 requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Start from an unresolved personal question and an authored revelation | Start from a valued construction, actor or world, a worthwhile ambition or tension, and a small set of interacting public laws |
| Treat revealing another passage as a candidate development | Trace a development to an action, prior state/history, a reusable law and an actual changed capability, relation or world response |
| Give characters dialogue and an autonomous-act flag | If an actor is used, its policy and acts must change the shared world; history can be legible through location, allegiance, activity or another state indicator |
| Add receivers and stage responses to extend the arc | Seek new uses, opposition and consequences of retained constructions; test whether the player adopts an ensuing ambition |
| Count narrative appreciation as support for the direction | Preserve appreciation separately from project alignment and primitive-generated development |
| Risk treating a text reduction as sufficient | Retain challenge, planning, skill, consequential alternatives or useful construction; an effortless catalogue is still a weak game |

Terrain as operand and A + B at a place are promising tentative substrates, not new givens. Place can enable or transform combinations while consequences travel across a persistent map. The praised availability logic should cover both directions: a concept reveals applicable places/results; a place reveals supported combinations in its current state. Logical testing exposes the same eligibility and functional rules. Hidden location recipes are not the challenge.

Time remains permissible as causal context, process, sequence or opposition. Earlier actions may change later affordances and leave visible history. This does not require an action quota, inventory economy or imposed deadline. No general prohibition on time is introduced.

## Check against the plays

Confluence's interest interventions 14–16 are the strongest retained example. Public living voices backflowed into an exact stored witness record and caused rain. The player kept public speech, changed transmission, then erased the local copy while retaining the original elsewhere. The history depends on real storage, audience and transformation laws. It survives removal of dialogue. In long interventions 40–41, changing a material relation blocked authority becoming compulsion while retaining a stored record. The same primitives acquired different roles.

Neither episode establishes the whole model's success. The interest player stopped at 20 interventions; the long diagnostic stopped at 42 interventions in 12 annotated episodes, far below the 180–240 target. Opaque eligibility and publicity laws weakened informed play. The mechanics review also showed prose claiming silent custody more strongly than Mneme's actual policy effect. Revision 7 therefore asks for relevant origin, stored-record and historical state in public observation and forecasts, without requiring a new UI.

Bell and Mneme produced genuine reported appreciation and two elective combinations each in their second versions. Those remain evidence under their original conditions. They mainly investigated supplied character questions and passages, with low mechanical challenge; the owner has rejected that as the desired vehicle. We should neither erase their positive reports nor promote their local continuation into support for a long emergent game.

## Coherence checks

- All game rules are authored; emergence means development through interacting reusable laws, not an impossible absence of design.
- Concise goals, labels, public rule explanations and compact factual logs remain useful. There is no ban on all text, theme or characters.
- Small primitive sets and no prose do not prove fun. The designer still proposes a positive engine and the critic tests its actual operation.
- Known recipes and visible terrain eligibility coexist with challenge above combination search.
- Valid closure, meaningful familiar enactment, nonresource tradeoffs and separate long/elective conditions remain intact. No per-action novelty or forced continuation requirement is introduced.
- The evaluator must keep causal evidence, reported pleasure, scope, project alignment and human uncertainty separate. Root owns the implementation update; revision 7 does not claim that it is already complete.

The next useful test is a small persistent world in which a combination at a place changes another place, process or actor through a public law; that change should support an adopted next intention or satisfying closure. Expanding that engine toward the longer scope remains an experiment, not a scene-production target.
