# Geometric Materials — prospective implementation plan

Separate new version; no prior engine, map, UI or player result is changed. No length estimate. All later route counts must come from replay of this version.

## Representation and minimal laws (planned, not yet verified)

* Coordinates in unit lengths, x east, y south, z up. Solids are exact axis-aligned boxes; ideal thin panels are rectangles and frame struts are line segments. Zero thickness is an explicit abstraction, not an invisible occupied interior. Rotations are cardinal quarter-turns with exact coordinates; numeric contact tolerance 1e-9.
* Root, lens and recording-plate bodies are unit cubes. A camera is an actual lens and plate separated by one unit center-to-center, plate behind lens. The lens front points along its public heading. Optical center is the lens center, not a goal stamp. Lens/plate thickness, center and own-part exclusions are inspectable.
* A clear roof above a root admits overhead light and protects that root from falling dust/rain. This is roof protection, not a claim of sealed enclosure; full enclosing boxes, when constructed, protect only actual contained bodies. Opaque roofs block overhead light. No scattering model is added.
* Structural supports must touch. Each structurally connected component must reach the unbounded supported z=0 apron; attached components may be supported through their actual touching joint. No unsupported floating camera or roof. Solids may touch but not overlap; boundary faces may touch a body face but not cut its interior.
* Joins select complete relative poses in one action. Whole relocation selects complete destination and orientation in one action; no cart-per-item setup or route microgestures. This explicit new handling abstraction replaces V1 cart/fold travel checks, not an invisible preservation of them. Body extent never shrinks. Hinge operations rotate only the selected connected subtree about its declared pivot and cardinal axis; endpoint collision/support only, no swept-path claim.
* Water/power/light links are abstract nonblocking service interfaces, not spatial tube/cable routing. They do not occupy cells. Moving either endpoint removes its links. Disclosed connectors are still actual constituent parts. This cannot establish simulated wire/pipe clearance.
* Unlimited primitive/design copies; design copies clear seating, records, samples and events. Physical record copying preserves immutable actual source observations. No identity quota. Dismantling, replacement, off-site staging and sequential cameras remain lawful.
