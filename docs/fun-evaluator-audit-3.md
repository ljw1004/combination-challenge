**Priority 1: distinguish primitive transitions, reviewed causal claims and prose exposure.**

`narrativeDisclosure()` in [fun-evaluator.ts](../experiments/fun-evaluator.ts) still does its narrow historical job correctly: it tracks exact strings in actual response fields and prior observations/previews, without claiming they were read or were semantically new. Those numbers describe the representation, not the amount of generated history, and must never rank the candidates' narrative development.

For these same three profiles, `structuralReuse.uses` is empty and F3/F7 are `not-checked`. The reuse extractor requires `observation.concepts` with newly created concept IDs used as later action inputs. Field/Confluence instead preserve places, routes, records and histories. The appreciated orchard-as-source development therefore falls outside that mechanism. This is an honest disclosed coverage gap, not evidence that no reuse occurred. The reviewed field ledger identifies the actual example, but the profile lacks a compact mechanical record against which its interpretation can be checked.

The negative control is already in actual Confluence play. Interest intervention 13 prints that Mneme chooses to keep an account silent, but his place remains archive/witness and every derived place field remains unchanged. Long intervention 32 likewise does not create custody. These are real future testimony-policy changes, with narrower consequences than the prose claims; existing quoted exact records remain. The [mechanics review](confluence-mechanics-review.md) establishes the distinction. Keeping that passage and the player's interpretation is correct. Treating their presence as evidence of an enacted institution or blocked transmission would violate revision 7. The long reviewed ledger warns about the overstatement, but this safeguard currently depends on a free-text warning elsewhere rather than an explicit checked development.

The smallest useful contract has three layers:

1. **Reconstructed transition evidence.** For a decision, retain its action, adapter/version, before and after projections, and changed persistent IDs/fields. Include the kinds needed by this game: place/link form, records and origins, authority, actor assignment/history, and lasting capabilities/conditions. Label this as reconstructed mechanical state. A delta says what changed; it does not by itself identify which incoming path or prior event caused the change.
2. **Available public information.** Separately link the exact captured public observation before and after the decision, plus relevant rule/preview access. Do not substitute today's richer field projection for what an older Confluence player could see. In particular, reconstructing origin masks during an audit does not establish that the old compact exact/public booleans disclosed them to that player.
3. **A reviewed development record.** For each important claim, name the earlier construction or history, the relevant action(s), the reusable law and its source/version, actual transition evidence, and the player's prediction/notice/value accounts with source paths. Give the effect a verified, contradicted or unresolved status. Keep project alignment—primitive interaction, authored passage, mixed or unknown—separate from whether the player appreciated it. A counterfactual or law-dependence check can strengthen a claim, but absent checks must remain absent.

This can start with three tiny records: field orchard regulator → seed source; Confluence public fragment + stored exact witness → flood and selective repair; Mneme's claimed custody → narrower policy effect, no charter change. It does not require automatically interpreting every event or enumerating the whole valley.

Coverage should follow the claim actually checked. F3 becomes **partial** when a specific consequence is checked against actual state and the applicable law; untested alternatives, feasible futures and deferred commitments stay untested. Raw before/after data alone warrants a transition-evidence label, not complete causal verification. F7 becomes **partial** after verifying that an identified retained construction has a later functional role—for example, orchard's earlier local water regulation later supplying seeds for village. Merely retaining an ID, repeating a recipe or changing a phase flag is insufficient. Reported value of that reuse stays a separate sourced account. Do not mark all F3/F7 obligations checked just because an adapter exists.
