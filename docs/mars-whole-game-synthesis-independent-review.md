# Independent design review: Mars whole-game synthesis

26 October 2026. Reviewed `docs/mars-whole-game-synthesis.md`, SHA-256 `2c6b1c51b372e42152b0ee486611ff37062d962f20f83ac453852475b84b6038`. Design-only review: no new laws, engine, route counts, player recruitment or UI changes.

## Disposition

**Proceed to one bounded, exact paper specification for adversarial compression—not to implementation, and not on the presumption that the campaign supports about 200 actions.** The proposal has a coherent motivating destination and better potential purpose variation than another archive sequence. Its lawful shortcuts could combine most chapters into one robust distributed camp/network. That may itself be a satisfying substantial creation. The unresolved risk is that the estimated workload and claimed phase progression reduce to much less useful building than proposed—not that a capable universal camp must be defeated. The 145–210 range is an unsupported workload hypothesis, not measured or specified play.

The useful change is survival/contact/mobility/work as different functions of a retained place. The inadequate version would be the same light/water/protection setup repeated at three coordinates, with relays replacing record copies and journeys replacing mounting commands. The next specification is justified if it can discriminate between those possibilities and is allowed to conclude that the larger arc is not worth building. Judge the variety and usefulness of what the player makes and discovers, not whether every phase stays difficult after perfect knowledge. A player may legitimately enjoy an optional expedition, a beautiful camp or mastering a reusable design even when none is necessary for the minimum endpoint.

## What is genuinely promising—and what remains only a label

| Proposed purpose | Plausible functional difference | Missing fact needed before crediting depth |
|---|---|---|
| Be found | Establishing an outward signal can be a clear, short opening with immediate payoff. | What actual line, endpoint and power relation must hold? An exposed mast solving it immediately is acceptable. |
| Make water useful | Producing usable water and supporting growth are different from signaling, and can share thermal/support structures. | Exact source, contaminant and purification semantics. A contaminant should produce an intelligible transformation need, not another obligatory named filter. |
| Changed exposure | A structure's protection, transmission and access may interact across public conditions. | Whether a transparent cover plus duplicated external collectors already satisfies every condition. Do not manufacture breakage because it does. |
| Distant contact | A live reachable endpoint can be different from collecting historical observations. | A new, explicit communications law with meaningful terrain/boundary relationships—not a global abstract link renamed as radio. |
| Terrace journey | Transport can require configurations that static placement does not. | If movement is compulsory, what useful outcome needs it once remote building, replacement and bridges are allowed? If exploration is optional, its interest may instead come from discovery, theme or self-chosen construction; do not presume that interest or count unplayed trips. |
| Productive home | Wet growing space and dry or thermal work can be genuinely different uses of a shared installation. | What work is actually enabled, at which useful locations, and why independent open-apron modules do not make the interaction irrelevant. |
| Leave it working | An already robust design can provide legitimate, satisfying closure. | This is not automatically another construction chapter. If all public rows already hold, recognize completion without a departure proof or maintenance sequence. |

No chapter must contain constant dilemmas. A simple opening and familiar useful enactment can be valuable. But seven motivations do not establish seven independent construction problems, and explicit novelty labels do not establish appreciated novelty.

## Strongest compression threats

**1. Ending first merges much of the arc.** If the endpoint is existence of a supplied, protected, communicating base under every disclosed row, a player may construct that base directly. Signaling, water, exposure robustness, local work and dependable departure can become properties of one initial solution. Staged presentation may help comprehension; it cannot be counted as unavoidable progression or as demonstrated changes of purpose when the same installation already solves the later cards.

**2. Unlimited distributed infrastructure can dominate adaptation.** Inexhaustible collectors, transparent covers, clean water branches, relays and independent workshops can make adding modules safer than reconfiguring them. If service is monotone—adding another legal source or relay never harms existing service—a robust universal network is a serious baseline, potentially a satisfying outcome rather than a design flaw. Mere physical collision is insufficient if extra components can be placed on the unbounded apron outside useful rays. If the argument for depth depends on interactions, the exact map must show them rather than assert that supports or beams “may” interfere. Alternatively, substantial varied useful construction and understandable discovery may carry the experience without persistent configuration conflict. Do not cure the problem with inventory, footprint, capacity or ownership limits prohibited by the proposal.

**3. Remote construction can remove the expedition.** The terrace is described as a better base footprint, but its indispensable benefit is not specified. If the player can choose a different regional base, build remotely and convey all sources without a distance consequence, remaining in the basin may meet the actual needs. Conversely, requiring a terrace base regardless of those needs would be a location certificate. For an assigned movement requirement, specify a real destination-dependent service or accept a solution with no expedition. Optional exploration does not need an indispensable mechanical payoff; it can remain available because a player may value seeing and making another place. That value must be observed rather than presumed in the workload total. A one-selection journey should stay one selection; folding, loading, unfolding and repeated trips are not justified merely by naming mobility as a chapter.

**4. Service-link abstraction can erase the proposed spatial problem.** Existing versions permit abstract links and endpoint-only placement. Those abstractions do not validate a new terrain-limited communications network or whole-journey clearance law. The paper sheet must state separately what water, power, heat and communication connections cross, how endpoints make contact, what obstructs them, and whether a known long connection is one complete operation. Otherwise a planner may freely assume either convenient global pipes or inconvenient repeated relay hops to obtain a desired count.

**5. Saved assemblies compress setup across all regions.** A learned module may combine its roof, collector, interface and work surface. Copies should not require repeated internal assembly, although genuinely local seating or source contact may remain. Count the final direct instantiation and selected placement, not the inferred construction tree. Requiring familiar interfaces to be rebuilt at each region would reproduce the earlier objection rather than answer it.

**6. Dynamic operation is not intrinsically better than robustness.** A hinge, controller or learner is worthwhile only if it serves a purpose the player values. A static all-condition answer is valid. Do not make weather sequences, host requests, duties or authority conflict exist solely so an operating mechanism has something compulsory to do. The pending host experiment may justify removing that layer.

## The 145–210 hypothesis

The arithmetic is correct, and the draft honestly declines to promise the 180–240 qualification range. The evidential basis is nevertheless weak:

- **65–85 build/discovery selections** have no enumerated distinct capability dependencies. Within the presently named functions, they could be repeated modules or an expanded noun-unlock tree. Neither is established to be worthwhile construction.
- **35–50 arrangements** depend on unspecified geometry. They could be consequential shared layouts, or the same place/attach/move setup previously criticized.
- **20–30 connections** depend on the undeclared network law. The number cannot be supported by imagining relay hops before defining whether they have a functional reason.
- **15–25 material operations** need actual material-use differences. Temporary-to-durable reuse has prior positive evidence; it does not license multiple obligatory versions of the same process.
- **10–20 journeys/operating changes** are particularly speculative for three regions with remote construction and no transport-stock budget. There is no stated sequence requiring that many meaningful travel choices.

The categories must also become mutually exclusive in the action ledger. A combination that discovers a design, selects its complete pose and performs a transformation is still one selection; it cannot contribute once to each workload row. Inspections, automatic changes, method internals, staged dialogue and checks add no accepted-play length. Normal, complete and saved routes are separate conditions, not components of a sum.

Thus **145–210 is a proposed budget of work to investigate, not a plausible lower bound or a validated expected range**. No exact replacement estimate is justified without the map and laws. A larger ontology could support substantial worthwhile creation, but player inefficiency, first-time confusion and decorative optional experiments cannot be the plan for the missing scale. Prior 76- and 47-action sessions and short constructive controls warrant skepticism; they neither mathematically forbid a longer game nor validate this one.

## Evidence boundaries

The staged crash/orbiter praise is **human feedback relayed by the parent**, not an independently inspected transcript here. It supports the appeal of brief motivations, staged tangible progress and the demonstrated concept. It does not establish long-game pacing, demand for a particular survival model, endorsement of restrictive traversal, or a preference for 200 selections. Presentation can be valuable without proving logical depth.

Agent reports separately support optional shared material/roof purposes and criticize repeated setup, transport and later archive work. They do not measure human enjoyment or duration. Mechanical witnesses establish feasibility and lawful shortcuts, not appreciation. The pending hosting integration has no fresh experience result in the reviewed draft; its short mechanics controls do not establish that teaching or civic ideas belong in this Mars campaign. No earlier count should be carried over as a completed chapter in new laws.

The “survival” framing also needs honest scope: with no death clock, depletion or consumable budgets, this is constructive life-support establishment, not a measured survival-pressure game. That is a legitimate design choice. Do not silently add resource anxiety later because the dramatic label suggests it.

## Required next artifact and stopping decision

An exact paper specification is useful if it exposes failure cheaply. It should contain:

1. One complete map with dimensions, actual sources/receivers/work locations, region relationships, access and exposure rows.
2. A finite property/recipe sheet stating what each new capability enables; no capabilities included merely to fill the construction allocation.
3. Exact endpoint predicates distinguishing current services, any genuinely necessary historical outcomes, and player-choice alternatives. Explain the terrace's functional purpose—or remove the compulsory terrace claim.
4. A minimal communications law and an explicit decision whether journey-clearance mechanics are necessary. Mark old abstractions that are retained and every genuinely new consequence. Do not validate them by analogy to camera rays alone.
5. A complete ordinary route plus complete-catalogue, rich-saved-assembly, ending-first, remote-duplicate and journey-free alternatives, using exact individually selected operations. Each route must cover the **whole** endpoint, not stitch together attractive local demonstrations.
6. An effect-removal comparison: remove the purported exposure/mobility/communication contribution while preserving actual endpoint requirements where possible. Identify which decisions genuinely cease to matter and which were decorative sequencing.
7. A short purpose-to-action account separating chosen synthesis/arrangement from repeated setup. This is not a demand that every action be novel; it is a defense against disguising the same chore with new nouns.

**Proceed beyond paper only if a coherent full game survives these lawful alternatives with enough distinct useful work and no compulsory-padding explanation.** Exact paper routes cannot prove enjoyment or a lower bound, but can reveal that a proposed long campaign has a short reusable-network solution. An expert complete-catalogue compression does not by itself invalidate a novice’s substantial appreciated discovery or building; describe the inference and construction that the compression requires. If it collapses, retain the short construction idea if worthwhile; do not implement the large arc first and then add anti-shortcut gates. If a short correct answer is the best account of the purpose, the paper specification has done its job.

**Keep:** retained place, clear survival/contact/work motivations, useful shared constructions, transparent alternatives and honest finite closure. **Change:** replace numeric workload allocations with explicit functions and exact operations; resolve the terrace and network semantics before claiming progression. **Reject:** renamed repeated service boards, mandatory transport rituals, forced lineage, universal-prefab suppression, or treating relayed human praise as long-campaign validation. The about-200 goal remains open.
