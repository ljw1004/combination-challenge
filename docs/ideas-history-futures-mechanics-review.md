# A Promise With Two Futures — independent pre-play mechanics review

2026-10-26. Role-separated, source-informed mechanics challenge; **not a fresh player, not enjoyment evidence**. Reviewed `AGENTS.md`, `docs/what-is-fun.md` revision 9, the ontology's proposed executable contract, engine, public CLI, screening script, existing tests and retained screen output. No engine, UI, frozen evidence, player sessions or Git changes were made. Review-owned artifacts are this report, `tests/ideas-history-futures-review.test.ts`, and `results/ideas-history-futures-review/`.

## Verdict

**Keep the honest short diagnostic; do not credit this as an established escape from permission-ledger play.** The scoped institutions have genuine, inspectable consequences for current participation and refusal. They are not merely collectible doctrine IDs. Yet the complete world is chiefly a permission configuration projected into fixed task/status fields. The garden plan never changes the promise, gardening, or any later possibility. The memories are a two-record eligibility condition, not experiences with different usable effects. There is no demonstrated extended actor life beyond those projections.

This is not a rejection because the answer is short, familiar or obvious. A six-combine construction may offer orientation, conceptual appreciation, comfortable enactment, or satisfying completion. All four remain unobserved until play. What the white-box evidence rejects is the stronger claim that adding automatic status responses has already resolved the ontology document's ledger-bookkeeping falsifier.

No functional blocker was found in the tested legal transition system. There is a **claim/scope blocker** if recruiting is premised on a mechanically substantial garden revision or developing lived history. A narrowly framed short scope-construction diagnostic is truthful; a broader claim needs a different substrate, not more doctrines or minimum moves.

The survey refusals are actual explicit response fields, not merely a predicate that refusal would be permitted. Lio's expedition participation is an explicit task and membership change. These are fair minimal logical enactments. Nonetheless they are recalculated from the current authority settings after every accepted combine; they produce no further downstream effects. Setting personal authority back to joint brings Lio immediately home and changes both survey responses to joint acceptance. Changing garden authority from joint to individual returns an already accepted garden revision to “waiting for authorized voice.” That is coherent as a snapshot of current authorization, but not as a durable accepted act or historical plan implementation.

Garden care is a preserved invariant, not an active restoration challenge: `settle` unconditionally opens the gate, and legal transfers only allow Mara or Ibis. This is not a correctness defect within the reachable toy world; no action can install an unwilling caretaker. It does mean the garden-access part of goal 1 never supplies an independent construction problem. Introducing periodic closure or artificial danger merely to add work would be the wrong repair.

## Generic modes, scopes and retained inputs

The mode/scope algebra is genuinely generic within this fixture: both individual and joint modes can be assigned to garden, personal or all decisions. All-scopes adoption replaces both; a narrower covenant replaces only its own overlapping scope. Releasing restores that scope's current identity default, rather than a cached previous charter. Kinship changes defaults without silently clearing explicit mandates. Merger explicitly clears them. All of these behaviors are public and covered by graph traversal.

There is no hidden requirement to discover a particular doctrine ID to win. The old actor, promise and idea operands stay on the shelf; knowledge does not consume them. Discovering every idea without adoption leaves all goals false. Full merger is legal and not scored morally inferior, but its common personal voice does not qualify as two individual refusals. Alternative caretaker care is legal and materially frees Mara; retaining Mara is equally valid. Preserve both.

The CLI source binds sessions to an engine hash and logs before/after states. Exact `preview` returns the current world consequences without committing. `offers` is a more compact forecast, not the entire resulting world; a player can inspect full previews and public laws. No new CLI player session was created for this review.

## Saturation and universal policies

The supplied screening result is accurately narrow:

- Six combines: scoped-personal federation plus reunion, without adopting kinship.
- Eight combines: kinship plus reunion plus shared garden representation.
- Eleven combines: the kinship route with Ibis's willing succession.
- Forward accepted-pair enumeration first succeeds at 24; reversed enumeration ends after 31 accepted combines with goal 3 false.
- Every idea alone does not solve the goals.

The forward screen stops at first success. It does **not** prove that continuing saturation preserves success. Both screens test one-use pair orders, not all universal policies or optimality.

The new stronger result is a legitimate **universal terminal repair** over all 90 reachable institutional configurations, once its capabilities exist:

1. Reunion + family, if not already shared.
2. Individual-personal covenant + family, if not already adopted.
3. Joint-garden covenant + family, if not already adopted.

At most three accepted adoptions solve every configuration. Redundant contacts can be skipped. This is not a fresh-state three-combine solution: capability preparation matters, and the exhaustive fixture spent 17 combines constructing the full shelf. Nor does it invalidate the existing six/eight/eleven routes. It does show that history, merger, prior mistakes and caretaker selection never require a different final construction. The relevant endpoint is shared knowledge + individual personal authority + shared garden authority; every authorized caretaker already keeps the gate open.

“Give everyone every right” is not represented as an accumulation of rights: all-individual loses common garden representation, and all-joint loses individual consent. “Merge everything” has only one family target and fails the independent-refusal requirement. “Fork everyone” similarly has one target; kinship alone leaves knowledge and common garden voice absent. “Bind every relative” meets Lio's real refusal. Leaving relations separate fails the shared-knowledge/voice conditions. These are meaningful counterexamples to crude saturation, but do not defeat the short scoped repair above. Do not ban that repair, demand kinship, or add quotas to rescue complexity.

## Are the combinations meaningful or clerical?

The six-combine witness has **two world-changing adoption combines and four shelf-materialization combines** (excluding appended explanation events). It contains one compact construction chain: consent + federalism → compact; compact + individual mode → template; template + personal scope → covenant; covenant + family → adopted institution. The choice of mode/scope has an intelligible consequence. Discovery/template/scope intermediates delay application and preserve inspectable reusable capabilities, but do not otherwise change the world, create risk, solicit a distinct response, or support an alternative use in this solution.

Calling all four intrinsically worthless would overreach: physically performing a known meaningful construction and seeing clear progress can be the point. Counting all six as separate consequential decisions would equally overreach. The unit of construction is the scoped charter, followed by shared memory. Fresh play must establish whether the intermediate enactment feels welcome or like clerical materialization; mechanical tests cannot answer that.

The three supplied goals are distinct constraints in one retained state, not three demonstrated episodes of different play. The shortest known route satisfies goals 2 and 3 together when memory sharing occurs; the eight-combine route exposes the missing common garden voice after reunion. Knowledge is retained and genuinely reused as a revision prerequisite. This is a small scope-composition puzzle, not yet three independent levels or evidence of a long intellectual history.

## Recommendation and next discriminating observation

**Retain:** public exact previews, legal alternative institutions, Ibis's succession, merger/refusal, all successful short routes, inexhaustible capabilities, and honest bounded scope.

**Do not claim yet:** sustained autonomous actors, material garden revision, strategic novelty per combine, human enjoyment, or anything approaching 200 decisions.

If a fresh public-only diagnostic proceeds, freeze and describe exactly this small model; use one retrospective, not per-combine rationalizations. Ask what the player found worth doing, whether the intermediate charter construction was satisfying enactment or unwanted setup, and what they believe the revised garden actually does. A belief that the plan changes irrigation would identify an overclaim, not demonstrate mechanistic engagement. Appreciated theme or clear progress counts as its own finding. Finishing satisfied is a valid ending.

If the decision criterion requires escaping permission-ledger play **before** recruiting, this implementation has not established that escape. Follow the ontology's stated alternative rather than multiplying families or doctrines: a present home with two genuinely reachable transformations (Unbuilt Inheritance). A possible repair to this substrate would require the revised garden agreement to cause an actual, publicly modeled change of garden process or layout, with the distinct memories making some transformations possible and a later combination using that changed world. That is a new hypothesis, not authorization to implement it here, and should not merely replace the accepted-plan boolean with more certificates.
