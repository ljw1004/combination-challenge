# Independent postplay review: linked future lives pilot A

26 October 2026. **One normal-condition AI-agent pilot; not evidence of human enjoyment or sustained long play.** Reviewer previously examined and tested this engine, and therefore is **not** a fresh player or blind critic. This review independently checked capture integrity and interpreted the frozen pilot; it did not prompt the player again, add gameplay, revise the capture, or recruit another participant.

## Result

**Retain the enacted continuity/transfer idea; change the unused prerequisite chain.** The player completed all five public goals in **19 accepted combines** and explicitly valued distinctions between having a method, personally experiencing an event, sharing an attributed account, and contributing after office. The actual trace supports those distinctions and connects the same score across traveller, home Iona and successor.

The player also explicitly called the Enduring Witnesses → Succession ancestry routine unlocking: neither capability was applied, and ordinary teaching/succession did the practical work. This is counterevidence for that discovery passage, not a reason to dismiss the whole finite construction or manufacture conditions that forbid the direct alternatives.

The run does **not** show that five discoveries were five appreciated ideas, that civic refusal was experienced, that the player was surprised by a possible life, or that they wanted a longer game. It supports a bounded positive report about causal continuity alongside a specific negative report about prerequisites.

## Player report versus evaluator inference

### Positive report with a matching trace

At the checkpoint, the player says shared care before the fork and later distinct lives were “worth doing” because the past was consequential rather than a copied label. They also valued whole-event enactment avoiding cue-by-cue work. The captured fork, distinct episodes and compact four-cue performance corroborate what those reports refer to.

At the end, they say the distinctions between method possession, personal episode, attributed account and continued contribution “made the collection coherent.” They explicitly describe the returned score and later successor teaching as connecting the projects rather than five unrelated badges. The sequential transfers at moves 8 and 18 and performances at moves 9 and 19 support **concrete useful reuse across assigned purposes**.

This is a positive AI-player judgment about the conceptual structure. It is stronger than inferring appreciation from success alone, but narrower than evidence of joy, wonder, surprise, human enjoyment or spontaneous new ambition. No report needs to be upgraded into those stronger terms.

### Routine work and direct alternatives

The player calls home-rest “mainly a routine witness” for the public check. That chosen extra step remains an accepted combine; do not remove it from the denominator or claim it was required. The known preplay 18-combine witness shows a shorter valid route; this player's 19-combine route is not a failure merely for taking one more action.

They say learning from the teacher instead of the public mapping was “a narrative choice, not a necessity.” That is an actual alternative selected and subsequently identified, but it is not evidence of long authored narrative or self-generated purpose: the main collection remained assigned.

The final report identifies Enduring Witnesses and Succession as routine ancestors used only to unlock Unowned. The trace directly confirms zero applications of either. They recommend shortening that chain or giving discoveries a distinct practical choice **without forbidding shortcuts**, and deliberately omit Reunion and Constellations because ordinary alternatives suffice. Preserve this criticism rather than recasting it as comfortable progress they did not report.

### Closure, not inferred appetite

The player expressly stopped because all five disclosed checks became true, not because every concept was collected. This is a chosen finite completion under an assignment that permitted stopping earlier. It is not evidence that they disliked finishing, that they demanded more content, or that they would voluntarily continue another level. The report supports closure at the supplied goal; no separate appetite test was run.

The trace contains no independent intention record for an unprompted project. The checkpoint's stated next aim is completing the remaining public checks. Accordingly, call the transfer **valued goal-directed reuse**, not spontaneous world renewal. Commands are not nineteen independently interesting decisions, and elapsed agent/tool time is not human playtime.

Keep Enduring Witnesses and Succession available as reusable concepts where their applications serve a purpose, and keep direct teaching/role transfer. This targets the player's concrete prerequisite criticism without inventing new moral traps, hidden requirements or compulsory demonstrations.

The falsifiable next question would be whether that route removes the reported unwanted unlocking while preserving the understandable linkage between learning, continuing people and civic infrastructure. It should not be tested by automatically recruiting replacements until someone praises the old chain. Any subsequent comparison or broader ontology experiment requires its own prospective decision, clear scope and publication gate; this review authorizes neither.

Do not respond by merely multiplying households, adding six more policy settings, or forcing the player to prove an installer refusal. The useful retained result is the performed connection between distinct lives and projects. The next genuinely different ontology branch would need a different concrete possibility, not a larger compliance ledger. That broader design remains open.
