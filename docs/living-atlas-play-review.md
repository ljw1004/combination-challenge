## 3. What transferred, and what was worked out

Every first board selection carries the exact global knowledge set without changing any physical board state. Every inactive board remains byte-identical during every actual combine/wait elsewhere. Physical persistence is therefore mechanically verified; **elective return or value from revisiting is unobserved**, because neither player revisits a board.

The assigned player's knowledge counts on first entry are 6 → 7 → 13 → 17 → 17 in the assigned order. The choice player's are 6 → 7 → 11 across the selected crossing/publication/intermittent sequence. These are capabilities available on arrival, not proof of understood concepts. The prospective predictions and concrete reuse supply the narrower understanding evidence:

- **Crossing:** both place roots at B2/D2 and let gentle seeded flow create rooted descendants at B3/D3. They predict the one-edge delay and wait rather than manually placing redundant roots downstream.
- **Ordinary alternation, assigned only:** Grove at C3 both learns a prerequisite and roots the shared supply. Four subsequent design-only acquisitions lead to a C3 Valve sensing C2. The player predicts two-beat phases/period four, realized by delayed negative feedback. These prerequisite actions mostly enact an already stated architecture.
- **Publication, both:** retain separate exact originals at B1/D1 **before** rooting their feeder cells; roots then convert exact streams into fragments while protecting the walking row. Channel ports send those fragments inward to C2, disconnecting the stored originals. Capture order and routing give the already-known Rootbed a goal-specific identity-transforming role, beyond its earlier walking/growth use.
- **Slow alternation, assigned only:** apply known Rootbed at C3, route C2 east to D2 and then south to D3, and have C3's Valve sense D3 instead of C2. The three-edge physical sensor path lengthens feedback while both groves still receive direct output. The player predicts period eight and four-beat phases; replay confirms both. No new concept is acquired on this board.
- **Intermittent publication, both:** add Archive/Beacon capability, allow actual A/B arrivals to populate separate retained records, route each Beacon south through rooted B2/D2, then send fragments inward. A record becomes a continuing source, not merely a passive original protected at an endpoint. Both predict a full four-phase proof rather than trust a favorable snapshot.

Both final intermittent architectures have B1/D1 south outputs and B2/D2 **only** east/west outputs. The north return edges are gone. This is retained-record **broadcast**, not continuing two-cell recirculation. A first A record occurs at local tick 3 and B at tick 5 in both runs. Neither learns an identity by learning the Beacon recipe; the new D1 component begins empty and must receive B physically. The choice player installs Archive at D1 before upgrading it to Beacon, accounting for its one extra intermittent combine. These are two closely related successful constructions, not two different persistence mechanisms.

The [earlier V3 review](living-works-3-play-review.md) separately verifies that actual circulating paths can satisfy the same goal with passive Archives. Atlas does not establish that the Beacon label is necessary for every valid solution; here it is the mechanism used in the chosen disconnected-record topology.

These are specific player reports, not calibrated enjoyment scores. No substantial redesign, failed action or long search is recorded. That limits depth-after-mastery claims without making the bounded constructions invalid.

It does not: automatic growth requires **soil**, whereas direct Rootbed installation allows soil/clay. The report explicitly recognizes the distinction; the original remains intact, and no repair is needed. This is a concrete corrected uncertainty, not evidence of hidden recipe eligibility or a major recovery episode.

### Beacon numerical disclosure caveat

The public recipe says a Beacon republishes its own retained identities; the flow rule repeats that description. Neither states the engine's precise law: a Beacon with an already-retained record sets its field's water to **2** and unions that record into exact identities. Records acquired during the current beat are committed after that output calculation, so retained-memory emission begins on the following beat. Actual views reveal resulting fields, and exact previews were available, but **neither player used a preview**. The assigned account expressly notes the missing pressure value and learns the persistent water-carrying effect through the run.

The relevant distinction is not recipe secrecy: ingredients, placement eligibility and the qualitative republication effect were public. It is incomplete numerical causal disclosure. Thus the runs support successful construction with inspection and observed effects; they do not establish perfectly informed advance prediction of every Beacon detail. If this candidate is revised, explicit pressure and capture/output ordering belong in the public law before a new exposure. The frozen V1/V2/V3/Atlas sources and these captures have **not** been patched or relabeled to erase the limitation.

## 7. Bounded decision

Retain Atlas as a functioning logical shared-ontology/explicit-goal candidate. The assigned sequence supplies coverage and specific transfer; the offered condition supplies actual uptake of two goals and a legitimate selected ending. These results are more informative than simply offering five cards or demonstrating an author's all-five witness.

They do not yet justify increasing recipe count, assuming every offered branch is appealing, or treating retained boards as a valued continuing world. The selected publication pair shares much of its construction, and both players call parts routine. Any next test should address a concrete unresolved source of useful construction or ontology breadth, while preserving honest finite closure and repairing the public numerical-disclosure gap before new exposure. This review adds no new game law, UI, human-enjoyment conclusion or obligation to continue.
