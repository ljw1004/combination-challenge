# Current design principles

Reconciled for the Markdown rebuild, 30 October 2026. The latest user instructions and [story specification](story-format-spec.md) control conflicts. These principles state design intent; they are not a claim that every story already meets it.

## The three givens

Combination, an enjoyable interactive game, and an empirical loop that can reject weak ideas. Everything else can change. The player chooses and combines actual things. Selecting a finished result and delegating its construction removes the central activity.

Avoid arbitrary ontology guessing. Normal interaction reveals compatible existing partners, not future output names. The task and visible things should suggest a reasonable action; afterward, the result should be recognizable and useful. Do not repair a missing ingredient, premise or purpose by adding a long explanation.

## What can be enjoyable

Informed, consequential choices are one route. Learning, clear progress, comfortable construction, curiosity, beauty, conceptual resonance and a satisfying ending can also matter. Not every gesture needs a dilemma. A plan may span several combinations. A short, clever solution is legitimate; neither recipe count nor forcing every optional branch establishes depth.

An approximate 200-decision campaign remains an inherited research ambition, not a minimum enforced by the engine or evidence about current stories. Do not equate action counts with decisions or human minutes. Keep the meaningful short route when it exists.

The user rejected inventory accounting as the default source of difficulty. No quantities, replenishment chores, production rates or action-credit economy are part of this rebuild. Authored consumption expresses what happened to a thing, with future work protected by the specification.

## Recognizable things and coherent places

Use ordinary things and clearly distinct hotspots. The core language is A+B→C, A@H→C, or A+B@H→C. A hotspot supplies context, acts as an operand, or receives a goal item; it never silently accumulates ingredients.

Named compounds are the explicit construction extension. Slots belong inside the thing. Independent parts can be installed in any order; a goal can require the completed configuration. Installation preserves existing slots. A transformation into a new identity wraps up the old compound details, as the new specification explicitly permits.

Names, artwork, physical access, supplies and task must agree. A whole greenhouse is not a portable tool. A photo, map or observation must answer a real story question. Do not manufacture calibration, labelling or paperwork steps merely to lengthen a route.

## A manageable working set

Visible things are clues about what matters. Prefer roughly 2–5 things for difficult abstract reasoning and up to about 9 for familiar physical scenes; these are authoring aims, not hard limits. Keep worthwhile alternatives and exploration. Review both arrival and peak clutter.

The Markdown needs algorithm governs carry and introduction. Every entered chapter gets its own surface; later chapters can reuse the same image and inherited hotspots. This supersedes the old rule forbidding same-place cleanup. Establish a truthful later visit or work session in prose when needed. Leave older surviving work in scrollback; never retroactively consume an object to tidy the board.

## A continuing story

Later chapters should make useful earlier creations matter. Merely carrying an unused token or raw supply is not continuity. Reuse can cross a gap and should change what the player can do or understand. Let play demonstrate this; introductions state the next purpose rather than retelling the last result.

Accept plausible alternative tools and preparation orders. In this declarative format, author those alternatives explicitly; the engine does not infer physical capabilities from arbitrary containment or similar names. Give optional products a worthwhile purpose, discovery or later use. Cut branches that exist only to preserve a recipe count.

## Honest intellectual and physical synthesis

Distinguish physical transformation, historical transmission, analogy, interpretation, criticism and deduction. A historical or artistic synthesis need not be a logically forced theorem. A new name alone is not a new intellectual achievement, either.

Do not make intellectual history an inevitable ladder toward the latest speaker. Religious and natural explanations can coexist. Attribute doctrinal claims to particular texts and traditions; completing their combination reconstructs a position, not a proof of religious truth. Keep genuine quotations accurate and research provenance in author notes.

Review factual authenticity separately from forward player intuition. A defensible retrospective explanation does not make a contrived combination understandable. The [authenticity backlog](authenticity-backlog.md) preserves this distinction.

## Evidence and revision

Freeze the source, player interface and assignment before a fresh trial. Keep developer routes out of the player's information. Preserve rejected moves, negative reports, legitimate shortcuts, satisfied early stopping and technical interruptions.

Ask what changed and what evidence would change our mind. Retain an observed success and its limitations together. Simulated play diagnoses confusion and pacing; it cannot overrule the user's enjoyment or certify human fun. See [fun and evaluation](fun-and-evaluation.md).
