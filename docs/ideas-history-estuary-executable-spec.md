# Estuary executable specification, version 1

27 October 2026. This settles the earlier paper gaps for a separate logical implementation. It is not evidence of play or authorization to change Mars. The earlier candidate/routes and independent critique remain preserved as design iterations.

## Scope and exact public rules

Keep all six projects: compressed translated experience; divergent lives exchanging usable experience; a shared cross-interface performance; successor-to-former-teacher learning; actual distant transmission/performance; role-addressed cooperation through succession. There is no ecological simulator, currency, material stock, compulsory civic-refusal ceremony or hidden correct recipe. All combinations yield named methods, scores, episodes, performers, families, invitations or schools with inspectable behavior.

Trajectories are piecewise linear exact rational knots. Angles range0–90, pressure0–1. A normal interface senses angle and actuates angle; pressure senses angle/90 and actuates angle; reversed senses angle and actuates90-command. A bound following practice computes the inverse disclosed sensor/actuator maps. A universal adapter computes the same inverse for every invertible interface. It and direct literal scores remain legal. Clipped sensing is not invertible; rich sensing remains available rather than rationed.

Public process knots:
- Sweep A: `(0,0),(1,45),(2,90),(3,45),(4,0)`.
- Journey B: `(0,0),(2,90),(3,30),(5,60)`.
- Live C: `(0,90),(1,30),(3,60),(4,0)`.
- Long Tide: `(0,0),(5,90),(15,90),(20,0)`.
- Later D: `(0,0),(1,90),(2,0),(4,90),(6,0)`.

D is not another occurrence of already-learned C. After taking over, the successor actually encounters D. Its episode is transformed into a specific replay score not previously in the former teacher's repertoire. The former teacher later reproduces that new sequence with **no live process input**. A universal following program alone cannot replay an unknown stored sequence on absent input. A player may directly author the same score; that is legitimate competence, but not automatically the specifically requested act of learning this later experience from the successor.

A score retains its exact latent angle trajectory and actual source episode IDs, plus an angle, pressure or dual-channel presentation. A pressure performance emits angle/90. A score's source links cannot be supplied through arbitrary action fields. Direct literal authorship has no invented actual source. Time rescaling changes its knot times exactly. A duet score must be explicitly constructed; it is not assumed to exist when performers meet. For this finite collection its linked roles enact the same source through angle and pressure, not an unconstructed compound of sweep and tide.

A performed score creates actual output, extends the actor's history and retains the replay ability. A controller run similarly retains its executable practice. Receiving a source episode can directly teach/enact its score through a compatible presentation. There is no separate unlock/install flag. Direct instructions are valid. First construction reveals a reusable named recipe; complete catalogue provides reusable methods but never fabricated actual runs or people.

Forking a willing person preserves the original as one continuing actor and creates a second with the actual shared prefix. Subsequent local and journey runs produce distinct histories. Combining the family with reciprocal teaching immediately transfers the selected actual divergent practices/scores and reveals the reusable reunion construction: the old N14/N15 ceremony is merged. Later each relative actually enacts the other's source experience. The evaluator checks the actual cross-source output and continuing histories, not a named reunion badge.

Teaching a host means it performs/retains a real practice through its declared interface. School+capable successor immediately changes request routing and reveals the named Continuing School construction. Former holders keep repertoire and personal actions. No civic status is required where it adds no behavior. School requests go to the actual current holder, not an original-ID exception.

An activity (controller run, replay, ensemble or festival) advances one world epoch, then delivers pending messages due at that epoch, then performs. Nonactivity construction/role transfer does not advance time. A sent message is due at the next epoch. This is disclosed scheduling, not a time budget; a whole meaningful activity is one selection and there are no wait clicks. Role-addressed messages deliver to the holder at arrival and persist in the school's mailbox. Personal messages stay personal. No distant adoption or knowledge is claimed before delivery. A festival request can itself advance delivery, a legitimate shortcut.

A Rendezvous Invitation contains a specific score and named school roles. Public willing-recipient policy accepts on receipt. It is not advance consent before receipt. A whole festival invocation may enact the accepted score at both current schools in one selection. Per-recipient automatic outputs are not additional decisions. The continued former teacher's own activity is independent of the school role.

## Six endpoint predicates

1. Actual tide source; rescaled duration4; pressure output by a receiver correctly reproduces its ordered continuous shape. Source remains somebody else's original observation.
2. Two continuing actors share an actual prefix, have distinct postfork controller episodes, and each actually reproduces an episode from the other after it exists. Direct teaching and universal methods can substitute for a named reunion.
3. Actual simultaneous angle and pressure performances by compatible distinct roles decode to the same actual sweep source. No mandatory ensemble name or invented tide component.
4. A real school succession; successor's later D episode; the former holder receives and actually replays a corresponding new score after that episode, with no earlier equivalent replay already retained. Merely repeating C or storing a new ID fails.
5. An actual local shared score is sent, received remotely and enacted there. Local prior lives remain; no assimilation of institutions is required.
6. A role-addressed invitation is sent before a real handover, arrives after it at the successor, and the actual successor enacts it; the former holder remains alive and performs its own later personal activity. One succession may satisfy4and6, no compulsory second handover.

Every predicate derives from histories and actual outputs. Known recipes, named prototypes and source IDs alone cannot satisfy performance. The complete and direct-author controls remain capable and may be short.
