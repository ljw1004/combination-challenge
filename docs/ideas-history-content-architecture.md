# A Common World, More Than One Future

26 October 2026. **Prospective content architecture only: no new engine, fresh pilot, implemented ecology or established long-game enjoyment.** The bounded live-host experiment remains frozen and awaits publication. This document addresses the larger intellectual-history possibility, not a claim that combining its chapter counts reaches 200 worthwhile decisions.

## The experience to aim for

Begin with people trying to understand and inhabit a place. End with several kinds of life that were not possible at the beginning: a person whose independent continuations exchange new experience; a house that has learned from its inhabitants; a forest whose history can become an art form; successors who inherit usable possibilities rather than a founder's instructions; communities that cooperate without becoming one community.

The player combines understandable ideas into reusable capabilities and combines those capabilities with particular people, methods, artifacts and places. A discovery should preview both the new possibility and a concrete example. Not every discovery must be immediately applied, surprising or difficult. The point is a growing vocabulary that changes what the player can make, not seven mandatory philosophy stamps before ordinary water service.

**Proposed setting:** an inhabited estuary and its connected observatory, school, forest and distant expedition. These are retained places, not reset boards. People and institutions have projects rather than a universal civilization score. Residents may choose continuity, divergence, shared embodiment, independent houses, extensive coordination or bounded cooperation. The player can know every idea without imposing every institution everywhere.

## Research, fiction and evidence must stay separate

The [historical source note](ideas-history-sources.md) establishes limited university/institutional facts and explicitly does not establish an Oxford–Bologna invention race or inevitable route to Nobel prizes. Those are guardrails for an optional historical discovery gallery, not rules assigning medieval schools intellectual strength. No particular *In Our Time* episode has been verified. The [SMAC research](smac-tech-tree-research.md) supports the design pattern of enablement, synthesis and critique; none of its named technologies or quotations are imported here.

All future concepts and the estuary are **authored speculative fiction**. Their parent combinations are intelligible design propositions, not historical derivations or scientific predictions. A source card can say “inspired by joining teaching and observation,” but may not say that an invented recipe caused Edinburgh medicine. Historical provenance must not become the main game merely because it is easy to verify.

Existing evidence is narrow: the causal inquiry was five selections; the linked lives pilot nineteen; the first family-permission kernel six. The lives pilot valued actual shared past, distinct selves and returning experience, and criticized unused prerequisites. Physical play valued changed uses of real constructions but disliked repeated setup. The current live-host control is 39 normal/37 complete selections, with fresh experience still unknown. None validates the landscape, art, future architecture or multi-community chapters below.

## One shared world language, several kinds of consequence

Use a small common set of object families, not one giant table of permissions:

- **People and learners:** continuing actors with actual episodes, acquired skills, public response policies and distinct choices. A shared account is not automatically a lived episode or copied authority.
- **Methods and models:** composable stimuli, controls and readouts with inspectable predictions. A method can act on an environment, not merely produce a certificate.
- **Places and processes:** actual arrangements and state transitions. Physical geometry and a future ecological model are distinct laws; neither can be faked by a goal label.
- **Experiences and translations:** playable sequences, sensory mappings and causal distinctions. Sharing a record differs from translating its rhythm or inhabiting a viewpoint.
- **Roles and agreements:** who acts for which purpose, with actual routing and response consequences. These support the world rather than replacing it.
- **Paths and configurations:** lawful transformations between functioning states, including replacement where permitted. A promise of two futures is not two named options on a menu.

Reusable operations include compose, intervene, teach, enact, fork, translate, reconnect and reconfigure. These are not automatically eight equally deep mechanics. Each must earn its place through a changed observable result. Use direct whole-method/whole-agreement actions; do not split authorization into redundant propose/accept/report clicks.

## A connected catalogue with concrete intermediate ideas

This is a **candidate subset**, not a compulsory research order. Basic parents such as adoption, controls, sensors, reversibility and time-lapse are public concepts. Intermediate names describe what the constructed thing does. Existing eighteen-node concepts are retained where useful; new bridge ideas below are original proposals. Alternative meaningful recipes remain admissible.

These 27 candidates supply intermediate constructions and cross-links, not 27 guaranteed worthwhile discoveries. Some may collapse together. In particular, Experience Addresses must not become an obligatory citation-stamp operation, and the various apprenticeship names must not require discovering duplicate synonyms to perform one act. An implementation may represent some rows as recipes for methods, not separately named research gates.

## Six connected movements, with genuinely different purposes

The sequence introduces concepts when a concrete project makes them useful. It is one proposed finite collection with a later return to earlier places; chapter order may branch where dependencies permit.

### 1. A school that can explain and make something happen

People at the observatory want to distinguish why a fictional glasshouse process persists, then use the same discovered persistence to produce a visible response through a dark interval. A later public counterexample distinguishes delay from adaptation. The player constructs a method, revises an explanation and uses it, rather than collecting three experiment receipts.

**Intermediate roles:** observer → method maker → teacher. **Retained objects:** Causal Portrait, actual apparatus, reproducible stimulus. **Alternative:** a richer universal method may settle several questions at once; keep it. **Failure test:** all projects reduce to selecting a target truth table, or constructing the apparatus contributes only clerical setup.

### 2. One past, two lives, a changed reunion

A willing person wants to continue the estuary workshop and take the expedition, not merely hire a substitute. Fork Kinship makes two continuing lives possible. Each actually experiences something different. The traveller later teaches a new spatial or temporal practice to the workshop relative; reunion preserves both itineraries. A consensual complete merger is a valid different life, not automatically two independent continuations.

**Intermediate roles:** relative → traveller/local practitioner → teacher/recipient. **Retained objects:** the original method and a new expedition episode. **Transfer:** an explanatory method becomes something a person can learn and adapt. **Failure test:** authority settings or lineage alone satisfy the goals; the world never changes because of what either relative learned. No named original actor is mandatory except where an explicitly requested continuity makes identity meaningful.

### 3. A landscape can be understood and experienced, not only catalogued

A trace-bearing grove changes under a disclosed small process model. The project is first to reconstruct a relevant earlier disturbance, then choose a restoration whose actual evolution preserves a stated living feature. Earlier methods can probe the new process. Borrowed Seasons translates its long change into a short performance; a visitor must be able to distinguish two meaningful temporal orders in the enacted score, not merely read a label.

**Intermediate roles:** investigator → restorer → translator/artist. **Transfer:** Causal Portrait and expedition experience become methods/material for an Embodied Score; the restored place continues evolving. **Alternative:** a plain accurate account or direct observation may meet a scientific purpose; it need not fulfill the explicitly chosen embodied-art project. **New law cost:** actual ecological traces and temporal/sensory translation are unimplemented and potentially expensive. **Failure test:** ecology is renamed pipes, or art is a narrative paragraph attached to a complete flag.

### 4. A home that leaves its successors room to become different

The workshop now hosts residents, a translated performance and ongoing observation. A generation wants a functioning present and two actual later uses: a private retreat and a shared teaching/performance space. The player can rearrange, replace, expand or retain earlier constructions as the public spatial laws allow. The project is not “never demolish” or “keep original object 7.” If replacement and inexhaustible copies make all futures trivial, record the collapse rather than invent a material budget.

**Intermediate roles:** builder → successor → later resident/host. **Transfer:** existing apparatus, scores and learned service acquire different spatial roles. **Alternative:** a permanent monument is a legitimate different project; this scenario states why these residents value changing use. **Failure test:** the entire inheritance is a single don't-fire-yet flag, or three arbitrary shape targets with no real present function.

### 5. The house becomes a participant, not an extra settings menu

A willing resident teaches actual controls of the already useful construction, hands over its hosting role and continues elsewhere. The machine's responses must be enacted on those same parts. A later resident revises the purpose: useful transfer would require adapting a method or applying it to a substantively different task, not replaying another east/west request merely to lengthen the game.

**Intermediate roles:** builder/teacher → learned host → former founder/adviser. **Transfer:** material methods and translated performances become teachable behavior; succession separates continuing knowledge from governing role. **Alternative:** static robust service and direct human operation remain valid ordinary service. Civic autonomy is an explicitly chosen project, not a prerequisite for survival. **Evidence gate:** current live-host pilot tests only the first small bridge. Expansion must wait for whether that bridge is experienced as worthwhile.

### 6. Cooperate without sharing one future

The expedition community has different local purposes and communication is delayed. It wants to share the Counterfactual Museum and a recurring meeting while retaining its own choices. A conditional rehearsal can show the consequences of announcing a plan; willing commitments make a particular event occur. A treaty passes the score, method or invitation across the interface, not universal authority. Later local succession tests whether the agreement actually continues or exits lawfully.

**Intermediate roles:** correspondent → coordinator → successor institution. **Transfer:** earlier experiences, scores and roles become shared projects across communities. **Alternatives:** ordinary schedules, fully optional conditional forecasts and a simple bounded treaty may solve the whole problem. Preserve them; no mandatory prediction crisis. **New law cost:** real message transit and actor response to publication are unimplemented. **Failure test:** one universal “offer all options” institution solves every later purpose with no worthwhile new construction, or the player only edits scope fields in a ledger.

## Progression, variety and the whole-catalogue control

The proposed collection offers explanation, enactment, divergence, translation, spatial transformation, learned operation and cooperation. That is a diversity hypothesis, not proof of variety. Persistent people, models and creations must visibly cross those purposes. A later task should explicitly show where an earlier construction is compatible, including a successful shortcut, rather than require rediscovering it.

Normal progression and complete catalogue must use the same worlds, actors and goals. Complete catalogue grants reusable conceptual capabilities, never fabricated episodes, physical service or agreements. The normal route can have pleasant understandable discoveries; it must not require ancestors with no intelligible relation solely to delay the player. A complete-catalogue shortcut measures what discovery contributed, not whether normal progression was automatically illegitimate.

Before implementing a broad collection, draw actual whole trajectories for normal and complete conditions and the strongest saturation policy: every capability available, willing replacement actors, all legal copies, whole methods and institutions, static alternatives, and unrestricted optional participation. Identify which goals remain substantial and why. If most disappear, narrow or replace those projects rather than hiding rules or banning convenience.

Do not assign a quota of actions per chapter. To approach roughly 200 decisions, the eventual complete experience must have enough interacting purposes and useful transformations that players actually choose substantial construction across the collection. If honest witnesses remain forty or sixty, call it that. Source-exposed long witnesses alone are not experienced length. A full fresh run with sparse chapter-boundary reports and independent replay is necessary before any scale claim.

## Relation to the Mars architecture

Mars survival → communication → productive base can stand on its own clear motivations. This estuary future-life architecture is a distinct possible campaign, not a philosophical tax on getting water. Shared ontology can be limited to actual apparatus, methods, demonstrations, traces and roles. If learned operations prove worthwhile, a Mars resident may choose them late for dependable services. If experience translation proves worthwhile, it could become an optional cultural project rather than a compulsory power upgrade. Do not combine two unvalidated campaigns merely to add their counts.

## Next decision, not implementation authorization

Await the bounded live-host pilot and independent interpretation. In parallel the larger architecture can be challenged on paper: choose one complete trajectory, mark actual state changes and cross-purpose reuse, and attempt universal static/manual/copy/optional-policy shortcuts. The high-cost unimplemented laws—ecology, embodied temporal translation, reflexive public plans and asynchronous institutions—must not be smuggled in as source-backed facts. Select at most one genuinely new substrate after the evidence, with a finite integrated endpoint and explicit controls, rather than another disconnected five-action illustration.
