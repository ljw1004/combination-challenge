# Living Works 3: an offered next goal and a fresh construction

Prospective protocol after Living Works 1 and 2 completed. The same ontology and physical laws are reused on the publication board, with explicit intermittent source schedules. No new recipe, narrative or resource economy is introduced.

## Goal and mechanical check

Over an exact full physical cycle, retain separate A-only/B-only originals, keep a dry connection, and continuously deliver A+B fragments without exact identities at the square, including source-off phases. Source schedules A=[2,2,0,0] and B=[0,0,2,2] are public. Any valid architecture is accepted: active remembered broadcast or real circulating-signal storage may both qualify under the existing laws. There is no requirement to use a named object.

Compare an existing direct source/filter route, which loses delivery during pauses, with legal persistent-delivery witnesses. Verify every phase of a repeated physical state, including source phase, not a favorable observation. Distinguish changing input conditions plus goal from a one-factor goal-only ablation.

## Two different evidence conditions

1. **Offered next goal, experienced player.** Invite the completed prebuilt-catalogue player to this explicit new level. Their previous assignment is over. Stop is valid; do not solicit invention of an entirely new purpose. If they actually choose the offered goal, record a prospective linked-session intention before creating its capture. This tests uptake of an offered level, not spontaneous self-generated renewal.
2. **Fresh assigned construction.** A new public-only player receives this one goal and all 24 known concepts. They supply basic construction/clarity evidence without earlier model exposure. Their completion is mandated, not elective.

These conditions differ in both experience and assignment; do not estimate a causal learning or enjoyment effect from their action-count difference. Both may provide concrete evidence about construction, selected branches and consequences. Human enjoyment remains unmeasured.

## Protocol

Only public CLI rules, state, legal actions and previews are available. No author witnesses, code, other player reports or automated move search. Record intentions before actions, predictions, failures and what was worth attention. Keep combinations, waits/beats and finish separate. An exact cyclic goal may require observing waits; waiting is not automatically a new decision.

For elective acceptance, link the new run through the existing CLI continue/nextSession mechanism before init, retaining prospective timestamps. If the player declines, preserve that result and do not manufacture a capture. If they continue, actual construction is stronger evidence than an intention alone, but still only agent-protocol behavior. Freeze all source and public observations before either condition begins.
