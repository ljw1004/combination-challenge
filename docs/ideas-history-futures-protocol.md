Test a small connected slice: kinship after copying, promises through divergent lives, shared memory without compulsory identity, and purpose-specific common authority. A player should be able to recognize an actual changed task or choice—someone joins an expedition, keeps a promise, refuses a request, or acts through a shared voice—rather than only certify a relation table.

V2's provenance and V3's causal-method pilots remain limited positive results. V3's player disliked separate recording of already-known forecasts. This candidate therefore has no propose→accept confirmation command: combining an offer with an actor immediately runs the disclosed willingness policy. Idea/template/scope preparation remains a possible administrative burden and must be evaluated honestly. Creating an operand counts under the prospective rule even if the player finds it routine; countability does not imply appreciation or depth.

Future-personhood wonder and comfortable clear progress are legitimate possible value. Do not require every action to introduce a dilemma or every player to discover a new theory. Conversely, an evocative name is not evidence of an unimplemented actor consequence. The principal falsifier remains **V2-style scope bookkeeping with a larger fictional description**.
