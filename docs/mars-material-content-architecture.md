# Mars content architecture: improvise with things, then discover more uses

26 October 2026. **Revised prospective architecture, not an engine, route-length promise or play result.** Delivered Mars UI/content and frozen research versions remain unchanged.

## Scope correction first

The owner’s Mars inspiration, relayed by the coordinating parent, was:

> “I wonder if ‘survive on mars’ could be a goal by combining the things that the astronaut did in the book/film The Martian. That wasn't godlike assembling from elements; just things”.

An initial draft overextended the larger-ontology diagnosis into ore-to-industrial bootstrapping. It is preserved as `results/mars-material-content-architecture/unselected-raw-industry-draft.md`, **not selected as the Mars backbone**. Familiar advanced starting equipment is appropriate here. The astronaut does not need to reinvent heaters, wire or radio to earn a longer game. A raw-element civilization scenario could be considered separately only if wanted; it is not a lengthening device for this mission.

A large reusable ontology can instead consist of **salvaged objects, their actual components, materials and alternative working arrangements**. The player recognizes things, discovers useful relationships between them through public previews, and makes an installation suited to this place. Recipe secrecy, finite supplies and mandatory manufacturing remain excluded.

## What the small paper result actually diagnoses

The minimal paper mission offered a small set of distinct useful roles and endpoints. A capable camp with shared warmth and a directly placed beacon met them cheaply. Supplying advanced components was not a design error; requiring an industrial prerequisite tree to replace them would contradict the intended improvisation.

The content limitation is that the same few devices mostly supplied water, heat, protection and a signal, while the abstract interfaces let them serve the whole map. More rich objects could provide genuinely different uses, but names alone will not overcome that structural compression. The next hypothesis is therefore **broader repurposing and distinct situations**, not more copies of the same supply network or a declaration that a short perfect-knowledge route is bad.

Keep evidence separated: Lucian’s positive response to staged crash/orbiter concept/storytelling supports brief visible motivation and tangible progress, not long-game enjoyment. Agent reports support optional shared-purpose creations and criticize repeated late specimen/copy setup. Neither proves this proposed content architecture will sustain the desired scale.

## A concrete mission and its things

**Make a dependable surface home and working expedition out of recognizable equipment, with useful contact to the orbiter.** Start with several visible existing constructions rather than a bare elemental periodic table. All actual parts, properties, faults and compatible results are inspectable. Disassembly is a physical operation, not a hidden-item search.

| Existing things | Publicly inspectable reusable parts/materials | Different possible uses |
|---|---|---|
| Suit cooling/service unit | flexible tubing, small pump, heat exchanger, membrane, protective shell | water delivery, local thermal coupling, a filter housing, a dry protected instrument mount |
| Damaged habitat section | frame, clear window, insulation, sealable panel, porous air filter | growing cover, shaded workspace, fitting interface, windbreak, reflective/transparent boundary arrangement |
| Rover running gear | motor, axle, wheels, chassis, joint/hinge | actual movement, rotating a useful device, a supported mast, a folding or deployable structure |
| Ventilation assembly | fan/rotor, motor, duct, guard | air passage, mechanical drive or wind-driven conversion if the public law genuinely supports reversibility |
| Radio/contact assembly | source/receiver, antenna or optical head, controller, protected casing | live communication, remote operation of existing controls, a differently oriented/protected source |
| Solar installation | collectors, mounting frame, cable, hinged support | reliable power under public exposure rows; mount can also support other apparatus |
| Food/growing equipment | tray, seed/rootbed, porous lining, transparent cover | growing service, clean/dry work support, a separator when the declared pore/contaminant relation suffices |
| Packing and repair materials | foil blanket, clear film, fabric/mesh, binder/seal, rigid panels | reflective collection, insulation, shading, watertight lining, dust protection, flexible joints |
| Hand tools and forms | clamp, cutter, drawing/forming surface, press, shaped former | change actual geometry/contact of a material; no separate compulsory tool-manufacturing civilization |

These are **content families**, not nine compulsory salvage missions. Some equipment may be supplied intact, some already usable and some publicly damaged. A repair is warranted by an actual missing function, not by requiring an original object’s ID. Replacing the installation or copying an already learned working design is lawful.

Concepts remain inexhaustible after they become available. There is no count of remaining hoses, harvested bolts, fuel, food or oxygen. A known component can be reused directly; taking it from an assembly once can expose an intelligible capability, but later uses do not demand repeated dismantling rituals. Do not make “salvage” a disguised finite-inventory economy.

## The shared ontology is a graph of uses

The proposed richness lies at three linked levels:

1. **Recognizable objects:** a suit loop, a rover, a fan, a radio, a habitat panel. These give immediate understandable purposes.
2. **Actual constituents and forms:** motor, tube, membrane, coil, window, hinge, sheet, cavity, contact face. The player can take apart, combine, reshape or reuse them without guessing the author’s recipe.
3. **Working arrangements:** pumping from a real source to a higher receiver, a protected but exposed collector, a mechanically driven device, a live signal path, a dry working surface beside a wet growing area.

An object is not valuable only because a later goal names it. A clear window can protect while transmitting; an opaque blanket can shade or reflect; the same flexible sheet can be a cover, a lining or a fitted interface. Some obvious constructions can be comfortable progress. Do not demand that every intermediate have a unique required objective or tradeoff.

Canonical designs remain convenient reusable examples. Equivalent property/contact arrangements count. A whole saved assembly should genuinely reduce later setup, not merely unlock a long repeated fabrication animation.

## Representative substantial chain: repurpose a service unit for a higher growing site

This is a **whole useful construction chain with branches**, not a prescribed player route or a claimed count. It begins with things the astronaut plausibly already has, not ore extraction. Exact proposed water/work laws must be specified before implementation; this is fictional game logic, not real survival guidance.

| Purposeful construction | Actual operation and consequence | Legal alternative to preserve |
|---|---|---|
| Understand the cooling unit | Public inspection identifies its real pump, hose, exchange surface and casing. Inspection is not counted as a game intervention. | Use another known pump or leave an already suitable unit intact. |
| Obtain a usable subassembly | Detach the actual pump/line group, preserving its internal joins and clearing only broken external links. | Copy a known suitable design; no mandatory original suit identity. |
| Obtain water at the seam | Bring a heater or reflective collector into an actual useful relationship with ice; connect an actual source-supported supply. | Passive solar warmth may be enough. Do not require an electric heater by name. |
| Prepare the source interface | Adapt a hose end, membrane or soft sealing surface to actual contact geometry. A ready fitting can be used directly. | Move the apparatus or choose a compatible outlet rather than perform a ceremonial molding step. |
| Make the water useful | Place a porous separator or other actual process where it changes the disclosed stream property. | Omit it for an already clean source or use another physically sufficient separator. No real-water-safety claim follows from this abstract game tag. |
| Choose how work reaches the pump | Connect electrical power to a compatible existing motor/pump, or couple a real mechanical drive. | A direct fan/rotor shaft can bypass electrical conversion if the implemented coupling law supports it; no mandatory generator chain. |
| Place the useful receiving site | Choose a growing/work location that changes light, protection, signal or access—not merely a named target square. | Keep the use site low, exploit gravity or supply another legal site. Terrace travel need not be compulsory. |
| Establish lifted service where needed | Join pump, real inlet and receiving conduit; actual source-grounded work permits a higher outlet. | Passive delivery is sufficient where the source is already higher. If old abstract links still move water uphill freely, this pump has no demonstrated role. |
| Arrange the growing environment | Combine actual root/tray, cover, light and warmth with the useful water endpoint. Preserve open-side/overhead versus sealed-enclosure distinctions. | Shared clear cover, independent modules, copied working bed or a different location are legal. |
| Reuse what is no longer doing its first job | An emptied protective casing, spare exchange surface, frame or blanket can become part of the dry workspace or communications installation. | Leave it unused when that is simpler. Optional reuse should not become another mandatory history certificate. |
| Leave the coupled installation dependable | Check all disclosed exposure/operating conditions; resolve actual source, geometry or contact failures. | A static robust answer may already work. No compulsory failure, maintenance round or repeated demonstration. |

The chain can involve actual disassembly, fitting, lining, joining, source placement, source conversion, routing and useful shared construction. It can also collapse into a short direct setup if the initial equipment already fits. That is a legitimate condition to test, not a failure to obey. The proposed variety comes from useful physical differences and alternative existing objects, not from forcing every row to occur.

## Material transformations that fit improvisation

Use a small number of broadly reusable processes on the actual objects:

- **Separate and join:** recover a real component or combine subassemblies while preserving their physical extents and inspectable connections.
- **Cut/form/fold:** a film, blanket, duct or panel changes actual shape and contact. A hinge moves only its connected parts. No arbitrary whole-assembly shrink.
- **Soften and fit:** an appropriate seal/lining takes an actual mating surface’s profile. Already matching alternatives remain valid.
- **Line or laminate:** make a formerly porous surface watertight or give an exposed surface a different optical/protection role. This should change actual service, not award a processing stamp.
- **Expose, shade and insulate:** rearrange physical boundaries around a real source/receiver or working surface. Avoid a generic “advanced enclosure” that solves every condition regardless of geometry.
- **Couple and repurpose:** a motor, axle, rotor or pump participates in a different working arrangement under the same public work/source rules. Do not rename a device and silently grant a new ability.

Metal/glass/ceramic fabrication may exist as optional possibilities where they serve a genuine construction, but the Mars mission does not require starting from ore or hand-manufacturing an existing pump. The unselected raw-industry draft is not a task list waiting to be restored.

## Minimal next new law, rather than three new simulators

The strongest candidate for the next exact paper specification is **elevation-aware water service with explicit source-grounded lift/work**. It gives an existing pump or repurposed drive a visible job and makes source/use placement consequential without water quantities or consumption.

State the law before using it:

- A passive connection cannot create an outlet above its actual source head.
- A compatible operating pump can support a disclosed higher outlet when supplied with actual work from a legitimate source.
- Work may be electrical or direct mechanical only if the same explicit port/coupling rules support it; a word like “pump” cannot hide a new success flag.
- Reaching a lower use site, moving the receiver, selecting a higher source, or using a different lawful device remain alternatives.
- A complete line is one selected connection, not a row of paid pipe tiles. No throughput, tank-fill or power-budget bookkeeping is introduced.

This is a genuine proposed law, not something the current geometric engine already does. Actual mechanical work/coupling needs explicit semantics even if implemented minimally. Do **not** simultaneously assume a complete electromagnetic simulator, fluid pressure solver, radio model, atmosphere and programming language. Existing laws for geometry, source provenance, actual material state and saved assemblies should do as much work as they can.

Live communications can remain a separately specified purpose or a later addition. The pending teach→host experiment belongs at most as an optional dependable-operation alternative; it does not justify making civic philosophy a prerequisite for water or power. Static robust layouts and direct human operation remain lawful.

## A content audit before another engine

Produce one complete mission sheet with:

1. The recognizable starting equipment, actual contents, condition and initially known capabilities. Do not remove good supplied equipment after observing a short solution.
2. All public material/device effects and the one chosen new law. No hidden canonical recipe requirement.
3. A complete site/source layout and functional ending, plus a route showing substantial useful repurposing where it actually occurs.
4. Complete-catalogue, direct/passive, replacement and reusable-rich-assembly alternatives. Preserve shorter answers.
5. A reuse graph showing which parts/materials serve genuinely different purposes, which steps are familiar enactment, and which are merely redundant setup.
6. Actual paper route lengths, with **no numeric duration promise**. Add neither obligatory objects nor repeated operating requests to meet a target.

A larger useful ontology is a credible direction to investigate, not proof of longer enjoyment. A short perfect-knowledge route does not automatically invalidate creation/discovery. Conversely, a long dismantle/reassemble checklist is not redeemed by calling it survival improvisation. Only complete actual play can establish how much varied, comfortable and worthwhile interaction this content supports.
