# Linked futures: exact prospective contract and paper witness

26 October 2026. **Finalized design candidate for an implementation decision, not implemented or played.** Refines [the first scenario proposal](ideas-history-linked-futures-scenario.md). It supersedes that proposal's suggestion that better ordinary house service is sufficient evidence of a newly possible life.

## Core purpose, chosen by a person

Iona is a consenting copyable, long-lived person. She wants **two continuing lives with their shared experienced past intact**: one remains involved in the house, one joins a travelling light-and-shadow ensemble. After they acquire different experiences, she wants them to exchange a selected new method without abandoning those distinct lives.

This is not 'use Fork Kinship' as a goal. Any mechanism producing the requested actual state is acceptable. An unrelated new steward can maintain the house, but cannot substitute for Iona's request to branch her own experienced life. Shared identity/history is relevant because the person's request concerns it, not because the designer needs an original-ID password. A merged or single itinerant life remains a lawful alternative ending, not a moral failure.

The house also needs working care. That purpose admits a willing human steward, direct player construction, a learning machine caretaker, or another lawful equivalent. There are **no seven compulsory concept gates**.

## Public initial objects

- **Iona:** one actor at the house, consenting to the stated branching project. She can enact and demonstrate known methods, accept a selected teaching offer, travel, and enter a voluntary role.
- **House:** a roof shade with states `open | spread`, a rest-room light `dark | lit`, and a work-room light `dark | lit`.
- **Installed caretaker:** initially a directly commanded executor, not a learning civic actor. It can enact any supplied method; it has no secretly missing service capability.
- **Public ensemble:** willing teachers with a visible score and method. No audition score, payment, rationed opportunity or hidden approval.
- **Basic methods:** `roof-care`, `rest-care`, `work-care`; these can be supplied directly to the executor or demonstrated and learned. The basic working house therefore has a short direct solution.
- **Primitive ideas:** all parents needed by the seven-node network are available indefinitely, with complete recipes and effects disclosed. Compound ideas are initially unmaterialized in the normal condition.

Locations are `house` and `ensemble`. Moving a whole actor is one action. There is no travel timer. Location determines which event an embodied actor directly experiences, not whether information can be communicated. Teaching can occur remotely in one operation; don't force travel as a prerequisite for knowledge.

## Exact event and learning semantics

A method is a finite public mapping from a **domain and cue** to a device operation. These are actual program objects, not confidence scores.

| Method | Exact mapping |
| --- | --- |
| `roof-care` | `(weather, glare) → roof.spread`; `(weather, cloud) → roof.open` |
| `rest-care` | `(house, rest) → rest-light.dark` |
| `work-care` | `(house, make) → work-light.lit` |
| `shadow-score` | `(score, begin) → shade.open`; `(score, accent) → shade.spread`; `(score, release) → shade.open` |

A device binding maps the abstract `shade` output to either the ensemble's screen or the house's actual roof shade. The operation and cue mapping are unchanged by binding. This gives a visible cross-domain use rather than an 'artist' tag. `roof-care` and `shadow-score` share the same open/spread output vocabulary but differ in input meaning; neither automatically substitutes for the other.

An **enactment** combines an actor/method binding with a public event sequence. The selected actor applies the mapping cue by cue; every output and intermediate device state is exposed. One selected sequence enactment is one decision, not one per automatic cue. Each directly participating actor receives an immutable episode containing input cues, selected method, outputs, context and its own participation. An observer may remember a witnessed event, but a taught method is marked as taught, not falsely recorded as personally lived.

A **teaching** combines a complete method or demonstration with a willing actor and adds that reusable method to its repertoire. One whole method can be taught in one action. It does not add the teacher's personal experience to the student's lived biography. Learning is exact copying of a disclosed mapping; there is no hidden induction or uncertainty.

A **fork** of consenting Iona preserves the existing actor as one continuing branch and creates another, copying her current repertoire and experienced past. Each gets a distinct identity and independently accepted itinerary. Later experiences and method changes affect only their actual recipients. Copying again is unrestricted; extra selves do not counterfeit another self's consent or lived experience.

A **reunion** combines willing actors and an explicitly selected method/episode set. They can teach methods and share inspectable accounts of their different episodes while retaining identities, bodies, roles and itineraries. Shared accounts are not rewritten as firsthand memories. A full merge is separately legal under their stated consent, but has a different result.

A **role** routes incoming event requests to its current holder. Succession changes routing, not the former holder's memory or ability to teach. An explicit mandate names domains; a civic caretaker follows accepted resident mandates and may reject an installer command outside them. A learner's acceptance policy is public: accept a nonconflicting requested service in a domain it can execute; decline contradictory simultaneous device operations and unrelated commands outside its accepted mandate. A refusal is a consequence, not another mandatory confirmation click.

`Constitutional Apprentices` adds teaching and declared mandate acceptance to the installed executor. `Unowned Infrastructure` separates its resident mandate from its installer's direct command channel while preserving the actual learned repertoire and device bindings. These enable another future-life project, but the core branching-life endpoint does not demand this branch.

## The seven ideas as reusable capabilities

Use the exact recipes in the original ontology for Enduring Witnesses, Succession Without Death, Constitutional Apprentices, Unowned Infrastructure, Fork Kinship, Promise Constellations and Reunion Without Erasure. Discovery produces a reusable capability object. Applying it creates/transforms actual actors, executable roles or transferable methods.

Enduring Witnesses exposes a living actor's retained episodes as usable demonstrations without removing the actor from their ongoing life. Ordinary recording/teaching remains an alternative. Promise Constellations revises who handles an existing event commitment, with the actual recipients' declared consent. It does not duplicate that commitment merely because a person forked. Ordinary direct voluntary stewardship remains an alternative. Recipes express fictional possible syntheses, not necessary historical derivations.

## Endpoint predicates, all visible from the start

**Working house:** its accepted responsible arrangements produce the declared rest, making and roof-care outputs when the public service suite is enacted. Current actual device states and resulting trace must match; an assigned role with no execution is insufficient. Direct executor service is valid.

**Two continuing lives:** two distinct current actors descend from the consenting Iona state after a real shared useful episode; one has actually enacted a home event after divergence and remains committed to the home itinerary, the other has actually participated in the ensemble performance after divergence and remains committed to the travelling itinerary. Shared past is preserved; later personal episodes are distinct. An itinerary is executable location/event behavior, not a label alone. A concrete next-step preview shows each actor can continue its stated itinerary independently.

**Experience returns without ending those lives:** a method actually used in the travelling branch's performance is subsequently available to the home branch, and the home branch enacts its house-bound translation. The two people and their distinct itineraries remain. Direct teaching, shared demonstration or another equivalent transfer qualifies; the checker does not require the Reunion concept ID. Merely copying an empty actor or declaring shared authority does not produce the required performance.

The 'after' relationships concern the person's requested life trajectory, not demonstrations inserted solely to count moves. All goals can be planned from the beginning; no hidden chapter gate requires a ceremonial report.

## Source-exposed paper witness

This is a hand-derived legal trajectory under the stated semantics, **not an engine replay or a player report**. It intentionally keeps a short route.

1. Supply the executor the whole basic care suite and enact it. In particular, Iona participates in the useful roof demonstration `[glare, cloud]`, producing `[spread, open]`. Her retained past now contains this real episode. Whole-suite application/enactment can be a compact interface operation; do not split it for counts.
2. Discover `Fork Kinship` from copyable minds and adoption. Apply it to consenting Iona: home Iona and travelling Iona share the roof episode and initial repertoire; they accept their respective itineraries. Direct care stays on the executor, so no invented inheritance crisis arises.
3. Enact the home branch's resting event, producing `rest-light.dark`. This episode belongs to the home branch only.
4. Move the travelling branch to the ensemble. Teach the public `shadow-score` if not already known. Enact `[begin, accent, accent, release]`, producing `[open, spread, spread, open]` on the ensemble screen. This personal performance episode belongs to the travelling branch only.
5. Either discover/apply `Reunion Without Erasure` or use ordinary remote teaching to transfer the score and its attributed account. Do not grant home Iona a false firsthand travel memory. Both actors retain their chosen itineraries.
6. Bind that same score to the house roof shade and have home Iona enact it: `[open, spread, spread, open]`. The house now hosts a returned light/shadow performance through an object previously used for care. Restore/enact the ordinary care suite if necessary; giving the performance never counts as meeting the glare/cloud care condition.
7. Endpoint: care works, both lives continue, and an actual learned/experienced method has returned without merging them. At least five of the seven compound ideas can remain undiscovered on this route. Their absence must not prevent success.

A longer **optional**, not compulsory, construction can make the installed caretaker a Constitutional Apprentice, teach it the same methods, and use Succession Without Death plus Unowned Infrastructure to let it take resident-governed care while Iona's branches teach and perform. Promise Constellations can revise an actual caregiving commitment if one was voluntarily attached to Iona. If no such commitment exists, do not require revising an empty promise.

This witness does not estimate 200 decisions. Interface atomicity and exact allowed alternatives must be fixed before any action-count claim. The expected diagnostic is modest; its question is whether the linked discoveries and lived reuse are worth experiencing.

## Controls and prospective falsifier

Normal progression starts with primitive concepts and all recipes visible. Complete catalogue starts with all seven ideas known but **no fabricated actors, fulfilled episodes, performances or learned life histories**. A separate complete-construction control may supply all methods and staffed services. The three conditions answer different questions.

Preserve the universal willing steward for services, early remote score teaching, unlimited extra actors, ordinary direct succession, full memory/method sharing by consent, and direct construction of both performances. Even a complete-catalogue player must enact the particular person's requested branching life rather than submit unrelated people as substitutes. No named recipe is required if another mechanism achieves that state.

The principal negative result would be discovering ideas that feel like compulsory menu unlocks while the actor differences are only labels, or enacting input/output tables without valuing the newly possible lives. The principal positive result would identify a particular understood possibility—branching a lived self, teaching across divergent lives, living after office, a learning house becoming a civic participant—and the capture must show it actually happened. Appreciating the shadow sequence alone is separate from appreciating personhood; report both honestly.

An expert shortcut does not falsify discovery value; seven recipe clicks do not establish it. Before implementation, independent critique must check whether these semantics remain an executable permission/programming ledger. Before recruitment, implement and challenge the whole finite scenario, freeze exact source/public assignment/protocol, publish, then run one fresh normal-progression pilot with a valid early ending. Comparison recruitment follows the prospective evaluation decision, never repeated attempts to obtain praise.
