# Next candidate hypothesis: one retained field station

26 October 2026. **Design proposal only: not implemented, frozen for play, or evidence of length/fun.** Derived from the independent Working Materials [campaign-prefix review](working-materials-play-review.md) and [early-transformation review](working-materials-early-transform-review.md). Keep the existing V2 model/UI and separate human browser checkpoint unchanged; any implementation must be a separately named candidate.

## The discriminating change

Replace a tour of reset-like construction boards with a finite project whose **actual early creations become useful inputs to later purposes**. Introduce material changes within the first project, without six familiar setup boards first. Retain inspectable laws, unlimited designs, player-selected operands, generous whole-object copying and whole-route movement. Do not make quantities, inventories, recipe secrecy or deliberate inefficiency the challenge.

Prediction: a fresh player can identify concrete earlier creations whose later changed use was worth doing, and reports useful/comfortable enactment into the later project rather than merely compliance. Contrary outcome: the retained project becomes the same checklist with longer travel, or appreciated play ends before the later purposes despite technical completion. An early satisfied ending also remains legitimate; it cannot be stretched into a full-length result.

Neither prior play demonstrates this mechanism: the two-board positive diagnostic carried **designs/knowledge**, not the same physical object between boards. The new candidate must implement actual shared state and inspectable identity/event continuity, not just narrate a common expedition.

## Finite purpose arc, not a quota of objects

Use one connected field-station site, with nearby source, viewing, working, wet-display and return anchors. Every purpose is disclosed from the beginning; chapter labels organize play rather than requiring ceremonial confirmations or hiding later rules. Permit player-chosen overlap and shortcuts. The concrete goal predicates below are a starting specification, not yet content ready to recruit on.

| Purpose | What the player creates or changes | What genuinely carries forward |
|---|---|---|
| Establish a living survey station | Bring useful light/water to a living place and make a real observation through an appropriate locally supporting boundary. Early clear/diffuse/reflecting alternatives have distinguishable functions. | Actual living assembly, working sources, a real observed instrument and its record, not a fresh “earlier expedition” record supplied later. |
| Make the station's records usable elsewhere | Produce a physical display copy of the player's actual observation for a wet public position while preserving a separate usable original/reference and an open contact copy where needed. | Earlier record IDs and actual carrier/protection; copies are legitimate, and no arbitrary intermediate recipe name is required. |
| Change the working form's job | An actual soft trial form fitted during construction becomes a durable surface serving a different site need; its shared geometry may be copied into an independently porous or lined service. | Actual impression and trial-fit snapshots on the worked original; copied geometry without invented history. Avoid requiring a useless trial merely as a password: the early fit must have an operational role. |
| Bring the completed station into a new service arrangement | Use some earlier working creation in a portable survey/return arrangement while preserving explicitly selected continuing services and delivering the actual records. | Original object/history plus genuinely useful copied replacements, not mandatory demolition/rebuilding or a second pristine board. A replacement service is needed only if the goal actually needs both services to coexist. |

These are four kinds of purpose, **not four levels multiplied into an estimated 200 actions**. The resulting game may be short. Do not prescribe a number of surfaces, copies, waypoints, fits or repeated observations just to enlarge it. Do not force the player to preserve every initial arrangement forever: distinguish what must currently work, what is a historical achievement, and what may be repurposed freely. Make the final endpoint unambiguous.

## Smallest new logical capability needed

A shared retained site with actual objects/events accessible across its purposes is the main change. Prefer reusing already reviewed local material, surface, geometry, interface, copy and history laws. Do not simultaneously add spatial packing, local hinge physics, timing pressure or an expansive chemistry catalogue; those would obscure the named hypothesis and create a much larger correctness burden.

Where a prior observation becomes a physical-copy source, check actual record identity and carrier readability. Where a fitted original changes material/use, preserve the selected material's identity and immutable events, not an unrelated root's flags. Reusing a whole object should not demand that the player dismantle it merely to count more combinations. Interfaces with earlier sources may remain intact when physically valid.

The unused-first-instance complaint has mixed causes. Actual rootbed/cart substitutions were locally legal; actual pulp could not simply replace a remote design copy because its real tool was elsewhere. Expose the distinction clearly. Do not remove locality based on one report or treat all abandoned instances as necessary gameplay. If a new candidate changes how learning/placement works, declare it as an additional independent design change and test it explicitly.

## Preplay rejection checks

Before fresh recruitment, specify complete public goal cards and exact finite endpoint, implement the candidate separately, and run source-exposed ordinary, full-catalogue, carried-design and broad-prefab controls. Audit previews, replay and public disclosure independently. In particular:

- Can an unchanged universal assembly solve every purpose with only a few placements/events? Preserve that legal answer and report its real cost; do not claim retained narrative supplies missing decisions.
- Does each later relationship actually depend on player-produced objects/history, or only a supplied identifier/renamed site?
- Can early and late goals be solved simultaneously in a much shorter order? Keep that order legal unless a real disclosed event dependency explains why it cannot occur.
- Does a reusable copy genuinely reduce setup, or do fixed-site requirements force nearly identical reconstruction? Unwanted repeated setup is the actual concern, not repeated syntax by itself.
- Do milestones have real public services/outcomes, or merely demand evidence that every authored operation was performed?

If the best-found full route is far short, describe a short candidate and identify what distinct **desired purpose** is missing before adding content. If none is justified, change the hypothesis rather than manufacture work. A large catalogue, dense object tree or narrative arc does not count as actual scale.

## Prospective empirical sequence

After independent controls and exact source/assignment publication, use the long-play protocol's A-first full-experience pilot with valid early stopping and sparse project-boundary checkpoints. Do not tell players the desired action range or construction plan. A viable candidate proceeds to the preregistered replication/opening-choice conditions; an unwanted/short/broken pilot is preserved and diagnosed, not outvoted by fresh recruits.

Seek reports of earlier useful constructions changing roles, and audit those claims against actual events. Comfortable direct reuse is a positive possible result; new dilemmas at every step are not required. Record all contrary reports, unused or abandoned objects, ending reason, known shortcuts, and actual selected intervention counts. The still-open target remains a naturally completed, replicated 180–240-action campaign with sustained reported value, not four planned chapters or several short sessions added together.
