### Actual consequences

- **Garden:** Frame + Clear Sheet establishes a reusable blueprint in the ordinary condition; complete already knows it. Clear Shell + Camp installs one fresh transmitting enclosure. The tray is enclosed and illuminated by **sunlight**, with no lamp or power component. Sunlight exits east; the northern observer neither receives it nor locates the camp.
- **Signal:** ordinary establishes Frame + Reflective Cover; complete already knows that blueprint. Reflective Shell + Camp installs one fresh opaque enclosure. Its disclosed fixed geometry redirects sunlight north, and the observer actually receives that ray and records the camp location. The tray remains enclosed but **dark**. This satisfies signal, not garden.

The observer's first-reception record occurs at signal combine 2 for ordinary and combine 1 for complete, exactly when the reflector is installed. Both current reception and retained location are true at finish. Neither player cuts the source afterward, so experienced distinction between stopped reception and retained knowledge is **unobserved**; it remains mechanical evidence from the earlier tests.

## Interpretation and limits

The simple tailored constructions remain valid; the universal lit-clear-shell solution was also available and is not grounds for adding artificial restrictions. Neither player chose it, so its use remains authored mechanical evidence rather than observed player synthesis.

For these two plays, the experience is close to selecting an answer whose functional success is already displayed. Ordinary adds two clear prerequisite joins, not observed search, repair or a multi-component inference. Positive reports concern clarity and comparison, while routine execution dominates their account of the work. This is evidence against claiming **this slice already supplies a substantial construction challenge**, not proof that every brief enactment lacks value. Removing truthful previews to manufacture difficulty would contradict the design contract rather than answer the result.

Keep the functional contrast as a bounded demonstration; further depth is unestablished. Any larger-game claim needs a different, concrete construction question, not more nouns, compulsory extra actions or an invented retention target. This review does not authorize or implement such an expansion.
