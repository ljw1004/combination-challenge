# Estuary of Continuing Lives — named-ontology candidate

27 October 2026. **Logical design contract, not implemented, independently played or validated at target length.** This candidate revises the larger architecture after the live-host review. It does not change frozen experiments or the deployed Mars game. A companion route sheet makes the whole collection and its missing laws explicit.

## One premise, not a progress ladder

An estuary community learns to make experiences portable without pretending that receiving an experience means having lived its source life. People learn from a changing place, branch into different lives, return with different practices, create a shared performance, teach a successor house, and continue a relationship with a distant school. The final community can be simple, capable and harmonious. Conflict and difficulty are not compulsory at each step.

The central new thing is an **enactable experience**: a visible sequence that can be transformed, learned and performed by actual actors. It differs from a provenance certificate because a learned practice changes the sequence an actor can actually produce in a later project. It differs from a generic assembly bag because every output has one named behavioral type and a public rendering of its actual sequence or actor transition.

The candidate is speculative fiction. It does not assert that memory copying, artificial personhood or sensation transfer are real technologies. Historical institutional inspiration remains in [the sourced guardrails](ideas-history-sources.md); no historical institution is assigned a truth score or claimed to have invented these fictional combinations. The [SMAC research](smac-tech-tree-research.md) supplies a structural inspiration—ideas may enable, synthesize or critique—not a borrowed tree or inevitable future.

## What is different from unlock → install

A combination produces a usable result immediately. `Observed Tide + Tempo Lens → Borrowed Tide` is an actual transformed score, not a permission to open a later “make experience” menu. `Divergent Relatives + Reunion Practice → Returning Family` changes the relatives' executable repertoires while retaining their distinct histories. Discovering the output also records its reusable recipe. A separate global “enable concept” click is not required.

Some recipes construct **methods** rather than particular performances. A method must already be a callable transformation with visible examples and consequences, not an empty unlock flag. Its first construction may be comfortable learning. Known methods can be reused without rebuilding their ancestors. A whole method, score or institution can be one operand where it has a specific meaning; it must not silently stand for an arbitrary sack of unrelated things.

## Minimal shared behavior substrate

The proposed common representation is deliberately narrower than a society or ecology simulator. The [independent challenge](ideas-history-estuary-independent-challenge.md) improves it: underlying episodes should retain actual continuous signals and interface functions, not just strings of event names. The companion route sheet instantiates this with following, rescaling and invertible sensor/actuator maps:

1. **An episode is an actual event sequence or piecewise-linear signal trajectory.** Each event has a time, source actor/process, channel, value and optional causal parent. The world distinguishes actual events from hypothetical scores. An archive copy never manufactures a second event.
2. **A score is an enactable sequence.** It contains relative times, channels, actions and named roles. A score preview displays the sequence and which actors can enact or receive it. A performed score produces actual events; it does not merely attach a “performed” label.
3. **A method transforms a score.** Proposed operations are temporal rescaling, channel translation, relative-coordinate translation, ordered composition and simultaneous composition. Their functions and preconditions are public. A translated replay and a live binding of the same map are different behaviors; genuinely lossy maps cannot be inverted by a label. All legal results are inspectable; no hidden target program is inferred from applause.
4. **An actor has a continuing history and executable repertoire.** Learning adds an actual score/method it can perform. Receiving another's episode does not add that episode to its own lived history. Actors may teach, perform, travel, fork or consensually merge according to disclosed rules. No inherited authority follows merely from copied memory.
5. **An ensemble is a specific role-bearing performance.** It is not a generic group container. Two roles may require distinct concurrent bodies because the declared performance actually has simultaneous parts. A single actor with two suitable bodies can be a legal alternative if that embodiment is modeled; do not reject it with a person-count password.
6. **A continuing role routes concrete requests.** Succession changes who receives the next request, while former holders keep their repertoire and actual history. A civic mandate only matters where it changes an actual response; it is not an automatic higher stage of intelligence.
7. **Messages have actual delivery state.** A distant actor cannot learn a newly created score before delivery. A whole activity can advance the declared schedule; no repeated “wait” selections are introduced to inflate length.

The substrate does not yet exist as one engine. Existing small kernels supply only partial precedents for episodes, learning, forks, temporal methods and validated physical replay. Symbolic scores are not proof of rendered sensation, broad intelligence, ecological realism or human fun.

## Concrete public starting material

- **Mara**, a consenting resident with a simple `Three-Step Walk` and an interest in maintaining the local school while exploring the distant inlet.
- **The Watchhouse**, an existing observation place with two public processes: `Beacon Sweep` (three ordered light directions) and `Long Tide` (rise, dwell, fall, dwell over a longer interval). These are explicit fictional event generators, not hidden natural laws or an ecological model.
- **Ilen**, a willing tactile performer; **Sori**, a willing visual performer. Their explicit channel interfaces, not assumptions about real disabilities, determine which scores they can receive and enact.
- **A blank learning host** with a declared input/output interface. It is not already a person or universally capable operator because its label says “AI.”
- **The Local School** and **Distant School**, whose own activities and incoming messages remain distinct. Neither is morally ranked or forced to adopt the other's local constitution.
- Public basic concepts: order, interval, repetition, simultaneous parts, perspective, embodied gesture, channel correspondence, controlled variation, reciprocal teaching, continuing memory, independent consent, copyable minds, role succession, delivery and bounded cooperation.

All starting interfaces and process timelines are inspectable. Full information is compatible with the pleasure of discovering and using understandable combinations. An ordinary willing steward remains a valid solution to ordinary service, but does not by itself enact Mara's explicitly requested divergent life or a recipient's learned new practice.

## Named catalogue: results with behavior

The names below denote understandable things, not completion checks. They are candidate authored recipes; alternate meaningful parents can be offered when they yield the same behavior. The route sheet uses a connected subset. No requirement says every node must be discovered or applied.

### A. Time and perspective become manipulable

| Parents | Named result | Consequence now, not a later unlock |
|---|---|---|
| Interval + repetition | **Pulse Loop** | A reusable periodic score with actual intervals and events. |
| Ordered observations + controlled variation | **Contrast Window** | A method that places two declared response sequences on a comparable interval without inventing observations. |
| Intervention + release sequence | **Release Experiment** | An actual stimulus/readout method that exposes the response after a control ends. |
| Temporal scale + order preservation | **Tempo Lens** | A callable rescaling method; ordered events and declared simultaneities remain ordered/simultaneous. |
| Perspective + relative displacement | **Journey Grammar** | A path method expressed relative to the performer rather than an absolute place address. |
| Observed Long Tide + Tempo Lens | **Borrowed Tide** | A shorter enacted sequence retaining rise/dwell/fall relations, not a claim that time itself accelerated. |
| Observed Beacon Sweep + Journey Grammar | **Lighthouse Dance** | An actual relative movement score derived from the sweep's directions. |
| Contrast Window + Release Experiment | **Causal Portrait** | A composable inquiry whose forecasts expose a declared response distinction. Optional here; not a prerequisite for all art. |

### B. An experience becomes available to another kind of participant

| Parents | Named result | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Channel correspondence + embodied gesture | **Sensory Loom** | A public event-to-gesture translation method with preserved distinctions and an explicit receiver interface. |
| Borrowed Tide + Sensory Loom | **Tidal Touch** | A tactile score that Ilen can receive and perform; not merely a caption on visual events. |
| Lighthouse Dance + Sensory Loom | **Beacon Duet Score** | A score with linked visual and tactile roles, retaining the shared direction sequence. |
| Actual episode + perspective | **Situated Account** | A shareable account anchored to a source episode; it is not a new lived episode for its recipients. No separate stamp is required if the episode already provides this information. |
| Tidal Touch + reciprocal teaching | **Tide Lesson** | A teaching practice that enables a willing learner to reproduce the tactile sequence. |
| Beacon Duet Score + willing performers | **Shared Lighthouse** | An actual ensemble with suitable assigned performers and a defined public performance. No generic pile of people or scores. |
| Causal Portrait + enacted alternatives | **Counterfactual Theatre** | A performance that enacts actual and hypothetical process alternatives distinctly. Optional; a label alone cannot make an alternative causal. |

### C. Continuity does not mean sameness

| Parents | Named result | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Continuing person + copyable minds/independent consent | **Fork Kinship** | A consenting person becomes a family of continuing independent actors with the same past and different possible next actions. The first enactment reveals the reusable kinship recipe. |
| Fork Kinship family + different voluntary projects | **Divergent Itineraries** | The relatives actually undertake different sequences and acquire different episodes/repertoires. Their names alone do not make lives distinct. |
| Selected accounts + reciprocal teaching | **Reunion Practice** | A method that transfers selected usable practices while marking taught rather than lived source experience. |
| Divergent Itineraries + Reunion Practice | **Returning Family** | Each continuing relative can enact a new practice learned from the other without erasing either itinerary. |
| Shared performance + independent commitments | **Plural Chorus** | A defined shared event can proceed while participants retain different next projects and distinct choices. Not extra votes for extra copies. |
| Returning Family + consensual integration | **Joined Continuity** | A legal alternative combined life with retained accounts and explicitly defined future agency. It is not automatically the same endpoint as two continuing lives. |
| Enduring experience + role succession | **Living Alumni** | Former officeholders remain active practitioners while new people receive the role's future requests. No death or forced amnesia is required. |

### D. Teaching can become reciprocal and outlive its founder

| Parents | Named result | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Demonstrated practice + learning host | **Apprentice House** | The actual host can replay that permitted practice through its real interface. No automatic physical construction or universal ability is fabricated. |
| Apprentice House + new observed event | **House Recollection** | The host records a genuinely subsequent event and can offer its learned account; it does not merely repeat the founder's original input. |
| House Recollection + reciprocal teaching | **House Lesson** | The host can teach a compatible practice based on that new event to a willing returning person. Exact translation rules, not a “wisdom” score, determine its content. |
| Local School + Living Alumni | **Continuing School** | Actual request routing transfers to a successor while the former holder can return to learn or contribute. |
| Apprentice House + resident mandate | **Civic House** | Actual request handling follows the disclosed mandate, including any out-of-scope refusal. Optional unless this community chooses that behavior for a concrete purpose. |
| Continuing School + House Lesson | **Reciprocal School** | A founder or former teacher actually learns something the successor encountered after taking over. This is a new capability/history relation, not an installation badge. |

### E. Different communities can share a moment without becoming one

| Parents | Named result | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Score + declared delivery | **Travelling Performance** | A specific message carries that score and becomes usable only on actual receipt. |
| Shared event + voluntary future commitments | **Rendezvous Practice** | A concrete agreed schedule makes participants enact the same event at the disclosed epoch. No oracle is implied. |
| Bounded cooperation + plural local standards | **Partial Treaty** | A specific shared project is executable while local activities and decisions remain separate. A whole sensible treaty is legal. |
| Partial Treaty + actual delayed communication | **Asynchronous School** | Local work continues while new messages travel; incoming knowledge is not treated as already present. |
| Living Alumni + Asynchronous School | **Epoch Concordat** | Role-addressed cooperation continues through a real succession and delivery; personal invitations still do not silently transfer. |
| Travelling Performance + Rendezvous Practice | **Estuary Festival** | An actual distributed shared event occurs while participants keep distinct local lives. No final philosophical regime is compulsory. |

This is a candidate vocabulary, not a count of guaranteed interesting discoveries. Some rows are alternative views of one object or automatically derived facts and should merge rather than become extra buttons. Named output deduplication must not erase different actual episodes, actors or bindings; copying an abstract score must not duplicate lived history.

## Whole collection: the requested possibilities

The finite endpoint has six projects, with concrete subconditions rather than named-recipe passwords:

1. **A long process becomes a short understandable experience.** An actor enacts an interval-rescaled version of the observed tide while preserving its public ordered/distinct parts. Another actor receives it through a different specified interface.
2. **One past continues as genuinely different lives.** Two willing continuations of Mara's experienced past actually undertake different voluntary projects, remain continuing actors, and later each use a practice learned from the other.
3. **Different perspectives become a shared performance.** Two compatible participants enact linked roles derived from the actual sweep and tide while maintaining the stated simultaneity/order relation. The particular names `Shared Lighthouse` or `Plural Chorus` are not goal predicates.
4. **The former teacher can learn from a successor.** A host or other willing successor takes over the school, actually encounters a subsequent event, and teaches a corresponding new usable practice to the still-living former teacher. The founder's original method alone cannot satisfy that genuinely later episode.
5. **A shared project crosses distance without erasing local life.** The distant school receives a newly made score through actual delivery, enacts the agreed shared event, and continues its distinct local activity.
6. **Cooperation survives a real change of officeholder.** A role-addressed later event reaches the actual successor and is enacted, while a personal project remains with the former participant. Do not add an extra signature ceremony if ordinary role routing already achieves this.

These are desired fictional lives, not a claim that every society should want them. A single ordinary steward can solve school service but cannot by itself supply two lived continuations of one past; a simple universal translator may solve all interface tasks and must remain legal. A complete end state can be straightforward and satisfying.

## Rules still needed before any implementation claim

The companion route sheet identifies exact missing semantics. Most importantly: what does an actor's “learned score” enable in another context, how are relational paths instantiated lawfully, how is new host experience generated, and how do direct literal authoring and a universal translator compare with reusable methods? Without these, the catalogue can look rich while every output is just a renamed history field.

No next pilot should test only one row. A future implementation must declare and witness the whole connected collection, then be challenged against whole-method, manual, direct-authored, static and complete-catalogue alternatives. The current source-exposed controls and single 44-selection live-host play remain separate historical evidence. The ledger was **44 selected interventions plus one automatic physical transition**, never 45 player choices or an automatic step included in 44.
