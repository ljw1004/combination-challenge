# Working Materials V2: complete content and exposure contract

26 October 2026. **Authored implementation plan and predicate audit, not player evidence, a shortest-path claim, or a fun finding.** This document accompanies the new `working-materials-*` modules. The six-map Workshop V1, its captures, frozen proposal and protocol remain unchanged.

## Hypothesis and limits

The [length diagnosis](workshop-length-diagnosis.md) found that V1's useful assemblies and actual records yielded a good short-route hypothesis, not the proposed campaign scale. Working Materials retains those purposes and adds local material transformation, a small public form profile, and actual impression/copy events. These are potential things worth making and changing, not a tax on the old greenhouse. A film greenhouse still works wherever film satisfies the goal.

The complete collection has **30 cards on 10 maps**: the original twelve purposes, four optics purposes, five form purposes, five archive purposes, and four integrated-outpost purposes. This count is content coverage only. Shared constructions can finish several cards, and an accepted combine is counted once. No duration or intervention total follows from this matrix; the executed ordinary, complete-catalogue and whole-assembly witnesses must replace estimates. If the collection is short, that result remains legal and reportable.

The first four discriminating constructions are part of this collection, not a separate claimed campaign:

1. `optics/supporting-window`: one local surface is rigid and clear while actually enclosing the retained camera's lens and plate.
2. `forms/soft-trial`, `fired-keeper`, and `open-and-lined`: an actual soft-fitted impression survives hardening, while simultaneous later services distinguish an open porous surface from a lined one.
3. `optics/diffuse-growth` alongside `supporting-window`: diffuse light is useful for real growth but not a faithful camera path. The observation operation itself must enforce that distinction.
4. `archive/weatherproof-copy` and `open-copy`: two genuine physical copies serve incompatible water/contact roles without moving the protected source original.

The two clear-view discriminator goals explicitly require `throughSurface: true`: the observation’s immutable view-surface evidence must name the same actual currently qualifying boundary and record the matching traits/profile at capture. Merely observing before adding a boundary cannot satisfy that claim. Other historical-record goals intentionally do not impose this requirement. The engine’s observe operation, rather than prose, establishes the clear-versus-diffuse path.

## Public completion semantics

Every card declares exposure rows. These are simultaneous fixed tests, not weather turns or waits. A current service must remain true with the other current services at the campaign endpoint. Three explicitly labeled lifecycle achievements remain earned after a legitimate later change:

- `optics/supporting-window`: the initial instrument is used at its first survey position, then travels to the wet station.
- `forms/soft-trial`: a real impression is fitted while moldable before hardening.
- `forms/fired-keeper`: that actual previously fitted form reaches its porous rigid stage at the drain, before the original is returned.

Every card still reports current truth and first achievement separately. No action quota, required recipe list, universal all-parts prerequisite, or forced optional branch is present.

## Deliberate nonclaims and review targets

- There is no claim of eighteen mechanically unrelated puzzles. Optics distinguishes local surface effects; forms develops actual process history; archive contrasts record/contact/exposure; the last map integrates and reuses them. Familiar actions are allowed to be comfortable, neutral or unwanted; only play reports can establish which occurred.
- Profiles are small public tuples, not a concealed place-name password. Edges and face are visibly compared by fit. Material result names must never occur in a goal predicate.
- Transform provenance may explain an input, but never confers its electrical, optical, water or historical capabilities on the output. The same local surface must satisfy combined traits.
- Inexhaustible design copies keep structure/profile but not actual observation, copy, impression, fit, trip or achievement events. Actual transformed surfaces preserve allowed prior events. Review must probe both halves.
- A saved complete construction may satisfy several current cards cheaply. That is permitted, and all executed witness counts must retain the saving.
- `encloses`, active-interface and exposure conditions need adversarial tests: a decorative surface, unlit reflector, source-free water loop, outside recording sheet or wet temporary copy must not claim the corresponding function.
- The full collection can still collapse into a short goal-insensitive method or label checklist. A completed witness is feasibility, not a rebuttal. If that happens, report the actual short route and revise the next hypothesis rather than prescribing more transforms.

Mechanical transition correctness, complete witnesses, source/property cuts, immutable provenance, replay, preview and independent review are owned by the engine/screen work and must be completed before fresh play.
