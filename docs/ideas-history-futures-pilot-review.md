## Mechanical limitation and shortcut challenge

The preplay review's core finding survives independent verification: **90 reachable institutional configurations, 832 accepted transitions, 2,138 refused/redundant contacts**, using a fully materialized shelf constructed in 17 combines. Every configuration is repaired with at most three accepted adoptions: reunion, individual-personal authority and joint-garden authority. This is not a three-combine fresh-state solution. The player's six-combine route exploits the already-valid common garden default and legitimately needs no explicit garden covenant. It matches a known developer witness but was not supplied as an instruction. It is not proven optimal here.

Scope assignments have real minimal logical consequences: task/member changes, explicit refusals, actual memory records and revision eligibility. They are more than doctrine collection. But `settle` recomputes tasks and revision from current settings; the accepted plan does not modify irrigation, a garden process, the promise, or later possibilities. Reversing authority can instantly reverse these apparent acts. Memory content supplies a fixed two-record prerequisite, not different memories with distinct usable powers. Garden access is invariant under lawful actions. Charter acceptance is a disclosed fixture assumption, not negotiation or preference discovery.

The universal repair therefore limits history-sensitive construction and extended-play claims, without invalidating the reported value of this bounded configuration. Multiplying family targets would not address it. Nor should the short route be prohibited, preview hidden, a minimum move quota added, or accepted preparation moves removed from the count to make the result fit a story.

## What remains untested, and the next discriminating experiment

**Untested by this player:** kinship adoption, explicit joint-garden covenant, all-scope replacement/release, refusal on Lio care, willing succession to Ibis, merger and reopening, recovery from mistakes, another goal using a retained capability, extended pacing, elective appetite, replication and human experience. Mechanics screens cover many of those transitions, but do not supply their experiential value. More optional branches do not establish missing scale. No approximately-200 claim was made or met by this short diagnostic.

**Keep:** public inspectable consequences, inexhaustible inputs, player-selected operands, legal alternatives/short endings, and the distinction between sharing experience and transferring authority. **Change direction:** do not spend another cohort merely shortening names or adding family tables in hope of stronger praise. A narrow future disclosure repair could be worthwhile if this toy is reused, but would test readability, not solve its substantive limitation.

For **Unbuilt Inheritance**, freeze a compact logical prototype only after mechanics show (1) a home already doing something useful now; (2) two materially different reachable future transformations, with public construction witnesses; (3) combinations changing structure/process and future reachability, not only permission flags; and (4) a retained creation that actually matters when a later purpose is pursued. Challenge “apply every reversible safeguard everywhere” and a universal reset/repair before recruiting. Preserve any short solution. Ask one fresh player what present function and retained future possibility were worth making, without demanding dilemmas or wonder. The falsifier is another certificate checklist or two named endpoints with identical mechanics. If it passes, a small sequence of distinct purposes could test transfer and pacing; multiplying it toward 200 before that evidence would be unjustified.

Overall profile: **constraint fit good; actual scale six; specific appreciation plus setup/exposition criticism; limited within-state reuse; replay/hash integrity strong with a follow-up logging caveat; permission-ledger counterexplanation remains.**
