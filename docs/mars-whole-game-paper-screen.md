# Mars whole-game paper screen: one explicit service network

26 October 2026. **Design-only exact screen, not a new engine, executable physics proof, player or enjoyment result.** This instantiates the preceding synthesis narrowly enough to expose its cheapest obvious answer. The numerical workload budget is not used as evidence. The delivered Mars game and all research sources remain unchanged.

## Scope and question

Can the proposed survival/contact/work arc create varied useful construction while keeping unlimited known designs, remote construction, replacement, open space and comfortable progress? The screen must also ask whether the arc currently supports a roughly-200 claim. These questions have different answers: a simple capable camp may be satisfying even if its entire construction is short.

This version deliberately has **no obligatory specimen/archive loop, no learned host, no compulsory vehicle, no material stocks and no wait actions**. A person can travel to any connected supported area in one action; travel is optional here because the endpoint is a working installation, not an arbitrary visit stamp. Consequently this screen can falsify a mandatory mobility phase rather than smuggle one in.

## Exact game laws and available concepts

These are proposed paper laws, not a claim that one existing engine already implements their combination.

The one-cell supported beacon retains its emitter's one-cell body; its frame is zero-thickness. Likewise a panel's supports do not erase body extents. A relay's two bodies occupy two cells. Roofs are actual thin canopies; sharing their two-cell sheltered footprint is legal. No arbitrary union of large objects becomes a one-cell design. A saved design copy preserves actual relative geometry and internal links, clears external links, and has no invented history. There are no history goals in this screen.

### Source and useful service

Power/water interfaces are abstract nonblocking connections selected between actual compatible endpoints, as in earlier material games; no tile cable or pipe placement. Unlimited branching is legal. A water connection requires actual source output, not merely the name of a source. A powered heater produces warmth; a heater face adjacent to the fixed ice body supplies the ice contact. The separator needs its own actual power input. An operating heater warms useful parts in its actually joined assembly, not arbitrary remote objects. Multiple useful parts in one assembly can share it; no hidden demand budget.

A growing bed requires clean water, warmth, light and clear overhead dust protection in all four disclosed rows. Its clear canopy transmits sky light; where terrain blocks a row's sky, a lawful alternative location is allowed. A dry productive workbench requires overhead dust protection and warmth in all rows; it does not require water, an original ID, or manufactured output tallies. Open sides are allowed: these are dustfall/overhead-protection services, not airtight pressure vessels. There is no oxygen model.

### Live contact

Every signal body has four horizontal surface ports at its face centers. A powered origin beacon supplies a live signal. A powered relay emitter supplies one only when its own receiver is actually reached from an origin through powered relays. Cycles without an origin do not generate a signal. A ray joins aligned exposed ports cardinally and is blocked by any intervening opaque body/wall; selected endpoint bodies do not block their own ports. Clear boundaries transmit. Relay constituents communicate internally through their actual joining contact, not through another free-space ray.

Fixed receiver points accept an aligned unobstructed ray at z0.5. This is **one-way live contact**, not bidirectional conversation or a stored image. One emitter can reach several aligned receivers in different directions. There is no range, bandwidth, fuel or battery budget. The endpoint does not require a particular relay count or route.

## Whole endpoint

Together, in every sky/dust row:

- At least one protected growing bed receives actual clean water and warmth.
- At least one protected dry workbench is warm.
- Both fixed receivers currently receive a source-grounded powered signal.

The ice/clean-water chain must genuinely operate because it supplies the bed, not because a separate “used ice” stamp was earned. All relevant laws and the full endpoint are visible from the beginning. A different water source is not secretly available in this finite sheet. Any design/position/connection satisfying the laws is allowed; the following route is not assigned to a player.

### A real flaw in this first route, and its lawful repair

An initial candidate placement of the garden at `(0,2)` does **not** receive east-row sky light: its eastward ray meets the rock wall atx3.5. This cannot be waved away by the clear roof. The finite sheet has no lamp recipe; do not silently add one. A direct lawful repair is to place the garden and roof above the wall from the start. The final table above replaces candidate rows17–18 with `home-roof` and `garden` at `(0,6)`; their east/west rays avoid both wall and the bodies at7,6/8,6/9,6/10,6 only if height/line do not align—here they **do** align, so `(0,7)` is the valid replacement. Its row7 is clear in both directions. All service links remain abstract nonblocking, so row19 stays valid; rows20–22 explicitly place/join/power the local heater at the repaired location.

The final table already uses the repaired positions. The repaired ordinary witness therefore has **33 selected interventions**, not35: it is an alternate prospectively selected placement, not a played mistake. The invalid candidate is preserved to make the reasoning inspectable. Warmth is local to actual joined heater assemblies: the paper route does not invent a remote thermal cable. The workshop does not require sky light, so its shadow is irrelevant to its actual dry/warm function. Its final position at9,2 instead of the convenient8,2 pad leaves the x8 live-signal path clear. Roof supports and panels remain disjoint from the solar array atrow8 and the communication paths atrow4.

## What this screen actually finds

- The complete-catalogue witness is the same actual construction without the ten learning selections: **23 interventions**. This is a paper upper bound, not an executable verification or optimum.
- A saved rich design may compress it further. No counted saved route is claimed yet; it requires an exact definition of internal joins/links and whole-copy geometry.
- A universal powered network, shared warmth and a garden outside the shadow legitimately solve the full endpoint. Mobility is optional and adds no necessary work. Calling it a long survival→mobility campaign would therefore be unsupported.
- Ordinary discovery adds ten real concept selections; they may be welcome clear progress. Nothing here proves they are boring, nor that a first-time player would find the spatial layout obvious. There is **no player report**.
- This concrete minimal interpretation does **not** support the earlier145–210 workload budget. It collapses before any target-scale implementation. Do not respond by requiring multiple gardens, relay hops, original cargo, finite power, repeated operations or expensive pipe tiles.

## Decision before implementation

Preserve this as a coherent **short network/base-construction option**, with new live contact serving a different purpose from archive repetition. It is not the requested200-action campaign, and it is not yet a mechanically verified short game. Independent paper review must check every ray/footprint/source and try even shorter ordinary/static/remote answers.

The broad synthesis could still earn a larger full game through a substantially richer useful ontology and genuinely different purposes, but this screen gives no license to add arbitrary recipes or obligations. Its most important result is that the unbounded abstract supply interfaces and globally available placement make large apparent geography very cheap. Whether those abstractions should change depends on an independently valuable activity, not on the desired count. Keep the owner's permission for comfortable discovery and satisfying universal creations intact.
