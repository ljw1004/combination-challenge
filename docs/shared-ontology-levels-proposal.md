# One ontology, different things worth making

**Proposal after the owner's 26 October clarification; no implementation or frozen-model changes.**

## Implication

The owner proposes ontology-heavy rather than narrative-heavy levels: a substantial reusable combination tree, with different goals in its shared possibility space. The final dictated phrase is treated as “simply has a different goal,” not a requirement for four levels or a particular simulation.

**Explicit level goals are legitimate.** A level can end well; the next can supply another purpose. The overall 180–240-opportunity aspiration is not a requirement that each level spontaneously sustain itself. Previous short endings remain evidence of their tested worlds, not a rejection of this different structure.

A visible catalogue can support informed exploration: which branches help here, and how should their creations be composed? Eventually building everything is not automatically a dominant shortcut. Having every capability available is allowed; the challenge may then move to using it. Individual combinations may be satisfying execution of one larger insight, not separate strategic decisions.

## Shared ontology: Living Works

**Next executable slice: 24 fully implemented concepts**, shared across three short goal boards. Six initial capabilities and eighteen derived concepts—not a purported huge final tree. A later 60–80-concept graph is a content hypothesis to earn through useful branches, not a target to fill with synonyms.

Every recipe, prerequisite, material condition and result is inspectable, including unbuilt descendants. The same recipe always means the same thing. No narrative chapters or hidden location recipes carry progression.

Starting capabilities are **Earth, Water, Air, Heat, Seed, Memory**. These are invented game recipes, not chemistry claims.

Shared behavior is concrete: channels route water and marks; roots soften water and fragment exact marks; fired forms retain exact arrivals but cannot grow; elevated aqueducts separate flow from a walking surface. Floats respond to local water, valves open actual outlets, and delays come from traversing the constructed route. Archives preserve actual arrivals, beacons transmit their records, and interpreters pass fragments. A feedback valve supplies connected components and ports—not a command that automatically produces the requested watering pattern.

The player physically combines inputs, then combines resulting creations with places or other creations. Known concepts remain inexhaustibly available **across levels**; no repeated manufacturing tax rebuilds an already learned Aqueduct. Boards reset world state, not acquired ontology. There are no material stocks, fuel collection or recipe budgets.

## Three goal contexts using those same things

### 1. Passage without stopping the river

**Goal:** connect two inhabited banks by an unflooded walking route while retaining flowing water through the valley and its living grove.

**Choice:** a rooted low crossing supplies passage and living growth but fragments the marks continuing downstream. An elevated ceramic route preserves exact transmission and separates walking from flow, but cannot itself root. A gate offers alternating rather than permanent passage.

**Causal example:** rooting before the downstream imprint is fired loses its exact arriving identity; capture first preserves it. This board asks for material choice and spatial arrangement. Filling or blocking every route fails the explicitly retained river, not an arbitrary resource budget.

### 2. Alternating rather than simultaneous watering

**Goal:** make two groves receive alternating wet phases, without permanent flooding or repeated manual switching. The disclosed source repeats forever; there is no deadline.

**Choice:** the first board's “connect both” solution wets both together. Use a float-valve to respond to actual arrivals, and determine which outlet feeds a return path. Route length, feedback connection and valve state determine the sequence.

**Causal example:** the same aqueduct can bypass the valve for immediate supply or enter its return path as a delay. Adding a shortcut can make the return arrive too early and destroy alternation. This board asks for temporal coordination and process construction, not another spatial coverage target. Once the process works, the level ends.

### 3. A shared signal, distinct originals

**Goal:** keep exact A/B originals at their separate homes; deliver a combined recognizable signal to the square without exact copies entering the other home; retain a walkable living connection.

**Choice:** joining everything spreads exact copies back into both homes. Rooting everything before recording can prevent exact archives from forming. Capture first, then put living interpreters on the outgoing routes; alternatively separate stable record-carrying routes from living public routes.

**Causal example:** an Archive → Interpreter branch retains A locally while sending its fragment onward. Move that interpreter upstream of the still-unmade archive and A is never captured. Gates/delays from board two can separate transmission phases, but only if actual storage/transmission laws satisfy the goal. This asks for information-preserving transformation, not a supplied privacy story.

## Concrete next test

Freeze the 24-node graph, shared laws and three boards. First implement two executable witnesses per board and compare their consequences; then run fresh public-only players through the explicit sequence, with prior concepts retained. Use another order where feasible, and a catalogue-already-built control to distinguish ontology navigation from later construction skill.

Record actual selected branches, new inference, useful transfer, appreciated execution, unwanted reconstruction and whether the **next offered level** is wanted. Compare purposeful construction with indiscriminate catalogue-building using actual action traces; do not reject the latter merely because it eventually succeeds.

**Falsifier:** knowing the catalogue reduces all three boards to the same short universal arrangement, or dependency clicking merely delays an unrelated puzzle. Change the ontology–goal relationship, not its prose or size. Conversely, different goal-directed selections and useful reuse justify a finite level sequence even when every level has a satisfying ending.
