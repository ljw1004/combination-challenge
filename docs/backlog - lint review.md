## Decisions

- Reviewed all **64 baseline warnings**: 21 goal-density and 43 mixed-fate cases. Retain original goal presentation and Kitchen fates. Adopt only the bounded Elements Ink change: preserve the writing/printing/drawing medium consistently. This is a design judgment supported by a reported model surprise, not measured human-fun improvement.
- Static uniformity is not observed consistency: continuation protection can retain a nominally consumed/transformed item. The proposed final Sponge transform made learned reuse less consistent; several preserve variants blurred upgraded-object identity.
- **183 recorded guided scenes across 73 source-isolated participants; 183 replay/hash verified.** These are 64 cases, not 183 independent experiments. Participants could play several scenes within one condition. Eight interface-confounded density originals were retained and replaced; two wrong-branch crawler probes were retained but excluded as target evidence.
- Lamp and corrected Recovered crawler encountered only one baseline declared fate category; their within-baseline role contrast remains partial. All 43 have actual targeted use, but not exhaustive branch coverage. The 27 mixed-fate comparisons comprise 26 uniform-preserve probes and the distinct Sponge-transform comparison; other 16 cases have baseline diagnostics and explicit comparison limits.

## Evidence boundaries

Engine/manuscript hashes and wrapper epochs are separate. Both main density arms use the same corrected conjunctive-next runtime; candidate disclosure preserves all goal obligations. The logical interface exposes all compatible partners and all-history items, unlike the browser’s per-item hints/current panel. It cannot establish visual burden, touch quality, human timing or human enjoyment. Focal names and checkpoints were supplied; unplayed history is not earned experience. Command-schema errors, waits and behind-card actions are not story-pacing evidence. See interface provenance and session-qualified citation corrections.
