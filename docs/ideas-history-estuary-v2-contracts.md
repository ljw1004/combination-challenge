# Estuary v2: consequences, not completion passwords

Separate version after the voluntarily finished 22-gesture v1 session. Original substrate, binary adapter, wrapper and raw capture are immutable. The v2 wrapper uses that same lawful binary action engine but evaluates new public contracts in a separate module. No retroactive change to the original three-of-six result is permitted. Re-evaluation of old actions is a source-exposed diagnostic, never new player success.

## Hypothesis and scope

The old session valued lived ancestry and divergent relatives, then stopped amid opaque goals and cumbersome identifiers/state dumps. This version tests whether honest observable purposes and concise feedback remove avoidable friction. It does **not** assume that fixing flags makes the collection fun, extends its interesting life, or supplies 200 decisions. No added chores, resource depletion, action quotas, compulsory visits or confirmation rituals.

The three unchanged purposes remain concrete and disclosed: tactile shortened Long Tide from someone else's actual Tide; diverged relatives who replay each other's actual experiences; an actual Sweep jointly expressed through angle and pressure. Their original causal predicates remain in use. The following change explicitly:

- **Reverse transmission after handover:** successor has a firsthand occurrence after succession; former holder later actually enacts that source experience. Any site's trajectory qualifies. Knowing its trajectory earlier does not invalidate the new occurrence/provenance. This is not necessarily learning a new skill. An actual source-derived replay counts without requiring a separate lesson command or an internal novelty bit.
- **Remote repertoire:** a locally shared score is sent, received, and actually enacted by a remote person (including an unchanged source-derived rendition through an invited output channel). Prior score capabilities remain available automatically: retained baseline, earlier personally enacted scores and earlier received scores. No redundant replay is required to prove what the engine already guarantees. This goal tests transmission without replacement, not a challenge to juggle inventories or demonstrate a particular prior trajectory.
- **Role continuity:** an invitation sent before succession arrives afterward to that successor; the successor actually enacts its score, and the former holder continues personally after handover. Solo and joint performances count, as does an unchanged source-derived rendition through an invited output channel. Equal angle motion cannot stand in for an invitation requiring pressure. A private message tag, named festival branch, second invitation, or specific lettered trajectory is not required. This is continuity of routed invitation and activity, not a claim that successor enactment was caused solely by delivery or that a joint festival happened.
