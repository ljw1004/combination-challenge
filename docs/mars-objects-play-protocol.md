# Mars Objects 1 — prospective bounded protocol

26 October 2026. Doctrine 8. **No blind player evidence yet.** Original toy construction, not a film reconstruction or real Mars survival advice. No UI, inventory quantities, costs, time pressure, chemistry or hidden recipes.

## Question and scope

Can familiar objects support understandable, purposeful synthesis across a garden/shelter goal and a signaling goal? Measure functional understanding, recognized reuse, valued completion and prerequisite burden. **Do not require lasting strategic tension, a new skill on every board, or voluntary continuation.** A short construction can succeed as an experience; model correctness alone cannot establish that.

Use six starting concepts: frame, clear sheet, reflective cover, solar panel, cable, lamp. Six composites make a representative twelve-concept ontology, not a final content target. Objects are inexhaustible capabilities. Concept combinations establish **structured blueprints**, and installing a known blueprint makes fresh component instances; it does not transfer the identity of an earlier installation. Each installation has separate identity and retains its actual component structure. Targeting an installed lead/shell/panel acts on that particular existing object. Arbitrarily many copies may be installed; there is no attachment quota. Extra lamps are binary redundant illumination, not counted power demands.

## Exact small world

Both boards contain the same designated camp, a tray at its interior, sunlight traveling **west to east**, and a northern observer. All construction happens around that camp; this is a whole-object functional slice, not a grid, spatial reach challenge, or assertion that other locations are impossible. Making an empty enclosure elsewhere is outside this slice rather than a prohibited competing solution. There is no movement, weather, day/night or pulse protocol.

An installed shell encloses the camp. Multiple shells are nested layers: every layer must transmit for light to pass between interior and exterior. Clear layers transmit; any reflective layer blocks transmission and redirects incoming east-going sunlight north. This fixed reflecting shape/orientation is part of the frame's disclosed toy geometry, **not a general claim about reflective blankets**. An uncovered camp admits sunlight but is not enclosed. Enclosure and illumination are the only modeled garden requirements; air, temperature, pressure and biology are not simulated.

A solar lead contains an actual exterior solar panel and cable. Installing a lamp on that lead makes the lamp illuminate the interior whenever that specific panel is lit. The cable has a built-in sealed fitting; no separately placed seams are needed. The panel stays outside all camp shells; its own transparent/reflective covers determine its exposure. Power is binary, unlimited and cannot be stored. An interior lamp emits north/east/south/west through transmitting shells, or into all directions if no shell exists. A reflective shell blocks its outward light. The model retains each panel/cable/lamp and each shell, rather than assigning a generic success property to the composite's name.

The observer receives northward light and then retains the fact that it located this camp. It does not forget when a source later goes dark. Its **current reception** and **retained location knowledge** are separate. This is an explicitly simplified visibility response, not a code puzzle or a physically realistic remote sensing simulation. Observer updates run on both boards, independent of their goal.

Goals:
- `garden`: the designated tray is enclosed and currently illuminated by a real source.
- `signal`: the observer has actually received camp light at least once.

Garden success can cease after a destructive change. Signal knowledge is persistent. Further combinations remain legal after success.

## Entire public combination grammar

Concept pairs are unordered. Parents remain known.

| Actual ingredients | Reusable result |
| --- | --- |
| Frame + clear sheet | Clear shell |
| Frame + reflective cover | Reflective shell |
| Solar panel + cable | Solar lead, with no lamp yet |
| Solar lead + lamp | Solar lamp, retaining panel, cable and lamp |
| Clear shell + solar lamp | Lit clear shell, retaining both structures |
| Reflective shell + solar lamp | Lit reflective shell, retaining both structures |

Any known composite + `world:camp` installs a fresh copy around/at the actual camp. Installing a lit shell preserves the blueprint's constituent structure, not IDs of earlier physical objects. It does not delegate an unknown construction. Known concepts can subsequently be reused without reconstructing prerequisites. In the complete-catalogue condition, **lit clear shell + camp can solve either board in one combine**. That is known whole-object placement, not evidence of synthesizing its internal construction. Do not conceal this baseline or add difficulty to prevent it.

Additional physical combinations, all disclosed:
- Lamp + an installed lead with no lamp connects a lamp to that actual lead and also establishes the reusable solar-lamp concept.
- Clear sheet or reflective cover + an installed shell adds that actual layer to its boundary. A clear layer cannot undo an existing opaque layer.
- Clear sheet or reflective cover + an installed solar panel covers that specific source. A reflective cover shades it. Another independent uncovered panel can continue powering its own lamp.

Adding the same kind of cover twice to one target has no new modeled effect and is not offered again. This is idempotent layering, not a finite material stock. New installations and independent sources remain unlimited. All legal actions include exact effect previews; the complete concept recipes are visible even before their ingredients are established. Invalid or unknown operands do not change the physical world or knowledge.

## Prospective adversarial policies and witnesses

Before any blind play, test:
1. **Tailored garden:** make/install clear shell. It encloses the sunlit tray but does not send sunlight north.
2. **Tailored signal:** make/install reflective shell. It sends sunlight north but leaves the enclosed tray dark.
3. **Alternative garden:** install an opaque reflective shell with its solar-powered interior lamp.
4. **Alternative signal:** install a bare solar lamp. No reflector is required.
5. **Universal prefab:** make/install a lit clear shell unchanged on both boards. Expected to solve both in five ordinary-catalogue combinations, versus two for each simplest tailored shell. **This is a valid positive alternative, not a flaw to conceal.**
6. **Build everything:** establish all six composites, then install all six, without tailoring to the goal. Report exact first success and final state, not just failure of a deliberately self-blocking policy. No ban on duplicate protections or ubiquitous lamps.
7. **Repurpose:** add reflective cover to an installed clear shell. Enclosure remains, sunlight leaves the tray and reaches the observer. Add an actual powered lamp to restore interior illumination; known shell identity must not silently override the changed layers.
8. **Source cuts:** cover actual panels; check current light and detector changes. Separate persistent observer knowledge from current reception. Shade a lead before attaching its lamp to show that a named solar-lamp assembly does not guarantee light. A duplicated independent lamp must survive shading only its neighbor.

Replay exact actions from fresh state. Validate input immutability, reversed operands, malformed/unknown actions, no action/knowledge loss from an invalid attempt, truthful previews, ordinary/complete catalogue equivalence of physical behavior, and source/component retention through composition.

## Rejected draft, not a frozen condition

An earlier sketch used a grid and considered a blink convention to defeat static lighting. A preliminary critic correctly warned that this could add arbitrary location restrictions and rote toggling. That sketch was dropped before implementation. The present bounded test deliberately accepts the universal lamp route and does not claim sustained strategic tension. Do not apply that draft's rejection to a stronger claim the current protocol does not make.
