# Independent challenge: make experience an executable viewpoint

27 October 2026. Source-exposed design recommendation, not play evidence, implementation authorization or historical assertion. Read against the content architecture, paper trajectory, live-host review and next synthesis. No frozen evidence or Mars design is changed.

## Decision

Select **situated temporal practices** as the one new substrate: actors perceive a changing process through a particular interface and execute an inspectable method through a particular actuator. Experiences retain those perceptions, actions and consequences. Translation changes the interface in which the experience can be encountered or its method used. This is narrower than a simulated estuary, but stronger than mapping three event names to three other event names.

Keep the estuary as connected places and people. Do not implement forest ecology, general spatial physics, political constitutions and reflexive prediction alongside this experiment. A grove can supply an authored changing signal without being advertised as an ecological simulation. Richer prose cannot supply missing laws.

The decisive added possibility is **learning a usable relation across viewpoints**, not possessing an episode identifier. A source record, a model, a replay and an executable practice must remain different things, although named combinations can construct one from another.

## Minimal common laws

1. **Processes are short, public, piecewise-linear paths.** A path gives a quantity over time, with explicit knots and optional publicly disclosed branches selected by an observed input. Whole runs advance automatically. This supports variation of duration, offset and direction without an enumerated response table. Start with one scalar per process; two only where a chosen project requires their relation.
2. **Interfaces are actual functions.** A sensor maps the process into what its actor receives; an actuator maps an actor's command back into the process or apparatus. Examples are reversal, scaling, clipping, delay and a finite observation window. Show these functions, their examples and what information they lose. No arbitrary requirement that a particular named person alone can understand a signal.
3. **Practices are small inspectable programs over those signals.** They can detect a level crossing or reversal, measure an interval, follow a value, and start/stop an actuator. Composition creates a named functional result, not a bag of arbitrary programs. A whole practice is an operand and a whole enactment is one player choice. Automatic ticks, branch evaluations and demonstrations are not choices.
4. **Episodes retain an actual run from an actor's viewpoint.** They contain the sampled trajectory and the actor's actions/consequences, linked to the underlying actual run. A replay is a new receiving episode, not retroactive participation in its source. Forking creates two continuing actors sharing the prefix; subsequent enacted runs extend separate histories.
5. **Teaching transfers executable practices, with bindings exposed.** It does not require a receipt for every prerequisite. An instruction can be taught directly; demonstration can be used to construct it. Translation may adapt its sensor/actuator bindings. A public counterexample can show a fixed delay failing where a crossing detector works. There is no hidden learner intelligence statistic or random competence gate.

These laws have honest limits. They model timing, perception and coordinated action, not consciousness, sensory qualia, broad intelligence or a complete society. Roles only route work to the current holder. Consent only determines participation. Neither is the primary content engine.

## One concrete connected collection

Use the same **following, interval and reversal relations** in three different purposes. They need not all be hard; straightforward discoveries are legitimate progress.

**Observatory — explain and operate.** A moving light follows a public path. A tracking instrument's orientation determines what it actually sees: visibility is an interval around its orientation, not a success tag. The player combines a sensor with a following practice to make a **Tracking Portrait**: a model plus an executable tracking method. A fixed pose can serve a stationary light and must remain legal. The same following method handles a later reversed path by changing its binding, rather than discovering another permission. Historical records cannot serve a current moving view.

**Divergent relatives — learn different practical viewpoints.** Fork a willing observer after this encounter. One relative remains with the instrument; the other participates in an expedition whose guide signals direction through pressure rather than angle. Both actually enact activities. The local relative learns a useful response to an occluded segment; the traveller learns an invertible angle-to-pressure map and its timing. A **Reunion Practice** exchanges selected practices and maps. Their next runs change: the workshop relative can use the pressure guide, and the traveller can operate the interrupted tracker. Shared abilities do not erase divergent episodes or command either relative's future.

**Public performance — translate and act together.** Combine the actual expedition episode with its mapping to create a **Borrowed Horizon**, a replay whose pressure trajectory lets another participant follow that particular journey without calling it their original journey. Combine its inverse mapping with the tracking practice to make a **Tactile Observatory**: the same pressure interface now controls a live instrument rather than replaying a past trip. This is the second use of the translation, not a second performance flag. A participant can learn the relation from the performance and use it on a different public trajectory.

A second small relation provides contrast: a **Held Interval** practice starts on an upward threshold crossing and stops on a downward crossing. It can expose an uninterrupted observation window and coordinate a sustained tone across a performance. Stretching a replay changes that tone's duration; replaying the original timestamps does not follow a differently paced live process. This distinction is actual behavior, not a rule that “art must use Borrowed Seasons.”

**Succession and correspondence — retain, do not enlarge, the substrate.** A learned host receives the same practice and actuates the same instrument when its operator leaves for an actual activity. A successor can bind it to a reversed actuator without retraining the original episode. Send the score/practice to a distant willing group with a different interface; its own mapping makes the performance operable there. Local practices continue. Add delayed delivery only if the collection really needs an arrival-time consequence; it is not necessary to make reciprocal learning meaningful.

A finite endpoint is enough: two continuing relatives have exchanged useful but nonidentical experiences; a source episode has become both a repeatable performance and a reusable live-control interface; a successor operates the retained instrument; a distant group participates through its own interface. This is an integrated collection, not a demand to repeat a tiny kernel until a length target is met.

## Meaningful binary grammar

Expose concrete named results with behavior previews:

| Named combination | What changes immediately |
|---|---|
| Moving Beacon + Following Method → Tracking Portrait | Construct a bound method and its visible predicted track; first enactment can accompany construction. |
| Angle–Pressure Map + Expedition Episode → Borrowed Horizon | Construct an actual transformed trajectory with source/time relationship inspectable. |
| Borrowed Horizon + Following Method → Tactile Observatory Practice | Extract/reuse its mapping to bind a live following method; this requires an invertible map over the relevant range. |
| Divergent Family + Returned Practice → Reunion Practice | Transfer the selected usable behavior while retaining two continuations. |
| Tracking Portrait + Successor Host → Inherited Observatory | Bind and route executable tracking to a new holder; no separate citizenship installation. |

These are proposed recipe types, not already implemented legal commands. Prefer precise names for the actual effect; do not create a new named node for every scalar parameter. A map may be a catalyst rather than consumed. Known methods/maps remain inexhaustible, outputs portable, and an existing object need not remain glued to a location to preserve its history.

## Strongest universal shortcut — allow it

A full-catalogue player can build a **universal public-interface adapter**: invert every invertible sensor/actuator mapping, use one parameterized following/interval program, and dispatch it to any consenting actor or host. They can copy whole practices, use direct instructions rather than demonstration, provide a stationary backup instrument, replay exact archived signals, and make all cooperation optional. An even stronger control gives everyone that universal program initially.

This legitimately solves most instrumental observation, hosting, transfer and coordination goals. It may solve the entire finite collection quickly once the relevant actual episodes and divergent activities are enacted. Do not obstruct it with compulsory misunderstandings, per-person installation, artificial scarcity, hidden maps or historical-authority certificates. Discovery of the adapter itself might be an excellent synthesis; receiving it from the start may be a pleasant construction toy.

It does not make an invertible map out of a genuinely lossy one: clipping two distinct angles to the same pressure loses their distinction. A richer channel or another observation can repair that, if available. It also does not make a replay into a live observation, create an unexperienced journey by copying a record, or produce two diverged continuations by renaming one actor. Those are semantic differences, not excuses to force duplicate labor. If the user only wants useful service, static/manual alternatives may fully satisfy them.

## What would make this recommendation fail

- If every practice reduces to a preselected sequence and each “new viewpoint” changes only its labels, reject the substrate as the old score sketch in new prose.
- If one generic adapter makes all later constructions indistinguishable, retain it and report saturation. Do not infer that normal catalogue learning was worthless; compare the actual pleasures separately.
- If interface algebra dominates while the people and places never become meaningful, this is a signal-programming game, not yet the desired intellectual-history world.
- If branching is just a fork badge and a travel badge, remove its claim to lived possibility. There must be distinct enacted encounters and later changed abilities.
- No current evidence supports approximately 200 decisions. This proposal establishes a bounded construction language and connected endpoint to challenge on paper. It does not authorize implementation or recruitment.

**Recommendation to the parent:** specify the universal adapter and one lossy-map counterexample alongside this complete endpoint before choosing a substrate. The essential question is whether the same learned relation becomes interesting as explanation, another person's practical knowledge, translated experience and successor action. If that cannot be shown without privileged labels, stop expanding the ontology.
