# SMAC technology-tree research: intellectual history, not a gadget ladder

Research date: 26 October 2026. Scope: base *Sid Meier's Alpha Centauri* (SMAC); expansion differences flagged. This is research and original design interpretation, not a proposal to transplant SMAC content.

## Findings worth using immediately

1. **Prerequisites express intellectual relationships, not just engineering requirements.** Cyberethics requires both network infrastructure and Intellectual Integrity. Eudaimonia requires both advanced economics and the philosophy it opposes. The tree can represent material enablement, conceptual synthesis, and historical reaction.
2. **Social ideas are discoveries; adopting a regime is a separate act.** Researching Eudaimonia permits the Eudaimonic social choice. It does not automatically transform every citizen or force that government.
3. **The future reinterprets the past.** Aristotle and Nietzsche reappear in technologically unfamiliar circumstances. The interesting question is not “what replaces philosophy?” but “what becomes materially possible, newly dangerous, or newly urgent?”
4. **An invention changes ordinary life and institutions.** Machine personalities imply upbringing; forecasting changes economic governance; longevity changes political power; transcendence changes ownership, bodily discipline, and religious practice.
5. **Do not reproduce the game's certainty uncritically.** Its claims about calculable ethics and scientific economic prediction are speculative worldbuilding, not established science. Make their failures and rival schools playable.

## Verification and source hierarchy

Primary check: the original [SMAC manual, Appendix 2](https://oldgamesdownload.com/manual/sid-meier-s-alpha-centauri-windows-manual-english/) (technology tables, printed pp. 185–190; Secret Projects pp. 170–173). Originally supplied PDF URL now redirects to an HTML transcription. Table extraction is column-disordered but abbreviations and rows permit cross-checking.

The per-node links below are community-hosted reproductions of Datalinks descriptions plus reference tables. They are not official current documentation. Narrative paragraphs are useful original-game-text witnesses; the tables often silently include *Alien Crossfire* (SMAX). The archived [official technology overview](https://alphacentauri2.info/official/SidMeier-sAlphaCentauriTechnology.html) verifies the broad design: research spans military equipment, base improvements, and social engineering, with Explore/Discover/Build/Conquer priorities. It does not expose sufficient detailed edges. The host currently contains unrelated gambling links; ignore those.

### Verified base-game distinctions

- Base **Threshold of Transcendence** requires **Secrets of Creation AND Temporal Mechanics**, not Secrets of the Manifolds. The latter is an expansion substitution shown in the wiki table; the same page's narrative retains the base predecessors.
- Base **Planetary Economics** requires **Environmental Economics AND Intellectual Integrity**. Adaptive Economics in wiki tables is an expansion substitution.
- **Clinical Immortality is a Secret Project**, unlocked by **Matter Editation** (the game's spelling), not a tech node. The manual describes one extra Talent per base and doubled election votes; the wiki describes two and adds expansion protection. Avoid presenting that wiki effect as universal across versions.

## Three especially useful intellectual arcs

### A. Ethics becomes infrastructure, then a condition of new intelligence

Social Psych → Ethical Calculus; Ethical Calculus + Doctrine: Loyalty → Intellectual Integrity; Intellectual Integrity + Planetary Networks → Cyberethics; Cyberethics + Advanced Military Algorithms → Pre-Sentient Algorithms.

This is NOT a straight “philosophy turns into AI” chain: each conjunction adds a different capacity. The inference worth borrowing is that mature capabilities need social concepts as well as machinery. A new intellectual-history game could model a dangerous prototype before its governing norms exist, rather than treating ethics as a magical technical gate.

### B. Enhancement generates an ideology and then its reaction

Biomachinery + Doctrine: Initiative → Homo Superior; Homo Superior + Centauri Psi → The Will to Power; The Will to Power + Sentient Econometrics → Eudaimonia.

The final link is unusually rich: experience of domination helps formulate an alternative, while material abundance makes it practicable. Future discoveries should sometimes be *responses to consequences* of earlier successes. Do not imply every historical society must pass through the same authoritarian phase; permit alternate origins and contested reinterpretations in an original game.

### C. Machine intelligence crosses from economy into ecology

Digital Sentience + Planetary Economics → Sentient Econometrics; Sentient Econometrics + Centauri Psi → [Secrets of Alpha Centauri](https://alphacentauri2.info/wiki/Secrets_of_Alpha_Centauri.html).

The latter's description says machine thinkers extend their inquiry beyond economic data to the planet-wide ecosystem. This is cross-domain transfer with a narrative agent: the machines pursue a challenge, rather than remaining passive tools. **Original design implication:** let an institution or new kind of mind develop intellectual interests the player did not commission.

## Category hygiene and cautions for the final response

- Technologies unlock options; social choices are adopted policies; facilities are repeatable local buildings; Secret Projects are separately constructed major achievements.
- Thus Eudaimonia (tech), Eudaimonic (social choice), and Telepathic Matrix (project) must not be collapsed into one thing.
- Clinical Immortality's political consequences are a particularly strong example of medicine crossing into governance. Do not call it a discovery node.
- Threshold of Transcendence is not itself the victory. The Ascent project completes the sequence, and Voice of Planet must already be operational (manual, p. 172).
- A prerequisite edge is a gameplay design statement, not historical evidence or a rigorous causal model. Some edges also serve game balance. Explain the most generative connections without pretending every pair is philosophically necessary.
- Avoid claiming Pre-Sentient Algorithms directly precedes Digital Sentience. It is an ancestor through intervening technologies, not one of Digital Sentience's two immediate requirements.
- No gameplay executable or unmodified original data file was available in this research pass. Exact modern patch behavior, especially Clinical Immortality's Talent count, is not established beyond the edition distinctions above.

## Original concepts suggested by this structure (not SMAC content)

These are speculative design proposals, not predictions or claims about existing institutions.

- **The Right to an Unoptimized Life**: predictive coordination plus a history of algorithmic overreach produces a doctrine protecting chosen inefficiency, opacity, and refusal. Institutions improve when participation is voluntary, but forecasts lose coverage.
- **Civic Apprenticeship for Artificial Persons**: adaptive AI plus theories of moral development yields supervised civic education rather than a binary certification of personhood. The player's schools become sites of constitutional conflict.
- **Intergenerational Rotation**: radical longevity plus republican anti-domination thought produces term limits on ownership, office, or institutional control without requiring biological death. Longevity now changes inheritance and authority, not merely lifespan.
- **Ecological Standing**: ecosystem modeling plus traditions of legal representation creates institutions that advocate for systems unable to speak in human political language. Models disagree; representation is contested rather than magically objective.

The reusable design test: every future idea should specify **what made it thinkable, what made it practicable, what daily practice changes, who resists it, and which new problem its success creates**.
