# Linked future lives — prospective full-collection diagnostic

26 October 2026. This is a short full-collection diagnostic, not a 200-decision qualification. No player recruited before exact source/protocol/assignment publication is verified.

## Hypothesis and controls

The scenario tests whether discovering understandable future concepts and applying them across a continuing community is worthwhile: actual branching lives, returning experience, a learning civic house, and succession with continued contribution. It does not test whether seven recipe gates can extend a six-action settings puzzle.

Developer witnesses currently complete in **18 normal, 13 complete-catalogue, 19 normal reunion/living-witness alternative** selected combines. These are witnessed routes, not proven minima. The normal direct route discovers five capabilities but applies only Kinship, Apprentices and Unowned; Enduring Witnesses and Succession are ancestors of Unowned on that route. Both have executable optional applications. Do not call that five exercised ideas or assume the player will use all seven.

The prior nine-operation one-discovery mechanics endpoint is preserved separately and is not the assigned collection. Its evidence cannot stand in for this pilot.

Complete catalogue grants all compound capabilities ready to apply, but no fabricated actual people, fulfilled care, lived episodes or teaching. Normal already discloses all recipes. A complete catalogue shortcut does not falsify possible value in discovery; a longer prerequisite route does not establish that value. First pilot is normal. Further comparison recruitment requires a postplay interpretation decision, not automatic replication to seek praise.

## Endpoint and counts

All five public goals are required for assigned completion, with valid earlier stopping. Accept all legal direct teaching, ordinary role transfer, extra selves, mergers and equivalent methods. Do not force concept coverage.

Count every accepted player-selected discovery, application, composition, teaching, travel, role transfer, request and enactment once, including comfortable and routine operations. An accepted in-world declined request counts once. Engine-invalid or redundant attempts are recorded separately. Automatic cues, actor responses, derived goal checks, previews, inspection, reports and finish do not add gameplay selections. Preserve supported/inclusive counts if a dispute arises; do not demote preparation because a player found it routine.

## Fresh pilot procedure

One fresh no-history agent, exact immutable assignment. It may read only the public guide and use the public adapter for this session, not implementation, witnesses, earlier captures, review or protocol. Record actual participant ID, condition, exposure declaration, exact prompt and source hashes on initialization. Keep exact delivered output, before/after states, refusal/errors and hash chain. No operator construction.

Use one sparse checkpoint after `twoContinuingLives` first becomes true, or a final report if the player stops earlier. Ask exactly:

> What are you trying to do now? Since the last check, what, if anything, was worth doing, and what felt routine or unnecessary?

At completion or chosen stopping ask:

> How was the experience as a whole? What would you keep, shorten, remove or change, if anything? What led you to stop here?

Also record which public material was read and technical issues. At most one neutral postplay thematic clarification is permitted, only if the report leaves the central distinction unresolved:

> What, if anything, did discovering the future ideas add to or detract from your experience?

No construction hints, praise-seeking prompting, extra gameplay or replacement pilot to obtain approval.

## Interpretation

Independently replay exact operations and audit goal histories, especially lived versus attributed experience, actual role tenure, executable current machine behavior, and the role of discovered ideas. Separate chosen reuse, required identity compliance, thematic appreciation, actual inference and JSON/interface burden.

Positive evidence must identify something worth doing and show the relevant life/world consequence occurred. Comfortable clear progress is legitimate value. A person appreciating a possibility may matter even when a source-exposed expert already knows a shortcut.

Principal falsifiers: prerequisite/menu unlocking without valued new possibility; actors remain uninteresting input-output tables or administrative biographies; later purposes repeat routine setup; apparent choice comes from hiding rules; a claimed lived transfer is only a provenance certificate. The complete successful collection may remain very short and may warrant retaining only as a small game. No aggregate across prior demos counts toward the long-play destination.

After raw capture is preserved, obtain independent review before choosing another experiment or integrating with material construction. Building a physical house and teaching its operations is a later hypothesis, not part of this pilot.
